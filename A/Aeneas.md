::: magazinestyle
Aeneas is a Trojan prince from classical mythology. Greek and Roman tradition names him the son of Anchises and Aphrodite. He carries the role of hero in the Aeneid. The Roman poet Virgil wrote the Aeneid as a Latin epic about him. In Ireland and Scotland the name Angus stood for his counterpart. The two heroes were once read as near equals across the cultures. Aeneas entered Celtic memory through that older comparison.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Addanc is one spelling of the Welsh water beast more often named Afanc. The creature hides in the whirlpool of the river Llyn yr Afanc. That river runs through north Wales and carries the monster name. Early reports call the beast both a giant beaver and a crocodile. Tales about the Addanc survive from as early as the seventeenth century. The Welsh Triads make the creature cause a great flood. Related beasts include the Scottish kelpie and the Irish each uisce.

:::
```

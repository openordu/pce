``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arianrhod is a Welsh heroine or goddess. Her name means silver wheel, a name for the moon. She is the daughter of the mother goddess Dôn. Her tale and her name suggest an earlier moon goddess. In the fourth part of the Mabinogi she steps over the wand of King Math to prove her purity. She then bears two sons, Dylan and Lleu Llaw Gyffes. She denies the boys a name and weapons. She lives on the coastal island of Caer Arianrhod. That name also marks the constellation Corona Borealis. The Welsh Triads compare her to the ancestral goddess Branwen. Her brother and lover is the great magician Gwydion.

:::
```

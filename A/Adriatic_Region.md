``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ancient sources of Celts of the Adriatic region point to Macedon. Philip II died from a Celtic sword in 336 bc. The story comes from the works of Ptolemy and Strabo. Alexander hosted Celts from the Adriatic on a campaign. Warrior graves at Altino hold Celtic material. Two inscriptions prove the link. One uses Venetic script but read as Latin. The other joins the name Kadriako to the god Belatucadros. The Taurisci formed a group near the Sava. Roman weight reached the Adriatic in the first century bc. Caesar built forts at Iulium Carnicum and Forum Iulii.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Almha is one of the several spellings that the Irish sources give for the Hill of Allen. The word stands in the same set as the forms for the place in County Kildare. MacKillop lists it among the variants of the hill named for the hero of the tales.

:::
```

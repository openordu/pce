::: magazinestyle
Ahes stands as a Breton goddess who raised the Roman roads in Brittany. The Welsh Elen also watches over roads, and some writers connect the two. Keep this Ahes apart from Ahès, a second name for the selfish Dahut. Ahes rules as the patron of road building.

:::

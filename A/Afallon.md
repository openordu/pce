::: magazinestyle
Afallon is the Welsh title of the isle better known as Avalon. The fuller phrase Ynys Afallon means the Isle of Afallon. In Welsh lore that island is the same as Glastonbury. The word springs from the root for the apple tree. So the name ties the orchard to the otherworld of plenty. Afallon carries the old happy isle image in Welsh legend.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill Aulomm, Ailill bare-ear, fathered Eógan and the lesser figures Dichorb, Aife, and Cian. He also fostered Lugaid mac Con, who became his foster-brother in a dynastic dispute. A later story said Ailill ravished the fairy goddess Áine and she slew him. O'Rahilly judged that tale a later invention to explain the epithet. His brother was Lugaid Lága. Do not mistake him for Ailill Áine.

:::
```

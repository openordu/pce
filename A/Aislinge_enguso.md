``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aislinge Óenguso is an Old Irish narrative about the love of a god. The text comes from the Book of Leinster of about 1150. The usual English title is the Vision of Angus. Angus Óg wasted in longing over a woman met once in a dream. Bodb Derg learned that the woman was Cáer. She was the daughter of Ethal Anbúail. For alternate years Cáer lived as a human and as a swan. Angus came to her in her swan year. He changed into a swan to join her. The pair flew off to Brug na Bóinne. Their song was so fine that none slept for three days and nights.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aillén Trechenn was a monster with three heads in Irish lore. His name uses the Irish word trechend, which means three-headed. Each year he came out of a cave or mound at Cruachain in County Roscommon. He moved on the eve of Samain, the festival known as Halloween. His coming brought ruin over Ireland. He saved his worst anger for the royal sites of Tara and Emain Macha. The poet Amairgin finally slew the beast. Some writers call the creature male and others female. The cave stood under the Hill of Cruachain, home to the war goddess the Morrigan. For that reason the beast may be tied to her. His work of ruin is hard to separate from that of the other Aillén, who burned Tara.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Ardmore is a common place name of Ireland and of the north of the land of the Scot. The name means the great height, a tall hill of the land. The name stands in many of the maps of the two lands. The best known Ardmore lies in the county of Waterford on the south coast of Ireland. The town holds the tall round tower of the monks, a known sign of the old church.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Achill is a large and mountainous island off the west coast of County Mayo in Ireland. The island measures about fifteen miles by four. Legend gives Achill an ancient hawk or crow. The bird was said to live for thousands of years. It could remember the remote past. It tells its memories to Fintan mac Bóchra. The tale of the long-lived bird links the island to the deep memory of early Irish legend.

:::

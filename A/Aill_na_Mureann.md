``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aill na Mureann, also written Ail na Mirenn, is a place name linked to Uisnech. Uisnech is the hill of the stone of divisions in Irish tradition. This Aill names a spot on that sacred hill. Readers should see the Uisnech entry for the site and its lore.

:::
```

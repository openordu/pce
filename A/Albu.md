``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Albu is the old Irish name used for the island. Writers first applied the term to all of Great Britain and the land that the poet called Albion. After the Irish settled in the region of the south-west, the word took a narrower sense. It then named only the northern part of the island.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Artaios is a Continental Celtic god. Some writers spell his name Artaius. He is a bear god, kin to the goddess of wisdom. MacKillop calls him an aspect of Gaulish Mercury. His name appears to mean bear god. He was equated with the Roman Mercury by some Gaul peoples. The female bear deity Artio is the larger Continental bear figure.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Athairne Áilgesach is a sharp poet of the court of the king and a man of satire. The old name marks one who sends the hard request. He plays the rule that a man of verse may never be refused. He asked for the eye of a king of the middle of the land as a single gift. He sought cattle, women, and much else from the people of Leinster. The men of the land built him a bridge over the river, then held back the women they had promised.

:::

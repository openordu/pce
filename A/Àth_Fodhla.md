``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Àth Fodhla is the Scottish Gaelic form of Atholl, the highland district of Scotland. MacKillop records the form as the Scottish Gaelic rendering, with Fodla as a parallel Irish place-name. The principal record sits under Atholl, with FÓDLA as a related headword.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Aneirin was a Welsh bard who lived near the year 600. He worked in the old north, the Welsh-speaking lands that later became the Lowlands of Scotland. The Welsh Triads name him the prince of poets. Some call him the British Homer. He is one of the cynfeirdd, the oldest of the Welsh poets. His songs survive in the 13th-century book Canu Aneirin. His best work is the Gododdin, a lament for the lords of Catraeth who lost their lives there.

:::

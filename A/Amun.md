::: magazinestyle
Amun is an Irish chieftain in the Ossianic verse of James Macpherson. He is the father of Feard, a warrior who fell alone in a fight against the hero Cúchulain. MacKillop places the figure in the invented heroic world of the Ossian poems.

:::

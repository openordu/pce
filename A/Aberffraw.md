::: magazinestyle
Aberffraw is a royal site of the Welsh kings in the SE of Anglesey. It was the principal seat of the Gwynedd kings, whose early line claimed descent from Cunedda. The site lies twelve miles southeast of Holyhead. The cantref of Aberffraw held two commotes, Llifon to the north and Malltraeth in the south. The name is Welsh in origin. It means the mouth of a river or an estuary. Court poets used the name as a byword for royal standing and lawful power. When Llywelyn ap Gruffudd, the last native prince, died in 1282, the site was pulled down.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aberffraw is a royal site of the Welsh kings in the SE of Anglesey. It was the seat of the Gwynedd dynasty, whose early line claimed descent from Cunedda. The cantref of Aberffraw had two commotes, Llifon to the north and Malltraeth in the south. Court poets used the name as a byword for royal standing and lawful power. When Llywelyn ap Gruffudd, the last native prince, died in 1282, the site was pulled down.

:::
```

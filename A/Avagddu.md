::: magazinestyle
Avagddu is the English form of the Welsh name Afagddu, a term that carries the sense of total darkness. The dictionary leads the reader who meets this spelling to the fuller entry under Afagddu. The name belongs to a figure of Welsh tradition with a place in the old stories of the cauldron.

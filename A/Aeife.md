``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aeife is one spelling of the warrior woman more often written Aife. The early Irish stories call her an Amazonian chieftain. They name her the most dangerous woman fighter of all. She lived in Alba, the land now known as Scotland. Aeife clashed again and again with the queen Scathach. The Ulster hero Cuchulainn defeated her in single combat. He later begot a son named Connla upon her. That son met death from his own father in a tragic tale.

:::
```

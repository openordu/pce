::: magazinestyle
Aife Foltfhind is the daughter of a king of Ulster in early Irish legend. Her name means Aife of the fair hair. The dictionary points the reader to the names Mongfind and Mong Ban. The fair mane in her name echoes her own. She is one of many women called Aife in the old tales.

:::

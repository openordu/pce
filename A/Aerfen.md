::: magazinestyle
Aerfen is a minor river goddess of early Wales. Her name carries the gloss renowned in battle. Legend links Aerfen to the river Dee and its mouth. The Welsh records place her among the local water powers. Her portrait stays thin and gives only a small shape. Aerfen served as one of the modest divine figures honoured in early Britain. The record preserves her name without much detail.

:::

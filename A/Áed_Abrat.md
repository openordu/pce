``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áed Abrat, light of the eye in Irish, is the father of Fand and the usual father of Li Ban. Some sources name Eochaid as Li Ban's sire instead. Áed Abrat took great pride in his daughters' healing song. A son of his is the Angus who invites Cúchulainn to listen while Fand and Li Ban perform the healing air. The principal record sits under the Fand headword.

:::
```

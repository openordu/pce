``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ábartach is a Fenian Cycle figure whose Irish name means the feat-performing one. He appears in the saga tradition as the offspring of the ruler of Tír Taimgire and the sire of Cáel's unnamed beloved. In surviving narrative his first appearance comes under the byname the Hard Gilly, with Giolla Deacair carrying a matching hard-servant sense. His daughter Aife underwent a transformation into a heron at the hands of her jealous rival Iuchra. The same name is occasionally applied to Céadach in related manuscript sources.

:::
```

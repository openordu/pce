::: magazinestyle
Old Atholl numbered among the four Pictish royal lands. Its bounds answer to the land called Perthshire until 1974, now the north-west of Tayside. The district is a wooded, mountainous place at the south tip of the Grampian range. The ground is hard to farm, yet it holds a wide deer forest.

:::

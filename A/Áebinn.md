``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áebinn is a variant spelling of Aibell, the Munster fairy queen of oral tradition. MacKillop reads the form as a regional variant of the headword and points readers to Aibell. The principal record of that Munster fairy tradition sits under the Aibell headword.

:::
```

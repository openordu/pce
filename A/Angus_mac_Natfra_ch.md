::: magazinestyle
Angus mac Natfraích was a leader of the Eoganacht in the fifth century. He ruled as king of Cashel. He was reputedly baptized by Saint Patrick. One tale says the staff of Patrick pierced his foot at the blessing. The king stayed quiet, thinking it part of the rite.

:::

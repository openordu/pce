::: magazinestyle
Angau, also spelled angeu, personifies death within early Welsh tradition. The dictionary sets this figure beside the death figures of the other Celtic lands. Those cross-noted figures are ankou in Brittany, the death coach and dullahan of Ireland, the far dorochai, and the yannig. Each of their records carries the fuller account of its own figure.

:::

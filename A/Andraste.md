``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Andraste was a British goddess of war. Her name carries the sense of the invincible one. The queen Boudicca of the Iceni summoned her name in a sacred grove. That came before the queen marched her army against the Romans. Worshippers offered hares to Andraste in the grove. A hare seems too small for a war deity at first. Yet the animal turns fierce when caught, so it suits the idea of defence. When the fight turned against her, Boudicca took her own life rather than face the mockery of her captors.

:::
```

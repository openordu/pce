``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Airmid was an Irish goddess, a healer among the divine race of the Tuatha Dé Danann. These were the children of the goddess Danu. She was a daughter of the physician Dian Cécht and the sister of Miach. When King Nuada lost his hand, her father made one of silver. With her brother she grew real flesh over the silver. In jealousy, the father killed his son Miach. Airmid then sought to sort the healing herbs that grew on her brother's grave. Her father scattered those herbs before she could set them in order. The Irish said the loss of her herb lore made human pain much greater afterward. Her gift for healing and her lost lore sit at the heart of her story.

:::
```

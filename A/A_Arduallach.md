::: magazinestyle
Aí Arduallach is the proud daughter of Fionn mac Cumhaill in the Irish stories. The part of her name that reads Arduallach carries the sense of arrogant. She angered her father by refusing the king of Scotland. She also refused any man who was not from Ireland. Her stubborn pride fills the tale about her.

:::

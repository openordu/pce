::: magazinestyle
Abaris the Hyperborean is a legendary figure of the far north. Greek tradition placed the Hyperboreans beyond the North Wind. Abaris was one of their number. Historical record says he once argued with the philosopher Pythagoras. Eighteenth-century writers guessed that he was a druid. They made him either Welsh or Scottish Gaelic. Modern scholarship takes a different view. The evidence suggests he was a shaman from central Asia. The tale of Abaris shows how the Celts entered the classical imagination as a mysterious northern people.

:::

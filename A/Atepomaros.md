``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Atepomaros is a word of the old Gaul that means the holder of the great horse. The image names one face of the bright god Apollo in the old lands. The scholars of the runes take it for a title of that god. The name stands among the many god words of the old continent.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Afagddu, also spelled Yfagdddu, was a boy of great plainness in Welsh myth. His name means utter darkness. People also knew him as Morfran, the sea crow. That was his second name. His mother, the hag-goddess Ceridwen, feared that no woman would take him. She brewed herbs in her cauldron, so that the power of verse might win him a wife. The servant Gwion drank the brew too early. He drank away its magic and rose as the great poet Taliesin. After that the tale tells nothing more of Afagddu. It is uncertain whether the cauldron bore Morfran as an ugly brother or as a second name.

:::
```

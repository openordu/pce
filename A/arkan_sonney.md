::: magazinestyle
arkan sonney is the Manx fairy pig. English speakers on the Isle of Man also call it the lucky piggy. Tradition usually gives the animal a white coat and red ears, a colouring shared by many Celtic supernatural creatures. The pig can change its size, yet observers report no change of shape. People believe it brings luck to anyone who catches it, but the animal is hard to catch.

:::

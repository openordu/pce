``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aided Énfir Aífe is the Irish tale of the violent death of a single son. Cú Chulainn, the great hero of the Ulster Cycle, had one son, named Connla. The boy's mother was Aífe, a woman warrior who lived in Alba, across the sea. Cú Chulainn met the youth by chance on a field and, failing to know him, struck the youth down. The deed is a slaying of kin, a great shame in Irish law. The hero takes the path for the good of his people, but the tale weighs his honour against the loss of his only child. A second version, the death of Connla son of Cú Chulainn, tells the same story.

:::
```

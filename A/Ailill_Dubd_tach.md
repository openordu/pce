::: magazinestyle
Ailill Dubdétach, Ailill black tooth, is the monstrous brother of the fair Delbcháem. In the Echtrae Airt meic Cuinn he challenges the hero Art mac Cuinn. Art kills the ugly brother in that contest. The tale sets a brute against a champion.

:::

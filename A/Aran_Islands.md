::: magazinestyle
The Aran Islands are a group of bare islands off the west coast of Ireland. They lie across the mouth of Galway Bay. The three great isles are Inishmore, Inishmaan, and Inisheer. The largest, Inishmore, carries the great stone ring of Dun Aonghusa. The island group is one of the strongholds of the old Irish tongue. The writer John Millington Synge drew the life of the island into his plays.

:::

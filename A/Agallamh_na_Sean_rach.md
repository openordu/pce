``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Agallamh na Seanórach is the modern Irish spelling of the older title Acallam na Senórach. This Fenian Cycle narrative dates from the late twelfth century. It survives in the fifteenth century Book of Lismore. The work presents the aged survivors Oisín and Caílte in dialogue with Saint Patrick. Agallamh na Seanórach means the colloquy of the elders.

:::
```

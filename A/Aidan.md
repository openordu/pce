``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aidan is a common English form of the Irish name Áedán. The headword Aidan therefore points back to Áedán. Many saints and heroes in early Ireland bore this name. The anglicized spelling Aidan is widely used today.

:::
```

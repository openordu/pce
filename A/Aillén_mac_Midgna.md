``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aillén mac Midgna was the great destroyer of Tara in Irish story. Each Samain he came to the royal court there. He played on his small harp, the timpan, until the men of Tara slept. Then he sent flaming rock from his mouth to burn the hall down. Tara was rebuilt again and again. For twenty-three years Aillén returned. At last the young hero Fionn mac Cumhaill came to the help of Tara. He breathed on the point of his own poisoned spear to stay awake. The poison was so strong that it forbade sleep. Thus he resisted the music and speared Aillén. The fairy mound of Finnam was his home, and it served at other times for the sea king Lir. The scholar T. F. O'Rahilly said that Aillén was really the warrior Goll mac Morna. Most later scholars do not follow him.

:::
```

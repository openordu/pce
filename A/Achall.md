``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Achall is an Irish heroine. Her father was the Ulster warrior Cairbre Nia Fer. A hill east of Tara, the seat of kings, carries her name. She died from grief when Conall Cernach killed her brother Erc. Such love for her brother placed her among the six loyal women of ancient Ireland.

:::
```

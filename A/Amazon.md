``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Amazon names the women warriors of the Greek tradition in the Irish book of the Lebor Gabála. The text cites the Amazons only briefly. The same role in the Irish tales more often falls to the women who teach the craft of arms. MacKillop lists among these the teachers Scáthach, Aífe, and Búanann.

:::
```

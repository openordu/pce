``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailbe Grúadbrecc, daughter of Cormac mac Airt, was sister to Gráinne. Her name means Ailbe of the freckled cheeks. Fionn mac Cumhaill courted her after Gráinne left him. He posed her a series of riddles. He asked her to dwell in the woods and become his bride. Cailte judged that Ailbe stood among the four finest lovers of the women of Ireland in his time. Tochmarc Ailbe, the Wooing of Ailbe, records her story.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aided Málae Fháthartaig maic Rónáin names the same tale as Fingal Róndín. That work tells How Rónán Killed His Son. The Irish title means the Death of Máel Fothartaig, Rónán's son. Both titles describe a single story.

:::
```

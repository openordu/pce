::: magazinestyle
Aonbárr is an Irish mythological beast. He is the enchanted steed of the Irish sea god Manannan Mac Lir. His name means froth, and it suggests the seafoam. MacKillop writes that Aonbárr could gallop across both land and sea with equal ease. He is also called Enbarr, and that name means foam or froth. Manannan usually rode him in a chariot, but sometimes he rode him straight on horseback. The sea god could then travel over the waves to reach the lands of the Otherworld. Aonbárr stands as a ready symbol of the roaring surf that borders the realm of the gods. Only a being with such a steed could pace the waves as the mighty master of the seas.

:::

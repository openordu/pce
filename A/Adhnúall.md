``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Adhnúall means sweet of sound. He is one of many hounds that served the hero Fionn Mac Cumhaill. A British prince named Arthur once stole the hound. Following a fight in Leinster, Adhnúall ran around Ireland three times. He came back to that battlefield afterward. He died there, where three Fenians and their lovers lay buried. He travels with his master less often than the primary hounds Bran and Sceolan do.

:::
```

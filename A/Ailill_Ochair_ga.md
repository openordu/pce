``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill Ochair Ága, Ailill edge of battle, fathered Máel Dúin. He served as a warrior of the Eóganacht of Ninuss on the Aran Islands. He begot his son by wooing a nun who came to strike the midnight bell. The mother died giving birth. A queen, friend to the mother, then fostered the child. Marauders from Laois later killed Ailill. The Immram Curaig Máel Dúin tells the voyage of his son.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arawn is a Welsh god. He is the lord of Annwn, the Welsh Otherworld. His world is a typical Celtic Otherworld. It is beautiful and unchanging, without pain, death, or disease. Its residents pass their time in merrymaking and games. Koch calls him the king of Annwfn in the Mabinogi's opening branch. Arawn owned a magical cauldron. It suggests a parallel with the Dagda, the Irish father god. Arawn had magical pigs, another motif of the Dagda. He handed the pigs to the mortal king Pryderi as a gift. The theft cost Gwydion his freedom, and Annwn held him prisoner after he took the pigs. Three magical animals of Arawn were stolen by the obscure god Amaethon. The motif holds that domesticated animals once belonged to the gods. People had to bring them to this world by force or trickery. In the Mabinogi tale of Pwyll, Arawn exchanged identities with the mortal king for a year. Pwyll spent his year in the Otherworld and killed Arawn's enemy and rival, Hafgan. Koch adds a rule to that combat. A single blow could kill Hafgan, but a second blow gave him back his strength. So the victor had to strike once and stop. Pwyll learned the rule and ended the long war in one stroke. In return Arawn built a model kingdom in Dyfed, known for warmth, kindness, and a clear sense of justice. An early poem about the spoils of Annwfn still remembers the king and his realm.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aíbgréne is an Irish heroine. In some accounts she is the child of the doomed lovers Deirdre and Noisiu. Her name is also written Aib Grene and Aoibhgreine. It carries the sense of radiance of the sun. Later tradition makes her the wife of Rinn. Rinn was a son of Eochaid Iuil, king of the Otherworld. The tale of her parents is one of the best-known stories of the Ulster cycle. Deirdre and Noisiu were fated lovers who fled Ireland together. Their return brought betrayal and the death of both. The tradition names Aibgrene as the fruit of that tragic union. Her single appearance adds a descendant to the doomed line.

:::
```

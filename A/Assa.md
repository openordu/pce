``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Assa is the original gentle name of the goddess Nessa. She is also spelled Ness and Nes. Nessa is remembered as the mother of Conchobar mac Nessa, king of Ulster. The name Assa means the gentle one. Her father Eochaid Sálbuide was a ruler of Ulster. He raised her in dignity and comfort. She studied under twelve tutors. The druid Cathbad killed all twelve tutors in one night to reach her. Nessa took up arms and gathered warriors to find the killer. From this violent turn she gained the name Nessa, meaning ungentle. She later bore a son whose name, Conchobar mac Nessa, means son of Nessa. Conchobar became king of Emain Macha and of Ulster. MacKillop tells how the druid Cathbad lay with Ness after she asked what the hour was lucky for. He answered that it was the hour to beget a king upon a queen. A rival version names Fachtna Fáthach as both father and husband. Ness used her skill to win the throne for her son. She asked Fergus mac Róich to give up the crown for a year. Conchobar reigned so that he could be called a king's son. The separate Welsh giant Llassar Llaes Gyfnewid is a different figure. He owned the cauldron of regeneration in the Mabinogi. He is not to be confused with Assa or Nessa. Where Assa means peace and gentleness, Nessa's hard course led to one of the greatest thrones of early Irish story.

:::
```

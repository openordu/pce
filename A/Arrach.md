``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arrach is a ghost of the folk tales of the Scottish Gaels. The old word also carries the sense of a small shape of a man. The stories of the land call the creature a monster that haunts the open fields. Writers often confuse this spirit with the named Tarrach. Some of the same tales give the ghost the other name of Biast.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aobh is the modern Irish text of the old Irish name that MacKillop writes as Ahbh. The name is that of a woman in the old tales, tied to the fey of the Irish. The dictionary keeps the modern shapes as a separate line.

:::
```

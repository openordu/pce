::: magazinestyle
Ardee is a town in the county of Louth in Ireland. The town stands on the river wee, about fourteen miles north of Drogheda. The name comes from the Irish words of the ford of Fordia. The place marks where the hero Ferdia died in the great raid for the cattle of Cooley.

:::

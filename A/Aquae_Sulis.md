``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aquae Sulis is a British goddess. The Romans named the shrine and hot springs at Bath in England Aquae Sulis, the waters of Sulis. The local sun goddess Sulis stood at the centre of worship there. The Romans merged her with the goddess Minerva, and the joined cult then took in several other native deities. Bath is still famous for its hot mineral springs. The temple there carried the name Sulis Minerva. Excavations recovered coins, offering vessels, and inscribed curse tablets. The names on the tablets are mostly Roman, beside a notable Celtic share.

:::
```

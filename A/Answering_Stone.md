::: magazinestyle
The Answering Stone is the rough rock of County Waterford in Ireland. Its Irish name means the rock that speaks. The old tale places the stone in a townland of the region. The rock would split apart if it were asked to give proof to a lie. The heavy stone works in Irish belief as a test of truth.

:::

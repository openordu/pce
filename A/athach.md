``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The athach is a monster of Scottish Gaelic folklore. Its name means giant, champion, or monster. It resembles Old Irish aithech, a word for boor or serf. The athach haunted the Scottish Highlands and killed passersby, hurling them into gorges or down rocky hills. Tradition also set it in lonely lochans and lakes. The athach had male and female variants. The female luideag haunted Skye. The male díreach of Glen Etive had one hand growing from his chest and one eye in his forehead. Folklore compares the athach to the bòcan, the díreach, the fachan, and the luideag. The figure connects to the Fachan.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Anluan is a title given to several figures in early Ireland. A set of these belong to the kin of the great Brian, later king in the south. The most famous Anluan was a son of Maga and a brother of Cet. He appears in the tale of the great pig of Mac Da Tho.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Alchfrith was a son of Oswydd, king of the Northumbrians. He stood as heir apparent at the battle of Winwæd in 655. His father refused his wish to make a pilgrimage to Rome with Saint Benedict. That refusal helped his half-brother Ecgfrith pass him in the succession. Alchfrith opposed the Irish clergy and their teaching. The Welsh Historia Brittonum names him, while the Irish sources omit him. His end is unknown; a quiet retirement to religion appears likely.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aillén (Ellen, Aillene) is a name given to several legendary Irish figures. A bearer may be a hero, a heroine, or a spirit. The name is related to Irish words for sprite and for monster. Several bearers are so similar that they may be the same being. They may also descend from one original. The most famous is Aillén mac Midgna, the burner of Tara. Another bearer is Aillén Trechenn, the triple-headed monster. One Aillén was the sibling of the fairy queen Áine. He came to love a wife of the sea god Manannán mac Lir. When Manannán turned his attention to Áine, he gave the wife in turn to Aillén. MacKillop records the variant spellings Ailéne and Oillinn for the name.

:::
```

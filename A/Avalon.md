``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Avalon is the Elysium of the Arthurian legends. The name comes from the old Welsh word for apple. It is the place where King Arthur and other heroes went at death. People usually set it on the western seas. Some writers identify it with Glastonbury. The island is a place of mild weather and eternal spring. Flowers and fruit grow on the same trees, and no storm rages there. A queen rules the island, sometimes named Argante. She and her maidens are great healers. A shadowy figure named Avalloc appears as its lord in some legends. To the Celts, Avalon was one of the western isles of the Otherworld where the dead and the divine mingle.

:::
```

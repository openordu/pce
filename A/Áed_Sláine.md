``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áed Sláine was a king of Tara near the year 600, named in many genealogies. Legend records his mother Mugain Mór, a territorial goddess of Munster, as birthing a trout before his own birth. MacKillop reads the form as a Tara high king with a wondrous birthmark. The principal record sits under the Tara headword.

:::
```

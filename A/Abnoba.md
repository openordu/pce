``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Abnoba is a Celtic goddess honoured in Britain and on the mainland. She gave her name to rivers called Avon, as the one in England that runs through Stratford. Rome called the Black Forest Mons Abnoba in her honor, and streams from its eastern slopes meet to form the young river Danube. A stone statue from Mühlburg shows her dressed as Diana, touching a tree while a hound and a hare rest beneath. A relief at the source of the Brigach adds a stag and a bird to her company. The hare ties her to the same ritual world as the goddess Andraste. Inscriptions from Muhlbach and Rothenburg keep her name, and a bronze native Diana in Köln may show her or the goddess Arduinna. Pliny and Tacitus both name her in their writings. Her image shows a huntress, her hunting dog, and a stag beside her.

:::
```

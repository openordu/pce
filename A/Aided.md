``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aided is an Old Irish word meaning violent death or the act of killing. It also carries the sense of an unhappy fate. Many Old Irish narrative titles begin with this word. The plural form is Oitte. The modern Irish equivalents are oidheadh and oidhe. Aided sets the tone of a tale.

:::
```

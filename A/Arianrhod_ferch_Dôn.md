``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arianrhod ferch Dôn stands as a daughter of Dôn within the Welsh Mabinogi. Her story belongs to the Third Branch, the tale of Math. She is the mother of Lleu Llaw Gyffes. She places hard conditions upon her own son. He may gain a name and weapons only when she grants them. Her refusals set the course of Gwydion's magic. Arianrhod is proud, aloof, and formidable. Later writers link her to a wheel of stars in the northern sky. She shapes the fates of kings through stubborn will and fierce words.

:::
```

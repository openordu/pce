::: magazinestyle
Adene is a variant form of the Old Irish name Etain. The poet William Butler Yeats favoured this particular spelling. His verse carried the name before many readers of his time. Etain appears as a beauty in the lasting Irish love stories. The name Adene belongs to a wider family of spellings for that one figure. Each form points back to the same legendary heroine of early tales.

:::

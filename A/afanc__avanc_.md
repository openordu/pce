``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The afanc (avanc), also called the Addanc, was a monster of Welsh tale. It haunted a whirlpool in the River Conwy in north Wales. Some stories place its lair at Llyn yr Afanc. People told tales of it as far back as the seventeenth century. It could take the shape of a giant beaver, or at times a crocodile. Its name means a water-dwarf, yet the beast was great. In the Welsh Triads, this monster causes the great flood. That flood drives two figures, Dwyfan and Dwyfach, to build an ark so the animals survive. In that account the afanc lives at Llyn Llion, which may be Bala Lake. The hero Peredur may have killed it, yet the stories went on. A person could tame the beast, but only by luring it to the lap of a maiden. It could then be held in chains. One maiden who tried lost her life, for the monster fled back into its lake. The creature resembles the Scottish kelpie and the Irish each uisce.

:::
```

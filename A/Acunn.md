::: magazinestyle
Acunn is a hero of Scottish Fenian narrative. The Fenian tales follow the deeds of Fionn mac Cumhaill and his warriors. The strait Kyle of Akin takes its name from him. That water lies between Skye and the Lochalsh shore. Acunn had a brother named Riadh. The hero's name keeps a hold on the geography of the western Highlands.

:::

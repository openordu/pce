::: magazinestyle
Áedán is a diminutive of the Irish name Áed. The form occurs widely across early Ireland and Gaelic Scotland, borne by no fewer than twenty-three saints and by many figures in lore. Common anglicized forms include Aidan and Edan. The saint-bearers span the foundation narratives of Iona and Lindisfarne. The legendary Áedáns include warriors and poets. The form stands as a generic Gaelic name covering all those bearers.

:::

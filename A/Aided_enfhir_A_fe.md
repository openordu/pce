``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aided Óenfhir Aífe tells of the tragic death of the only son of Aífe. It serves as a short foretale to the Táin Bó Cúailnge. The hero Cúchulainn kills his own son Connla in combat. He does not know the boy's identity at the time. The story mirrors the Persian tale of Sohrab and Rustam. Scholars assign it the folk motif N731.2.

:::
```

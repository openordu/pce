``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aeron is a god of battle or slaughter in early Wales. His name appears to derive from the Irish figure Agrona. The link between Aeron and the Scottish place name Ayr stays disputed. Some scholars defend the connection, while others reject it. Aeron holds a place among the war gods of ancient Britain. The records keep the details of his cult thin and uncertain.

:::
```

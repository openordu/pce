``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arvor is the name of the old people of Brittany for the coast of that land. The sense of the word is the by-the-sea, the shore of the west. The people set the name against the word that marks the inner wood of the coast. The modern region at the north of the land draws its title from the same root.

:::
```

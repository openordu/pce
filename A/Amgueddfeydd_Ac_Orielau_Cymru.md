::: magazinestyle
Amgueddfeydd ac Orielau Cymru is the Welsh title of the national body that runs the museums and galleries of Wales. This institution is a principal cultural and heritage body of Wales. It was founded in 1907. Its mission teaches the world about Cymru and teaches the Welsh people about their land. Today the organisation runs seven public sites. Its collections hold around 4.7 million items. The main archaeology and art collections sit in Cardiff city centre. These include Bronze and Iron Age treasures such as the Caergwrle bowl. St Fagans presents the Museum of Welsh Life as an open-air site. It preserves buildings from several centuries and records the Welsh language and its dialects.

:::

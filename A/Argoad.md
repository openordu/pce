``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Argoad is a word of the old tongue of the people of the northwest of the coast. The word names the inner wood of the province of the land of the west. The people of the coast set this name of the forest against the name of the shore. The two words mark the line between the wood and the sea in the old speech.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Alcluith was the old British name of the stronghold on the rock. The rock stands where the river meets the Clyde at Dumbarton in Scotland. Its form appears in the History of Beda for the year 731. The name gave the root for the later poetic name Balclutha.

:::
```

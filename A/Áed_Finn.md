::: magazinestyle
Áed Finn, the fair Aed in Irish, is the author of the Imram Curaig Máile Dúin tale. One surviving manuscript names him the chief scholar of Ireland. A second Aed Finn appears in the annals with a death recorded in the year 777, as a king of Dál riada, though no mention of scholarly attainment appears there. The principal record sits under the imram headword.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aoibheall is a modern Irish spelling of the name Aibell. The word also appears as Aoibhell and Aoibhil in the source. Aibell counts as a fairy queen of the old Irish, ruling the north of Munster. Her fairy hill is the rock Craig Liath, close to the town of Killaloe in County Clare. She was a patron of the O'Brien kindred and a rival of the goddess Clidna. In one tale Clidna cast a charm over her and turned her into a white cat.

:::
```

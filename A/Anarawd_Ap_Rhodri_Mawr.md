``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Anarawd ap Rhodri Mawr ruled Gwynedd from 878 to 916. He became the strongest king in Wales during his long reign. The contemporary writer Asser counted him among the six sons of Rhodri. In the 880s Anarawd made an alliance with Northumbria and its Viking king at York. This alliance was aimed against Alfred of Wessex. The southern Welsh kings instead joined Alfred, which blocked a unified Wales under Gwynedd. By the early 890s Anarawd had broken with the Vikings and submitted to Alfred. By 893 Asser could call most of Wales under Alfred's overkingship. His death notice crowns Anarawd as king of the Britons.

:::
```

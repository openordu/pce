``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailean nan Sop, Allan of the Straws, is a well-known figure in Scottish Gaelic folk-tales. A jealous wife of the man's own father delayed his birth by twenty-four months. Because of that delay he was already carrying every tooth when he arrived. J. G. Campbell put the figure in Witchcraft and Second Sight, his study of the Scottish Highlands and Islands.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ánroth, travelling or glowing wheel in Irish, is a mythological ancestor of the Éoganacht dynasty of Munster. In the older tract Cóir Anmann this Ánroth is identified with the figure Rothechtaid Rotha, the Great Traveller of the Wheel. The form appears in early pedigrees of the southern Irish dynasty. The royal epithet pairs the wandering wheel with the named ancestor in the genealogical tradition. MacKillop reads the figure as a key figure in Éoganacht royal myth.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áindle is the Old Irish spelling of Ainnle, one of the trio of sons of Uisnech in the longes tradition. MacKillop reads the form as the early Irish variant of the headword. The principal record sits under the Ainnle headword.

:::
```

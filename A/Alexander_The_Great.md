::: magazinestyle
Alexander the Great, the Macedonian king Alexander III, lived from 356 to 323 BC. Celtic envoys from the Adriatic met him and won his audience. Asked what they feared most, they said they feared nothing but the sky falling. They prized his friendship above every danger. His link to the Celts runs through coinage. His gold and his father Philip II of Macedon's modeled the later Celtic money of Europe and Britain. The Welsh poets of the Book of Taliesin and the court poet Cynddelw cast him as a martial paragon.

:::

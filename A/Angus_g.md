``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Angus Óg stands for youth and beauty among the Tuatha Dé Danann. A love god is possible too. His father was the Dagda and his mother was Boand, the river. To hide the birth, his parents gave him to Elcmar as foster father. His home was Brug na Bóinne, the house of the Boyne. He kept a cloak of invisibility and a magic sword. He guarded the hero Diarmait of the Fenian cycle. He shared the drink of endless life, and four swans flew about his head. The dream tale of Angus tells how he found his love in sleep and joined her as a swan.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill mac Máta, sometimes mac Máthach, was the husband of Medb in the Táin Bó Cuailnge. Many call him a man of Connacht, yet the text locates him in Leinster within the kin of Russ Ruad. In the pillow talk at the start of the tale he seems subdued, yet Medb praised his courage. He insisted that Medb hold no more goods than he. Their daughters were Finnabair and Faife. In the Táin Bó Fraích he opposed the marriage of Finnabair to Fráech. He helped Angus Óg to besiege Ethal Anbúail. In the Echtra Nerai he sent Nera on his quest. Medb later found him unfaithful and Conall Cearnach slew him at her urging.

:::
```

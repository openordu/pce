``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ana is the chief goddess of the old faith of Ireland. She is the mother and nurse of the folk called the Tuatha Dé Danann. The Cormac's Glossary of the tenth century links her name to wealth and to the land. The D-form turns her name into Dana or Danu. She is likely the grandmother of Ecne, the personification of knowledge. She also stands as mother to the three sons of Tuireann, whose names are Brian, Iuchair, and Iucharba.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Assaroe is an Irish mythological site. It is a waterfall, once a cataract, on the River Erne. It lay one mile west of Ballyshannon in County Donegal. The falls were famed for their beauty and their salmon. Legends made them famous. In some stories Fionn mac Cumhaill caught the salmon of wisdom there. That salmon bore the name Goll Essa Ruaid. The druid Finneces had awaited it for seven years. A hydroelectric project has now flooded the fall in modern times. The salmon pass by a fish ladder. A place-name story says the falls were named for Aed Ruad, Aed the red. Aed was the father of Macha Mong Ruad, a famed warrior woman. In Irish the falls are Eas Ruadh, the red waterfall. In later legend the falls recall a goddess named Ruad. Ruad drowned there after mermaids lured her by song. Her death marks the release of her power into the living water.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Accasbél was a Partholonian of early Irish legend. The Partholonians were among the early settlers of Ireland. The tradition credits Accasbél with erecting the first guest-house on the island. A guest-house held a place of honour as a centre of welcome and hospitality. His deed marks an early step in the civil life of the island as the legends describe it.

:::

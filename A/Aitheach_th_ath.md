``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aitheach-thúath names the vassal peoples who paid rent. The Irish plural aithech-thuatha carries the same meaning. In the Lebor Gabála they were pre-Gaelic peoples of Ireland. These people rose against the Gaelic Milesians. Their rebellion won only short-lived success. The Aitheach-thúath were sued for the later power of the invaders.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áeda is a MacKillop cross-reference pointing to the Áedán headword. MacKillop reads the form as a parallel Gaelic name form attached to the same bearer. The principal record of the common Gaelic saint-name tradition sits under the Áedán headword.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Avoca is a name that Thomas Moore made famous in his song The Meeting of the Waters. The place answers to an older form in the geography of Ptolemy. It is the same as the modern Avonmore in County Wicklow. The meeting of the Avonmore and Avonbeg rivers gives the song its subject and its title.

:::
```

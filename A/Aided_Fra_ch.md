::: magazinestyle
Aided Fraích belongs to the great Ulster saga. This work forms one piece of the Táin Bó Cúailnge. That saga goes by the name Cattle Raid of Cooley. Many editors count it as chapter five. The tale records the violent end of the hero Fraích.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Alesia was a fortified town in the eastern part of Gaul, in the region today called Burgundy. The historian Diodorus Siculus wrote that the Greek hero Heracles founded the town after an affair with the Gaulish princess. The town served as the base of the chieftain Vercingetorix in his war against Rome in 52.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Annals of the Four Masters is the English title of an Irish chronicle written between 1632 and 1636. The record pretends to start near the end of the biblical flood, at a stated year of the world 2242. Much of the early part is a bare list of names, dates, and battles. The text turns into a smoother literary history as it reaches its own era. It stands apart from the narrative history of Geoffrey Keating, drawn up at the same time. The leading compiler was the Franciscan lay brother Micheal O Cleirigh, a man of County Donegal. The later title Four Masters was fixed by Sean Mac Colgain at Louvain around 1645. John O'Donovan printed an English text in seven volumes at Dublin, 1849 to 1851.

:::
```

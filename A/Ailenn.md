Ailenn is a variant of Dún Ailinne, the royal hillfort in eastern Ireland. The alternate spelling marks the same place in early legend. Ailenn fits the same frame as the fuller Dún Ailinne. Readers should see the Dún Ailinne entry for details of the fort and its rites.

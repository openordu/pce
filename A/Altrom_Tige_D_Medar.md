::: magazinestyle
Altrom Tige Dá Medar is a late Middle Irish tale of the Mythological Cycle. Its title is the Nurture of the Two Houses of the Milk. The text sits in the Book of Fermoy of the fifteenth century. It tells how the Tuatha Dé Danann scatter into the world of men. Their defeat came at Tailtiu and Druim Aiglen. On the advice of Manannán mac Lir the people go to the fairy mounds and choose Bodb as their king. Manannán gives the folk the veil of invisibility, the feast of Goibniu, and the Pigs of Manannán. Some scholars see a distant echo of the French story of the Holy Grail.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Andarta was a Gaulish goddess. Her name, read in the old tongue, says great bear. People scratched her name into stones at Bern in Switzerland. They also wrote it on stones in the south of France. The closing syllable art appears in the names of many bear deities. Scholars therefore place her among the bear gods. Some readers link her with the war goddess Andraste. Others link her with the bear goddess Artio. She may have been the partner of Artio in that office.

:::
```

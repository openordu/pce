``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aurmumu is the old-Irish term for the eastern part of Munster. The word means the east of Munster and answers to the later name of Ormond. The early books of the island use the form for the eastern reach of the South. It stands as the counterpart of the name for the whole of Munster.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Armes Prydain is the name of a poem of the old Britons. The title of the work means the prophecy of Britain. The verse calls on the many peoples of the island to unite and push the Saxons from the shore. It was written in the tenth hundred of years of the present age. The poem stands as a proud piece of the old Welsh books.

:::
```

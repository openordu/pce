::: magazinestyle
Áed Ruad, red Aed in Irish, was the father of Macha, who founded Emain Macha. The annals note that he ruled Ireland for seven years as the third in a 21-year joint sequence with his kinsmen Cimbáeth and Dithorba. The pact was sealed by seven magidans, seven bards, and seven Ulster lords who served as guarantors. Aed Ruad later drowned in the waterfall that bears his name, the Assaroe at Eas Ruaidh. After his death his daughter Macha won election to rule in his place. Cimbáeth and Dithorba first opposed her, but she reconciled with Cimbáeth and wed him, and she killed Dithorba.

:::

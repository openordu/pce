``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ard Éireann marks a peak inside the Slieve Bloom range of central Ireland. The phrase translates as height of Ireland. The summit falls short of the tallest point in the land. Myth names that summit as the place where Ireland itself was born. MacKillop lists the headword Éire and calls it a modern spelling of Ériu. The dictionary entry points readers from Éire to the older goddess form. Together the two sources treat Ard Éireann as a geographic anchor that carries a mythic weight. The peak links a real mountain to the origin story of the island.

:::
```

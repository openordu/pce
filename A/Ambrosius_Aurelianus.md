``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ambrosius Aurelianus led the Britons after their revolt against the Saxon settlers. Gildas describes him as the final true Roman in Britain. His ancestors had worn the purple, a mark of Roman rank. He lived in the late fifth century about ad 500. The Saxons grew strong and revolted and laid waste the Christian towns. The Britons then rallied under Ambrosius and their war at Badonicus mons ended in victory. Gildas does not name the commander of that battle. Welsh tradition later gave the credit to Arthur. In the Historia Brittonum, the hero is a young seer who interprets uneasy wonders. He reads the sign of the Red Dragon in a Snowdonia stronghold. The Latin name passed into Welsh as Emrys. Geoffrey of Monmouth made him Aurelius Ambrosius, brother of Uthr Bendragon and Arthur's uncle.

:::
```

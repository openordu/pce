::: magazinestyle
August festival is the dictionary name for a Celtic feast of early August. The work sends the reader who searches this headword to its own entry under Lughnasa. The feast thanked the god Lugh at the first of the harvest. Ireland and the Isle of Man both kept it at the close of summer.

:::

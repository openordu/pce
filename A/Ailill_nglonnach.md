``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill Ánglonnach, also written Anguba, was the brother of Eochaid Airem. His name carries the sense of warlike or valiant. He grew sick with longing for Étain, the wife of Eochaid. The physician Fachtna told him that medicine could cure neither the pain of love nor the pain of jealousy. Étain agreed to be his mistress to spare a royal life. She met a man she took for Ailill at a hilltop. That man was Midir, her husband from an earlier life. Tochmarc Étaíne tells this part of her story.

:::
```

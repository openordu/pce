``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Abred is a term from the bardic cosmogony of Llewelyn Siôn. He wrote in the sixteenth century. Abred is the innermost of three concentric circles. These circles represent the totality of being. The soul moves through Abred in the stage of transmigration. It travels toward the higher states of existence. The word comes from a Welsh form that means to transmigrate. The scheme places Abred within a broader account of the created world.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aileel is a variant of the Irish name Ailill. Anglo-Irish writers, W. B. Yeats especially, took up this spelling. The form changes the letters but keeps the sense. The chief bearer who meets most readers is Ailill, a king of Connacht and husband of Medb. Readers should see the Ailill entry for the full story.

:::
```

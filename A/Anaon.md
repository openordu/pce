``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Anaon is a Breton word for the dead and for the land that lies beyond death. The name gathers the souls of the departed in the speech of Brittany. MacKillop connects the word with the Welsh idea of the other world that appears under Annwfn.

:::
```

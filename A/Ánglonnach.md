::: magazinestyle
Ánglonnach is a MacKillop cross-reference to the canonical Ailill Anglonnach headword. MacKillop reads the form as a fragment of the longer personal name, pointing readers to Ailill. The principal record of that tradition sits under the Ailill Anglonnach headword.

:::

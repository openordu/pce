``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Abermenai is the water that cuts Anglesey off from the Welsh mainland. The Welsh word aber names the mouth of a river or a strait. Anglesey is the historic island off the north-west coast of Wales. Abermenai marks the crossing that links the isle to Gwynedd. In the Welsh tales the strait appears as a place of passage and encounter.

:::
```

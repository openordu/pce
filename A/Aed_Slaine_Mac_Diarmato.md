``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aed Sláine mac Diarmato was the Uí Néill king and son of Diarmait. His first name Aed was a common Irish man's name meaning fire. Slán, the everyday Irish adjective, meant whole, well, or healthy. His father Diarmait mac Cerbaill ruled the same royal line. The Irish kings list and the Uí Néill pedigree record his descent.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Argetlám is a shorter form of the older name of a god of the old tales. The name means the silver hand, and the word carries the sense of the swift arm. The god of the land keeps the same title in the fuller books of the myth. The reader will find the long tale under the fuller head of the name.

:::
```

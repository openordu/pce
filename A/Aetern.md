``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aetem is an early form of the name Edem in the Welsh record. The Welsh Edem appears to match a pseudo saint of Brittany. That Breton saint once held an important cult in Finistere. The same name recurs in the figure of a warrior in Arthurian tales. Scribes carried the name across two lands and two languages. Aetem therefore preserves the oldest spelling of a shared saint and hero.

:::
```

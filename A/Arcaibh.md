``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arcaibh is the name that the Scottish speech gives to the Orkney islands. The word means among the boars in that tongue. The band of islands lies in the far north of the sea of Britain. The name of the wild swine and the sound of the word mark the group of the Orkney isles.

:::
```

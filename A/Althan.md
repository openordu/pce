``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Althan was the chief bard to Art, the king of Ireland, in the Ossianic verse of James Macpherson. Macpherson wrote the work in the year 1760 with the title Omen. The figure belongs to the invented poetic past that Macpherson built from Celtic themes.

:::
```

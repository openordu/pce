``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Agrona, known in early lore as Agroná, was a British goddess of slaughter. She appears in Britain and in Wales. Her name gave the Welsh battle-god Aeron his title. Some scholars describe her as kin of the Irish death queen Mórrígan.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Aithed is an Old Irish word meaning elopement or flight. The head also prints the forms aitheda and aithedha. It names one category of narrative in Old Irish literature. The dictionary cross-notes tochmarc, the wooing tale, for comparison. Both classes turn on the pursuit of a woman.

:::

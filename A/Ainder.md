``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ainder, also called Ainnir, means a young woman in the Irish language. She appears in the Fenian Cycle as the daughter of Barran and the wife of Cailte. Her story ties her to the warrior fellowship of Finn. MacKillop keeps the name under this headword in his dictionary.

:::
```

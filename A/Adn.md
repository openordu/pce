``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Adna is the oldest early explorer of Ireland in the old books. The tales name him the son of Bith and the brother of Cesair. The pseudo-historical ruler Ninus of Syria sent him on his mission. The Old Irish gloss carries a sense of shame or modesty. The record writes the name Adná beside the alternate spelling Aidne. This figure thus ranks among the first settlers of the island in invented Irish history.

:::
```

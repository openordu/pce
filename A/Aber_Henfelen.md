::: magazinestyle
Aber Henfelen is a Welsh name for the sea that lies between Wales and Cornwall. In Welsh narrative the term refers to the Bristol Channel. The word aber names the mouth of a river or a strait. Aber Henfelen belongs to the imaginative geography of the Welsh tales. It marks the water boundary that separates the two western lands of Britain.

:::

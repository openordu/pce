::: magazinestyle
Angus is a man's name written in many forms across early Ireland and Scotland. MacKillop records the spellings Óengus, Áengus, Aonghus, and Aonghas. English writers turned it into Ennis, Neese, or Nicholas. The name is borne by gods, kings, poets, and saints. The best known bearer is Angus Óg, the son of the Dagda.

:::

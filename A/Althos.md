``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Althos was one of the sons of Usnoth in the Ossianic poetry of James Macpherson from the year 1760. Macpherson gave Usnoth a set of sons and placed them in his invented heroic past. Althos appears only in this verse cycle.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aulomm is a second way of writing the name of the Munster king Ailill Aulom. The dictionary points the reader who finds this form to the longer entry. The man belongs to the legend of the kings of the South and of the cattle raid of the island.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Ailill Ólum, also written Olum or Ólom, is a variant of the name Ailill Aulomm. The three spellings mark the same figure in Irish legend. Ailill Aulomm fathered Eógan and fostered Lugaid mac Con. Readers should see the Ailill Aulomm entry for the full portrait.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ail na Mírenn, sometimes written Aill na Mureann, points to Uisnech. Uisnech stands as the sacred center of Ireland. There the provinces met at the stone of divisions. Ail na Mírenn literally names that stone.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Ablach is an Ulster princess of early Irish tradition. Her name evokes an orchard, a place rich in apple trees. She bore Eochaid mac Fiachnai, a ruler of the ninth century. Her name also recalls Emain Ablach, a place that means Emain of the Apples. The storytellers used that phrase for a realm of the Otherworld. Ablach thus links a royal mother to the magic apple trees of legend.

:::

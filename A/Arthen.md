::: magazinestyle
Arthen is a god of the rivers and of the bear in the early faith of Wales. The old word carries the sense of the bear in the former tongue. Some shadowy figures of the chronicles also bear the same name. One of them is a king of the land of Ceredigion in the ninth hundred of years.

:::

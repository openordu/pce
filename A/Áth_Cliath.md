::: magazinestyle
Áth Cliath, ford of the hurdles in Irish, is the literary designation for the city of Dublin in medieval Irish tales. Baile Átha Cliath, town of the ford of hurdles, remains the official Irish-language form for the modern capital. MacKillop reads the term as a place-name rooted in the hurdles-crossing geography of the Liffey crossing. The principal record sits under Dublin.

:::

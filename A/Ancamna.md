``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ancamna was a Romano-Gaulish goddess. She was worshipped at Trier in the valley of the Moselle. There she often stood beside the war god Mars, who bore the title Lenus. North of Trier, at the town named Mohn, she was the partner of the god Smertrius. Some readers take Smertrius to be another form of Mars. Her worship shows how names of that land were pressed into written Roman usage.

:::
```

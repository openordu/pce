``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áedán mac Gabráin stands as the founder of Dál Riata in Argyllshire. He was a close friend of Colum Cille, also known as Saint Columba. He pushed his control eastward into Pictish territory and is cited in battle against the Saxons. His son Eochaid Buide later styled himself king of the Picts. The principal record sits under the Dál Riata headword.

:::
```

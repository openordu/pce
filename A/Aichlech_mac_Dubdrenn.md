``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aichlech mac Dubdrenn slew Fionn mac Cumhaill. The killing took place at Áth Brea, a ford on the Boyne. Aided Finn records this version. A more famous account sets Fionn's death in Cath Gabhra, struck down by five warriors.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Antenociticus is a British god. He is a youthful male deity worshipped in Roman times. A temple dedicated to him was excavated at Benwell in the Roman fort of Condercum. He is also spelled Anociticus and Antodicitus in the records. The god is known only at a single site. Like many other local Celtic deities he may have been a genius loci, the guardian spirit of the place. A carving shows his hair swept forward in a way that suggests the antlers of a young stag. This stag-like image links him to the forest and the hunt. His site at Condercum stands near the modern town of Benwell, in Northumberland, on the line of Hadrian's Wall.

:::
```

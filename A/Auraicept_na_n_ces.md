``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Auraicept na nÉces is a work of Irish learning on the beginnings and the classes of the Irish tongue. The title may be read as the primer of the scholar. The bardic schools used it in the years before the Norman age. It lists the sequence of the Latin and Irish letters and the marks of ogham. It also treats gender, the forms of the verb, and the parts of rhyme.

:::
```

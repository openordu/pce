``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
AM is the common abbreviation for Anno Mundi. The Latin term means the year of the world. It counts the years that passed since the creation of the world. Medieval scholars reckoned that date in different ways. The Irish Annals of the Four Masters placed the creation in 5090 BC. Under that count the year 1000 BC became 4090 AM. The Venerable Bede, an Anglo-Saxon historian, proposed 3952 BC. Other writers cited 5200 BC and 5198 BC. The abbreviation appears in the dating of many early Irish annals and chronicles.

:::
```

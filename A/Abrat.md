``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Abrat is a name that appears in early Irish legend as a cross-reference. It points to the figure Áed Abrat and to the place Cenn Abrat. Áed Abrat was the father of the goddess Fand and of Li Ban. He took pride in the healing song of his daughters. Cenn Abrat was the site of a legendary battle. There Lugaid mac Con fell before the forces of the Eóganacht. The site is placed among the Ballyhoura mountains in County Limerick.

:::
```

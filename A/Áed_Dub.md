Áed Dub, dark fire in Irish, fulfilled the prophecy of the druid Bec mac Dé by killing Diarmait mac Cerbaill. MacKillop reads the form as the prophetic agent in the death of the high king. The principal record sits under Diarmait mac Cerbaill.

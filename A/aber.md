::: magazinestyle
Aber is the word for a river mouth or estuary in Welsh, Cornish, and Breton. Countless place-names open with this element. The dictionary cross-notes Old Irish inber, Modern Irish inbhear, Scottish Gaelic inbhir, and Manx inver. Those related forms carry the same geography into Ireland, Scotland, and the Isle of Man.

:::

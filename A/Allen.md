::: magazinestyle
Allen is the name of a hill and a great bog in the middle of Ireland. The Hill of Allen stands in County Kildare. The tale marks it as the home of the hero Finn and his followers. The Bog of Allen spreads across the central plain. The two works bear the same name.

:::

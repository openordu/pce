``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
aonach names an Irish celebration combining a fair and an assembly. MacKillop records the forms aonaighe and áenach alongside the headword. In the early twentieth century, aonach gatherings met at Millstreet in County Cork. Gatherings there opened on the first day of March, of July, of September, and of December. Millstreet lies roughly twenty miles west of Mallow Junction. In Old Irish the word appears as óenach. Those gatherings met at set times at Tara, at Tailtiu, at Tlachtga, and at Uisnech. Their programme staged games and contests rather than trade. MacKillop separates aonach from festival, from féile, and from feis. The compound Aonach Tailteann points onward to Tailtiu.

:::
```

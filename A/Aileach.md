``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aileach, also written Ailech, Oileach, or Grianán Glige, was a great stone fort. It likely dated from early Christian times. It stood at Inishowen Head in north-east Donegal. The fort sat west of Lough Foyle. It rose about five miles from the region's chief city. The name Grianán means thatch of sunlight. That name carries an old link to the site. Still, the structure was not a sunroom. Aileach directs the reader to the full entry on Grianán Aileach.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ard Macha is an Irish name for the heights of Emain Macha. It is the name of Armagh in today's Northern Ireland. The name means the height of the goddess Macha. Emain Macha is a late Bronze Age hillfort in County Armagh. It stood as the mythical capital of the old province of Ulster. Ard Macha, the Christian city, lies about two miles east of that fortress. One tradition tells of the warrior queen Macha Mong Rua. She marked out a vast hillfort with a brooch and made her captives build it. Today the site is called Navan Fort. It stands equal in rank to Cruachan, Tara, and Knockaulin.

:::
```

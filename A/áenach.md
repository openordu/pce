::: magazinestyle
áenach is a one-line variant spelling of aonach in MacKillop's Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The dictionary treats the form as a typographical cross-reference to the parent article on aonach, with no independent mythography or narrative attached. The aonach page sets out the early Irish popular assembly, the place-names where the gatherings met (Tara, Tailtiu, Tlachtga, Uisnech), and the modern fair-ground uses (Millstreet, County Cork). MacKillop's áenach entry is a stub redirecting the reader to the full treatment.

:::

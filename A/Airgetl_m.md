``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Airgetlám means silver hand in Irish. The name is an agnomen given to the god Nuada. MacKillop gives Argetlám as a second spelling of the byname. Nuadu earned the title from his silver hand. Readers should see the Nuada entry for the full story.

:::
```

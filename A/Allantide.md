``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Allantide is the name for the Sunday in Cornwall. The day is the same as the start of the old festival of Samain. Local speech also knows it as the day of the apples. MacKillop gives the word as a counterpart of the festival of the same season.

:::
```

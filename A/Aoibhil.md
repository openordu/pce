``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aoibhil is one of the many spellings of the name of an Irish fairy queen. The dictionary holds Aoibheall, Aoibhell, and Aoibhil under one head. Aibell is the fairy queen of the north of Munster. She is a patron of the O'Brien kindred of that region. Her fairy hill is the rock Craig Li near the town of Killaloe in County Clare. The goddess Clidna works as her rival. In one account Clidna changes Aibell into a white cat. Later Irish verse and the Munster courts keep her memory alive.

:::
```

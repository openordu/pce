``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Awen is the Welsh term for poetic inspiration and insight. It flows from the cauldron of Ceridwen. That cauldron grants the gift of divine vision. Talhaearn Tad Awen means the father of the muse. The ninth-century Historia Brittonum remembers him among the Five Poets. Taliesin embodies this inspired gift. The Gogynfeirdd, the Poets of the Princes, held the awen set them apart from lesser poets. The awen carries the power of vision. It raises the poet beyond common craft.

:::
```

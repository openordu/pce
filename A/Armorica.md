``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Armorica is an ancient place-name. It names the coast of Gaul facing the Atlantic. Under the Romans the Tractus Armoricanus stretched from the Seine estuary to the Loire. This region is roughly the modern Normandy and Brittany combined. Caesar used the phrase civitates Armoricae to mean the Armorican tribes. He defined the name as the lands of the coast-dwelling tribes. He listed the Curiosolites, Rédones, Ambibarii, Caletes, Osismii, Veneti, Lemovices, and Venelli among them. The name means country facing the sea. Koch also records the older spelling Aremorica. Modern works often use Armorica for the land later called Brittany.

:::
```

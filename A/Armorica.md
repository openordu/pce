``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Armorica is an ancient place-name. It names the coast of Gaul facing the Atlantic. Under the Romans the name Tractus Armoricanus stretched from the Seine estuary to the Loire. The land is roughly the modern Normandy and Brittany combined. Caesar used the phrase civitates Armoricae for the Armorican tribes. He defined it as the lands of the coast-dwelling tribes. He listed the Curiosolites, Rédones, Ambibarii, Caletes, Osismii, Veneti, Lemovices, and Venelli among them. MacKillop says the name came to mean Brittany in medieval times. That place then held two parts. Domnonia lay north of the River Elom, in present-day Côtes-du-Nord. Cornoualle stretched south past the Elom to the Ellé. The name means country facing the sea. Koch also records the older spelling Aremorica. Modern works often use the name for the land later called Brittany.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Agandecca is a character in Macpherson's Poems of Ossian. She is the fair haired daughter of Starno, the king of Lochlin. Agandecca told Fingal about a plot laid against his life. Her father slew her for giving this warning. Her death makes her a tragic figure in the Ossianic verse.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Ardnurcher is the older name of a place on the edge of the county of the middle of Ireland. The town is known today under the name of Horseleap. The book sends the reader to the full head of the ancient name in the same rolls. The old English words of the name carry the sense of a beast making a leap over a stream.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Angharad is a Welsh name that means the dearly loved one. Gerald of Wales and the early records give the name to several women. One Angharad was the daughter of Meurig ap Dyfnwall, and she became the wife of the king named Rhodri. A second, the daughter of Meredydd ab Owain, married Llywelyn ap Seisyllt, king of Gwynedd. A third was the wife of William de Barry and the mother of the chronicler named Giraldus Cambrensis. The Arthurian tale adds a further Angharad, an obscure heroine whom the knight Peredur courted. Some give her the added names Angharad Law Eurawc and Angharad of the Golden Hand. Readers suggest she began as a goddess of the land's rule, the prize a worthy king must stand and win.

:::
```

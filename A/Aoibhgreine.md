``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aoibhgreine is a modern Irish spelling of the name. The source writes the older form as Aibgreine, built from a Gaelic word for the radiance of the sun. In the exile tale of the Uisnech kindred, Aibgreine is the daughter of Deirdre and Noise. Some accounts name her among the kin of the northern kings.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aoibhinn is a modern Irish form of an older woman's name. The word carries the sense of a fair and radiant one. The book keeps the name near that of the fairy queen Aoibhe of the early speech. The fuller story rests under the longer head of the same name.

:::
```

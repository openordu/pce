::: magazinestyle
Abac is the Irish word for an afanc. The form Abhac is a later Modern Irish spelling. An afanc is a water creature of Celtic legend. In Welsh tradition it is a fierce monster that haunts lakes and rivers. The Irish word also carries the sense of a dwarf. Early Irish literature uses the form abacc for a dwarf. The Scottish Gaelic form is abhac. This small word links the Irish and Welsh traditions of the water beast.

:::

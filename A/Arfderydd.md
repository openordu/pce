``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arfderydd is a battle of the far north of the Britons. The fight of that place drove the wise man Myrddin into a state of madness. He fled into the forest of the north and lived in the wild for half a century. In that mad time he gained the gift of foreseeing the days to come. He also mourned the fall of a lord on the same field of the fight.

:::
```

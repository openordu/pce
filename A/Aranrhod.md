``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aranrhod is one of the written forms of the name of a Welsh woman of the old tales. The longer form of the same name is Arianrhod. The full story of the woman rests under the longer head. This short form keeps a place in the book for the reader to find.

:::
```

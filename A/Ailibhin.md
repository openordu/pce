::: magazinestyle
Ailibhin is a variant of Ainnle, a son of Usnech in Irish legend. The Scottish texts favor this spelling. Ainnle ranks among the three sons of Usnech who appear in Deirdre's tale. Readers should see the Ainnle entry for that fuller portrait.

:::

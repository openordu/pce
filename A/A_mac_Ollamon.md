::: magazinestyle
Aí mac Ollamon is the poet of the Tuatha Dé Danann. His name carries the sense of the son of a master poet. The tales speak of his skill in verse. Before his birth a sudden gust moved the house and a seer spoke of a wondrous child. He was later sent to stand against the sons of Carman.

:::

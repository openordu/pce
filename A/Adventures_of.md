The headwords Adventures of form a cross reference in the dictionary. The note directs the reader toward the separate entry eachtra. The Irish word eachtra means an adventure or a journey. Many folk tales open their titles with the words the adventures of. The older Irish form echtra marks a special kind of otherworld journey. The cross reference points the seeker to the native Gaelic term instead of the English phrase.

:::

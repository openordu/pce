``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ard is an Irish and Scottish Gaelic term for something high or raised above its surroundings. MacKillop also records the word as a prefix, ard-. Place-names and titles use it very often. The prefix sometimes stands apart, as in Ard Macha. It sometimes attaches to the next element, as in Ardmore. For this reason the dictionary sorts every ard- entry letter by letter instead of grouping the forms.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aithechda, also Aithechdae, means vassal, peasant, churl, or plebe. In the Lebor Gabála this figure is a son of Japheth. Japheth himself is a son of Noah. Aithechda counts as the ancestor of Partholón. He is the oldest figure whose name is non-biblical at the head of that line. The Lebor Gabála remains the Book of Invasions.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áes dána is the Old Irish term for the people of art or of a craft trade, and especially for poets. The phrase carries the sense of a professional class whose skill defines them, distinct from the warrior or the field-labourer. In early Irish literature the áes dána names a social category whose privileges and obligations the law tracts record. A modern Irish form, dána, survives as a related word.

:::
```

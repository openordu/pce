Ailleann is a variant of Dún Ailinne, the royal hillfort of eastern Ireland. The alternate spelling marks the same site in early legend. Ailleann fits beside its longer Dún Ailinne form. Readers should see the Dún Ailinne entry for details of the fort and its rites.

::: magazinestyle
Ainnle, also written Ainle or Ante, is a hero name from early Ireland. He ranks among the trio of sons born to Uisnech. Ainnle followed his brother Noise in eloping with Deirdre to Scotland. He was said to have a tenor voice, unlike his brother Ardan who sang baritone. Macpherson shaped the figure of Sithallin from him.

:::

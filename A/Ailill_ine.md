``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill Áine, also written Áne, was the father of the hero Labraid Loingseach. That son stands as ancestor of the Laigin, the early people of Leinster. This Ailill should not be confused with Ailill Aulomm, who was tied in later story to Áine, a fairy goddess. The name links a king to a founding people.

:::
```

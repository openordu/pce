``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Athnurcher is where the hero Cét slings a brain ball at Conchobar mac Nessa, the king of Ulster. The attack stands in the old Irish narrative of the North. The parish on the border of Westmeath and Offaly now carries the names Ardnurcher and Horseleap. The old name keeps the sense of the ford of the throw.

:::
```

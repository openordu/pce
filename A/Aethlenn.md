``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aethlenn is a corrupt form of the Irish name Eithne. Scribes reshaped the single name into many spellings over time. The variants include Aethlenn, Ethlenn and Athlenn in the old books. All these point back to the one underlying figure. Eithne herself holds many roles across Irish legend. She may appear as a mother of gods or a daughter of kings. The head Aethlenn reminds the reader how copyists scattered one name.

:::
```

Aberystwyth is a town in Wales at the mouths of the rivers Ystwyth and Rheidol. The Welsh word aber names the mouth of a river. Aberystwyth is one of the places that tradition names as a burial site of the poet Taliesin. The town grew into a centre of Welsh learning and craft. It holds a long association with the cultural life of the country.

:::

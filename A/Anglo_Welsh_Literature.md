::: magazinestyle
Anglo-Welsh literature means writing in English by people of Wales. In Wales, the two languages Welsh and English have lived side by side since the late Middle Ages. The term was adopted about 1922 to name this English-language writing. Some scholars now prefer the name Welsh writing in English. The modern movement grew strong in the twentieth century. An early monument is a long English poem shaped by Welsh strict metre. Henry Vaughan wrote English poetry enriched by Welsh speech. Writers translated Welsh prose, verse and legend for English readers. Anglo-Welsh literature keeps the two languages of Wales in lively exchange. It remains a distinct branch of world literature.

:::

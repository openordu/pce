``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aíbell is a goddess and fairy queen of the stories of the north of Munster. Her mound stands at Craig Liath near Killaloe in County Clare. The tales set her beside goddesses such as Áine. They make her a rival of Clidna for the love of a man. In one account she travels with King Brian Boruma to the battle at Clontarf.

:::
```

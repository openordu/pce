::: magazinestyle
The Attecotti were a people of the British islands that Roman writers name in their records. The lists of the later empire include the group among the distant and feared provinces. Scholars connect the name with war bands that left early Ireland. The evidence is thin, so the link stays a reasonable guess rather than a proven fact.

:::

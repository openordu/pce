``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Annals of Tig[h]ernach are the oldest native Irish record of the early years. A tradition says that Tigernach drew the text together at the monastery of Clonmacnoise. The manuscript rests today in the library at Oxford in fragment form. It is the most valuable of the early Irish annal collections.

:::
```

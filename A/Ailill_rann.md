``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ailill Érann is another name for Bolg or Bolga, an ancestor of the Fir Bolg. The Lebor Gabála, the Book of Invasions, treats him as a founding figure. This spelling links to the people of the Fir Bolg. Readers should see the Bolg and Fir Bolg entries for the fuller account.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áebhric is a young man, perhaps a monk, living at Erris in County Mayo, who listened to the singing of Lir's children as recorded in the Oidheadh Chlainne Lir tradition. MacKillop reads the form as the witness to that famous lament among the children of Lir. The principal record of the tragic story itself sits under the Oidheadh Chlainne Lir headword.

:::
```

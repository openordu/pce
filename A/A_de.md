``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Aíde is the second wife of the old king Énna, as the Irish book of the five ways records. The tale links her death and the death of Énna's first wife to the naming of the city of Dublin. Her part in the story is small, but the ragged name of the place keeps her memory. The old book of places sets her in the first rank of the queens of the plain.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Arberth is a Welsh mythological site. Pwyll's kingdom held the mound, a place of fairy magic that bore this name. Koch identifies it with Narberth in Pembrokeshire and calls it the royal seat of the old kingdom of Dyfed. People went there to see visions. The mound itself bears the name Gorsedd Arberth, and strange events gather around it. It is the scene of much of the Mabinogi, standing at the heart of the first and fourth branches. The mystifying horsewoman Rhiannon appears on the mound in Pwyll's tale. Her horse appears to walk, yet no one can catch her. In the tale of Manawydan, a mist descends on the mound and takes the people of Dyfed away.

:::
```

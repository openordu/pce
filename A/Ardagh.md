::: magazinestyle
Ardagh is a common Irish name of a place of the high field. The word of the land is a high meadow of the east of the country. Eight towns on the modern map carry the name. The site of the county of Longford stands by a stronghold of the plain. One famous village of the county of Limerick holds the gold of the old church. The finest piece is the bowl called the Ardagh Chalice.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Aengus is a variant spelling of the name Angus preferred by Yeats. The Irish Aengus Og stands as the god of youth and beauty. The Tuatha De Danann place him among their divine company. His father was the great god the Dagda. His mother Boann gave her name to the river Boyne. The name carries the sense of a young son in Irish. So Aengus names a love god central to the Irish pagan age.

:::

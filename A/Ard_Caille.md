::: magazinestyle
Ard Caille is a place in the north of County Cork in Ireland. The name of the high ground is the height of the land. The old poems of the early age name it as the grave of the hero Fionn mac Cum. A more common site is the plain of Luachair Ded in the west of Munster. Both places die for the resting ground of the great man of the tales.

:::

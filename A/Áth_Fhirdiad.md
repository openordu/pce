``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Áth Fhirdiad, ford of Ferdia in Irish, marks a crossing of the River Dee in County Louth, fourteen miles north of Drogheda. The ford is the site of the famous combat between Cúchulainn and Ferdia in the Táin Bó Cúailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley. MacKillop reads the form as a place-name rooted in the central Ulster-cycle tale. The principal record sits under Ferdia.

:::
```

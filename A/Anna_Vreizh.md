``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Anna Vreizh is the Breton name of Anne of Brittany. She served as the last duchess of a fully free Brittany. Her father François II of the Dreux line fought long to keep the duchy autonomous. Anna came to the throne at eleven years old. Breton nobles and foreign kings pressed her to act weak. She proved strong and capable instead. She cared deeply for the welfare of Bretagne. She became a known patron of art and literature. Her prayer book rests in the Pierpont Morgan Library in New York. She made the Tro Breizh pilgrimage around Brittany in 1504. She wed Maximilian of Austria by proxy in 1490. Later she married two French kings, Charles VIII and then Louis XII. Her daughter Claude married the king and joined Brittany to France.

:::
```

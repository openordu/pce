::: magazinestyle
Whitehorn is a name belonging to Finnbennach, the white-horned bull of Connacht. The fuller record of that bull and his feud sits under the Finnbennach head. The Tain Bo Cuailnge turns on the quarrel between him and the brown bull of Ulster.

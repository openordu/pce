``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
White Book of Rhydderch is a great storehouse of medieval Welsh prose, printed from the Welsh Llyfr Gwyn Rhydderch. Scribes copied it on parchment around 1325. It holds versions of the Mabinogi and every other narrative of the Mabinogion, the Dream of Rhonabwy alone excepted. It also holds many religious texts. For her Mabinogion translation, Lady Charlotte Guest turned to the later Red Book of Hergest instead. The transcriber of the White Book is not known. Dialect evidence places his home in Deheubarth, the south-west of Wales. The name Rhydderch points two ways. One is Parc Rhydderch, a house that gave poetry its patronage. The other is Rhydderch ab Ieuan Llwyd, the master of Parc Rhydderch. The book appears to have been his in the late fourteenth century. The book now sits in two volumes. In 1940 the two volumes got a rebinding in white leather at the National Library of Wales. The library keeps them still.

:::
```

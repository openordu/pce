``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The wild man is a figure of Celtic legend. He first appears as a king or poet. A terrible defeat in battle drives him mad. He flees into the wild wood. There he lives a solitary life like an animal. Myrddin, later known as Merlin, is the finest example. Suíbne Geilt offers the Irish form of the tale. Lailoken supplies a third version in northern Britain. The wild man gains prophecy in his exile. His hardened body and his frenzy separate him from sound men.

:::
```

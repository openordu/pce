``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugaid mac Ítha is a constructed pedigree name. Its makers gave the hero of Munster, Lugaid mac Con, a father drawn from the invasion tradition, the Milesian figure Íth. The invented patronymic supplied genealogies with an illustrious forebear. Tradition also gives this Lugaid a wife, Fial. The families that became the O'Coffeys, the O'Driscolls, and other kindreds traced their line to the couple.

:::
```

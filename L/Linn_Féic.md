``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Linn Féic is a pool in the River Boyne, in the region of Slane in County Meath. It was one of two spots where Fionn mac Cumhaill found the Salmon of Wisdom. The place is known today as Fiac's Pool, named after a man who drowned here. The same ground later held the Battle of the Boyne in 1690.

:::
```

lochramán is a MacKillop variant spelling of the headword leprechaun. MacKillop treats lochramán as an alternate form that resolves to the same record. The fuller record of the leprechaun, his shoemaker's trade, his gold crock, and his capture by mortal tricksters sits under leprechaun.

::: magazinestyle
Lóegaire stands among the most common personal names of early Ireland. The dictionary lists the variant forms Láegaire, Lóeghaire, Laeghire, Laoghaire, Laoire, Lóegure, and Leary. Legendary heroes, kings, and saints all carried it, and the best-known bearer is probably the Ulster Cycle champion Lóegaire Búadach.

The name organizes a whole family of entries. This batch of the encyclopedia documents the champion Lóegaire Búadach, the fierce Lóegaire Lorc, the adventure hero Lóegaire mac Crimthann, and the Tara king Lóegaire mac Néill. Readers chasing a saint or a ruler of the conversion era should start with the mac Néill entry, while students of the champion's portion contest should start with Búadach.

:::

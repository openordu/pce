``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
luchragán is a MacKillop variant spelling of leprechaun, listed with the alternate form luchramám. MacKillop records the form with a question mark as one of several dialect spellings of the same folkloric being. The full record of the leprechaun tradition in Celtic folklore sits under the headword leprechaun.

:::
```

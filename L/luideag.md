``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The luideag is a female being of Scottish folklore. She haunted a lake on the Isle of Skye. She had a plain form and a quick temper. People kept away from the water because of her. She belongs to the larger body of Scottish water lore. The Scottish Gaelic name luideag comes as a diminutive of luid, a word for a rag or slovenly person. Folk tradition classes her as a killing female demon of the Scottish Gaelic oral record. She stands beside the athach as another fearful spirit of that lore. Her looks were filthy, and her will was wicked. Rather than one lake she troubled several small pools on the Isle of Skye. She showed up most often at a Skye pool called Lochan of Black Trout. Cataloguers mark her under motif numbers Ó11.3 and G346.2.

:::
```

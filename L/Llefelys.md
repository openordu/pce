``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llefelys is a Welsh king and the brother of Lludd. He is a son of Beli Mawr in the early genealogies. He shares the leading role of the tale Cyfranc Lludd a Llefelys, the Tale of Lludd and Llefelys. He ruled on the Continent while his brother Lludd ruled Britain. MacKillop says he became ruler of France by wedding the daughter of the dead king. In that land he sat on a foreign throne. When Britain suffered three plagues, Lludd turned to his brother. Llefelys knew the cause of each blow and how to end it. He taught Lludd the remedy for every plague. One plague was a race of fighting demons called the Coraniaid. Another was a pair of dragons that fought at every Maytime. The third was a giant wizard who stole the provisions of the realm. With Llefelys in counsel Lludd overcame all three. Lludd dug a pit in the middle of Britain to catch the dragons. He beat the giant in battle and made the giant his vassal. So the two brothers freed the island. Llefelys stands as the wise brother across the sea. His name also appears as Lleuelys and Llevelys. He is a separate figure from Lleu Llaw Gyffes, the hero who appears in the final branch of the Mabinogi. His counsel saved his brother's realm, and the pair together shaped one of the best-loved Welsh tales of wit and wonder. Wise counsel from across the water saved the whole island of Britain.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
lianhan shee is a MacKillop anglicized spelling of the Irish leannán sídhe, the fairy lover. MacKillop treats the anglicized form as a brief pointer to the same record. The fuller record of the fairy lover, the love-sick bond with a chosen mortal, and the deadly end of such unions sits under fairy_lover.

:::

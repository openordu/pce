``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lewys Glyn Cothi was a Welsh poet who worked from 1447 to 1489. He was also called Llywelyn y Glyn. His home lay in the royal forest of Glyn Cothi near Llanybydder in Carmarthenshire. He may have trained as a scribe at Abergwili. He is the first Welsh poet recorded as keeping copies of his own verse. Two manuscripts in his hand survive. His 238 poems come down in reliable copies. He wrote for patrons across Wales. Some patrons backed the Lancastrian cause during the fifteenth-century dynastic wars. He composed verse to the exiled Jasper Tudor. He also worked for Yorkist patrons such as the Herberts. His best-known piece is a lament for the loss of his young son Sion.

:::
```

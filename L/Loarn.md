::: magazinestyle
Loarn mac Erc stands among the three brothers who established the kingdom of Dál Riata in Argyll during the fifth century. Erc fathered him alongside Fergus Mòr and Óengus, and the three sons carried the kindred from Ireland into western Scotland. Highland genealogies later claimed him as an ultimate ancestor for many Scottish families. The district of Lorn in Argyll carries his name forward on the map.

:::

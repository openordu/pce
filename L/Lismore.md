::: magazinestyle
Lismore is a town in Co. Waterford in south-east Ireland. Its name is joined to the Book of Lismore, a 15th century manuscript that preserves tales of the Fianna. For Celtic lore the name therefore connotes the manuscript more than the modern town.

:::

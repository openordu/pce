``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Luchta serves the Tuatha Dé Danann as their carpenter god, recorded also as Luchtar, Luchtain, Luchtaine, or Luchtline. His craft rank is sóer, the carpenter or wright among the free arts. He forms one of the tri dée dána, the three gods of craft, beside Credne and Goibniu. The three together cover the smith's forge, the metal-worker's art, and the carpenter's bench. Tradition ranked his shield and spear-shaft work above all other craftsmen, the arms outfitting of the Tuatha Dé Danann's hosts. Genealogies of the gods make him a son of Lúachad, which yields the patronymic mac Lúachaid. The variant spellings in the record show how the single craftsman god reached the manuscripts under several names, with the PCE holding the main article under Luchtar.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llyfr Du Caerfyrddin, the Black Book of Carmarthen, was penned by a lone scribe around the year 1250. It stands among the earliest manuscripts composed wholly in the Welsh language. The codex works like a one-volume library, opening with the historical texts Ystorya Dared, Brut y Brenhinedd and Brut y Tywysogyon. A wide run of Gogynfeirdd poems follows, the verse of the medieval court poets of Wales. The book also holds poems about Myrddin, the legendary wild man of the Old North. The manuscript has an old link with Carmarthen Priory and later made its way to Cardiff. It now lives as National Library of Wales MS Peniarth 1.

:::
```

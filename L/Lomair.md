::: magazinestyle
Lomair appears in tradition as a sibling of the hounds Bran and Sceolang, the famous dogs of Fionn mac Cumhaill. The listing makes the name part of the Fenian dog kindred. No separate tale of Lomair survives in the dictionary record, and the entry stands as a short cross-reference to the Fenian hounds.

:::

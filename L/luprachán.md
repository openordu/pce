``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
luprachán is a MacKillop variant spelling of leprechaun, listed with the alternate forms luprecan, lúracán, lurgadán, and lurikeen. MacKillop treats these as the same headword with multiple dialect spellings. The full record of the leprechaun tradition in Celtic folklore sits under the headword leprechaun.

:::
```

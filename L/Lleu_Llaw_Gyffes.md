``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lleu Llaw Gyffes is the central hero of the fourth tale in the Mabinogi. His name means the bright one of the sure or steady hand. He is a son of the goddess Arianrhod. His brother is Dylan. Arianrhod conceived him only when the magician Math tested her virginity. Her brother Gwydion took the child and raised him. The boy showed great strength by his first birthday. By his second he could go to court on his own. Gwydion then presented the unnamed child to his mother. Arianrhod was furious at the sight. She laid three curses on the boy. He should not get a name unless she gave it. He should not bear arms unless she equipped him. He should not take any human woman of this world as his wife. Gwydion defeated each curse by craft. He won the boy a name and then arms. For the third he made a wife from flowers. The lovely Blodeuwedd was shaped entirely from blossoms. She did not prove a good wife. While Lleu was away she took the hunter Gronw Pebyr as her lover. She helped him follow the steps needed to kill her husband. Lleu could not be slain by an ordinary blow. Wounded, he turned into an eagle. He flew with a piercing shriek to a magic oak tree. Gwydion found him and restored him to human form. He shamed Blodeuwedd by making her an owl. Lleu then sought out Gronw and killed him in the same way the hunter would have killed Lleu. He became lord of Gwynedd in north Wales. Scholars link him to the Irish hero Lug of the long arm and to the god Lugos. That ancient Gallic god became the Mercury whom Julius Caesar described. Lleu Llaw Gyffes thus carries a long and royal line.

:::
```

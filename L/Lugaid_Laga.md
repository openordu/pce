``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugaid Lága is the faithful henchman of Lugaid mac Con. His recorded name forms include Lagha, Lagae, and Laigne. He stood by his king through defeat, exile, and the restoration of power, yet he abandoned that king when he stepped down in favour of Cormac mac Airt. Lugaid Lága had earlier killed Art mac Cuinn, Cormac's father. The young king let that killing trouble him not at all. Cormac's court in time came to hold Lugaid Lága in high esteem. A single list once placed him among Ireland's five great warriors, in the company of Cúchulainn, Lug Lámfhota, Conall Cearnach, and Fionn mac Cumhaill. He counts as a brother of Ailill Aulomm. Tradition binds him closely to the Glen of Aherlow in County Tipperary. He acted as protector of Uime, mother of Bran and Sceolang, after she returned to human form. Modern commentators read his characterization as a local Munster adaptation of Lugaid mac Con. Some older accounts made him the father of the Fenian warrior Mac Lughach.

:::
```

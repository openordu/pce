``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Láeg, also written Laeg or Loeg, served the hero Cuchulainn. He drove the hero's chariot, and he acted as his friend and his messenger. When Lugaid mac Con Roi flung a spear meant for Cuchulainn, Láeg took the blow instead. The weapon killed him, and he gave his life for his lord. He joined the guard and the service of the great hero of Ulster. He met his end because he stood ready to shield his master. His loyalty to Cuchulainn marked the whole of his career in the tales of the Ulster cycle.

:::
```

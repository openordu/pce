``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llyfr Aneirin is the Welsh title that means the Book of Aneirin. The volume ranks among the Four Ancient Books of Wales. This thirteenth-century manuscript carries nearly the only full copy of the Gododdin we now hold, the famous corpus of early heroic verse. Two scribal hands appear inside it, usually labeled A and B. They wrote near the same period, though A's work seems slightly older. The pair drew on different exemplars instead of copying one scroll in sequence, so their sections differ. The pages keep an old-fashioned orthography rich in archaic spellings. Edward Lhuyd's Archaeologia Britannica of 1707 first gives the manuscript the name Llyfr Aneirin.

:::
```

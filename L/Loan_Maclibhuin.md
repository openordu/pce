``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Loan Maclibhuin is the swarthy smith of Drontheim in Manx legend. Drontheim may point to Norway, the Norse town Trondheim. He forged the sword Macabuin for King Olave II Goddardson, who ruled Man. His daughter Emergaid the Fair became Olave's wife. That marriage founded the Manx royal line, joining the smith's kindred to the island's kings. The smith Lon mac Líomtha of Lochlainn is a kindred figure in Irish Fenian tradition, and the two tales belong to the same smith-lore family.

:::
```

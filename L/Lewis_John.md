``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lewis, John, was a Welsh barrister and antiquary. He lived from roughly 1548 to 1616. He came from Llanfihangel Nant Melan in Radnorshire. He was born at Harpton Court in Old Radnor. He gained admission as a barrister in 1570. Lewis held Geoffrey of Monmouth in high regard. He defended the Brutus legend against its critics. He also supported the Arthurian traditions. His only published work was The History of Great Britain. The book finally appeared in 1729. The antiquary Hugh Thomas of Breconshire helped ready it. By then the Tudor quarrel over the chronicle had cooled. Lewis thus stands as a guard of the founding myth of Britain. His writing kept an old national story alive.

:::
```

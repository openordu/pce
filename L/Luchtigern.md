``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Luchtigern combines Old Irish lug, light or brightness, with tigern, lord, and echoes luch, mouse. The name therefore reads as the Mouse-lord, a lordship of the small beast rather than a battlefield ruler. He holds that title at Kilkenny and dies slain by Banghaisgidheach, an enormous cat. The tale gives Kilkenny a fable-beast legend to stand beside its better-known cat stories. It also pairs a tiny lord with a huge killer, a match of scale the name itself prepares.

:::
```

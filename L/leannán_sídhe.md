leannán sídhe is the modern Irish form of the MacKillop headword fairy lover. MacKillop lists the Irish term alongside several anglicized spellings including lennan sídhe. The fuller record of the fairy lover, the love-sick bond with a chosen mortal, and the deadly end of such unions sits under fairy_lover.

::: magazinestyle
Llyn Llion is the Lake of the Waves, a magical body of water in early Welsh tradition. The overflow of this lake brought the great flood from which only Dwyfan and Dwyfach survived, in a ship built by Nefyd Nav Neifion. The lake was also home to the water-monster Afanc, and it may answer to the real Efala Lake in Wales.

:::

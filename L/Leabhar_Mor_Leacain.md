``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Leabhar Mór Leacáin, the Book of Lecan, is a medieval Irish vellum manuscript. It belongs to the Mac Fhirbhisigh household, a line of writers and record keepers. Gilla Isu Mor Mac Fir Bisig wrote the main hand at Leacan in County Sligo and drew the chief part himself. Two of his learners joined his hand. The book began before a local king died and was likely done about early 1418. It first held three hundred and eleven leaves. Nine of those leaves broke off and later joined a volume at Trinity College Dublin. The work is set out in double columns with plain capitals. Its pages hold a wide range of matter. The cases are the Book of Invasions, the verse primer, the code of rights and the lore of places and saints. These match the offerings of the related Book of Ballymote. The great codex reached the Irish academy after a long stay in France.

:::
```

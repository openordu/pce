``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugna is a king of Connacht and a diminutive of the name Lug. He fostered the infant Cormac mac Airt. Étaín became pregnant by Art mac Cuinn and received the instruction that Lugna should foster her child. When she bore the boy, a she-wolf first carried him off. Lugna took up his duties only after that first episode.

:::
```

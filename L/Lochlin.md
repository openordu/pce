``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lochlin is Macpherson's name for a realm of dangerous invaders in his Ossian poetry of 1760. The construction clearly follows Lochlainn, the older Irish realm of enemies. Macpherson departs from his model on geography and locates Lochlin in northern Germany, between the two great rivers Rhine and Elbe. The shift moves the threat from the Norse world to a continental one.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Liber de virtutibus sancti Columbae is a book of the miracles of St Colum Cille. Cumméne Find wrote it. He was the seventh abbot of Iona from 657 to 669. The work survives only as a fragment. It was preserved inside the Vita Columbae of Adomnán. The fragment records a prophecy by Colum Cille. He warned King Aedán mac Gabráin of Dál Riata. God would bring disaster on the king's descendants. The harm would follow if any of them hurt the saint's relatives in Ireland. The account says the warning came true. Aedán's grandson Domnall Brecc attacked the saint's cousin in 637. The battle at Mag Roth brought harm to Dál Riata. The text links the supremacy of Northumbria to the saint's intercession.

:::
```

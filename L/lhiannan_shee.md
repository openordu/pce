``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
lhiannan shee is the Manx anglicized spelling of the Irish leannán sídhe, the fairy lover. MacKillop treats the Manx form as a brief pointer to the same record. The fuller record of the fairy lover, the love-sick bond with a chosen mortal, and the deadly end of such unions sits under leannán sídhe.

:::
```

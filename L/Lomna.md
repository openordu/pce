``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lomna serves Fionn mac Cumhaill as a fool. He exposed an adulterous affair between one of Fionn's wives and her lover. The lover murdered him for the betrayal, a punishment the tale treats as the fool's indiscretion repaid. Marvel tradition later gave the dead man a speaking head, which addressed the assembled feast. The tale joins Lomna to the small band of Fenian figures remembered for a single grim scene.

:::
```

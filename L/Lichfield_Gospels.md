``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Lichfield Gospels are an early medieval gospel book. The work is also known as the Book of St Chad. It dates from the second quarter of the eighth century. The manuscript may have been produced in Wales. Northumbria is another possible place of origin. The book holds a small corpus of Old Welsh writing. These marginal notes include the memoranda called Chad 1 to 7. They are diplomatic records in mixed Welsh and Latin. The records deal with who owned property and name many witnesses. Chad 2 is the famous Surexit Memorandum, a very early example of Welsh prose. The marginalia throw light on early Welsh social organization.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Luglochta Loga is a fortress belonging to Forgall Monach at Lusca. The name means the garden or cradle of Lug, and the variant form Luglocht Logo is also recorded. Lusca matches in extent the village of Lusk in County Dublin. Cúchulainn goes a-wooing at this stronghold in the Táin Bó Cúailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley.

:::
```

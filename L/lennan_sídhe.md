::: magazinestyle
lennan sídhe is an anglicized spelling of the MacKillop headword fairy lover. MacKillop treats lennan sídhe as a brief anglicized pointer that resolves to the canonical record. The fuller record of the fairy lover, the love-sick bond with a chosen mortal, and the deadly end of such unions sits under fairy_lover.

:::

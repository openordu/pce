``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugach is a daughter of Fionn mac Cumhaill in ballads of the Fenian Cycle. She is the mother of Mac Lughach. The name also appears in the form Lughach, and a masculine variant of it serves as a name form for the figure Lugaid.

:::
```

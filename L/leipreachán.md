``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
leipreachán is the preferred modern Irish form of the MacKillop headword leprechaun. MacKillop treats leipreachán as the canonical Gaelic spelling of the same record. The fuller record of the leprechaun, his shoemaker's trade, his gold crock, and his capture by mortal tricksters sits under leprechaun.

:::
```

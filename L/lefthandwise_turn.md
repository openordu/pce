``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
lefthandwise turn is the English term for the MacKillop headword withershins, the deiseil-in-reverse ritual direction. MacKillop treats lefthandwise turn as a brief English-language pointer to the same record. The fuller record of the withershins turn, its ill-omened meaning, and its place in Celtic folklore sits under withershins.

:::
```

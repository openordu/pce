``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugaid Conmac serves on occasion as the eponym of Connemara, taking the place of the region's usual founder Conmac. Tradition makes him a by-blow of Medb and Fergus mac Roich. Commentators treat him as either an emanation of Lugaid mac Con or a figure confused with that king.

:::
```

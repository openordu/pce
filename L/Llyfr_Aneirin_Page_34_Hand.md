::: magazinestyle
Llyfr Aneirin, page 34 opens a folio set down by scribe B. Its opening lines celebrate the warriors Madauc Eluet and Cynon. After those verses the page shifts into a denser survival of archaic orthography, spelling more conservative than the awdl that starts the book. There the Gododdin verse speaks of the stone of Lleu's tribe and the tribe of Lleu's hill-fort along the Gododdin frontier, a border that was held. The passage begins with the line 'Leech leud.' This page typifies the archaizing spelling that scribe B preserved across his folios of the Llyfr Aneirin.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Lùnasdal is a Scottish Gaelic form of Lughnasa. Variants include Lùnasdain and Lùnasdainn. All point to Lughnasa, the great early-August festival named for the god Lugh. Speakers of Scots Gaelic use these spellings for the seasonal feast of harvest gatherings, hilltop assemblies, and ritual races. The forms show how the festival name traveled from Irish tradition into Gaelic Scotland.

:::

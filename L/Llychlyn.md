``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llychlyn is a marvelous or infernal region under the sea in early Welsh texts, for example the Dream of Rhonabwy. Like its Irish cognate Scotland, the word has widened in meaning. In present-day Welsh it stands for Scandinavia.

:::
```

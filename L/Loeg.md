``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lóeg is a variant spelling of Láeg, the friend, messenger, and charioteer who serves Cúchulainn. Readers who meet the spelling with the acute accent on the first vowel should follow the main entry under Láeg. The form appears in the standard renderings of the Ulster material alongside the spellings Laeg and Láeg.

:::
```

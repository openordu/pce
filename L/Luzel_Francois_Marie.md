Luzel, François-Marie (1821–1895), also styled Fañch an Uhel, was a Breton who collected the folk songs and folk-tales of Brittany. Born at Plouaret, within the Treger speech region of Côtes-d'Armor, he gathered field material while young, yet his true career only opened when, in 1863, he brought the Breton mystery play into print. He afterwards issued a gallery of oral ballads, followed by a companion volume of airs. He stands among the loudest critics of the claims once made for the verses in Barzaz-Breiz, a disbelief that prevailed until Donatien Laurent partly restored their standing in 1989.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Lóegaire Lorc carries the epithet Lorc, which the source glosses as fierce. He is a son of the high king Úgaine Mór. His death opens the tale Orgain Denna Rig, the Destruction of Dind Rig, and his son Labraid Loingsech later avenges him. The entry is brief because the tradition itself records little more: the killing and the vengeance frame the whole notice.

:::

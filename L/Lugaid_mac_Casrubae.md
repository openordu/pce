::: magazinestyle
Lugaid mac Casrubae is a shadowy father figure in the Ulster Cycle. The sources give him one role only. He fathered Dubthach Dóeltenga, an Ulster Cycle warrior famed for his sharp tongue. Commentators warn that readers may confuse him with Lugaid Riab nDerg, a better-attested namesake.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Liadain, also written Liadan and Liadin, is a woman of early Irish story, possibly one who truly lived. The ninth-century Meeting of Liadain and Cuirithir tells how the poet Cuirithir son of the otter loved her. The account is often read as a native echo of the later doomed love of Heloise and Abelard. Liadain, from a good family of the Corcu Duibne in today's Kerry, met the poet while traveling through northern Connacht. The tale, moving from joy to sorrow, is treasured as early love-tragedy.

:::

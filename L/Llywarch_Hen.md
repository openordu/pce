::: magazinestyle
Llywarch Hen is the central figure of the Songs of Llywarch the Old, a Welsh cycle of early poems composed as monologues and dialogues. The words give the high emotional peaks of a larger story that has not come down to us. Llywarch laments his bent and weary old age against the mirth and boldness of his younger years. Older teaching made him the author of the poems, but the modern scholar Ifor Williams showed that he is only their subject.

:::

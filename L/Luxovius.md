``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Luxovius is a Continental god of the ancient Celts. His Latin name holds the root for light. Scholars know him from a single holy place, the warm springs of Luxeuil in the east of France. This site lies in the Saone valley. By his name and his place, he seems to bind the meaning of light to that of water. He served a cult that linked the two symbols. The shrine stood at the warm spring, and people prayed there for the healing the waters could bring. His consort is the goddess Brixia, also written Bricta. The same holy place yields evidence of the worship of Sirona, another deity of healing springs. A sky horseman carrying a solar wheel appears in the art found on the site. That horseman rounds out the deities honored at this one center of worship.

:::
```

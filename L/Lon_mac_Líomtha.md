``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lon mac Líomtha works as a smith in the Fenian Cycle. He also trains other smiths as their teacher. Story material notes his fearful appearance. His chief recorded deed is the making of Mac an Luin. He forged that blade for Fionn mac Cumhaill. It stands as Fionn's famous sword. His smithy lay in Lochlainn. MacKillop places that smithy at the site of modern Bergen, Norway. Lochlainn marks the Norse world in which this smithing tradition sits. The entry adds a cross-reference to the headword Loan MacLibhuin.

:::
```

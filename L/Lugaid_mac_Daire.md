``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugaid mac Dáire is a pedigree name for a son of Dáire. Medieval genealogists apply it to any of Dáire's five or seven sons, above all to Lugaid Laígde. Commentators hold that the figure first emerged as an aspect of Lugaid mac Con. It then took on a life of its own. Pedigrees and genealogies across the Middle Ages cite it often. The Corcu Loígde named him as their ancestor.

:::
```

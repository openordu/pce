``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llyfr Du Caerfyrddin lives today under the shelfmark National Library of Wales MS Peniarth 1. That collection label identifies the Black Book of Carmarthen, held to be the earliest surviving volume written wholly in the Welsh language. The book is thought to have stayed at Carmarthen Priory until Sir John Price rescued it. Later the antiquarian Robert Vaughan of Hengwrt secured the codex. The Hengwrt books then rested at Peniarth near Tywyn. In 1905 Sir John Williams, baronet, bought these volumes for the Welsh nation, and in 1909 the group moved to the newly opened National Library of Wales at Aberystwyth. From that time the Black Book has carried the Peniarth 1 number.

:::
```

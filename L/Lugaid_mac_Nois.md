``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Lugaid mac Nóis, a son of Alamiach, shares a fosterage with Cúchulainn in the Táin Bó Cúailnge. While Cúchulainn is away, Forgall, the father of Emer, seeks a husband for her. He fixes on Lugaid, a king of the south. Then Lugaid mac Nóis learns that Cúchulainn himself desires Emer. He draws back at once and will have nothing further to do with the match. The tale paints a rival who yields the woman to the hero without complaint.

:::
```

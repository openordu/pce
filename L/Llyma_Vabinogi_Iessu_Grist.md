``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llyma Vabinogi Iessu Grist is a Middle Welsh prose text. Its opening line can be read as 'Here begins the Narrative of Jesus Christ'. The work puts into Welsh an apocryphal infancy gospel, a telling of the early years of Christ and his mother that the canonical accounts pass over in silence. It stands wholly within the religious prose tradition of medieval Wales, which carried many holy and edifying narrative tales to readers of the language. The story is based on the Latin Gospel of the Pseudo-Matthew. It also shares ground with the wider Gospel harmony, blending legendary episodes of the Christ child into one connected account.

:::
```

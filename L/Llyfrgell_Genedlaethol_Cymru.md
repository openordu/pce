``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Llyfrgell Genedlaethol Cymru, the National Library of Wales, stands at Aberystwyth. It is the pre-eminent documentary resource for Welsh and Celtic material in the twentieth and twenty-first centuries. Its collected holdings include some 4.6 million published items, built partly through legal deposit and partly through careful overseas purchasing. The early manuscript core quickly gained a much larger set of archives, so the Library became a working record office for the whole of Wales. It preserves the papers of the Church in Wales, of landed estates, and of many public bodies and notable figures. It also gathers maps, paintings, prints, and some 750,000 photographs. The National Screen and Sound Archive for Wales, founded in 2001, cares for Welsh-language and other sound and film recordings. Preservation is as important as collecting, and a new visitor centre opened in 2004. Digital and digitized material now plays a growing role in making the collections available. The Library remains the single most important home for early Welsh manuscripts, such as the Peniarth series.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Llyfr Coch Hergest is the Red Book of Hergest written in Welsh. It is one of the great medieval Welsh manuscripts, usually dated 1382 to 1410. It holds the Mabinogi of the Mabinogion, poetry of the Gogynfeirdd, histories, grammars, and proverbs, though it excludes religious works and laws. A single scribe copied nearly all of it.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Lewis, Saunders, led modern Welsh drama in the twentieth century. He lived from 1893 to 1985. He studied at Liverpool after the First World War. Early English and Welsh theatre left deep marks on him. He moved to Cardiff in 1921. In 1925 he helped found Plaid Cymru, the Welsh nationalist party. He wrote plays in Welsh for the stage, radio, and television. Blodeuwedd appeared in 1949. Siwan followed in 1954. He also wrote novels, poems, and criticism. His essay Tynged yr Iaith warned about the fate of the Welsh language. He received an honorary doctorate from the University of Wales. He was nominated twice for the Nobel Prize in literature.

:::

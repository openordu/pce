::: magazinestyle
Lúí is the modern Irish spelling of Lugaid. Lugaid is one of the most common hero names in early Irish tradition, borne by several kings and warriors of legend. Modern texts render the older spellings with this accented form. The entry points readers to the Lugaid family of figures, several of whom hold separate articles in this encyclopedia.

:::

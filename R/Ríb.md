``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ríb, printed also as Ribh, is the brother of Eochu mac Maireda. He serves as foster-son to Ébliu in two comparable place-name stories, one for Lough Neagh and one for Lough Ree. The second lake carries the Irish form Loch Ribh, and Ríb may stand as its eponym. Both stories tell how the lakes received their names.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
rígfhéinnid names the leader of the Fianna in Irish tradition. The compound ríg and fiinnid gives king-warrior as the literal reading of the title. MacKillop ties the rank to Fionn mac Cumhaill as its best-known holder. The rígfhéinnid stood at the head of the warrior-band in the Fenian cycle and led the hunt across Ireland. The title marked the highest office among the Fianna's roving warriors in early Celtic tradition.

:::
```

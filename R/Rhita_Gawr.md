``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Rhita Gawr is a Welsh giant, his name built on cawr, giant. He wore the beards of the kings he had slain, and he counted that tally as his boast. A place-name story tells how Arthur killed him and ordered his men to heap stones over the body. That heap forms Gwyddfa Rhita, Rhita's Cairn. The phrase stands as an old Welsh term for Snowdon.

:::
```

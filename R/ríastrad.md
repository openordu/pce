``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ríastrad, with the variant spellings ríastradh and ríastarthae, names the warp-spasm or battle-frenzy of the hero Cúchulainn. MacKillop points the reader to the Cúchulainn headword for the fuller record of the figure and his transformations. The ríastrad forms part of the warrior's distorting combat shape.

:::
```

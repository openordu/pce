::: magazinestyle
Rheged was a Welsh-speaking kingdom of the fifth to seventh centuries in what is today southern Scotland. Its king Urien drew high praise from the poet Taliesin. The realm centred on the Eden valley around modern Carlisle, yet no account fixes its exact borders or size. Its influence reached Strathclyde in the north and Gododdin in the east, and ran south into present Lancashire and Yorkshire. In earlier times the Novantae held this same territory.

:::

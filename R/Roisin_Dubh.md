``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Roisin Dubh, the dark little rose, is a personification of Ireland. Her name may go back to a seventeenth-century poem. Owen Roe MacWard is the credited author. James Clarence Mangan recast her as Dark Rosaleen in 1847. That English version outshone the Irish original in fame. In Aisling verse her name marks a dark-haired maiden. She walks the roads by herself. She looks for a king with the strength to undo her chains. She stands as a later shape of Sovereignty, the old goddess who watched over the land.

:::
```

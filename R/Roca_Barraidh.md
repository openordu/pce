::: magazinestyle
Roca Barraidh is a name used on Barra, one of the Outer Hebrides. It marks an enchanted island far out on the dim western horizon. Privileged fishermen alone may glimpse it, and only on rare occasions. The Scottish Gaelic roc covers anything that tangles a fishing hook, and the tops of seaweed above the water. The name joins the family of western otherworld islands beside Tír na nÓg. Such horizon islands belong to the same seam of story as the western otherworld itself.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Reilig na Rígh is an Irish phrase meaning cemetery of the kings. The printed forms include Reilg na Riogh and Relig na Ri. Since the eighteenth century the name has marked a large, ringed enclosure. It sits at Cruachain in County Roscommon, though writers call it a fanciful label. Tradition makes the enclosure the resting ground of Connacht's kings, yet no modern archaeological evidence supports that claim. The site still belongs to the Cruachain royal landscape.

:::

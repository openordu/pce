``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
roth rámach names the fabulous flying machine constructed in Christian legend by Simon Magus with the help of Mug Ruith. The craft appeared at the gathering of Tlachtga, a site named for the daughter of Mug Ruith. Later tradition reads the machine as a deadly contraption that would cross Europe before Judgement Day. The punishment runs through the way each nation sent disciples to Simon Magus. Mug Ruith grounds the wider legend of the contraption.

:::
```

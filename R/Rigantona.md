::: magazinestyle
Rigantona is a hypothesized title meaning great queen, compared with Old Irish rigan. The name would fit a goddess of Roman Britain whose shape stays shadowy. Scholars treat the figure as a forerunner of the Welsh Rhiannon. The title remains a hypothesis, drawn from name comparison rather than from any cult record.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Riothamus is a Romanized name. Several shadowy figures of fifth-century Britain carry it. The scholar Geoffrey Ashe proposed that two chronicle citations of the name could match the historical Arthur. Twelve thousand emigrants left Britain for Brittany under one Riothamus. Domnonia, a Breton petty kingdom, also had a ruler of that name. No chronicle gives any of them a clear biography, and the identifications stay debated. Ashe set out the case in an essay on Arthur's historical origins. The essay appeared in a 1988 Odense collection on the legend's vitality. The name remains a loose thread between British and Breton history.

:::

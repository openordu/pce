``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Rawlinson B 502 is a great Irish vellum manuscript. It now rests in the Bodleian Library at Oxford. The codex gathers much of the oldest Irish learning. It holds genealogies, sagas, and historical poems. A late-eleventh-century copy of certain charters shares the volume. The main language of those charters is Latin. They still carry great value for Celtic study. The charters hold over a thousand place names and over six thousand personal names. Most of these are Old Breton, with some Latin and Frankish forms. The manuscript also preserves other key Irish texts. One part has been joined to the Saltair na Rann and its kindred works. Another name put on the collection is the Book of Glendalough. Rawlinson B 502 stands among the greatest witnesses to medieval Irish letters.

:::
```

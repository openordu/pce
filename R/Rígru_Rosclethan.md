``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Rígru Rosclethan, also printed Roisclethan, means of the large eyes. She is the wife of Dáire (2), and she rules as queen of Tir Taimgire, the Land of Promise. The pair earns the name sinless because they come together only to produce Ségda Sáerlabraid. Conn hopes that son will lift the moral blight from Ireland. At Tara Rígru saves the boy from sacrifice. She takes the shape of a wailing woman beside a lowing cow. She also warns Conn Cétchathach that Ireland stays a wasteland until he sets Bé Chuma aside. Conn Cétchathach carries the title of the Hundred Battles. The couple's sinless state serves the tale's design, for their union exists to produce one needed child.

:::
```

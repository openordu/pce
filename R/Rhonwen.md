``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Rhonwen is a Welsh name built from rhon and wen. The first means pike or lance, and figuratively tall or slender. The second carries the sense fair. Welsh tradition assigns the name to Hengist's daughter. Hengist stands as the semi-legendary fifth-century Saxon invader of Britain. Geoffrey of Monmouth, writing in the twelfth century, swapped the name for Rowena, which became the better-known form.

:::
```

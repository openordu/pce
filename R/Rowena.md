``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Rowena names the daughter of Hengist in the semi-legendary history. Hengist stands as the fifth-century Saxon invader of Britain. Old English elements hreod, banner, and wine, friend, sit behind the form. Its possible Welsh antecedent is Rhonwen. Sir Walter Scott chose Rowena as the Saxon heroine of Ivanhoe in 1819.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pryderi is a chief hero of Welsh legend. He is the son of Pwyll and of Rhiannon. He alone appears by name in every branch of that four-part saga. The child vanished a short time after his birth. Teyrnon Twrf Liant and his own wife found the boy, and they gave him the name Gwri Gwallt Euryn, which means golden hair. Branch one of the Mabinogi collection tells how this exchange of children happened. On the night of the feast of Beltane the newborn son of Rhiannon disappeared, and blood appeared on her mouth. The court accused Rhiannon of killing and eating her own child. A strange creature had carried the boy away and had set him down at a distant farm. On that same night Teyrnon saved a colt from a monster, and he took the foundling into his house. Teyrnon raised the child under the name Gwri. When Gwri learned the truth about his birth, he went to claim his place as a son of Pwyll. He freed Rhiannon from her burden of shame, and she gave him the name Pryderi, a word that means care, for all that she had suffered. The poem Preiddiau Annwfn carries an English title about spoils from Annwfn. It shows Pryderi beside Pwyll. There he states that the prison which holds Gwair is strongly furnished. Parts of his story fed the later figure of Perceval in the legends about Arthur. Through this link, through his birth in the otherworld, and through his rule after Pwyll, Pryderi binds the whole cycle of branches into one tale.

:::
```

Pliny the Elder (AD 23-79) was a Roman scholar, historian, and scientific encyclopaedist. His work supplies some of the closest ancient examination of the druids among the classical commentators. Pliny learned about the Celts at first hand. He served first as an army officer and later governed Gallia Narbonensis in southern Gaul, near modern Marseille. He wrote prolifically, yet only his thirty-seven-part Historia Naturalis and his letters survive. The Natural History reached English readers through the Loeb Classical Library edition (10 volumes, Cambridge, Mass., and London, 1938-63).

:::

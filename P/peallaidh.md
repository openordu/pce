::: magazinestyle
peallaidh names a regional Scottish Gaelic water-haunting spirit related to the ùruisg. The form is especially tied to the town of Aberfeldy, thirty-two miles northwest of Perth. The town borrows its name from this creature. Although not a water-spirit proper, the peallaidh was said to haunt rivers, lochs, and the seashore. The wider family of such figures also includes the fuath, and the tradition sits within Highland folklore.

:::

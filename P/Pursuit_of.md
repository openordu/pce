::: magazinestyle
Pursuit of is the English opening for Tóraigheacht. Irish narrative titles conventionally begin with this phrase. The formula marks a whole class of pursuit tales. The article on Tóraigheacht carries the main discussion. Tóraigheacht Dhiarmada agus Ghráinne is a noted example.

:::

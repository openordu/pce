``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pen Annwfn names a title Pwyll earns by honouring his pact with Arawn. The phrase means head of Annwfn. Arawn rules the Welsh otherworld, and Pwyll serves him in his shape for one year. The story opens the Mabinogi's first branch. Winning the title proves Pwyll kept every term of the pact. The form Pen Annwfii appears in some editions, and readers meet the same reward under that spelling.

:::
```

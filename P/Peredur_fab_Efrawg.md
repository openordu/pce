``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Peredur, the son of Efrawg, is a hero of Welsh legend. He stands as the Welsh counterpart of Perceval, the knight of French romance. His tale forms one of the three Welsh Romances, called Y Tair Rhamant. He travels to the court of King Arthur. He asks to be made a knight. He rides out on a long quest. His story holds the pattern of a search. That search mirrors the search for the Grail in France. Welsh writers shaped his name and his deeds. The hero stands apart from the foreign Perceval. He shows that Welsh legend kept its own champion. 

:::
```

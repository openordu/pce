``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Proto-Celtic weapons show how the Celtic peoples outfitted their warriors. Reconstructed words reveal a rich weapon vocabulary. The word for spearhead carries a strong record. A range of terms survives for spear, sword, hilt, and sling. The finds match the words of the language. Spearheads form most of the weapons from graves of the later Bronze era and the early Iron Age. They were the cheap weapon of choice then. Heads range from small blades to ornate long forms. Leaf-shaped bronze heads appear down to about 750 BC. Iron took over later. Large iron spearheads reached about fifty centimetres. Some were lances or the ornament of banners. The words also cover the sling and maybe a catapult. These names bind Celtic speech to the world of war.

:::
```

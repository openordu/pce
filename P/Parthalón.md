``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Parthalón is a variant spelling MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to Partholón, who leads Ireland's second mythic invasion in the Lebor Gabála and stands in full under that headword. The main article derives the borrowed name, since earliest Irish had no letter P: the source is probably Bartholomaeus, which St Jerome with Isidore of Seville expound: the name means one who holds back the waters, a figure who lived through the biblical Flood. Partholón descends from Magog, yet the story makes him a Greek prince: he kills his father Sera and his mother to seize their kingdom, and the deed costs him his left eye. After seven years of wandering he lands in Ireland with his wife Dealgnaid, his sons, and their households. His druids are the three brothers Eólas, Fios, and Fochmarc. He dies after thirty years near modern Tallaght, and a century later the plague takes the rest of his people. A shard of his persona survives as the harvest demon Parthanán. Readers meeting this spelling should follow it back to Partholón.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pfalzfeld is the site in Germany where a carved stone stele was found. The stele stands in the early Celtic style. Each side carries a human head. The heads wear moustaches and narrow beards of palmette form. They also bear headbands and a small palmette on the brow. Floral and curving motifs cover the whole stone. The heads and borders recall early La Tene ornament. The carving sits in the La Tene Early Style of the later fifth century BC. It blends Western floral patterns with bands that run around the stone. The result looks like small metal jewelry copied into a great rock. The stele now rests in a museum at Bonn.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pedair Cainc y Mabinogi is the full Welsh title, the Four Branches of the Mabinogi, under which MacKillop 2008 treats the masterwork of medieval Welsh literature inside the Mabinogi entry. The branches number four: Pwyll, Branwen, Manawydan, and Math. The dictionary holds that the collection drew from extensive oral tradition, hangs together only loosely, and was probably put together by a single hand before the middle of the twelfth century. The two complete manuscripts that carry it are the White Book of Rhydderch and the Red Book of Hergest, with fragments surviving in collections older than the fifteenth century. The first branch, Pwyll Prince of Dyfed, follows its hero into the Otherworld, through his marriage to Rhiannon, and to the birth of Pryderi. The wider form Mabinogion, which Lady Charlotte Guest coined in the translation she issued between 1838 and 1849, covers twelve tales including the branches plus Culhwch ac Olwen and the dreams of Macsen Wledig and Rhonabwy. Readers should continue at Mabinogi for the full account.

:::
```

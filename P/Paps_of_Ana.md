::: magazinestyle
Paps of Ana is the English heading under which MacKillop 2008 files the twin hills treated in full at Dá Chích Anann, the Irish name meaning the two breasts of Ana. The main article places the two round-topped summits in County Kerry, 2,273 and 2,284 feet in height, three miles south of Knockacappal and seventeen miles southeast of Killarney. Their survival into modern Irish speech, the dictionary notes, bears testimony to the association of the goddess Ana with the province of Munster. Locally the hills are known simply as the Paps. A reader who meets the English or short local form should follow either back to Dá Chích Anann, where the dictionary keeps the entry, and onward to Ana herself for the goddess behind the landscape.

:::

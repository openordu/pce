::: magazinestyle
piskie, with variants pisky, piskey, pisgie, pigsie, pixie, and pixy, names the Cornish fairy of the western English countryside. Cornish piskie was metathesized in Somerset and Devon to forms like pixie and pigsie. The figure plays tricks on roads and rivers, sending travellers the wrong way. The Cornish conception is a wizened old man who threshes corn and rides horses at night. Variants range from earthy and sturdy in Somerset to slight, white, and naked in Devon.

:::

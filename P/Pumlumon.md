::: magazinestyle
Pumlumon is a mountain of central Wales, 10 miles west of Llanidloes, rising 2,468 feet. Its Welsh elements are pum, five, and lumon, whose sense covers peaks or beacons. Springs on the mountain give rise to the Severn and the Wye. Tradition treats Pumlumon as the central point of Wales, an omphalos. Readers set it beside Uisnech in Ireland and beside Kermaria in Brittany. Thomas Gray and other eighteenth-century Celtic revivalists preferred the Plinlimmon spelling.

:::

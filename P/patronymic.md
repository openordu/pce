``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
patronymic names a male ancestor in Celtic naming. The father usually gives the form in early Welsh and Irish records. The construction ap Rhŷs or mac Cumhaill works as a clear example. In early periods such names did not function as family surnames in the modern sense. Companion to the matronymic, which records a female ancestor. Patronymics shifted over time as fixed family names became the standard. The shift moved through different regions at different speeds.

:::
```

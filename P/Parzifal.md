``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Parzifal is a spelling MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the Grail hero treated in full at Percevel, where the variant forms Perceval, Percival, Parsifal, and Perchevael are gathered with it. The main article sets the hero inside the Arthurian legends, with Celtic antecedents and parallels that include the Amadán Mór, Fionn mac Cumhaill, Peredur, and Peronnik, and and sets his literary birth in the Conte del Graal, Chrétien de Troyes's romance of about 1182. The Parzifal spelling carries its own weight in the tradition, since it is the form associated with the German branch of the Grail story, best known through Wolfram von Eschenbach's great romance, and Wagner later chose the related form Parsifal for his 1882 music drama. Every retelling keeps the same shape of hero: an innocent quester, a rustic raised to the hero's seat. Readers meeting Parzifal should follow the form back to Percevel for the fuller account.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pryderi fab Pwyll is the son of Pwyll and Rhiannon. The boy goes missing, and Teyrnon of Gwent Is Coed raises him as a ward. Early years grant the child a golden head of hair. He is later restored to his true kindred and named Pryderi. That name carries the fear that his absence raised in Rhiannon. She had been punished in error while the child was lost. The First Branch closes with the quiet death of Pwyll. Pryderi then takes the kingship of Dyfed. In the Third Branch he stands beside Manawydan and is caught as a prisoner. A magic castle holds him captive in the later part of the tale. At last the Fourth Branch ends his life in single combat with Gwydion. That duel caps a war that Gwydion had set in motion. The slaying of the king makes a place for the rise of Lleu.

:::
```

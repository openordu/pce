::: magazinestyle
pangs/debility of the Ulstermen marks the curse of Macha on the warriors of Ulster in the Táin Bó Cuailnge tradition. The king's spouse forced his partner to race a horse, and she punished the watching Ulstermen with the pangs of childbirth across nine generations. The episode sits at the opening of the great Cattle Raid of Cooley. MacKillop lists the Irish form as noínden Ulad. The full record of that epic is filed under Táin Bó Cuailnge.

:::

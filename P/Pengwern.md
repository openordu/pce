::: magazinestyle
Pengwern served medieval Wales as one of its three principal courts. The ninth- or tenth-century poem Eryr Pengwern (The Eagle of Pengwern) cites the court by name. Earlier scholars placed Pengwern at Shrewsbury. Current readings keep it in Shropshire, yet no firm identification stands. Jenny Rowland treated the court in her 1990 Cambridge study of early Welsh saga poetry. Patrick Sims-Williams added a note in the Welsh Historical Review, 17 (June 1994).

:::

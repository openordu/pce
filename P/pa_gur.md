::: magazinestyle
Pa Gur yv y Porthaur is a short Welsh poem. The title asks who guards the gate. It forms an early catalogue of the heroes of King Arthur. The poem names their feats more briefly than later books. It belongs with the triads and the graves englynion as a smaller store of old matter. The verse lists many champions. It gives the names of Cai, Beduir, the son of Modron, and the son of Lasr. Manawidan son of Llųr also appears. Uthir Bendragon has a place. The deeds include a combat at a spot that may be the court of Arthur. Another deed is a fight with a great cat. The poet also tells of a clash with the dog-headed people, folk with faces like a dog. Those beings call to mind the far heads written of by older writers. A named hill marks this fight. It stood as the seat of the Gododdin lord. A story of the taking of a giant's daughter waits in the later work about Kulhwch, but the poem gives no sign of that tale.

:::

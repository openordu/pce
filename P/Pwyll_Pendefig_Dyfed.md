``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pwyll Pendefig Dyfed is the hero of the First Branch of the Mabinogi. The First Branch opens the Welsh four branch cycle. Pwyll rules the southern realm of Dyfed. He weds Rhiannon, and the pair gain a son. The child vanishes during the same night that he arrives. Rhiannon then suffers a charge that she killed the boy. She must carry arriving guests upon her back as punishment. A lord named Teyrnon finds the child and takes him in. He and his wife raise the boy, a child with golden hair. At last the youth comes to his father's court. Rhiannon says that her cares are over, so the son is named Pryderi. The name holds the sense of worry in Welsh. After Pwyll's death the young ruler holds Dyfed and weds Cigfa. The tale draws on echo of Celtic myth, pairing Rhiannon with Epona. A wronged wife, a rash promise, and a giant claw all play their part. Pwyll changes from a hasty youth into a careful ruler.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
pencerdd names the chief poet of early Wales, a title that demanded preparation across nine years of study. Subjects included the rules of language, the patterns of verse, and the lineages of the great houses. Only a holder of the title could teach a bard. As late as 1547 the poet Simwnt Fychan received a written licence to bear the rank, and the document survives. The Welsh rank roughly parallels the early Irish ollam.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
pwca names a Welsh solitary fairy of the countryside. The figure is a friskier version of the bwci, and the link to the English Puck of folklore is widely accepted. The Cornish bucca and Manx BUGGANE belong to the same wider family of goblin figures. The pwca ranges across Welsh oral tradition as a creature of the night air. In some tales the fairy guides travellers, in others it leads them astray. Modern retellings keep the dual nature.

:::
```

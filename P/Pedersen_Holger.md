``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Pedersen, Holger is better known to modern readers as Holger Pedersen. He was Danish, a Celticist, and a student of the Indo-European family. His long series of comparative Celtic grammars made him a master of the field. The centrepiece of his output was the Vergleichende Grammatik der keltischen Sprachen. This two-volume work is known in English simply as the Comparative Grammar. It was printed from 1909 to 1913. He wrote the Concise Comparative Celtic Grammar with Henry Lewis. That appeared in 1937. Two earlier books set out his range. The dissertation of 1895 was titled Das indogermanische s im Slavischen. The 1897 study Aspirationen i Irsk dealt with aspiration in Irish. A further work, Les pronoms démonstratifs de l'ancien arménien, appeared in 1905. Pedersen closed his life in Copenhagen. He died there in 1953. His grammar books still count as standard guides. They serve both the Celtic and the wider Indo-European languages.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Caradog Prichard was a Welsh poet and novelist. He was born in the slate country of North Wales. His first poems appeared in the journal Awduron Cyfoes. He won the singer's crown at the great festival of Wales three separate times. The poem Y Briodas tells of his mother's oath and decline. The second, Penyd, carries that story further. The third, Y Gan Ni Chanwyd, turns down a fresh path. Much of the verse is deeply personal. He is best known for the novel Un Nos Ola Leuad. The work, One Moonlight Night, sits in his home district. It gives a child's view of life in the slate towns. A collected poems came out in 1979, the year before his death.

:::

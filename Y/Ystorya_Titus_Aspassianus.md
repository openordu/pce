``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ystorya Titus Aspassianus is a Welsh prose work of the Middle Ages. Its roots lie in Latin texts of the Christian apocrypha. The source of the story is Vindicta Salvatoris, which means the avenging of the Saviour. That Latin text deals with the punishment that followed the crucifixion. The Welsh tale places the Jews at the centre as the chief culprits. It builds out the biblical account of what came after Christ's death. Scribes carried the story into several leading manuscripts. These include Peniarth 5, also called the White Book of Rhydderch. Another copy survives on a Llanstephan roll dated to about 1400. A later witness, kept today in Cardiff's national collection in Wales, dates from 1450. The story is one sign of how far apocryphal legend reached in medieval Welsh prose.

:::
```

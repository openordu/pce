::: magazinestyle
Hall, Lady Augusta (1802-1896), born Augusta Waddington, led campaigns for Welsh culture. Her bardic name was Gwenynen Gwent. She promoted the Welsh language, the triple harp, Welsh music, and Welsh dance. From peasant dress she developed the national costume. In 1837 she helped form the Welsh Manuscripts Society. She also assisted with Llandovery College and the magazine Y Gymraes. (Lady) Llanofer gave her patronage to eisteddfodau.

:::

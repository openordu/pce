``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The harp is the stringed instrument that represents Ireland today. Ancient people tied it to the beneficent god the Dagda. The Dagda's harp flew across the land at his call. It struck down anyone who stood in its path. That harp produces only three melodies. They are sleep, grief, and laughter. A similar harp appears in the Welsh tale of Culhwch ac Olwen. Koch's account records a deep history for the instrument. The oldest Irish word for such an instrument was crott. The word sits close to the chrotta named by the poet Venantius Fortunatus. That poet linked the chrotta to the Britons. Early Irish verse uses both crott and cruit. Ireland later adopted the triangular frame harp. The eleventh-century shrine of Saint Maedoc shows that form. The famous Brian Boru harp also shows it. That well-known instrument dates from the fourteenth century. From that same century the harp was a heraldic device of Ireland. Henry VIII set the harp on Irish coins in 1534. The English court kept an Irish harper named Cormac MacDermott. Elizabeth I and James I both maintained his post. James I also added the harp to his own royal emblem for the first time. Romantic nationalism later made the harp its leading emblem. Nationalist movements adopted the harp as a broad symbol. The Guinness Corporation uses the harp for its logo. The harp still heads Irish coinage and stationery. Edward Bunting gathered and printed much of the old music. His collections appeared across the years from 1796 to 1840. The Belfast and Dublin Irish Harp Societies hired older musicians to teach. Arthur O'Neill, a blind harper, was one such teacher. Public taste moved away from the old wire-strung sound for a time. Since the 1960s a new group has revived that practice. Musicians study Bunting's pages and run experiments to rebuild it. The Welsh harp line also carried on. The triple harp lived on in the playing of Nansi Richards. She lived from 1888 to 1979. That triple span now returns with many fresh players. Folk musicians play the small harp. The pedal harp accompanies the cerdd dant singers. The same pedal harp also works as a solo instrument.
```

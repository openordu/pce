::: magazinestyle
himbas forosnai is a variant spelling that MacKillop records in the entry for the canonical headword imbas forosnai. The phrase names the inspiration of prophetic illumination in early Irish tradition. MacKillop treats the variant as interchangeable with the canonical spelling. He notes that the full record of imbas forosnai appears in the Historia Bríttonum and other early Welsh and Irish sources. MacKillop places the variant as a cross-reference to the headword imbas forosnai.

:::

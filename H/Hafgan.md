``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Hafgan is the Otherworldly rival whom Arawn of Annwn must face. The name may carry the sense of summer white. Year after year the two were meant to fight one another. Arawn once persuaded Pwyll to wear his shape for a full year. He told Pwyll to deal Hafgan a single blow, for a second would bring him back. Pwyll struck once and laid Hafgan low, then returned to his own world. Some see echoes of Hafgan in the Green Knight who tests Gawain. Others pair him with the Arthurian figure Gromer Somerjoure.

:::
```

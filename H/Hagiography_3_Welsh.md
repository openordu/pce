::: magazinestyle
Hagiography: Welsh covers the Lives of saints such as David, Cadoc, Deiniol, Gildas, Illtud, and Teilo. These local figures were founders and missionaries rather than martyrs, unlike most Continental saints. Their cults stayed near home until about 1100, when the Norman Conquest drove clerics to compile Latin Vitae in Wales. Welsh translations exist for only David and Beuno, and other traditions were set down in English or Latin.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Highland Games are a social gathering built around music and sport. Tradition ties the Highland Games to a hill run ordered by King Malcolm Canmore at Braemar. The modern form began in 1816 with the Braemar Wright's Society. Queen Victoria gave the meeting royal backing in 1848. Competitions include sprinting, hill races, and jumping. Heavy events feature putting the stone and throwing the hammer. Teams also toss the caber and tug at the rope. Bands play the pipes and Highland dancers perform. The Games became a tourist attraction that satisfies a global image of Scotland. The revival is bound up with Balmorality and the Mac Can Mór tradition. Walter Scott and Highland Gatherings carried this romantic image forward.

:::

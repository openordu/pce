::: magazinestyle
Hagiography: Irish covers the lives of saints in Latin and in the vernacular, over a period of one thousand years. From four early Latin Lives created around 700, rival churches at Armagh, Kildare, and Iona shaped accounts of Patrick, Brigit, and Colum Cille. Further vernacular Lives of these saints, and of Adomnán, followed from the ninth century. Monks gathered many into collections such as the Codex Salmanticensis. The Franciscan John Colgan printed them at Louvain in the 1640s.

:::

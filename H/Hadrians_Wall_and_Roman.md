::: magazinestyle
Hadrian's Wall and Roman Britain's northern frontier is a map caption. This stone and turf barrier ran about 117 km from Bowness (Maia) on the Solway to a Wallsend fort on the Tyne. The west was turf-built, the east part stone. Mile castles and turrets lined it, and the vallum ditch guarded its south. Not a solid defence, it served as a forward post controlling traffic and trade between the Roman province and the free northern tribes.

:::

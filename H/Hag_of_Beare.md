::: magazinestyle
The Hag of Beare is the English name for the sovereignty figure whom the Irish call the Cailleach Bhéirre. She greets a hero or knight as an ugly old woman and asks for his love. Once she gains love, she turns into a beautiful young woman. The hag lives through at least seven stretches of her youth, and so each of her husbands ages and dies while she stays young. She raised fifty foster children on the Beare headland. That headland lies in south-western Ireland, where her story is strongest. In this reading the Hag of Beare stands for the land itself, renewed afresh by each new king. Writers also give her the names Digde and Duineach.

:::

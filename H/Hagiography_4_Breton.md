``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Breton hagiography holds the largest body of early medieval Celtic saints' Lives to survive in Latin. The First Life of Samson of Dol is held to be the earliest of the Celtic vitae, dated to the seventh or eighth century. The Lives tell of missionaries who crossed the Channel and founded Domnonia. They treat saints such as Gildas, Samson, and Paul Aurelian. Their authors drew on old inscribed stones and older records. Healing miracles feature widely, and many tales echo an older head-cult story.

:::
```

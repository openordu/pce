``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Hychdwn Hir is a Welsh name that means tall piglet. He is an offspring of the gods Gwydion and Gilfaethwy. In the tale the two brothers took the shape of a boar and a sow. The offspring that came of this pair was Hychdwn Hir. He is one of the strange animal children that the two gods produced. The story forms part of the wider account of the transformations of Gwydion and Gilfaethwy.

:::
```

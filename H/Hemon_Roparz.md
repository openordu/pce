``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Roparz Hemon was a Breton writer and language revivalist. He came into the world in 1900 at Brest, a port on the Atlantic coast of Brittany, under the name Louis-Paul Némo. He became one of the most influential voices of the Breton movement. He was a prolific writer of dictionaries, grammars, translations, essays, and literary works. He set up the Breton-language journal Gwalarn, which ran from 1925 to 1944 and boosted modern written Breton. In 1941 he led Breton broadcasting on Radio Roazon-Breiz and headed the Celtic institute of Brittany. He also set up the journal Sterenn and the weekly paper Arvor. His wartime role cost him his civil rights. He later settled in Ireland and worked at Dublin's advanced studies institute. He founded the journal Ar Bed Keltiek in 1951. His life ended in Ireland in 1978.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Heithiurun is a different spelling of the name Etharún. The Irish Metrical Dindshenchas calls the figure the great idol of the early Britons of the island. The core of his name may bind him to Taranis, the thunderer of the Celts. The same text also names a lesser thunder god, Etirun. Heithiurun therefore calls to mind a native British deity of thunder known through Irish place-lore.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Heinen Fardd served as the leading poet at the court of the sixth-century king Maelgwn Gwynedd. In the Welsh story he is put to shame by the singer Taliesin. Taliesin proves himself the greater bard, and he comes to embarrass the court poet. The tale forms part of the contest between Taliesin and the poets who waited on Maelgwn. Heinen Fardd stands as the rival whom the young singer of wonders had to overcome.

:::

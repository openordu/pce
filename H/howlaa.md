``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
howlaa names a Manx form that MacKillop records as a cross-reference to the headword Manx Black Dog. MacKillop treats howlaa as a regional label for the Isle of Man's spectral hound. The fuller record of the Manx black dog, with its omen lore and its traditions, sits under Manx Black Dog. MacKillop notes the related spellings doo ineyoie and dooinney oie as further Manx variants.

:::
```

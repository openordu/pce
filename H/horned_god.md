``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
horned god names the British and Gaulish deity that MacKillop records as a cross-reference to the headword Cernunnos. MacKillop treats the seated horned figure of the Gundestrup Cauldron as the standard illustration of Cernunnos. He notes that the British horned god has sometimes been linked with the deity Mogons of north Britain. The fuller record of the Celtic horned god sits under the headword Cernunnos. MacKillop cites Haverfield's Archaeologia Aeliana article on the altars of Di Veteres as a key source.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Havgan is the anglicized spelling of the Welsh name Hafgan. The first branch of the Mabinogi makes Hafgan the rival of King Arawn of Annwfn. Each year the two kings are bound to fight. Arawn persuades the prince Pwyll to take on his body for a year and to fight in his place. Arawn warns Pwyll to give Hafgan a single blow, for a second blow would restore him. Pwyll strikes once and the rival falls. Some believe that Hafgan helped to shape the Green Knight, the challenger of Gawain, and the Arthurian figure Gromer Somerjoure. Havgan thus belongs to the Welsh Otherworld and to the later romance.

:::
```

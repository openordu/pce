Herla is a being of British folklore. In Herefordshire people believed an ancient king roamed the land. His name was Herla. He appeared as a tiny half-animal man. He rode a large goat. He had been trapped in the Otherworld centuries before. His duty was to lead the Wild Hunt. The hunt was called Herla's Rade. It ran on moonlit nights. Some sources say he passed this duty to the English king. He then rested in the Otherworld.

MacKill tells the fuller tale of how the king came to wander. At his own wedding a small king, a cat thy and a goat's feet, asked to join the feast and then bade the earth king to his own home a year from that day. K dated the date, and the small king took him below through the opening of a wide cavern into its hidden realm. There he took a tiny hound and a command that he must not set it down until it left his palm on its own. When he walked out he found his old crown gone. A shepherd could not answer in the tongue of his day, and said only that the king had not been seen for two hundred years. The Saxon speech now filled the land. Some of his own men who leapt to their feet touched the dust and vanished.

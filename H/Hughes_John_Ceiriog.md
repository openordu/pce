::: magazinestyle
John Ceiriog Hughes was a Welsh poet. He lived from 1832 to 1887. He was born at Pen-y-bryn in Llanarmon, Denbighshire. He went to the local school first. He then worked briefly on his father's farm. He did not enjoy that work. Next he took an apprenticeship with a printer in Oswestry, which the Welsh call Croesoswallt. He wrote the words for about fifty traditional airs. Brinley Richards gathered those airs into the book Songs of Wales. John Ceiriog Hughes filled the songs with a warm patriotism. That collection sold many copies.

:::

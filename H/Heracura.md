``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Heracura is another spelling of the name Aeracura. The goddess carries both Celtic and Germanic traits. A stone tablet from Oberseebach in Switzerland pairs her with Dis Pater, the god of the world below. She also won worship near Stuttgart in Germany. Scholars think she was once a goddess of the earth before Dis Pater took her place. Her name may echo Hecate, the Greek queen of the underworld. A male form, Aericurus, is recorded in Northumberland. Heracura therefore names a deity of the earth and of the grave.

:::
```

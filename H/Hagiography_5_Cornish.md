``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Hagiography: Cornish considers the written Lives of Cornwall's saints. About one hundred and forty such saints are known to records. Surviving hagiography reaches us only for a few. St Petroc alone has Lives composed wholly in Cornwall. Bodmin Priory produced that work. The Life of St Piran adapts the Irish saint Ciarán of Saigir. The Life of St Rumon reworks the Breton St Ronan. Most evidence rests on church dedications and place names.

:::
```

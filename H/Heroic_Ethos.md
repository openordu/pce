``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The heroic ethos runs through the early Celtic literatures. It binds bravery, honour, and gift-giving. A hero wins fame in a struggle against stiff odds. He feeds the tribe and defends its life. Feasting shows rank through the distribution of portions. The finest share, the champion's portion, marks the senior warrior. Slights demand revenge. This code shapes both Irish and Welsh war tradition. The hero must die well to keep his name alive.

:::
```

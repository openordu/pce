``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Hyddwn is a Welsh name that means little stag. He is an offspring of Gilfaethwy and Gwydion. In the tale the two brothers took the shape of a stag and a hind. From this pairing came the fawn Hyddwn. Like his brother Hychdwn Hir, Hyddwn is one of the animal children born of the two gods. The story belongs to the cycle of shape-changing that the brothers share.

:::
```

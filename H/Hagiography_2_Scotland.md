``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Hagiography: Scottish saints' lives drew on Gaelic and Latin traditions. Scotland's hagiography stayed thin between the early writings and 1500. Most medieval collections survive only as fragments. The Aberdeen Breviary of 1510 reshaped this record. It gathered saints for every part of the nation. It honoured national figures such as Ninian and Margaret. The Breviary also passed off some Irish saints as Scots.

:::
```

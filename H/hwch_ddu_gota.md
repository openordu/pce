::: magazinestyle
hwch ddu gota is a MacKillop headword naming the Welsh bob-tailed black sow. MacKillop treats the form as a brief Welsh pointer without a fuller Welsh folklore article. The fuller record of the Welsh black sow tradition in Celtic folklore sits under related headwords for swine and the Tylwyth Teg.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
A. G. van Hamel was a Dutch Celtic scholar. He lived from 1886 to 1945 and died at Utrecht. He took his first degree in Amsterdam and moved to Berlin in 1911 to study under Kuno Meyer. He held a post at Bonn as lecturer before taking a Celtic post at Leiden in 1920. From 1923 he was professor at Utrecht, where he founded the chair of Celtic Studies. That chair still runs today. Van Hamel wrote widely on Irish and Breton scholarship. His standard editions cover the Middle Irish version of the Historia Brittonum, the birth tale of Cú Chulainn, and the Irish voyage tales known as immrama. He also worked on the mythical history of Ireland in Lebor Gabála Érenn. His many Irish studies in Dutch remain hard to find outside the Netherlands.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Welsh Hymns are verses of praise for God. People sing them together in worship. The earliest Welsh religious verse that survives is a set of 33 poems. These date from the 9th to the 12th centuries. Most older public hymnody used Latin. The best known early hymn is Gogonedog Arglwydd, henffych well, which means Greetings, glorious Lord. Carmarthen's Black Book preserves it around 1250. The Church in Wales still used it in 1941. The Poets of the Princes wrote more religious verse. The Reformation favoured the singing of psalms. William Midleton made a strict metre version of the Psalms in Welsh.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
hooded crow names the bird MacKillop records as a cross-reference to the headword crow. MacKillop treats the hoodie as one of the named regional labels for the corvid. The fuller record of the crow in Celtic folklore sits under crow. MacKillop notes that the hooded crow plays a role in banshee lore, particularly as the form taken by the spirit of a murdered woman.

:::

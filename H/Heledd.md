::: magazinestyle
Heledd may have been a woman of the seventh century. She is famous as the speaker of Canu Heledd, the Song of Heledd, a cycle of Welsh verse from the ninth or tenth century. Heledd is the last survivor of the royal line of Powys. In the verses she mourns her dead kin and her lost estate. Above all she grieves for her brother King Cynddylan, who fell in the early years of the seventh century. The centre of the cycle is the poem called Stafell Cynddylan, the Hall of Cynddylan. Some scholars see in her the land shown again as a goddess. Heledd thus gives a voice to the sorrow of a fallen kingdom.

:::

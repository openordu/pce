::: magazinestyle
dessel is the Old Irish word for the sunwise turn, the clockwise ritual direction recorded in early Irish sources. The MacKillop entry records it as a one-line see-reference pointing the reader to the headword sunwise turn. The full record of the sunwise ritual direction and its uses sits under the headword sunwise turn.

:::

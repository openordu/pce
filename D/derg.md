``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
derg is an Old Irish spelling of Modern Irish dearg, glossed as red. MacKillop uses derg to convey a red or ruddy hue. The word covers the colour of blood and flame. It also covers the orange of a tawny hue, as seen in ale or gold. The term enters compound forms such as dáire derg. It also names the place Lough Derg. The MacKillop entry treats derg as a colour adjective rather than a self-standing figure.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Daron is an obscure goddess of the oak in early Britain. The dictionary gives her a single line and no myth of her own. Her name survives in the landscape rather than in story. A rivulet in Gwynedd commemorates it, and that stream is the whole of her surviving cult. She stands beside the oak in the way other Celtic deities stand beside rivers and springs. Scholars rank her among the shadowy early figures whose worship left no written tale behind.

:::

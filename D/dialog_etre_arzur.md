``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Dialog etre Arzur ha Guynglaff is a verse piece in Middle Breton. Its full title names its two speakers. Arthur leads the exchange as a ruler of the Bretons. Guynglaff answers him across the whole talk. The piece works in the manner of a prophecy. It belongs to a set of Middle Breton sacred verse. That set includes later religious poems of the region. Within this body the dialogue ranks as the leading work. Its standing gives it a special worth. It gives rare proof of prophecy verse in Breton. The piece holds a central place in the Breton record.

:::
```

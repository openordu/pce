``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dea is the Latin word for goddess. Some commentators use the term when naming several Continental Celtic deities. The recorded pairings include Dea Arduinna, Dea Artio, Dea Matrona, and Dea Sequana. The word works as an epithet rather than a name of its own. Readers will find each goddess under her own entry.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Del Chliss was the spear of Cúchulainn. The weapon first belonged to Nechtan. It killed the sons of Nechtan Scéne. Before its famous career the name belonged to the goad of a charioteer, a split piece of wood. Readers should follow the Cúchulainn entry for the hero and the Nechtan entry for the weapon's first owner.

:::
```

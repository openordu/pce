::: magazinestyle
Declan, also written Déclán or Déaglán, was an important saint of early Ireland. He founded the monastery of Ardmore in what is now County Waterford. He served as patron of the Déisi, and people called him the Patrick of the Déisi. His life is hazy. Scholars cannot prove he lived before Saint Patrick or in the sixth century. Tradition traces his ancestry to the incestuous queen Clothra. His written life holds chronological contradictions. Tradition says he visited Rome twice, and the second journey may have included Dewi Sant in Wales. People credited him with many miracles and gave him deep veneration. His feast day of 24 July was observed across Ireland.

:::

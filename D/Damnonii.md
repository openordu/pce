Damnonii is a variant spelling of the name Dumnonii. The dictionary points the form to the Dumnonia record. Dumnonia was the Brittonic realm of south-west Britain that covered roughly modern Devon and Cornwall. Readers will find the kingdom and its people under the Dumnonia entry.

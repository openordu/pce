``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
De Gabáil in t-Sída belongs to the Mythological Cycle of Ireland. Its title means the taking of the mounds. The mounds stand for the circle of the Otherworld, where the Tuath Dé and the spirit people dwell. The tale fixes each great being to a mound in the landscape. Brug na Bóinne holds place among these homes, and the plain about it is near and fair. The taking assigns a place to every figure in the story. The subject joins legend to the older orders of the earth. Its links to old Irish lore and to the hero cycles help the reading of the myth.

:::
```

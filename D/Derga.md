``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Derga is the host whose name the dictionary routes to the tale of his ruined hostel. The full record sits with that story. Da Derga is an Irish god or hero whose name means the red god. Some texts give his name as nephew of the red goddess. His hostel is the setting for the tale of King Conaire. The king breaks his sacred vows there and dies of an unquenchable thirst. Readers will find the whole account under the record of Da Derga and his hostel.

:::
```

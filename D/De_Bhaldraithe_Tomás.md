``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tomás De Bhaldraithe was a leading scholar of Modern Irish. He was born at Ballinacor in County Limerick in 1916. He studied French and Irish at University College Dublin. A year at the Sorbonne deepened his dialect knowledge. He carried out field research on the spoken Irish of Conamara. He published these results in two detailed books. He earned his doctorate at the Dublin Institute in 1942. He then joined the university's Department of Modern Irish. He compiled the great English-Irish Dictionary in 1959. This dictionary remained a vital tool for Irish learners. In 1954 he created the university dialect archive. Later he held the chairs of Modern Irish and of Dialectology. He edited many key Modern Irish texts and journals. He served the Royal Irish Academy for many years. De Bhaldraithe died suddenly at a public event in 1996.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
díguin is an Old Irish term whose fullest sense is the breach of a lord's safeguard by the wounding or killing of a protected person. Among the gravest wrongs a person could commit against a king or great lord, the crime stood in a special class. Early Irish law treated the bond between a lord and those under his protection as a sacred trust. To harm such a person was to dishonor the host. The injury carried both political and ritual weight in early Ireland. díguin marks how seriously early Irish jurists viewed attacks on those under royal safeguard.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Daolghas is an Irish hero of the Fenian tales. Fionn mac Cumhaill tells his story in Feis Tighe Chonáin, the Feast at Conán's House. The tale answers a riddle. It asks which man was son to his own daughter. Daolghas lay dying. His daughter stooped down to kiss him. A burning spark leapt from his mouth into hers, and she conceived. She bore a son and named him for her father.

:::

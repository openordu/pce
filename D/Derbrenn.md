::: magazinestyle
Derbrenn is the first love of Angus Óg, the young god of the Tuatha Dé Danann. She acts as foster-mother to six children whom their own mother changed by magic into pigs. A band of one hundred heroes hunts the six. They beg help from Angus Óg. He refuses them until the six have performed the chain of wonders he demands. When those tasks are done within a year, Medb hunts the swine down. She slays all of them but one.

:::

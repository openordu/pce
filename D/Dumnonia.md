``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dumnonia was a Brittonic realm in south-west Britain whose lands covered roughly modern Devon and Cornwall. Roman rule organised the Dumnonii as a civitas centred near present-day Exeter. After the empire withdrew, Dumnonia survived as an independent kingdom. Its rulers held territory that would later become the English counties of Devon and Cornwall. Raids and incremental English expansion in the early medieval centuries pushed the kingdom's western edge slowly backwards. The kingdom declined as Anglo-Saxon power grew, and its identity faded into the separate cultural provinces of the south-west.

:::
```

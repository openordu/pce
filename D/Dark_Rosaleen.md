``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Roisin Dubh means Dark Rosaleen. This name belongs to an aisling figure. An aisling is a vision poem. The figure is a dark-haired maiden. She wanders the roadways alone. She hopes to meet a king. The king must be strong enough to break her bondage. She is a late form of the ancient goddess of sovereignty. The name carries political weight in Irish verse. Poets used the veiled figure to speak of the nation under English rule. Dark Rosaleen stood for Ireland herself. J. C. Mangan's translation of 1847 fixed the name in English verse. The dictionary enters Dark Rosaleen as that rendering of Roisin Dubh, the personification of Ireland.

:::
```

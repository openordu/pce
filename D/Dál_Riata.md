``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dál Riata was a Gaelic kingdom that spanned the western seaboard of Scotland and the Antrim region of north-east Ireland during the early medieval era. The ruler of Dál Riata claimed descent from the legendary Fergus Mór, while centres such as Dunadd and Iona gave the kingdom its political and religious weight. Saint Columba founded Iona in the sixth century, and kings such as Aedán mac Gabráin carried Dál Riata into wider northern politics before it faded under ninth-century pressures.

:::
```

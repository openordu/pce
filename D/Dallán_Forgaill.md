::: magazinestyle
Dallán Forgaill is a fabled poet of sixth-century Ireland. The form Forgael appears beside his name, and dall is the Irish word for blind. He is perhaps the first poet to whose name any poem may be pinned. Tradition credits him with Amra Choluimb Chille, a praise poem for St Colum Cille set down about 590. Some narratives style him a Bard of All Ireland. The dictionary identifies him with Eochaid Éigeas and sees him as the model for Forgoll. Both of those poets contend with Mongán in the tales. Yeats may have used him for the fictional Forgael in his drama Shadowy Waters of 1906.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Deoch is a proud and vain princess of the South in the tragic tale of the children of Lir. Her land is Munster. A curse had turned the children into swans. It was to endure only until a southern woman joined with a northern man. Deoch is the daughter of the Munster chieftain Finghein. She marries Lairgnéan, a prince of Connacht, and so the prophecy finds its shape. She voices a wish to own the four singing swans. Lairgnéan then sets out to win them for her. The violence of his seizure breaks the magic that disguised the children. The dictionary prints the spelling forms Deocha, Deoca, and Deichthe beside her name.

:::
```

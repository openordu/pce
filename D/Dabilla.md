::: magazinestyle
Dabilla is the lapdog of the river-goddess Bóand. The place-name story says Bóand's little dog perished on two sea rocks off the east coast of Brega. The rocks are identified with the 148-foot Rockabill rocks, whose old Irish name was Da Billa. They stand five miles north-east of Skerries in north County Dublin.

:::

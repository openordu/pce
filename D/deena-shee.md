::: magazinestyle
deena shee is a single-line cross-reference that MacKillop marks as a phonetic anglicization of the Irish word daoine. The phrase preserves the spoken sound of the Irish fairy name in English spelling. The conventional PCE target for the entry is the headword daoine sí, the people of the fairy mound in Modern Irish and Scottish Gaelic. The full record of the fairy people sits under daoine sí.

:::

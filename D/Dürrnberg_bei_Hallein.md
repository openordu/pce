::: magazinestyle
Dürrnberg is an ancient salt-mining site near the town of Hallein, Austria. It sits in the Salzburg area. This site is a key Iron Age mining centre. Celtic miners extracted the salt here from around the 6th down to the 1st century BC. They dug shafts of up to 200 metres long into the rock. The saline ground preserved organic tools and textiles. Found were two chariot burials. The first chariot went down in 1932. A second was opened in 1954. The finds include a beaked bronze flagon, gold wares, and a rich warrior grave. Long-distance trade linked the Dürrnberg with the Alpine south. Large graves held Etruscan and Greek vessels. The site grew into a major trade and crafts centre.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dana is a variant spelling of the Irish goddess Danu. Both names refer to the same figure. Danu appears in Irish tradition as a mother goddess. No myths about her survive today. Her name appears across many place-names. The Dane Hills carry her name. The river Danube in eastern Europe carries a related name. The Paps of Danu stand in Munster. These are two breast-shaped hills. The divine race called the Tuatha Dé Danann (Tuatha De Danann) takes its name from her. This name means the people of the goddess Danu. Some texts equate her with Anu. Scholars treat the names as related but distinct. The Tuatha Dé Danann later became the fairies of folklore.

:::
```

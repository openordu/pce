``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
daoine maithe is an Irish-language name meaning good people. MacKillop records it as one of several euphemisms for fairies in Modern Irish folklore. The phrase belongs to a family of polite substitutes that Irish and Scottish Gaelic speakers used in place of the direct fairy name. The related term daoine sídhe (often shortened to daoine sí or sidh) names the people of the fairy mound. The label daoine uaisle, meaning noble people or gentry, sits in the same cluster of flattering alternatives. Slua sí, meaning fairy host, names the troop of the otherworld in another turn of phrase. Each of these expressions lets a speaker name the fairies without speaking their proper name.

:::
```

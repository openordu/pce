::: magazinestyle
Der is a poetic Irish word meaning daughter or girl. The dictionary treats it as the first syllable of many figures from early Ireland. That syllable sometimes stands apart from the rest of the name. The dictionary alphabetizes such entries letter by letter. Readers who meet Der- at the head of a name should look for the full form elsewhere in the record.

:::

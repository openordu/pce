``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
duma is an Irish word meaning mound, tumulus, or barrow, and it names a burial-ground in the landscape. The term appears as a recurring element in many ancient Irish place-names. A recorded example is Duma na nGall, the mound of the foreign hostages, located at Tara.

:::
```

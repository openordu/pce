::: magazinestyle
Dering is an anglicization of the name Diorruing. The dictionary lists Deering beside it. Diorruing is the clairvoyant Fenian hero, a friend and attendant of Fionn mac Cumhaill. He sees distant events and things still to come through the darkness behind his head. Readers will find the full record under the Diorruing entry when the encyclopedia grows one.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dafydd is the most popular man's given name in Wales. The form Davydd appears beside it. The name comes from the Hebrew word for beloved and answers to the English David. Countless figures in Welsh history carry it. The patron saint of Wales keeps his own separate name. He is known as Dewi Sant in Welsh and as David in English. Welsh usage never calls him St Dafydd.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Deimne, also given as Demna or Demne, was the childhood name of the Irish hero Fionn mac Cumhaill. The adult name Fionn means fair or brilliant. He bore the name Deimne in his boyhood before he became the celebrated leader of the Fianna. The two names track the same figure from youth to fame in Irish legend. MacKillop 2008 gives the fuller forms. Deimne Maol is the Modern Irish spelling, and the classical Irish boyhood name is Demne Máel. The name appears as Domhnach or Deima in oral tradition. Demne itself implies assurance and firmness. Máel denotes short hair, and it carries links with druidism and the Otherworld. J. F. Nagy studied the significance of the name in Celtica and in The Wisdom of the Outlaw.

:::
```

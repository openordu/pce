``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Deiniol served as the first bishop of Bangor in Gwynedd, north Wales. Pilgrims travelled to the cathedral to view his miraculous image. Annales Cambriae places his death in the year 584. That record calls him Daniel Bancorum, or Deiniol of the Bangors. The name links him to the house at Bangor Is-coed as well. A twelfth-century text stations him at the Synod of Brefi in 545. There he joined David and Dubricius. Deiniol gained his schooling at Bangor Is-coed beside the river Dee. His father oversaw that community as Abbot Dunawd. The abbot was brother-in-law of Brochfael, king of Powys. Several churches under his charge stood in Flintshire.

:::
```

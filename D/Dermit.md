::: magazinestyle
Dermit and Dermot are anglicizations of the Irish name Diarmait. The dictionary routes the spellings straight to that record. Diarmait Ua Duibne is the great heroic bearer of the name. He is the Fenian warrior who eloped with Gráinne, the promised bride of Fionn mac Cumhaill. Kings of later history also carried the name. Readers should follow the Diarmait entry for the name's full record.

:::

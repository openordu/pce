::: magazinestyle
Deirgderc is an Irish name meaning red eye. The red eye is the sun. The dictionary gives the name as another name and a standing epithet for two figures. One is the Dagda, the great god of Irish tradition. The other is Eochaid, the sun god. Readers should follow the Dagda entry and the Eochaid entry for the full records.

:::

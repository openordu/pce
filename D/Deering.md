::: magazinestyle
Deering is one anglicization of the name Diorruing. The dictionary lists Dering beside it. Diorruing is a Fenian hero, a friend and attendant of Fionn mac Cumhaill, and a member of the Fianna. Dobar Ua Baiscne was his father. He is credited with second sight. He only had to shut his eyes and peer into the darkness behind his head. That sight showed him distant events and things still to come. When the flight of Diarmait and Grainne opens the tale, he speaks to the aged Fionn. He names Grainne as the one woman in Ireland worthy of the king. Readers will find the full record under the Diorruing entry when the encyclopedia grows one.

:::

::: magazinestyle
This image shows a gold neckring from the Erstfeld hoard in Switzerland. The neckring is a form of torc made from precious metal. Celtic people deposited valuable jewellery during the late Bronze Age and Iron Age. They placed the hoard in a mountain pass at Erstfeld. Votive deposits often held weapons, shields, and fine jewellery. Craftworkers sometimes damaged the items before burial. This neckring survives as evidence of religious offering rites.

:::

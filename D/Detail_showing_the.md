::: magazinestyle
The shoulders and part of a buttoned sleeve survive from an early woollen garment. Archaeologists found this woollen garment at Moy, County Clare, Ireland. The fragment preserves close-fitting, tailored details from medieval dress. Buttons ran down the sleeve of the garment. This find gives physical evidence for early Irish woollen clothing. Surviving woollen textiles are rare. The Moy garment therefore holds clear value for the study of Celtic dress.

:::

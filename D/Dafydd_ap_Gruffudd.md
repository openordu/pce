::: magazinestyle
Dafydd ap Gruffudd was the younger brother of Llywelyn ap Gruffudd. Writers usually reckon Llywelyn the last native prince of Wales. Dafydd served in that role for six months after him. His brief tenure closes the line of native Welsh princes.

:::

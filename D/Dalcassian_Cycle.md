::: magazinestyle
Dalcassian Cycle is the Irish Dál Cais. It is a small set of medieval Irish narratives. The stories centre on Brian Bórama and Murchad, his son of the Dál Cais. The dictionary treats the group as a subdivision of the Cycle of the Kings. The scholar Alan Bruford coined the name for it. The older anglicization Dalcassian points to the Shannon estuary country and to the O'Brien family. Bruford's study of Gaelic folk tales appeared at Dublin in 1969.

:::

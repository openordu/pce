``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The classical accounts of the druids come from Greek and Roman authors. All the reports derive from outside observers. No pre-Christian druid wrote of his own school. Caesar says the druids learned a great number of verses by heart. They stayed some twenty years in training. They refused to put such knowledge in writing. Posidonius supplied the oldest layer, echoed by Strabo and Diodorus. The later authors describe druids as priests, philosophers, and seers. They also report claims of human sacrifice among the order. The authority of the druids rested on memory and secret doctrine.

:::
```

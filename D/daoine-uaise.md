::: magazinestyle
daoine uaisle is an Irish-language name meaning noble people or gentry. MacKillop classifies it as a euphemism for fairies. The term belongs to the same Modern Irish cluster of polite substitutes as daoine maithe (good people), daoine sídhe (people of the fairy mound), and slua sí (fairy host). The MacKillop window for this headword ends partway through the entry, so the full article is not preserved in the source slice. What the window does preserve is the gloss noble people, gentry and the simple classification as a fairy euphemism. The reader who needs the full record should consult MacKillop's printed article under this headword.

:::

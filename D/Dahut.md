``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dahut is a Breton heroine or goddess from the city of Ys. She is the daughter of the Christian king Gradlon. Dahut keeps the old religion and worships the old powers of the land. With the help of the sea magicians called the korrigans, she builds the city of Ys. The dwarfs paint the great walls so they look like gold. Only Dahut holds the key to the gates that keep back the waters. She wears it on a chain at her neck. She captures sea dragons that bring her wealth from abroad. Each night she gives a masked visitor a mask that grows tight and kills him. One night a dancer turns out to be Cado, a sea demon who wants her realm. He steals the silver key and opens the sluices, and the sea pours into Ys. Gradlon only escapes when he pushes Dahut into the sea at the urging of a saint. Some tales say she dies in the abyss where her victims rest. Other tales say she lives on as a mermaid above her sunken city, luring men to their doom. MacKillop gives the alias forms Dahud, Ahes, and Ahe. He calls her the foolish daughter of Gradlon. Despite repeated warnings she led her people night after night in revels and wild displays of pagan ritual. Punishment fell on her and on the city for it. In Christianized tellings she may take the name Keben, a sorceress counted among female druids. Some tellings equate her with Li Ban.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
David is the English shape of one of the commonest men's names in Wales. The name honours the saint the Welsh call Dewi Sant. The usual Welsh equivalent of David is Dafydd. Readers will find the Welsh side of the name under the Dafydd entry, with the saint at Dewi Sant.

:::

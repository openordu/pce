::: magazinestyle
Deheubarth is a Welsh name meaning the right hand part, that is the southern part. It names the southern portion of Wales. Its exact sense shifts across historical periods. Often it stands as the counterpart of Gwynedd, the north Wales realm. In earlier use it distinguished southern Wales from the P-Celts of northern Britain. Those northern people were the men of the north, the Gwÿr y Gogledd. The name must be told apart from Dyfed, which covers only the south-west part of Wales. Readers will find the regional record under the Dyfed entry.

:::

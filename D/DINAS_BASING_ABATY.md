``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Basingwerk Abbey sits in Flintshire in north-east Wales. The name Dinas Basing anchors the place in early history. The community began as a Savigny house in 1131. Ranulf, earl of Chester, acted as the founder. Builders raised the present structure around 1157. The standing remains date to the thirteenth century. Monks of Basing copied the Book of Aineirin in that era. The writer Gutun Owain called Basingwerk his home. Part of the Black Book of Basing survives in his hand. The abbey held the manor of Holywell. Its monks controlled the shrine of Saint Winifred and her well-chapel.

:::
```

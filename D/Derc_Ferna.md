::: magazinestyle
Derc Ferna is an alternate name for Dunmore, the huge three-chambered cave in County Kilkenny. The dictionary routes this spelling straight to that record. The cave lies about six miles north of the city of Kilkenny. Old Irish literature and folklore knew it as the darkest place in Ireland. The monster cat Luchtigern kept Dunmore as his home. Plunder came to the cave in the year 928 from Godfrey and his Dublin Vikings. Tradition says they slaughtered more than one thousand people there. The many human bones found inside are read as witness to that event.

:::

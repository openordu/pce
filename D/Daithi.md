``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Daithi is a variant spelling of the name Dathi. Dathi is the shadowy fifth-century king of Connacht whom some annals style the final pagan overking of Ireland. The dictionary files this spelling as a pointer to that main record. Readers will find the full dossier under the Dathí entry.

:::
```

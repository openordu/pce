``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dara is an anglicization of the Irish name Dáire. The Dáire entry itself notes that writers shape the name into English as Dara. It also offers the oak-wood word doire as a possible root. Readers should follow the Dáire entry for the composite dossier of the name and its many bearers.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Dealra Dubh is an opponent of Fionn mac Cumhaill. The narratives that carry him come from oral tradition. His name is glossed against the Irish dealramh dubh, meaning a dark sheen or radiance. Not one word left his mouth without a taste of blame in it. The fault-finding tongue is the whole of his character.

:::

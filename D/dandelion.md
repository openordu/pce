``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
dandelion names the common plant Taraxacum officinale, a yellow-flowered weed whose leaves are edible. Celtic peoples credited the plant with curative powers. The strongest reputation lay in treating heart problems and in warding off diseases blamed on fairies. Irish speakers called the plant caisearbhán, or caol dearg when used as medicine. Scottish Gaelic speakers named it am beàrnan Brìde. In Manx the plant was lus y minnag. Welsh speakers called it dant y llew. Cornish speakers used dans-lew. Breton speakers used c'hwervizon.

:::
```

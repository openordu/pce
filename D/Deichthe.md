::: magazinestyle
Deichthe is a variant spelling of the name Deoch. The dictionary lists Deocha and Deoca beside it. Deoch is the proud and vain princess of the South. Her land is Munster in the tragic tale of Lir's children. A curse had turned the children into swans. The spell was to last until a southern woman joined with a northern man. Deoch, child of the Munster chieftain Finghein, married Lairgnéan of Connacht. When Deoch spoke of her wish to own the four singing swans, Lairgnéan moved to seize them. The violence of his seizure broke the magic that had disguised the children. Readers should follow the Deoch entry for her full story.

:::

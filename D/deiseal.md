::: magazinestyle
deiseal names the sunwise turn in Modern Irish and in Scottish Gaelic. MacKillop records the headword alongside the variant spelling deiseil and glosses both as Irish and Scottish Gaelic terms for the sunwise turn. The sunwise turn is a ritual circuit taken in the clockwise direction, following the path of the sun, used in Irish and Scottish Gaelic practice for blessing and protection. The deiseal stands against the tuathal, the left-hand or widdershins circuit, which moves against the sun. The fuller ceremonial and folkloric treatment in MacKillop sits under the headword sunwise turn. The MacKillop source slice preserves only the opening line of the deiseal article, so the detailed treatment belongs to the cross-referenced target.

:::

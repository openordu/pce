::: magazinestyle
Decies is the Anglicized name of a region in medieval Ireland. The region lies roughly coextensive with western County Waterford. The name comes from the early Irish people called the Déisi. One early record set the bounds of Decies. It put the Suir on one side and the sea on the other. Lismore and Credan Head marked the ends. The area was later settled by the Power, or de Paor, family. That settlement gave the district its other traditional name, Power Country.

:::

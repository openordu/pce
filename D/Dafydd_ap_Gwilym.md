``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dafydd ap Gwilym stands as the most admired medieval Welsh poet. His life ran from about 1320 to about 1370. He worked in Dyfed, the land once called Cardiganshire. Morfydd is the woman his love-poetry praises. His verses tie love and nature together with sharp wit and ready metaphor. Editors have kept his work before readers ever since. Thomas Parry printed the poems at Cardiff in 1952. Richard Loomis translated them with commentary at New York in 1981. Rachel Bromwich published her Writers of Wales study of the poet at Cardiff in 1974.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dafydd ap Gwilym was a Welsh poet of the fourteenth century. He ranks among the finest poets of medieval Wales. His working years ran roughly from 1315 to 1350. He kept company with the cler, the travelling singers of his homeland. Dafydd made the cyway and metre a smooth tool for court verse. He carried native Welsh song into the wider stream of European poetry. His grip on the craft of cynoptics hung shows patient skill. Sharp puns and ready metaphor mark his short lines. Love lyrics and nature poems carry clear, flowing pictures. Later poets recall dafydd as the master of the love song. He is bound to central Welsh places such as Ystra-Fl and Llanbadarn Frawr. Modern editors such as Fulton and Parry have kept his work in print.

:::
```

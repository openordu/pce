::: magazinestyle
dulachan is recorded alongside dulacaun as variant spellings of the dullahan, the headless phantom of Irish and Scottish Gaelic tradition. Both forms redirect the reader to the canonical headword. The full record on this headless rider sits under the headword dullahan.

:::

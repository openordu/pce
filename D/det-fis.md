::: magazinestyle
dét fis is the Irish phrase for the tooth of wisdom. The term appears in different rites within Irish tradition. Wisdom-tooth symbolism recurs across early Celtic lore, where the teeth of sages and heroes were treated as tokens of insight. MacKillop records the phrase as a brief ritual item without a longer article body.

:::

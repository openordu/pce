``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
draíocht is an Irish term with two literal senses. The first sense names druidic art or druidism. The second sense names witchcraft, magic, charm, and enchantment. MacKillop records the alternate spelling draoidheacht beside the headword. The power or talent the word describes is attributed to the Tuatha Dé Danann. Their magic let them overcome the Fir Bolg, who had lived in Ireland before them. The Irish term draoitheach, meaning magician or wizard, names the person who carries this power. The article cross-references the entry for the druid. Modern readers find draíocht useful as a single-word frame for several overlapping strands of Celtic magical practice.

:::
```

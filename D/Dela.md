::: magazinestyle
Dela was a leader of the Fir Bolg. His five sons came from Greece to invade Ireland. They later divided the island into five parts. The dictionary names him in the story of his son Gann. Gann led the invasion and took half of Munster with his brother Sengann. Readers should follow the Fir Bolg entry for the people.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Dewi Sant, also known as Saint David, serves as the patron saint of Wales. He founded ascetic monasteries and became a symbol of the Welsh nation. A Latin life of Dewi was composed in the late eleventh century by Rhygyfarch. He reportedly founded twelve monasteries across Wales and England. He is said to have died on 1 March, likely in 588 or 602 AD.

:::
```

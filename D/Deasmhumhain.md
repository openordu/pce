::: magazinestyle
Deasmhumhain is an Irish spelling of Desmond. The dictionary prints the form Desmuma beside it. The Modern form Deasúin reads as a retranslation of the name made in a nationalist spirit. Desmond names a territory of southern Ireland, recognized before the seventeenth-century carving out of counties. Its bounds match County Kerry today and much of County Cork and County Limerick, and the name means South Munster. In the sixteenth century the land saw armed resistance to English rule. The fight was led by the Fitzgerald family, the Geraldines. The name has carried romantic associations ever since. Readers will find the regional record under the Desmond entry. Until recent times the name stayed rare in Ireland. By the century's end, England had taken the name up in a modest way.

:::

::: magazinestyle
deas-sail is a single-line cross-reference pointing the reader to the entry sunwise turn. MacKillop records the headword as a variant spelling of deiseal, a Modern Irish and Scottish Gaelic term naming the sunwise turn. The full record of the ritual concept, a clockwise turn taken for blessing or protection, sits under the headword sunwise turn.

:::

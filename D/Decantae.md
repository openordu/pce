::: magazinestyle
The Decantae were a British tribe of early Scotland. Roman writers recorded their name. Their territory matched Easter Ross, a district of the central Scottish Highlands that kept its old bounds until 1974. The tribe belongs to the layer of Roman-era peoples that the encyclopedia tracks alongside its mythological material. Their name survives only through classical and later geography.

:::

Deluge is a dictionary cross-reference, not a story of its own. The dictionary sends readers from the word Deluge to the linked entries on flood legends and submerged cities. It points to the tale of Dwyfan and Dwyfach, the Welsh flood pair. It also names the drowned city of Ys off the Breton coast. Readers should follow those entries for the flood traditions of the Celtic world.

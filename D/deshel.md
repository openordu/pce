deshel is a Hiberno-English term used in Irish folklore studies for the sunwise turn. The MacKillop entry records it as a one-line cross-reference pointing the reader to the headword sunwise turn. The full record of the sunwise ritual direction and its uses sits under the headword sunwise turn.

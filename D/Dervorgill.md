::: magazinestyle
Dervorgill and Dervorgilla are anglicizations of the name Derbforgaill. The dictionary routes the spellings straight to that record. The name was borne by several personages of myth and history. The best known are two women. One is the princess of Lochlainn who loved Cúchulainn. The other is the Leinster woman of the twelfth century. Her flight with Diarmait mac Murchada drew the Anglo-Normans to Ireland. Readers should follow the Derbforgaill entry for the full record.

:::

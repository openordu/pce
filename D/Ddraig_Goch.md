::: magazinestyle
Ddraig Goch is the red dragon, the national symbol of Wales. The Welsh name means exactly that. The dictionary sets the symbol apart from the gwiber, a winged serpent whose name means viper or adder. The gwiber is a dragon of story, but it never carried the nation's badge. The red dragon alone flies as the emblem of the country.

:::

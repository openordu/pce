``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Eamon de Valera shaped Irish politics for half a century. He came into the world in New York on October 14, 1882, and carried the name Edward de Valera. He took a mathematics post at Rockwell College in 1901. Valera joined the Gaelic League in 1908 and changed his name to Eamonn. He commanded a Volunteers battalion during the 1916 Easter Rising. British troops took him after the last surrender. A court spared him, likely because he held American citizenship. Valera won the East Clare by-election after his 1917 release. He escaped Lincoln prison in 1919 to lead the first Dail. He opposed the 1921 treaty and backed the anti-treaty side in the civil war. Valera founded Fianna Fail in 1926. He became Taoiseach after the 1932 election and held the office for years. His 1937 constitution named the state Eire and claimed the whole island. He kept Ireland neutral during the Second World War. Valera served as President from 1959 until 1973.

:::
```

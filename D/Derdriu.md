``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Derdriu is the preferred Old Irish spelling of the name Deirdre. The dictionary routes this form straight to her record. Deirdre is the tragic heroine whose flight with the sons of Uisnech fills the tale of her sorrows. Her story ends with the killing of Noísiu and his brothers. A year of grief at Emain Macha follows. She dies at last on the grave of Noísiu. Readers will find the full record under the Deirdre entry.

:::
```

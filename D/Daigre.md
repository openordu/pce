Daigre is another name for the figure the dictionary numbers Dáire (4). The form Daighre appears beside it. The Dáire entry carries the composite dossier of that name, which Eoin MacNeill read as a set of overlapping personalities. Readers should follow the Dáire entry for the fuller record.

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The saga englynion are a cycle of medieval Welsh verse. The englyn is a traditional stanza form. The saga cycles use it for old British legend. In the Llywarch Hen cycle, the aged hero speaks. He grieves the deaths of his sons in battle. In the Heledd cycle, a woman laments her fallen brother Cynddylan. The Urien cycle mourns the darkened court of Rheged. Heledd and Llywarch Hen shape the best-known cycles. Their lines survive in the englyn stanza.

:::
```

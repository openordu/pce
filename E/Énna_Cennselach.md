::: magazinestyle
Énna Cennselach is a legendary forebear of the kings of south Leinster. In a note supplied by medieval Irish commentators, he is presented as the killer of Eochaid Mugmedón. MacKillop reads the figure as the royal ancestor of the Leinster ruling line in the surviving genealogies.

:::

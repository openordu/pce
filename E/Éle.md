``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Éle, also written Éile, is a legendary Irish figure whom most accounts pass over. Her famous sister Medb stands as the queen and goddess of Ireland. The name Éle ties her to the Fairy race, also called the elfin race. MacKillop records Éle as a daughter of Eochaid Feidlech. The same entry places her among the sisters of Medb. It adds Clothra, Eithne, and Mugain to that sister group. Éle remains the overshadowed sibling beside her celebrated sister. Her fairy name marks her place within a mythic people. The article itself leads from Éle into a separate British headword Elen, also called Elen Luyddog and Helen. MacKillop traces that Welsh virgin princess to the dream vision Breuddwyd Macsen Wledig. The prince Macsen Wledig is the likely historical figure Magnus Maximus who died in 388. Macsen grew weary of imperial rule in fourth century Britain. He raised an army and marched to take Rome itself. Once seated as emperor he dreamed of the Elen he had left behind.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Éireamhón is the modern Irish spelling of Éremón, the mythological founder of one of the two main branches of the early Irish. MacKillop reads the form as the contemporary rendering of the older name. The principal record sits under Éremón.

:::
```

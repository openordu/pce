``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ecgfrith was King of Northumbria, ruling from 670 to 685. He waged aggressive war on Celtic peoples. He seized British church holdings in the Pennines, likely from the kingdoms of Elfed and Rheged. In 684 he attacked Brega in Ireland and destroyed churches. On 20 May 685, at Nechtanesmere in Pictland, the Pictish monarch Bruide mac Bili slew Ecgfrith and most of his army. The Picts took back lands the English had held, and Anglo-Saxon sway over the Celtic nations declined.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Eó fis is the fish of knowledge in Irish lore. Bubbles named eó fis rise in a sacred well. Hazel nuts drop into the water. They give the fish its wisdom. Eating the fish passes wisdom to a person. MacKillop records eó fis as an Irish term for the salmon of knowledge. He adds two near words, fìosach and fìos, that carry the same sense of knowledge. Feasach is a third term and means knowing or aware. This salmon of wisdom is sometimes called Fintan. See also SALMON.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Éirne is a legendary princess of Irish tradition, the daughter of Búrc Búiredach, whose personal name became the Erne waterway's title. The river runs seventy-five miles northwest from Lough Gowna in County Longford, through Lough Oughter and Lough Erne in counties Cavan and Fermanagh, before emptying into the Atlantic. MacKillop reads the form as the eponym of the Erne. The principal record sits under Érne.

:::

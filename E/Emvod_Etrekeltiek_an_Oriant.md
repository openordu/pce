::: magazinestyle
Emvod Etrekeltiek an Oriant is a Celtic music and culture festival. It is the Festival Interceltique de Lorient. The event happens each August in Lorient (An Oriant), a port city in Morbihan, Brittany. The festival has run for over thirty years. It grew from an earlier event, the Festival des Cornemuses. That was a Breton piping and pipe-band championship. It began in Brest in 1953 and moved to Lorient in 1971. Musicians arrive from the six Celtic nations and from the wider Celtic diaspora. The festival also welcomes Galicia and Asturias. It draws a worldwide audience of all ages. Recent editions drew over 500,000 spectators. The festival stages concerts, parades, lectures, sessions, and communal dance. It adds jazz, rock, and classical music to the traditional forms. It has commissioned major new works, such as the 1983 Lorient Festival Suite. Emvod Etrekeltiek an Oriant is a prime showcase for modern Celtic music.

:::

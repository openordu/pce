::: magazinestyle
Echtge is an Irish place-name for the mountain range between Counties Clare and Galway. The hills rise to 1,243 feet between Loughrea and Lough Derg. English usage usually calls the range Slieve Aughty. W. B. Yeats and Lady Gregory often cited the name in their writings. Place-name legend hands the mountains to Echthge, a lady of the Tuatha Dé Danann. She received the range as her dowry, so the story runs. Lady Gregory gave the abandoned Irish-speaking villages of these hills credit for much of her folklore store. Her book Visions and Beliefs in the West of Ireland appeared at London in 1920.

:::

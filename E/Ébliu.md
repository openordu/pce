``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ébliu is a sister of Lug Láthfhota and the wife of Fintan mac Bóchra in early Irish myth. Her name is given in several Old Irish forms including Éblenn and Éibhlear, all derived from the root meaning sheen, beauty, or radiance. She is closely associated with the southern province of Munster, and is held to have left her name on hills south of Nenagh in County Tipperary. MacKillop reads the form as a variant of Ériu. The principal record sits under Ériu.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
elopement is the standard English translation of the Irish Aíthéd. Aíthéd stands as the first word of a recognised tale category in Old and Middle Irish literature. A well-known example is Aíthéd Finn, or The Elopement of Fionn. Writers occasionally misuse elopement as a translation of Tóraigheacht, which actually means pursuit. The two tale-types share narrative surface but name distinct genres. The English term thus sits at the hinge of two related Irish tale categories.

:::
```

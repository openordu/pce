::: magazinestyle
Énna Airgthech, the plunderer in Irish, was a legendary ruler of Munster celebrated for being the first to commission silver shields on Irish soil, which he then distributed to his chieftains. The shields were crafted near what is today called Rathbeagh, beside the Nore in northern County Kilkenny. MacKillop reads the figure as the royal innovator of the Munster arms tradition.

:::

Éamonn an Chnuic, Edmund or Éamonn of the Hill in Irish, is a name from tradition for Edmund Ryan, a seventeenth-century Tipperary bandit of the rapparee tradition. Ryan was a patriotic outlaw whose daring reputation long outlasted his own life. The rapparees were men displaced from their holdings during the Cromwellian settlement who turned to plundering the new owners of their former inheritance. MacKillop reads the figure as a Tipperary folk hero. The principal record sits under Edmund_Ryan and the wider rapparee material.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Étaín Fholtfind, whose name means she of the fair hair in Irish, is a beautiful maiden named in two rival genealogies inside MacKillop's Dictionary. One genealogy calls her the daughter of Étar and places her living in the fairy residence on the hill of Howth. The other genealogy names her as the daughter of Gebann, the chief druid of Manannán mac Lir, and gives her brother Clidna. The two accounts cannot be reconciled, and MacKillop records both without choosing between them. The figure occupies roughly six lines of the 2008 Oxford entry and has no independent narrative beyond these rival birth-tales.

:::
```

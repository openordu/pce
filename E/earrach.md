``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
earrach names the season of spring in Modern Irish and Scottish Gaelic. In ordinary use the word denotes the springtime of the year. Some writers use the same word to imply Imbolc, the February festival that marks the Celtic turning toward spring. The term thus carries both a seasonal sense and a calendrical sense in Celtic usage.

:::
```

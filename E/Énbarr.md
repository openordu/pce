::: magazinestyle
Énbarr, froth or foam in Irish, was the enchanted horse of Manannán mac Lir, the sea-god of the divine host. The steed was also known as Aonbhárr in modern Irish, a name carrying the sense unique supremacy. MacKillop records the form as one of the attested spellings of Manannán's otherworld mount. The principal record sits under Aonbharr.

:::

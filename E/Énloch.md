``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Énloch, bird lake in Irish, is a lake near Mag Ai and Cruachain in what is today County Roscommon. In the older Connacht narratives the kings of that province are said to have convened gatherings of nobles beside the Énloch waters. MacKillop reads the form as a place of princely assembly in the Connacht tradition. The principal record sits under Mag Ai.

:::
```

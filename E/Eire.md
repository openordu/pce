``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Eire is the Modern Irish name for Ireland. The Old Irish form is Ériu, and the two names belong to the same eponymous goddess. Koch 2006 explains that Ériu derives from a Primitive Irish name meaning the fertile land, and that the same root produced the old people-name Iverni. The spelling Éire stands for the island and for the goddess in later Irish usage. See the fuller entry for Ériu.

:::
```

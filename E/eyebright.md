::: magazinestyle
eyebright, also written eye bright, names any of several flowering plants of the genus Euphrasia. The plants bear white and purplish blossoms. European folk medicine credited the herb with curative power for complaints of the eye. In Irish the plant attracts especially affectionate names. Two recorded phrases are soilse na súil, light of the eye, and radhairdn, little sight. The plant carries its own name in each Celtic language. Modern Irish calls it roisnin. Scottish Gaelic calls it lus-nan-leac. Manx calls it lus ny sooilley. Welsh offers effros, arian gwynion, and math o blanhigyn. The entry cross-references the FAIRY HERB record.

:::

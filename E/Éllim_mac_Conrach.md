::: magazinestyle
Éllim mac Conrach is a shadowy early ruler of the Dál nAraide people of Ulster who, alongside three confederates, killed King Fiachu and seized the high throne of Tara. The killing drew divine punishment in the form of a famine across the island. Éllim held the kingship for a score of years until Fiachu's offspring Tuathal Techtmar avenged the murder and reclaimed his patrimony. Tuathal Techtmar would later rank among the greatest of the pre-Patrician high kings of Ireland.

:::

Étaín Echraide is a rare epithet recorded for Étain in early Irish tradition. The form appears once in MacKillop's Dictionary of Celtic Mythology as an occasional label for the first Étain, the sun-bride of Midir in the Tochmarc Étaíne tale. Scholars treat the name as a variant tag rather than a separate figure, because Étaín Echraide has no surviving narrative apart from that of Étain herself. The entry therefore functions as a cross-reference, pointing readers to the parent article for the full mythology. MacKillop's note is brief, occupying roughly three lines of the 2008 Oxford edition. Readers who meet the epithet in the parent Tochmarc Étaíne footnote apparatus can follow the link back to the main Étain article without loss.

:::

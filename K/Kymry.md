``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kymry is an older anglicized form of the Welsh name Cymru. Cymru is the modern Welsh name for the nation of Wales. In earlier usage Cymry named the Welsh people. The Welsh tongue is called Cymraeg.

:::
```

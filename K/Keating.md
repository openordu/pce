::: magazinestyle
Geoffrey Keating was an Irish historian, poet, and priest. He was born in Tipperary and lived between about 1570 and 1645. He studied at a bardic school and at the universities of Bordeaux and Salamanca. He returned to Ireland as a priest and a doctor of theology. He compiled the Foras Feasa on the story of Ireland. The book was finished between 1620 and 1633. Keating wrote in a joined narrative with many vivid scenes. The Annals of the Four Masters give bare dates. The two books were written about the same time, but they differ in method. Keating answered those who defamed Ireland, among them Spenser and Giraldus Cambrensis. His history is praised as a model of Irish prose. He came at the start of modern Irish history. It was among the last important European books sent out only in manuscript.

:::

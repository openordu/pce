``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kathleen Ni Houlihan is an English form of the Irish name Caitlin Ni Uallachain. The title is a personification of Ireland itself. The poet William Butler Yeats used the name in his work. Kathleen Ni Houlihan therefore stands for the country of Ireland as a person.

:::
```

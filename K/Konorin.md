``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Konorin is a pseudo-saint of Brittany whose being comes about twice by magic. Lawless men murder the first Konorin, a plain mortal, by cutting him apart and burning the pieces. Only a strange apple survives him. A young virgin eats that apple and carries a child. Nine months later she bears Saint Konorin, whom tradition calls the child of the apple. The fruit is seen as a token of wisdom.

:::
```

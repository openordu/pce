``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kymidu Kymein-Voll is the anglicized spelling of the name Cymidei Cymeinfoll. A giantess, she appears in Branwen, the second tale of the Mabinogi. Her husband is the giant Llassar Llaes Gyfnewid, and she stands twice his height. Matholwch finds the pair carrying the cauldron of regeneration. Disturbed by their unruly children, he sets out to burn the parents inside a house of iron. The two flee to Britain and give the cauldron to Bran in thanks. A prophecy says that Cymidei will bring forth a warrior already dressed for battle. Proinsias Mac Cana reads in her the twin marks of the Celtic goddess, fruitfulness and a love of war.

:::
```

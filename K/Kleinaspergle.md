``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kleinaspergle sits just below the town of Asperg in the district of Ludwigsburg, Baden-Wurttemberg. It marks the resting place of an Iron Age prince of Celtic stock. The mound connects to the hill-fort of Hohenasperg, stands 6 m high, and spans 60 m across. Excavated in 1879, the main chamber had already been plundered. A smaller adjoining chamber held a rich Early La Tene burial with jewellery and drinking vessels. Two great drinking horns wrapped in gold leaf are the most celebrated finds, finished with ram's head terminals. Greek imports such as Attic kylikes give a close dating and help fix the early La Tene chronology.

:::
```

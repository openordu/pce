::: magazinestyle
Kerman Kelstach is an English form of the name Cromm Cruaich. The Old Irish name, Crom Crúaich, means bloody crescent. Christian accounts of early history call Cromm Cruaich the leading idol of pagan Ireland. It stood on the plain of Mag Slécht in County Cavan. A central golden figure was ringed by twelve stones. At Samain the idol received the first-born child in each family, and likewise the first-born of each herd.

:::

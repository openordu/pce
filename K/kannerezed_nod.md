``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
kannerezed nod is the Breton equivalent of the washer at the ford, the Irish bean sí and the Scottish washer woman. MacKillop treats the Breton term as a brief pointer to the same record as the wider washer-at-the-ford tradition. The fuller record of the funerary fairy at the ford, the linen she washes, and the omen of her appearance sits under washer_at_the_ford.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kitter is a legendary pirate of the Manx record. He appears as a Viking earl. The witch Oda lured him into a storm-tossed sea. A rock off the south-west shore of the Isle of Man, called Kitterland, keeps his name. The stone stands where the sea that took him rolls onward.

:::
```

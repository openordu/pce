::: magazinestyle
Keyne is a fifth-century Welsh virgin and a reputed founder of a church on Anglesey. Her story spread elsewhere in Britain. She may be one of the twenty-four children of King Brychan Brycheiniog. Her Welsh names include Ceinwen and Cainwen, both tied to the idea of beauty or blessing. William Camden sets her heritage in Somerset. Three holy wells or more are tied to her. The most famous lies near Liskeard in eastern Cornwall.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kevredigez Broadus Breiz was a Breton regionalist body ordered in five sections dealing with administrative decentralization, the economy, history and literature, the Breton language and its literature, and the fine arts. Its president from 1902 to 1946, Régis de L'Estourbeillon, tied the body to economic and political conservatism and to defence of a country way of life that prized a strong traditional sense of propriety. A split led by people active in professional and economic groups, by the bards of the Goursez and by partisans of the language, gave birth to the Fédération régionaliste de Bretagne. Kevredigez Broadus Breiz kept the division in View and stayed busy through the inter-war years into the 1940s. After the Second World War the ' Comité d'étude et de liaison ( capital composed of different interests ) bretons adopted several of its aims.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Korneli is a pseudo-saint of Breton folk tradition linked with the great stones of Carnac. In local legend enemies chase him in an ox-cart. He turns and lays a curse that hardens them into long rows of enormous boulders. Elsewhere he guards horned beasts, not oxen alone. That office leads some scholars to see in him a Christian shell around the older god Cernunnos.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Knockshegouna is the anglicized spelling of the Irish name Cnoc Sídhe Úna. Writers also record the form Knockshigowna. The place is a hill in County Tipperary. It is tied to Úna, the wife of the king of the fairies Finnbheara. Úna, also called Oona, is counted the fairest of women, with sweeping golden hair. The hill is sometimes said to be her own dwelling, set apart from the court of her husband.

:::

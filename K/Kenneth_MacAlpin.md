``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kenneth MacAlpin is the English form of the name Cináed mac Ailpin. He ruled from the year 844 to his death in 858. The chronicles make him the first king of Scotland. He joined the Scoto-Irish settlers of Argyll with the native Picts. His father's family gave him the first people. His mother's family gave him the second. Some writers call him Kenneth the First.

:::
```

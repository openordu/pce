::: magazinestyle
Keeronagh is an English form of the Irish word Caoranach, the name of a monstrous being. Saint Patrick sent the monster to the lake called Lough Derg. The being was taken as female. Story said she was the mother of demons. Keeronagh keeps the English form of a dread of Irish tale.

:::

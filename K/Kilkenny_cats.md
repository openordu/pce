::: magazinestyle
The Kilkenny cats are the subject of a well-known Irish folk-tale. Two cats fight until nothing remains but their tails and claws. The story alludes to the enmity between the Anglo-Norman settlers of Ireland and the native Gaelic Irish. A small stream alone divided the two groups in the town of Kilkenny in Leinster. The Anglo-Normans tried to hold their privileged standing under the Statute of Kilkenny of 1367. Modern commentators reject the later claim that real cats fought this way.

:::

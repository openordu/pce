``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
At Kostolac-Pecv ine a late Iron Age burial ground has been uncovered on the Pecine fallow, beside the Roman city of Viminacium, now Kostolac in Serbia. The find shows that La Tene material moved into the Balkans from the north-west around the 3rd century bc. This intrusion is tied to the peoples who arrived and formed the Scordisci. Greek and Roman writers judged that group to be Celts. Forty newly found graves included some from the older local population. The rest, mostly inhumations and cremations, carried La Tene-style goods and are linked to the Scordisci. Male graves held weapons.

:::
```

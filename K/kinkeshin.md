``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
kinkeshin is a MacKillop pointer headword that resolves to the fairy stroker tradition. MacKillop lists kinkeshin and kinkishin as alternate spellings of the same brief pointer. The fuller record of the fairy stroker, the stroke it leaves on its victims, and its role in Celtic fairy belief sits under fairy_stroker.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kian is an anglicized spelling of the name Cian. Cian is a hero of early Irish narrative and the father of Lug Lámfhota. One tale tells how his magic cow Glas Ghaibhleann is stolen by Balor, and Cian seduces Balor's daughter Eithne in revenge. Another version has him win Eithne through long negotiation. He can change his form to a pig when threatened.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
kinship system is a MacKillop pointer headword that resolves to derbfhine, the Irish extended-family kinship unit. MacKillop treats the English term as a brief pointer to the same record. The fuller record of the Irish derbfhine, the four-generation family group, and its role in early Irish law sits under derbfhine.

:::
```

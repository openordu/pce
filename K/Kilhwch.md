Kilhwch is a variant spelling of the name Culhwch. Culhwch ranks among the most important texts for study of the Arthurian cycle. It is prized for its antiquity and its kinship with early Irish narrative. Folklorists know its main plot as the Giant's Daughter. Other spellings include Kulhwch.

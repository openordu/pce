knacker is a MacKillop variant spelling of the headword knocker. MacKillop treats knacker as an alternate form that resolves to the same record. The fuller record of the Cornish mine-spirit, the knocking it makes before a cave-in, and its role in Cornish mining tradition sits under knocker.

:::

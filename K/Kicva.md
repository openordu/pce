``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kicva is an anglicized spelling of the name Cigfa. Cigfa is the wife of Pryderi and the daughter of Gwyn Gohoyw. The pair appear in the Mabinogi. After her husband disappears she fears the advances of Manawydan.

:::
```

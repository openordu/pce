::: magazinestyle
Keu is a variant spelling of the name Kay. Kay holds the office of seneschal to King Arthur in English romance, drawn from the Welsh figure Cei. English authors make Sir Kay an irascible and churlish retainer. He is also Arthur's foster-brother and a frequent companion in adventure.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kaherdin is a prince of Brittany in the Arthurian tale of Tristan and Iseult. The texts name his father Anoel or Jovellar. He becomes a friend of Tristan. He travels with the hero and his sister is Iseult of the White Hands. In 1927 the scholar W. S. Loomis sought a Welsh root for the figure. Kaherdin belongs to the second branch of the Tristan story.

:::
```

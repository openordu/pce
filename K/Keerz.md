::: magazinestyle
Keerz is a variant form of the name Kay. Kay is the seneschal of King Arthur in the romance of the court. The Welsh figure Cei is behind him. The English Kay is always a sharp and moody man. Keerz keeps one more spelling of the officer of the court.

:::

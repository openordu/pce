``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Karadoc is the Breton counterpart of the Welsh hero Caradog. His name belongs to stories gathered near Vannes in the south of Brittany. In the best known tale his unnamed father sleeps with a changed sow. The druid Eliaves arranged the night. From the union of the father a wild boar is born. The boar Tourtain is the Breton match of the Welsh beast Twrch Trwyth.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Keva is an English form of the name Cainche. Cainche is the daughter of Fionn mac Cumhaill in the Fenian ballads. Her byname, Cainche of the White Skin, marks her pale appearance. She mates with the enemy Goll mac Morna and bears a son named Feadha. Fionn kills Feadha, and this death prompts Goll to make his final split from the hero.

:::
```

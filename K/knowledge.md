``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
knowledge in Celtic tradition names a deeper kind of knowing than the English word usually implies. Celtic knowing is esoteric and metaphysical, more akin to inspired perception than accumulated erudition. The Celts treated knowledge as something the seer received rather than something the student acquired. Knowledge often derived from dead ancestors, who passed it down through the otherworld. Fionn mac Cumhaill touches the salmon of knowledge and gains wisdom that guides him for the rest of his life. MacKillop treats Celtic knowledge as a gift from beyond, not the product of human study. The tradition links wisdom to death, inspiration, and the otherworld rather than to learning alone.

:::
```

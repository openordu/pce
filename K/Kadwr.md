::: magazinestyle
Kadwr is a spelling of the name Cador. Cador is a ruler shown around King Arthur in the Welsh and Arthurian tale. The spelling with K changes the hard c of the Irish and Welsh ways of writing. Kadwr therefore keeps one form of an Arthurian name.

:::

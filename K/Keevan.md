``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Keevan is an English form of the name Ciabhan. Ciabhan is a man who loves the woman Clidna in Irish story. She comes to Ireland with him. His way takes him to the Land of Promise. At a feast he throws nine willows and catches them. Clidna becomes moved by his skill. She leaves with him in his boat. A wave takes her while she sleeps. Keevan keeps the sense of curling hair. It is the name of the lover of Clidna.

:::
```

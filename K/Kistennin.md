``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kistennin is an alternate spelling of the name Custennin. The name appears often in the early Welsh written record. The best-remembered bearer is the shepherd who helped Culhwch find Olwen in the tale Culhwch ac Olwen. His son is Goreu, the young man who finally slew the giant Ysbaddaden.

:::
```

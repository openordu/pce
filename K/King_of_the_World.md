``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
King of the World is a proud title given in Fenian stories to certain arrogant invaders. The title may recall the might of a late Roman emperor or of the Holy Roman ruler. It can also point to the emperor Charlemagne. In the tale known as the Battle of Ventry this office belongs to Dáire Donn. Elsewhere the same boast is carried by a hero named Sinsar.

:::
```

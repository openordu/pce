``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kala-Hañv is the Breton name for the day that opened summer in the Celtic year. The word carries the sense of a convocation. In Breton speech it stands for the festival Beltaine. That festival fell upon the first day of May. Kala-Hañv therefore keeps the summer beginning of the year.

:::
```

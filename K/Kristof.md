::: magazinestyle
Kristof is a small boy whose deeds set much of the Breton legend of Ys in motion. He fishes with a crooked stick and pulls in a magic fish that offers wealth for its freedom. The princess Dahut laughs at the bargain, so the fish lays a spell that leaves her pregnant with a child who can name no father. Her father Gradlon seals Dahut, Kristof, and the infant in a cask and sends them adrift. The fish then raises a palace first, and afterwards a whole city. Meanwhile the walls of Ys fail because Kristof has carried off the holy oak that held back the sea.

:::

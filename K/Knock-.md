``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Knock- is the anglicized spelling of the Irish word cnoc, which means a hill or a height. The prefix opens an immense number of Irish place-names. It also appears in many Scottish Gaelic place-names. The Welsh cousin of the word is cnwc. The two hills Knockfeerina and Knockaulin show the prefix at work.

:::
```

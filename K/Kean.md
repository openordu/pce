::: magazinestyle
Kean is a variant spelling of the name Keyne. Keyne was a Welsh virgin of the fifth century. Tradition makes her a founder of a church on Anglesey. She is counted among the children of king Brychan. Holy wells in the west keep her name. Kean records one more form of her name.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Kiltartan is the name shared by a river and a barony in the south-east of County Galway in Ireland. The former estate of Coole Park stood within this district. The house was demolished in 1941. The estate had been the home of Augusta, Lady Gregory. She took the place-name for the titles of two of her collections of folklore. Those collections were the Kiltartan History Book and the Kiltartan Wonder Book, both printed in Dublin. Her phrase Kiltartan English, sometimes written Kiltartanese, describes her attempt to set down the poetic speech of the country people of the area. The phrase is now sometimes used with scorn, to suggest a patronizing falsity.

:::

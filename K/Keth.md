``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Keth is an English form of the name Cet. Cet is a warrior of Connacht in the Ulster Cycle. He appears most famously in the tale of Mac Da Thó's Pig, where he shames the Ulstermen with his record in combat. He wounds Conchobar mac Nessa by hurling the hardened brain ball of Mesgegra. Conall Cernach kills Cet. The spelling Keth keeps the same warrior under a different anglicized shape.

:::
```

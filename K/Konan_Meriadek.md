::: magazinestyle
Konan Meriadek is the Breton form of the name Cynan Meiriadog. Breton tradition also spells the same figure Conan, and sometimes Konan Meriedek. Cynan Meiriadog ranks as a legendary holy man of the fifth century. He is said to lead Welsh settlers to Armorica and to found Brittany. In the Welsh tale Breuddwyd Macsen Wledig he appears as a British general who earns Armorica as his reward.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Keingalet is the Breton spelling of Ceingalad. Ceingalad is the horse of the hero Gwalchmai fab Gwyar, whose Welsh and Breton tales name him. He is the figure behind Gawain in English romance and Gauvain in French romance.

:::
```

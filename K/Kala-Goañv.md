``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Kala-Goañv is the Breton word for the opening of winter in the Celtic year. The term carries the sense of a calling or convocation. In Breton speech it marks the festival Samain. That festival stood upon the first day of November. Kala-Goañv therefore names the winter start of the year.

:::
```

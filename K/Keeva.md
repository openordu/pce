``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Keeva is an English form of the Irish name Cainche. In the ballads of the Fianna she is a daughter of Fionn mac Cumhaill. She was with the foe Goll mac Morna to make a child. The slaying of the child is told as the end of the band. Her title is Keeva of the White Skin. Keeva names the daughter of Fionn.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Knockfeerina is a hill of 948 feet in County Limerick. It stands east of the town of Ballingarry. The hill is ranked among the best-known seats of the Otherworld in Munster. Its Irish name is Cnoc Firinne. Writers sometimes spell the place Knockfierna.

:::

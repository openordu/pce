``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Visucius is the Latin name of a dimly seen deity, Celtic in all likelihood. Inscriptions naming him run from Aquitaine in south-western France as far as the border between Gaul and Germany. At some sites the female consort Visucia joins him, the pair a divine couple. The surviving evidence leaves his standing ambiguous. He may be a figure in his own right, or an aspect worn by both the Gaulish Mars and Mercury. So wide a band of inscriptions marks him across much of Gaul. The doubt leaves him among the less certain figures of the Gaulish pantheon.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Virotutis is a sometime epithet of the Gaulish Apollo. The name has been rendered benefactor of humanity, though with a question mark. A survey of Gaulish cult records adds that Apollo under this name had two shrines. Both belonged to the cult of the Gaulish Apollo.

:::
```

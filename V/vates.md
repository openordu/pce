``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
vates names the Celtic religious figure attested in Strabo and other classical commentators. The word, of probable Celtic origin, meant interpreter of offerings and of natural lore in the Gaulish priestly world. The vates held one of three ranks of high honor in Gaul: alongside the bards and druids. The vates foretold the future by augury and by the slaughter of victims. Although apparently below the druids in rank, the vates certainly held some religious standing of their own. The Irish fáith is the closest Celtic cognate of the Latin form.

:::
```

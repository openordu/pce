Vision is a cross-reference entry. It directs the reader to the Aisling article. Aisling is a form of Irish vision-poetry. A woman-poet figure appears in the dream-vision. MacKillop pairs vision with the Otherworld entry and treats the headword as a pointer to the wider dream-vision record that sits under Vision Literature.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
voght is a MacKillop anglicization pointing to the canonical fuath headword. MacKillop records voght and vough as the Lowland Scots phonetic anglicizations of the Scottish Gaelic fuath. The principal record of the Highland water-spirit family sits under fuath.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Vitiris is a British god whose name appears in many written forms. The spellings include Veteris, Vitiris, Vetus, Hveteris, and Hvitiris. More than fifty inscriptions record the name, many of them from northern England. One inscription at the Romano-British fort of Castra Exploratorum, near the western end of Hadrian's Wall, gives the name Mogons as an epithet of Vitiris. The same name Mogons stands alone as a separate deity at Voreda, the old fort at Penrith in Cumbria. The word seems related to a root that means great. His worshippers came from within the Celtic world and beyond it. No source states plainly what he represented, yet some images show him with a pig or a serpent. The finds cluster near the lands of the Brigantes and along Hadrian's Wall.

:::
```

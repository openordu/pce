::: magazinestyle
Vindonnus, also spelled Vindonus, is a Gaulish aspect of Apollo. His name means clear light or white. His worship sat at a site coextensive with Essarois, in Burgundy in eastern France. Bronze plaques that nearby excavations brought to light depict eyes. Those plaques suggest worshippers credited him with cures for eye diseases. Linguistically the name Vindonnus links with Fionn mac Cumhaill. Another notice adds that his shrine held a temple of oak and stone, also promising cures for eye afflictions. Apollo, a Roman god, here wears a Gaulish aspect. The white or clear-light sense of his name suits that role.

:::

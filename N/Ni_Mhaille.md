::: magazinestyle
Ní Mháille is the family name under which MacKillop 2008 files the pointer to Gráinne Mhaol, the celebrated pirate better known in English as Grace O'Malley and treated in the dictionary under granuaile. The headword line reads Ní Mháille, Gráinne Mhaol, with the see-reference to the main article, where her career as pirate of the Mayo coast and her folkloric afterlife as a personification of Ireland are narrated. The row exists so the Irish name form finds its subject directly.

:::

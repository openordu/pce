::: magazinestyle
Nechtansmere, also called Dun of Nechtan, is the site where the Picts broke a Northumbrian army in 685, a win that let King Unuist take back lost ground. Scottish Gaelic knows the spot as Linn Garan, the pool of the crane, and tradition identifies it with the ruin at Dunnichen or Dunnechain near Forfar in Tayside. MacKillop derives the name from Nechtan the historical Pictish king who had adopted Roman church ways a generation before the battle.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Early medieval northern and central Ireland is the region that Koch maps to show the Uí Néill groups and their lands. The Uí Néill were the leading dynasty of early medieval Ireland, and their districts across the north and centre of the island are drawn in black on the map. The subordinate Airgialla are grouped with them. Other peoples and provinces are drawn in grey. The map captures the political geography that shaped the early medieval Irish annals and the genealogical record.

:::
```

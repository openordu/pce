``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nefyd Naf Neifion, also spelled Nefyed Nav Nevion, is the Welsh builder of the ship that lets Dwyfan and his wife Dwyfach escape the flooding of Llyn Llion, the deluge that strips Britain of every living thing but the pair in the old genealogies. His second element echoes the god Neptune, a Latin borrowing in Welsh dress. In his Taran Wanderer series, published between 1975 and 1985, the American novelist Lloyd Alexander reshapes the name into Newid Nav Neivion for his readers.

:::
```

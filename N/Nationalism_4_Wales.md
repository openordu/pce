Nationalism [4] Wales considers the growth of Welsh national consciousness. The term Cymry came into use by the late sixth century or early seventh, and the 10th-century poem Armes Prydein is an early expression of the national idea. The rebellion of Owain Glyndŵr and the Welsh humanists of the 16th century drew on this patriotism. Political nationalism became a significant force only in the later 19th century, fostered by Welsh-language publishing and by Continental nationalist writings. Plaid Cymru, founded in 1925 under leaders such as Saunders Lewis and Gwynfor Evans, emphasised the safeguarding of Welsh-speaking districts. A 1997 devolution referendum passed narrowly, and the first elections to the Welsh National Assembly were called in 1999.

:::

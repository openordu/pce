``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ny Shee names a folkloric figure native to Manx speech. The form belongs to the dialect of that island and points to the fairy folk that Irish speakers call the Sidhe. MacKillop records the headword shee as a short dictionary entry. That entry sends the reader onward to the broader entry for sidh. The Manx word shee stands in for the Irish Sidhe, the fairy people of older Celtic tradition. The spelling with the prefix Ny marks the Manx usage and sits alongside the bare anglicized shee.

:::
```

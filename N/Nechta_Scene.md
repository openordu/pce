::: magazinestyle
Nechta Scéne is a variant spelling of Nechtan Scéne, whose name belongs to the three warriors Fannell, Foill, and Tuchell, the sons of Nechtan Scene. The three had killed half as many Ulstermen as were then living before Cuchulainn met them in his very first combat and dispatched them, using the spear Del Chliss that belonged to Nechtan the husband of Boand. The tale marks the opening feat of the boy warrior's career in the Ulster material.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Niall Glúndub, the dark knee, was an Irish king whose life ended in 919 on the Liffey field against a Norse army. From him the O'Neills take their name, and the dictionary points readers back to the earlier Ui Neill entry for the dynasty his descendants built. His death at Dublin against the Norse host became a fixed date in the annals and a measuring point for the later family's claims.

:::

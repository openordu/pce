``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
nuts of knowledge/wisdom is a MacKillop headword that points the reader to hazel. MacKillop treats the form, also glossed as wisdom, as a brief English-language pointer without a fuller standalone article. The full record of the nuts of knowledge tradition in Celtic folklore sits under the headword hazel.

:::
```

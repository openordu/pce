::: magazinestyle
Nationalism [1] Ireland treats the rise of Irish national consciousness. The idea of a Gaelic Irish identity drew on a Milesian origin myth, the Gaelic language, a cherished literature, the Brehon law, and an independent religious history. Tudor conquest from the reign of Henry VIII pressed hard on Ireland, and the defeat of Hugh O'Neill at Kinsale in 1601 is seen as a turning point. The 19th century brought Catholic emancipation under Daniel O'Connell and the trauma of the Great Famine, which left about a million dead. Under Parnell the Irish Parliamentary Party pressed for Home Rule in the 1880s. The 1916 Rising led to the Dáil and to the partition of Ireland set out in the Government of Ireland Act of 1920.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Neimheadh is the Modern Irish spelling of Nemed, the leader of the third invasion of Ireland in the pseudo-historical scheme. Under the older form his people clear twelve plains, dig two royal forts, and fight the Fomorians until plague and oppression break them; their survivors scatter and seed later invader lines. MacKillop's dictionary files the Modernized spelling as a see-entry to that figure and his folk.

:::

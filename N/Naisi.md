``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Naisi is a variant spelling of Noisiu, the son of Usnach whom Deirdre loves and elopes with to Alba. MacKillop's dictionary routes the form as a see-entry to that lover, whom Ulster's king Conchobar mac Nessa lures home under safe conduct and then kills after the safe conduct breaks. The three sons of Usnech fall with him in the tale, and Ulster pays for the deed when Fergus mac Roich deserts the province in rage. The modernized spelling Nooise or Naoise carries the same story.

:::
```

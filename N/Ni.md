``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ní is the Irish feminine patronymic that MacKillop 2008 explains in a short usage note. Figures bearing this form are alphabetized under their first names, even when the particle is capitalized. The rule matters in a reference dictionary where family names of women, such as Ní Mháille, would otherwise scatter entries away from the given names by which the tradition knows them. The dictionary applies the convention consistently across its alphabetical run, so the particle functions as a filing instruction rather than a biographical headword.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nichtan is a variant spelling that MacKillop 2008 files as a short pointer to the historical Pictish king treated under Nechtan (3). The dictionary's own article on Nechtan (3) carries the variant pair directly in its headword line, so a reader who meets Nichtan in a source is directed to the converted ruler who brought the Pictish church toward Roman observance around the early eighth century and whose shrine association with St Andrews the article records. The spelling shows how scribal habits doubled certain dental consonants when the name moved between Latin chroniclers and the vernacular annals.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Nemhain is the Modern Irish spelling of Nemain, one of the pair of battle furies wed to the war god Neit. Her scream is a weapon in itself: in the Tain Bo Cuailnge it mingles with Badb's cry to throw the Connacht camp into night terror, and warriors wound their own ranks. MacKillop's dictionary carries the form as a short see-entry to the older spelling, where the Rhine goddess Nemetona stands as her continental echo.

:::

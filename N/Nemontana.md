``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nemontana is a variant spelling of Nemetona, the goddess of sacred grove and shrine worshipped in the Rhineland. Her cult partner is the war god Mars, and inscriptions pair them from Trier to the Winternheim stone near Mainz. MacKillop notes that an Irish war-goddess of similar sound, Nemain, may echo her name, and files the spelling as a variant entry to the main article on the grove goddess.

:::
```

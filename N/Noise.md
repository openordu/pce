::: magazinestyle
Noíse is one of the spelling cluster that MacKillop 2008 gives in a single line: Noise, Naoise, Naisi, lover of Deirdre. The dictionary treats the variants as one persona, the young Ulster hero whose flight with Deirdre to Alba and whose death at Conchobar's hand form one of the saddest threads of the Ulster Cycle. The entry's brevity is a filing decision, not a judgement: the full narrative sits under the main headword, and this row exists so that a reader who meets any of the three spellings in a source finds the same man.

:::

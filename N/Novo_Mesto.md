``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Novo Mesto is a town in south-eastern Slovenia on the Krka river. It sits within a region that the Celts occupied from the early phases of the La Tène period. Archaeologists have recovered an important cemetery at nearby Kapiteljska njiva and other discovery sites. The earlier finds here belong to the Taurisci tribes. The later Beletov vrt horizon belongs to a successor group called the Latobici. Weapon graves form the oldest group of burials in the area, and several contain iron helmets. Middle La Tène graves carry ribboned shield bosses, chains for fastening swords, large choppers, and iron fibulae. A kantharos with figurative handles and a mask ornament counts among the richest finds from the site. The Taurisci and Boii joined a battle against the Dacian king Burebista around 50 bc, and this conflict appears reflected in a belt plate and clay cups found with a Beletov vrt grave. La Tène pottery at Novo Mesto was turned on a wheel and carried horizontal ribs or round seals. Some handmade vessels decorated with rope and triangle prints preserve a Hallstatt tradition. The finds show the mixing of Celtic settlers with the older pre-La Tène peoples of the region.

:::
```

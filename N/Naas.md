``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Naas is the dictionary's routing entry for Nás, counted among the four wives of Lug Lámfhota. Folk invention went on to claim the Kildare town of Naas as the goddess's birth- or burial-place. The town's Irish name, Nos na Riogh, carries the sense of an assembly ground for royalty. The record itself makes the site a royal residence for Leinster's kings down to the 10th century, and so a meeting place for great gatherings.

:::
```

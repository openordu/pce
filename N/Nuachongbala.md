::: magazinestyle
Nuachongbála is the heading MacKillop 2008 uses to route readers toward the Book of Leinster. The entry is a bare cross-reference: the medieval manuscript that carries Nuachongbála in its story of origins is treated at length under its own title, where the dictionary discusses its contents, date, and role as a repository of Irish saga. This row keeps the place-form spelling findable in its own right.

:::

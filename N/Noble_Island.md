Noble Island is MacKillop 2008's heading for the translation of Inis Ealga, the poetic nickname for Ireland. The entry is brief but exact: the old phrase paints the island as noble or generous, and the dictionary files the English rendering so that readers who meet it in verse or prose can find their way back to the Irish original. It stands with the other poetic names of Ireland in the tradition of place-praise, where the island's epithets carry as much mythology as its geography.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Neachtan is a variant spelling carried by two separate figures the dictionary files under Nechtan. The first is the waters husband of Boand, the only person besides his three cupbearers who may visit Connla's Well, whose breaking of the taboo turns the rising well into the River Boyne. The second is a crewman in the Voyage of Bran who grows weary of the sea and turns home to Ireland, a character later read as touched by the Roman sea-god Neptune. A third sense, the historical Pictish king, takes the spellings Nectan and Nichtan instead.

:::
```

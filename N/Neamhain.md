::: magazinestyle
Neamhain is the Modern Irish spelling of Nemain, the war-fury who shrieks over armies in the Tain Bo Cuailnge and terrifies hosts into rout. Under her older form she is consorted with the war god Neit alongside Badb, and MacKillop notes the pairing need not make Neit an adulterer, since he may wed either fury. Her German echo is the goddess Nemetona, whose link to Mars on the Rhine may lie behind the Irish figure; a stone inscription survives at Winternheim near Mainz.

:::

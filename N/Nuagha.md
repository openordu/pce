``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nuagha is recorded by MacKillop 2008 as a corrupt form of Nuadu. The dictionary files it at the end of the spelling family that also carries Nuada, Nuadha, Núadha, Nuado, and Nuda, all pointing to the same divine name. A reader who meets Nuagha in a source is directed to the main article, where the name's suggested senses and its bearers, mortal, divine, and saintly, are gathered.

:::
```

Nechtan Scéne, also spelled Nechta Scéne and Nechtan Scéine, is the parent of the three warriors Fannell, Foill, and Tuchell in Ulster story. The three sons of Nechtan Scéne had cut down Ulstermen in such numbers that half the province lay dead before Cuchulainn, on his first arms-taking, met them and killed them with the spear Del Chliss, the weapon of the waters god Nechtan. The exploit stands among the deeds that open Cuchulainn's career in the Tain cycle.

:::

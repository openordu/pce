::: magazinestyle
Niall Caille, whose name echoes the Old Irish word for wood and forest, was king at Tara in the ninth century, remembered chiefly for two humiliations. In 838 he submitted to Fedlimid mac Crimthann, and the same king later abducted Niall's queen Gormlaith along with her whole female retinue. Earlier, in 827, his sword broke the Airgialla in a decisive battle that killed many of their leaders and left the confederacy looking to Tara. MacKillop records him among the legendary and historical kings who bear the name Niall.

:::

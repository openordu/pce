::: magazinestyle
Nadcranntail, also written Natchrantel, is a champion of Connacht serving Queen Medb in the Táin Bó Cuailnge. He leads a third of the army on a bold raid into Ulster, comes upon the Brown Bull in Antrim, and drives both bull and herd back toward home. He will not fight Cúchulainn, judging him a beardless boy. Cúchulainn answers with a false beard and cuts him down at speed.

:::

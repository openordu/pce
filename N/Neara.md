::: magazinestyle
Neara is the Modern Irish spelling of Nera, the Connacht hero of the Adventure of Nera. In the tale he follows a spectral corpse on the November night, carries it on his back to a house, and discovers a world of the sidhe, where he weds and serves a year before carrying fire to destroy the elf-mound whose warning dream foretold the destruction of Cruachan and of Medb's bull. MacKillop's dictionary routes the spelling straight to that adventure.

:::

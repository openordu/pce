``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Niav is an anglicization of Niam, the name of several women of Irish tradition. MacKillop's dictionary files the spelling as a short variant entry pointing to the main article, where the bearers include Conall Cernach's wife who nursed Cuchulainn, Celtchair's daughter who uncovered Conganchnes's weakness, and the golden-haired lover of Oisin who led him to Tir na nOg. The form shows how the modern tongue carries the same name into English dress.

:::
```

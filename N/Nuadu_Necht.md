``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nuadu Necht is the ancestor-deity of the Leinstermen, and MacKillop 2008 judges him probably the same figure as the divine king whom the Mythological Cycle names Nuadu Airgetlam. The name is glossed from a folk etymology on necht, washed, clean, pure, possibly related to Nechtan. In the political legend he follows Eterscel, father of Conaire Mor, on the throne of Tara. Fenian-cycle material makes him the first holder of the Hill of Allen, Almu, a fortress also credited to his son Tadg mac Nuadat, though that son may truly be a mask for Nuadu himself. Through Tadg, whose daughter Muirenn mothers Fionn, Nuadu Necht stands, on the mother's side, as a reputed forebear of the hero Fionn, grandfather or great-grandfather depending on how the alias is read. The article closes by proposing that Nechtan (1), consort of Boand, drew part of his characterization from Nuadu Necht.

:::
```

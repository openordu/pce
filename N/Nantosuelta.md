``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nantosuelta, also written Nantosvelta, is a Gaulish goddess of the raven whose cult is attested at several sites, Britain among them. Her images puzzle interpreters. One hand holds a saucer or patera, evidently for sacrifice on an altar, and in some images a small pot takes its place, perhaps recalling the great Celtic cauldron. Her other hand bears what seems to be a small house at the tip of a long pole. The patera and the house-on-pole suggest prosperity, well-being, and domestic life, while her steady company of the carrion-eating raven speaks of death. Her usual cult partner is Sucellus, whose name means the good striker.

:::
```

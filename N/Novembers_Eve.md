::: magazinestyle
Novembers Eve is the calendar name for 1 November that MacKillop 2008 files as a cross-reference heading. The entry routes the reader to three articles: samain, the great Irish feast that opened winter; hollantide, the Scottish and Manx form bound up with Hallowmas; and allantide, the Cornish counterpart. The dictionary's habit with calendar cross-references is to keep the variant English and anglicized names under their own alphabetical slots so that a reader arriving from any folkloric source lands on the same body of seasonal material.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Nemon is a variant spelling of Nemain, the Irish war-fury. The form carries the same figure: a wife or consort of the god Neit, a screamer over battle lines whose cry in the Tain Bo Cuailnge spreads night terror through the sleeping camp. MacKillop's dictionary files the spelling as a one-line variant entry pointing to the main article, where her link to the Rhine goddess Nemetona is also recorded.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Natchrantal is a variant spelling of Nadcranntail, the Connacht champion who serves Queen Medb in the Tain Bo Cuailnge. Under that name he leads a third of Medb's host on the raid into Ulster and drives the Brown Bull of Cooley and its herd out of Antrim toward home. He will not cross weapons with Cuchulainn, judging him a bearded boy, and dies when the youthful warrior strips the beard and slays him with one cast. MacKillop's dictionary headwords the name under the Nadcranntail spelling.

:::

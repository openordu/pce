``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Néit is a dim early Irish god of war whom MacKillop 2008 often places among the Fomorians. Portrayal gives him two wives or consorts, Badb and Nemain, a doubling that does not make him an adulterer: he may be wed to either of them alone, or one consort's identity may drift into the other's or into their shared husband's. Grandsons Balor and Goibniu face each other across the great Second Battle of Mag Tuired, and T. F. O'Rahilly judged both figures to be outgrowths of sun-belief that might in the end be identical. A god though he may be, Néit perishes at Mag Tuired, and Nemain's place at his side echoes the older trio of battle goddesses elsewhere in the dictionary's coverage.

:::
```

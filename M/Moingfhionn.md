``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Moingfhionn and Moingionn are Modern Irish spellings of Mongfhind, a name carried by several women of early Irish story. The best known of them, Mongfhind (2), is the wife of Eochaid Mugmedón.

:::
```

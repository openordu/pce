``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Macutus is a saint linked to the Isle of Man. Hagiography states that the Irish saint Brigit received the veil from Macutus. This stresses the bonds felt between the earliest Christian founders of Man and those of Ireland. Seven keeills on Man are dedicated to Brigit. These small churches point to the early Christian tie between the two lands. The monastery of St Macutus belongs to this pattern of early Manx Christianity on the island.

:::
```

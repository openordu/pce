::: magazinestyle
Moore, Arthur William (1853-1909) was a Manx writer and politician. He became an influential voice in the late-nineteenth-century Manx revival. He entered the House of Keys in 1881 and rose to become its Speaker in 1898. His two-volume history of the island nation appeared in 1900. He also founded The Manx Note Book, which ran from 1885 to 1887. His Manx studies covered surnames and place-names, in a book of 1890, and the island's folklore, in a book of 1891. He led the Isle of Man society devoted to natural history and antiquities. In 1899 he became the first president of the Manx-language body Yn Cheshaght Ghailckagh. After his death at Ramsey his book collection went to the Manx Museum.

:::

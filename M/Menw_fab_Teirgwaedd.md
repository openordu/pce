``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Menw fab Teirgwaedd is a wizard who travels with Culhwch in Culhwch ac Olwen. His gift is invisibility, and he uses it at the court of the giant Ysbaddaden Bencawr. He rides with Arthur in the hunt of the Twrch Trwyth. The party sends him into Ireland to check that the treasures the hunt seeks truly sit between the boar's ears. He can take the shape of a bird. In the Triads he takes his place among Britain's three renowned enchanters.

:::
```

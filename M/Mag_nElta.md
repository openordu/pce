::: magazinestyle
Mag nElta, later Moynalty, is the old plain of the bird flock. Its older form Senmag nEltair means the old plain of Elta. The name covered the ground between Tallaght, Clontarf, and Howth in County Dublin, an area that today's city has largely swallowed. The Partholonians of the Lebor Gabala, the Book of Invasions, settled this ground in the earliest legendary occupation of Ireland.

:::

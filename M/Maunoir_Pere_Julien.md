::: magazinestyle
Maunoir, Père Julien (1606–1683) was a Breton Jesuit missionary from the seventeenth century. He wrote a dictionary and a grammar that appeared in 1659. Scholars accept these books as the gateway to Modern Breton as written speech. His missions taught through the Breton spoken around him. He set aside the archaic medieval words that literary men still kept alive. Chief works include his two French-Breton dictionaries and a devotional guide, Le sacré collège de Jésus, both printed in 1659. A biographer, Boschet, told his life story fourteen years after death.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mag is the Irish word for a plain. It appears in countless actual and imaginary place-names across early Irish tradition. English forms often render it phonetically as Moy-, so Mag Tuired surfaces as Moytura or Moytirra. The spelling Magh represents the Modern Irish form of the same word. Compare tir, the Irish word for land, which builds a parallel family of place-names.

:::
```

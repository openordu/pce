``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Morgan, also known as Morgen, Morgan Le Fay, Morgaine, and Morgana, was an Arthurian heroine. She was one of king Arthur half sisters, children of his mother Igraine and her first husband, duke Gorlois of Cornwall. The sorceress and the queen Morgause are often confused. They share similar names and may have once been one figure. Morgan learned magic as a child in a convent. Some legends say she furthered her study with the magician Merlin. When her lover Guyomard slighted her, she created the Perilous Valley, where an unfaithful knight was trapped by illusions. She married a minor king, Urien, and became the mother of Owein. While her brother Arthur triumphed at Camelot, Morgan worked to bring disaster on his court. She stole and destroyed the magical scabbard of Excalibur. The scabbard was what kept Arthur safe from wounds. Yet it was to Morgan that Arthur was brought at the end of his earthly life. She could take him to the Otherworld, to die or be healed there. Her name Morgan Le Fay means Morgan the Fairy, or more powerfully, the fate. Like many fairy lovers, she tried to seduce the knight Lancelot, but without success. Morgan may connect with the Irish goddess the Morrigan, a divinity of death and battle. The name Morgan survives as a Breton name for sea fairies who kidnap human men. The Fata Morgana, a sea mirage of palaces and columns, is said in medieval legend to have been created by her. Malory gives her a son named Mordred who dies fighting Urien.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mac-ind-Óg is an Irish spelling of the name of Angus Óg. The form means the young son, or the son of the young. Irish sources give the same figure the name Aengus. MacKillop sends readers from this spelling to the article on Angus Óg.

:::
```

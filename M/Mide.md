``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ancient Ireland kept four provinces. Leinster stood in the east, and Munster
held the far south. Connacht took the west, and Ulster braced the northern shore. A
fifth province, named Mide, hung in the middle of the whole. It was not a land of the
visible world. It was the unseen high center of the island. This matches the old
European view of a world of four quarters. The fifth quarter of that world is the
hidden core. The word Mide carries the two senses of a neck and a middle. It was the
place where the claim of the land to rule meets the body that takes it. That meeting
was sealed upon the hill of Uisneach. A stone of parts stood wet in that field. The old
word pointed to the stone of divisions and the open crown.
The wide name of Mide did not always hold the same ground. At the start it named only
the hill, and then it named the crown of the glen. It stretched to a belt of the plain
through the heart of other zones. The kings of the land sat in a low mound of the west,
the hill of Tlachtga. The high tide of a hundred years later clears the edge of the
throne. A second lord, Tuathal Teachmhair of the second turn, set the fifth in its tall
row. The name of the later county of Meath keeps his memory in the maps. This lord did
this with a single end. He placed a chief upon the seat of Tara who bent to no lord of
the four. That the spine of the whole of the crown should stand alone. A. the weights of
the heart of the ruler.
The book of the many waves gives another note of the name of Mide. It tells of the
druid of the same name. He came of the wandering host of the family of the ancient
north. That bringer lit the first fire upon the old hill. The fire held its flame for
seven whole years. Every other hearth of the island drew its light from that ring. That
gift gave the druid the claim of a sack of grain and one pig from each other house in the
middle of every hour. But the other druids fought the toll that grew so high. Mide cut
out their broad tongues as he bound them for the fire within his feats. They lay in
cline in the secret mound of the hill hill. His mother praised the child full of his
weight.

:::
```

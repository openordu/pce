::: magazinestyle
Mag Dá Cheó is the plain of the two mists, a place in early Ireland. It lay between Cruachain and Athlone, inside the realm of the Ui Maine of Connacht, in what is today County Roscommon. Tradition attaches otherworldly associations to the site. Some accounts connect it with Mag Mell, the pleasant plain of Irish story, and the two names are at times confused with one another.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mes Buachalla, also Mess Buachalla or Messbuachalla [Ir., a cowherd's fosterling], is a young woman often drawn as a foundling, and the early texts give her different fathers. Togail Bruidne Da Derga, the tale of Da Derga's hostel going down, names her a daughter of Cormac and of a woman who arrived in bird shape. Stepmother Étaín Óg seeks the child's death at first, then melts before her charm. Tochmarc Étaíne, the wooing tale of Étain, plays out earlier, and though her name is absent there the story implies an incestuous begetting: Eochaid Fedlech fathered her on his own daughter Étaín Óg. Before that story closes, she marries Eterscél and bears Conaire Mór. A third strand holds that Eochaid Airem and Étain (1) produced Ésa, who in turn became the mother of Mes Buachalla. Across all strands she stays a bridge figure between the Étaín material and the fall of Da Derga's hostel.

:::
```

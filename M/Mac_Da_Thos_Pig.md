::: magazinestyle
Mac Da Thó's Pig is a cross-reference heading. It sends readers to Scéla Mucce Meic Dathó, the saga about the feast of Mac Dathó's swine. In that tale the hospitality contest over the champion's portion turns on the carving of a vast pig. The host Mac Dathó owns the animal. His hound Ailbe decides the war between Connacht and Ulster that the feast ignites. The story stands as one of the liveliest saga pieces of the Ulster Cycle.

:::

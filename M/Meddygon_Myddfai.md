``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Meddygon Myddfai names a family of physicians who worked at Myddfai in Carmarthenshire for about five centuries. Their active years began near 1200 to 1230. An old preface in many late manuscripts records the learning of Rhiwallon and his three sons. Rhiwallon bore the title the Physician. Those sons, Cadwgan, Gruffudd and Einion, served Rhys Gryg, prince of Dinefwr. Written evidence is scarce before the fourteenth century. Legend makes a fairy of the lake their ancestor, a tale tied to Llyn y Fan Fach. The family still holds the title of the Hippocrates of Welsh history.

:::
```

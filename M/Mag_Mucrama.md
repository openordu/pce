``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mag Mucrama is remembered as the plain on which the battle of that name was fought. The dictionary treats the site only through its battle and sends the reader to Cath Maige Mucrama, the Irish title translated as the Battle of Mag Mucrama. That narrative belongs to the Cycle of the Kings.

:::
```

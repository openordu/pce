``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Miodhchaoin, with the variant spellings Midchain and Mochaen, is an ogre who stands guard over a hill in northern Lochlainn. In Oidheadh Chlainne Tuireann, the tale of the Tuireann children's doom, Lug Lámfhota sends Tuirenn's three sons to wring three shouts from him. That errand is one of the tasks that ends in their deaths. Miodhchaoin's own sons are named as Áed (io), Conn, and Core.

:::
```

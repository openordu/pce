``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
MacGill-Eain, Somhairle stood among the leading Scottish Gaelic poets. He joined the campaign to put Gaelic into the school syllabus across Scotland. He received five honorary doctorates. In 1987 he became freeman of Skye and Lochalsh. In 1990 he was given the Queen's extra Medal for Poetry. He died aged 85 on 24 November 1996. His works include 17 Poems for 6d, the cycle Dain do Eimhir together with Dain Eile, and From Wood to Ridge. His prose collection is Ris a' Bhruthaich.

:::
```

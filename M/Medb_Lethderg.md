``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Medb Lethderg, also Leathdhearg [Ir., red-side, or half-red], holds the sovereignty goddess role at Tara while reigning as a queen of Leinster. Stories picture her apart from Medb of Connacht, yet she is that Medb's double and probably the older figure. On this reading, Medb of Connacht may well have grown out of Medb Lethderg. She was a daughter of Conán of Cualu, in the Dublin and Wicklow country. She married nine kings of Ireland in succession. Her husbands included Conn Cétchathach, the king later called Conn of the Hundred Battles, and that king's son Art mac Cuinn. Cormac mac Airt, another of the nine, could not pass for a true king until her bed had made him one.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Monte Bibele is an Etruscan-period settlement and cemetery in the Apennines near Bologna. The site shows early contact and trade between peoples. Celtic metalwork appears among the finds. Burial goods include Montefortino-type helmets and Attic helmets. They also include cuirasses and greaves made from fourth-century BC Italy. The warrior elite at Monte Bibele proved receptive to Etruscan culture. The recovered material mostly dates from the fifth to the second century BC. The latest objects belong to the Roman period.

:::

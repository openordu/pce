::: magazinestyle
Maeve is the common English spelling of the queen Medb. Medb was the warrior-queen of Connacht and a leading lord of the Irish hero cycle of Ulster. The old queens and the old gods flow into her portrait. She is the most full-bodied character that the early authors put forward. Her seat stood at the widening fortress of Cruachain. In telling she could outrun any horse, and the glance of her cost two men their strength. She led the cattle raid that is the longest of the old tales, and she stood at the heart of the action. Her speakers call her queen of the fair head and queen of the night. Her power grew out of her when she wed a spirit of the sun. The age of the tales shaped her many times, and the poets keep her vivid. Her whole story is a lesson in early Irish kingship, and every telling sets her name as the queen of the west.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mauthe Doog is the Manx name of the Black Dog. Monaghan directs the reader to the fuller Black Dog entry. This spectral creature was usually shaggy and about the size of a calf. It was known throughout the insular Celtic world. The dog was an indication of great change and probable death. Black Dogs could sometimes be helpful. A single glance from the dog's eyes could kill. The spelling mauthe doog serves as a place-by-place form of moddey dhoo, the famous black hound of Manx tradition. The poet Sir Walter Scott made use of this spelling in his Lay of the Last Minstrel, in the sixth canto published in 1805.

:::
```

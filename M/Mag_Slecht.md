::: magazinestyle
Mag Slécht, also written Magh Sieacht and anglicized as Moyslaught, is an expanse of flat land in County Cavan. It lies one mile north-east of Ballymagauran. The plain is best remembered as the worship site of Crom Cruach, the idol that demanded human sacrifice at the festival of Samain in pre-Christian times.

Tradition holds that Crom bowed in the end to the superior power of Saint Patrick. Even so, a festival called Domhnach Croim Dhuibh, the Sunday of Black Crom, kept its hold on the area. It was held each year on the final Sunday of July and survived into the early twentieth century.

:::

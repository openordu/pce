::: magazinestyle
Menez-Dol is a granitic massif rising 65 m amid the coastal marshes of Dol. It stands in north-east Brittany east of the bay of Mont-Saint-Michel. Its summit forms a large plateau. A Palaeolithic deposit found at its foot in 1872 dates from about 125,000–100,000 bc. The Historia Brittonum may carry the earliest reference. There mons Iovis bounds the region of the Breton migrations. Joseph Loth and Gwenaël Le Duc read Leoteren as *Lec-Teren, the pool or sacred place of Taranis. Taranis the god of thunder matched Jupiter by Roman practice.

:::

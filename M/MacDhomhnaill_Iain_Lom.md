``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Iain Lom was a Scottish Gaelic poet. His family name is MacDhomhnaill. He wrote from about 1624 to after 1707. He supported the MacDonalds. He came from the Keppoch branch. Stories about his life vary. Some say he could not read. Others say he studied for the priesthood in Spain. His poems cover family and national matters. He wrote a song about the battle at Inverlochy. He mocked the Campbells in that poem. He wrote about the death of a Keppoch chief. He wrote on the return of King Charles in 1660. He wrote against the Union of 1707. He praised the leader Montrose.

:::
```

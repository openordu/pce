::: magazinestyle
Morris-Jones, Sir John (1864-1929) was a scholar of the Welsh tongue and a poet. He was born at Trefor, a place inside the parish of Llandrygarn on Anglesey. Schooling carried him to Friars School at Bangor and then to Christ College at Brecon. A scholarship in mathematics took him to Oxford, where Sir John Rhys guided his study. He lectured from 1889 and held a professorship from 1894 at the University of North Wales, Bangor. His grammar of Welsh from 1913 applied comparative methods from Indo-European study to a Brythonic language for the first time.

:::

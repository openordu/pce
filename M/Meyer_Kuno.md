Meyer, Kuno (1858–1919) was a German Celtic scholar. Born in Hamburg, he first met Scottish Gaelic during a trip to Scotland in 1874. He studied Germanic and Celtic languages at Leipzig from 1879. He won his doctorate in 1881 for a version of the Alexander saga in Irish. He lectured from 1884 and rose to professor at Liverpool University in 1895. In 1911 he took the Berlin Chair of Celtic Studies. His war propaganda cost him friends in the Celtic countries. He founded the Zeitschrift für celtische Philologie in 1896. He co-founded and first directed the School of Irish Learning. He also founded its journal Ériu.

:::

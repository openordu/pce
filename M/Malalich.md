::: magazinestyle
Malalich, also recorded as Samaliliath, belongs to the Partholonians, the first legendary settlers of Ireland. The tradition credits him with bringing the drinking of ale into the country. He also ordained divination, sacrifice, and ritual, which makes him a founder figure for religious practice in the Partholonian account.

:::

::: magazinestyle
moddey dhoo names the great black dog of Manx folklore tradition. The beast is sometimes said to haunt the corridors of Peel Castle along the western coastline of the island. Manx folklorists give two competing portraits of the creature, either the size of a young calf with irises as broad as dinner plates, or a hefty dark spaniel with rough matted fur. A local rendering of the same name is mauthe doog. Sir Walter Scott invokes the form in his 1822 novel Peveril of the Peak, where the regional black-dog stories enter English letters.

:::

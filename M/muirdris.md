``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
muirdris is a MacKillop headword naming the older label for the fearsome sea-monster that the hero Fergus mac Léti slays in a lake of Co. Down. MacKillop notes that later texts call the same monster sinach. The full record of this sea-monster tradition sits under the headword sinach.

:::
```

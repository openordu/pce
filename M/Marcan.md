``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Marcán [Ir. marc, a steed] names several men of early Ireland. The best known is the cuckold king of the Uí Maine, whose wife Créd (2) carried on an affair with Cano mac Gartnáin. Many commentators hold that this king fed into how the Arthurian King Mark of Cornwall was drawn. A second bearer, Marcán mac Cennetig, was a brother of Brian Bórama, and the source says to keep the two apart.

:::
```

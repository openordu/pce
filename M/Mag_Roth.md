``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mag Roth is the plain, now called Moira, in County Down where two armies met in 637. The high-king Domnall mac Aedo, whose power lay with the Northern Uí Néill, won the field. He overcame a joining of forces led by Congal Caech of Ulaid and Domnall Brecc from Scotland's Dál Riata, and Congal lost his life in the rout. Writers at Iona read the result as breaking the treaty of Druimm Cett made in 575. A later work, the Liber de virtutibus sancti Columbae, used the battle to justify English rule over the Gaels of Scotland. Early Irish literature gave the day lasting weight. Legend held that Cenn Faelad gained his learning after a blow at the fight, and the story of Suibne Geilt ties that king's madness to the same battlefield.

:::
```

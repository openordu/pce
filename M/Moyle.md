::: magazinestyle
Moyle, the Sea of, is the archaic, poetic-sounding title of the narrowest waters between Northern Ireland and Scotland at the Mull of Kintyre, the crossing most people now call the North Channel. Its Irish name describes a current under a bare, rounded hilltop. When Northern Ireland's districts were redrawn in 1974, the unit beside the North Channel, once part of Co. Antrim, took the name Moyle.

:::

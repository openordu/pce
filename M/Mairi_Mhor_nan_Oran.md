::: magazinestyle
Mairi Mhor nan Oran (1821-98) stood out as the leading poet of the land agitation movement that swept the Highlands in the nineteenth century. She was born and raised on the island of Skye. Much of her adult life unfolded on the mainland, at Inverness, Glasgow, and Greenock, until she came back to Skye for her final years. She wrote more than nine thousand lines of verse, all of them from the 1870s and 1880s. She could read her own poems but wrote none of them down. A theft conviction in 1872 that she held to be false set her on the path of composition. Her plea of innocence runs through her poetry as a constant theme.

:::

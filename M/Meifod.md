::: magazinestyle
Meifod is a parish about ten kilometres north-west of the town of Welshpool. It stands close to Mathrafal, which tradition regards as the principal court of the Powys princes. Meifod Church served as the leading church of Powys in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries. A tenth-century decorated cross-slab still endures at the site. The poet Cynddelw Brydydd Mawr wrote a song to Tysilio that calls the church the abode of three saints. He names Gwyddfarch, Tysilio and Mary as those saints. The burial ground there, he says, held kings. The prince Madog ap Maredudd, who died in 1160, lies buried at Meifod.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Muirenn Muncháem, also written Muireann Mun-chaomh, Muirne, Murna, Morna, and Murine, is the mother of the hero Fionn mac Cumhaill. Her name, drawn from words for back of the neck and fair, beautiful, gives the English form Morna of the White Neck. A descendant of Nuadu Airgetlám, she is the daughter of Tadg mac Nuadat, who refused the match with Cumhall and brought about his death. She bore Fionn after that loss. Oral tradition held that she could not raise the infant herself, so nurses took him: Bodhmall, a druidess who was Muirenn's sister, and Liath Luachra. Her sister Uirne is the mother of the hound Bran and of Sceolang. Greek myth assigns the same mark to Hera, whose white neck is famous too.

:::

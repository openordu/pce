Maine Mór mac Echach is the purported founder of the Ui Maine, the tribe and family of the medieval west. Their lands lay in Galway and Roscommon. The dictionary records the claim of foundation without endorsement, and the patronymic mac Echach, son of Eochu, ties him to that genealogy.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Math fab Mathonwy is a lord of Gwynedd in the Third Branch of the Mabinogi. A certain defect keeps him from making a circuit of his own lands. He must rest his feet in the lap of a virgin. When that needed aid fails, he turns to his two nephews. These are Gwydion and Gilfaethwy, the sons of Dôn. Gwydion is a master of magic and trickery. Gilfaethwy's lust moves the story forward. Their schemes draw Math into conflict with his own kin. The tale turns on honor, shape-shifting, and stern justice. Math answers sly craft with harsh penalties.

:::
```

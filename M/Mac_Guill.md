::: magazinestyle
Mac Guill is a corrupt spelling of Mac Cuill. Mac Cuill is one of the three Tuatha Dé Danann brothers who killed Lug Lámfhota. The brothers shared Ireland after that deed, each god taking a name drawn from the land. Readers who meet Mac Guill in a text should follow it to Mac Cuill, husband of Banbha.

:::

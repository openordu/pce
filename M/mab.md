::: magazinestyle
mab is a MacKillop headword naming the Welsh prefix meaning son or boy. MacKillop lists the form alongside its mutated variants ap, fab, and vab. The fuller record of Welsh patronymics sits under the prefix mac, with bibliography to Morgan and Morgan's Welsh Surnames.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Morgan, William (c. 1545-1604) translated the whole Bible into Welsh. He served as rector of Llanrhaeadr-ym-Mochnant from 1578 to 1595. Morgan later became bishop of Llandaff and then of St Asaph. He published the entire Bible in Welsh in 1588 at the command of Elizabeth I. Many scholars call Morgan the father of Welsh prose. He built his work on the efforts of earlier scholars. His translation helped standardise the Welsh literary language. Morgan also held the rectory of Llanfyllin and the sinecure rectory of Pennant Melangell.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Mug Ruith, also spelled Roith, Mog Ruith, Ruth, and Mogh Ruith, is the celebrated druid of early Ireland, at times called the archdruid and at times one-eyed or blind. His father is Cethern, and the goddess Ana stands as his patron. O'Rahilly argued that the figure began as a sun-god, since his single eye suggests it. His breath alone could dry waters or raise storms. Because he championed paganism against Christianity, a learned medieval Christian interpolator turned him into an associate of Simon Magus, who in later church legend opposes St Peter. Together the two build the flying machine called the roth rámach, the rowing wheel, which was later seen at Tlachtga, the assembly named for Mug Ruith's daughter. In the tale of the Siege of Knocklong, Fiachu Muillethan awards him a broad holding in the north of Co. Cork, and tradition also ties him to Valencia Island in Kerry.

:::

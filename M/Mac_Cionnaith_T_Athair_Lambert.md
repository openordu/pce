::: magazinestyle
Mac Cionnaith is the Irish name of the Revd Lambert McKenna (1870–1953), an Irish Jesuit teacher and a scholar of the Irish language. He belonged to the tradition of devoted editors and translators whose work on bardic poetry remains largely unsurpassed. His specialism was verse of the Classical Modern Irish age, from about 1200 to 1650, with a strong interest in poems on religious themes. Born in Dublin and dying there, he prepared editions of several bardic texts, among them the anthology Dioghluim Dána, the seventeenth-century poetic dispute Iomarbhaidh na bhFileadh, and the books of Magauran and O'Hara. His scholarship is remembered for preserving the literary heritage of the classical Gaelic learned order.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Murthemne is a routing entry that sends readers to Mag Muirtheimne, the level plain by the Irish Sea that lies between Dundalk and Drogheda at the Boyne's mouth. The dictionary marks that plain as Cúchulainn's inheritance.

:::

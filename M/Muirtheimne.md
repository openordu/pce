``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Muirtheimne is the dictionary's routing entry for Mag Muirtheimne, the plain district of Co. Louth that reaches north from the Boyne toward Cuailnge. Oral and literary tradition fix it as Cúchulainn's homeland. His death story also carries the name: Brislech Mór Maige Muirtheimne, the Great Rout of Muirtheimne, tells how the hero breaks his geis on dog meat offered by three crones, falls to Lugaid mac Con Roi's javelin, and is beheaded at a pillar-stone near Faughart in Co. Louth, where he has bound himself so as to die standing.

:::
```

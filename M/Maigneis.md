``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Maigneis appears in the variant forms Maighneis, Maignes, Maighnis, and Maighean. She is the second wife of Fionn mac Cumhaill, and the hero grieves for her as Toraigheacht Dhiarmada agus Grainne, the Pursuit of Diarmait and of Grainne, opens. The tale records her as the daughter of Garad mac Morna, also called Glundub, meaning black knee. Her brother is Goll mac Morna. Her place in the story is fixed by that grief, which sets the opening movement of the pursuit in motion.

:::
```

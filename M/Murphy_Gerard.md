Murphy, Gerard (1901-59) was an Irish Celticist born in the county of Monaghan (Contae Mhuineacháin). His chief concern was the early poetic tradition of Ireland. From 1938 until 1959 he taught in Dublin, where he held a professorship in the history of Celtic literature. His edited works include Early Irish Lyrics from 1956 and Early Irish Metrics from 1961, both still cited as standard. He died in the city of Dublin (Baile Átha Cliath).

:::

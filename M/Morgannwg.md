::: magazinestyle
Morgannwg is a Welsh regional name with shifting borders. A petty kingdom of that name stood in pre-Norman south-east Wales, at times allied with its eastern neighbour Gwent. In the Mabinogi, Pwyll widens his realm by taking land in Morgannwg. After the 1536 Act of Union joined the region with Gower to make Glamorgan, the old name lived on in the literary tradition that ran, with pauses, from the 14th century to the age of Iolo Morganwg.

:::

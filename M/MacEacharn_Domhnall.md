MacEacharn, Dòmhnall, known in English as Donald MacKechnie, lived from 1836 to 1908. He wrote Scottish Gaelic poetry but gained more notice for his prose. He was born on Jura in the Hebrides and later settled in Edinburgh, where he joined a circle of urban Gaels. He served a newspaper trade and took an active part in Highland societies. His prose is remembered for a plain, forceful style that helped shape modern Gaelic writing. He produced a series of travel sketches and essays that recorded the life of the Highlands in his day.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mac Cécht is a king and a warrior once of the Tuatha Dé Danann. His name marks the son of the plough. He joins with his brothers Mac Cuill and Mac Gréine to bring the hero Lug. The same fighter also slays Mechi, a son of the goddess Mórrígan. Writers view this figure as a human echo of the healing god Dian Cécht. He also pairs with Fódla, who stands for the land of Ireland. When the leader named Nuadu dies, the three carry his body to Ailech in County Donegal. There the brothers plan to divide Ireland among themselves. They ask the Milesian man Ith to rule on the shares. Distrusting his answer, the brothers put him to death. The deed then brings the Milesian host against Ireland. The warrior Éremon overcomes Mac Cécht in the war that follows. A separate champion of the same name guards the ill-fated king Conaire Mór. He follows that ruler to the Hostel of Da Derga on the fatal night. In another telling this watchman brings the golden cup to find water for the thirsty lord.

:::
```

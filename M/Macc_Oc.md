``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Macc Óc is a variant spelling of the name of Angus Óg. MacKillop gives the form a one-line entry and directs readers to Angus Óg. The spelling denotes the same divine youth whom Irish tradition also names Aengus.

:::
```

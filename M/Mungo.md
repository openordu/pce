::: magazinestyle
Mungo is the familiar name of St Kentigern, patron saint of Glasgow, who died in 603. He served as an early bishop in Strathclyde, and many legends gathered around him. Tradition counts him a grandson of Urien of Rheged and a kinsman of King Arthur; his mother was Thenaw, a Christian and a daughter of King Lot of Lothian. She and the child survived two attempts on their lives, once drifting to safety in a coracle. A famous tale has Kentigern catch a salmon which had swallowed the king's thrown ring, and the fish and ring still figure in Glasgow's arms. He is said to have baptized Merlin, to have spread the faith in Cumbria, and to have established the Welsh see of Llanelwy, now St Asaph. His feast-day falls on 14 January.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mechi was a son of the Mórrígan. Per the Dindshenchas, Mac Cécht slew Mechi at the River Barrow. When the body was opened, Mechi proved to have three hearts, and each heart held a serpent. Left alive, those serpents could have brought disaster on Ireland. Mac Cécht burned the corpse and cast the ashes into the river, and so ended the threat.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mac Gréine, also called Gréne and sometimes Cethor, was a king and warrior of that divine folk, the Tuatha Dé Danann. He wed Ériu, the earth goddess for whom Ireland is named. With his brothers Mac Cuill and Mac Cécht he made a trio of kingly deities whose wives were the three land goddesses. His name, son of the sun, recalls that the Irish word for sun is feminine, so scholars treat it as a matrilineal title rather than proof of an ancient god of the sun. He was sired, like his brothers, by the god Ogma. The Milesian poet Amairgin killed him. Of the three brothers he appears least often in story and owns little mythology of his own.


:::
```

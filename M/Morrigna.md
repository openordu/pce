Mórrígna, with the spelling Mórrigna, is an Irish name meaning great queens. It labels the trio of war-goddesses usually given as Badb, Macha, and Mórrígan. Nemain sometimes takes a place in the list, whether as a double for Badb or for Mórrígan or as her substitute. Some commentators go further and read Mórrígna as identical with Mórrígan alone, with Badb, Macha, and Nemain as her separate aspects.

:::

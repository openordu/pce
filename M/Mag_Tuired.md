``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mag Tuired is the plain where two great battles of Irish legend were fought. The plain of the pillars, and of the marsh, gives its name to both fights. The better known victory fell in the west, on a flat of land by a lough. This is the battle of the second contest, the tale that all the poets retell. The people of the mother goddess, the Tuatha De Danann, took the field first. The men they met were the Fir Bolg of the land. In that first war the king of the Tuatha lost his arm. The heal put a silver hand upon the king, and so he held the rule. The second battle set the Tuatha De Danann against the Fomorii, the wild folk of the sea. Their one-eyed lord, Balor, could lay a plain waste with his gaze. The young hero Lug took the field and put an end to that cold one. The plain still shows its old stones to this day. The name, the plain of the pillars, stays in the west of the island.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mšecké Žehrovice is an Iron Age site in Bohemia in the modern Czech Republic. It became known after 1943, when a carved stone head of a man was found while digging for sand. The carving shows the typical features of La Tène art: a band of hair, almond eyes, a curved moustache, and a torc around the neck. The site was a La Tène industrial settlement with iron smelting, blacksmithing, and production of sapropelite ornaments. Occupation of the site reaches back into earlier periods, including the Late Hallstatt and the Roman era. The head was deposited in a pit with other objects and is now often seen as an image of a heroic ancestor or an elite member of the community.

:::
```

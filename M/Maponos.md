``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Maponos, Latinized as Maponus, is a Celtic god honoured across Roman Britain, his name meaning the divine youth or great son. His powers centre on music and poetry. His cult stood chiefly in the north of Britain, where a shrine at Chesterholm in Northumberland carries the inscription Deo Mapono, to the god Maponos. His name turns up on inscriptions in present-day France as well, possibly those of Chamalieres in Gaul.

The god is linked with Gaulish Apollo, above all Apollo Citharoedus, the harper or cithern-player. Most commentators hold that the Welsh divine youth Mabon grew out of Maponos. Patrick K. Ford argued in 1977 that Maponos and Pryderi are, for practical purposes, the same deity. Eric Hamp argued further that the word Mabinogi should truly be glossed as the material held in common about the god Maponos.

Irish tradition equates the god with Angus Og, especially where that figure carries patronymics such as Mac Og, Mac Oc, Mac-ind-Oc, and Macc Oc. Maponos probably fed the Arthurian pair Mabuz and Mabongrain as well. Ford's 1977 study of the Mabinogi remains the standard entry point.

:::
```

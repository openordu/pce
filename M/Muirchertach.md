::: magazinestyle
Muirchertach, also written Muircheartach and Moriartogh, is an Irish male name meaning skilled in seacraft, a mariner. Pedigrees and genealogies cite it countless times in early and medieval Ireland. Its best-known bearer is the legendary high king Muirchertach mac Erca. Murtaugh, Mortimer, and Monty are the commonest of its many anglicizations.

:::

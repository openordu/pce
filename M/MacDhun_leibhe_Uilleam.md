``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
MacDhun-lèibhe, Uilleam lived from 1808 to 1870. He ranks with Màiri Mhòr nan Oran as a noted Gaelic poet and nationalist. His prose found its first outlet before his verse, and his history of Scotland, Alba, together with his Vindication of the Celtic Character of 1850, shows him as a strong partisan. He accused the Clearances of destroying Gaelic culture, linking that loss to mass emigration and the Jacobite cause. In his verse he turned his sympathies into song, yet his output proved uneven in quality. His most forceful lines cluster in his battle pieces and laments. He was born a Catholic and trained in law before turning to poetry. His chief books are Alba and the Vindication of 1850.

:::
```

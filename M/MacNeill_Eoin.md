::: magazinestyle
MacNeill, Eoin was born John MacNeill in Glenarm, Co. Antrim. He studied in Belfast at St Malachy's College, then at a Royal University in Ireland. He learned Irish and became one of the founders of Conradh na Gaeilge in 1893 with Douglas Hyde. He became a driving force of the Irish-language revival. In 1908 he was elected professor of early Irish history at Dublin. His scholarship defined early Irish history and drew a line between it and myth. He helped found the Irish Volunteers in 1913 and served as Chief of Staff. In 1919 he became Minister for Finance in the first Dail. He supported the Free State and held the presidency of the Royal Irish Academy. He died in 1945.

:::

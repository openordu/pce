``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Morgenau, whom the sources call Morcenou in Old Welsh, was bishop of St David's (Tyddewi) from 984 to 999. He held office when the realm of Deheubarth, soon a part of south Wales, endured repeated raids by Vikings who sailed from Ireland. The Annales Cambriae record his name. So do the Latin and vernacular lives of St David. Giraldus Cambrensis relates that Morgenau brought meat into the regular foodways of the clas community at St David's. He thus broke an older vegetarian rule of the house.

:::
```

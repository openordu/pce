``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
mac is a MacKillop headword naming the patronic particle used in Irish and Scottish naming. MacKillop notes that through Welsh mutation, the prefix may also be spelled fab, mab, or vab. Welsh examples are alphabetized under the mutated form, so Gwyn ap Nudd sits at ap Nudd, Gwyn. The fuller record of Irish and Scottish patronymics sits under the prefix ua.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Muilearteach, also written Muileartach and Muilidheartach, is the water shape taken by the monstrous Cailleach Bheur of Scottish Gaelic tradition. A heroic poem, the Duan mu Muileartaich, or Ode of Muilearteach, keeps the record of that form.

:::

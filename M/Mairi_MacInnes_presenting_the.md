::: magazinestyle
Mairi MacInnes was a Scottish poet of the twentieth century. Born in the north of England, she later lived for many years in the United States. Her poems turn often on place, family, memory, and the wounds left by war. She put out a series of collections across several decades. Koch's Celtic Culture encyclopedia records her in its mass-media pages through a photograph of 2003. In that image she presents a Gaelic children's programme called Na Daoine Beaga, a title meaning The Little People. The shot shows her supporting the production of Gaelic broadcasting for young viewers.

:::

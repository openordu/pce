::: magazinestyle
Mynyddog Mwynfawr rules the 6th-century petty kingdom of the Gododdin. In the poem Y Gododdin he gathers a war-band for war on the Angles and Saxons, and the venture ends in disaster. His capital sits at Din Eidyn, probably Edinburgh. A speculative reading renders his name as 'Lung of the mountain', and English texts often call him Mynyddog the Wealthy.

:::

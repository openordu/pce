``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mag Mell is the plain of dreams in Irish lore. The name, the plain of delight, names one of the blessed lands of the other world. Many a hero came to it on a voyage. The seafarer Bran crosses Mag Mell while sailing to the apple isle of Emain Ablach. In the early text the plain lies within the sea, and there the fish play like calves. In many tales the plain is little more than a fair land, close to the apple isle and to the Land of Youth. Sometimes it lies to the southwest of Ireland. The plain can even attach itself to a real place beside a great lough. The rule of the plain belongs to a swift warrior of the plain and to the captain of the plain with the sword. The pleasant name later gave a composer his title. The fair domain holds the dreams of the early poets, and its plain stays a home to the royal.

:::
```

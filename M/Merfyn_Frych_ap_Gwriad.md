::: magazinestyle
Merfyn (Frych) ap Gwriad was a king of Gwynedd, known as the Freckled. His accession followed destructive infighting among male claimants and Viking turmoil across the Irish Sea. Some Historia texts give the work a redaction in the fourth year of his reign, hence 829. His change of regime may have pressed forward the assembly of historical materials. The Annales Cambriae link his death to a conflict at Cetill in 844. Koch links the epithet Brych to Domall Brecc. Koch notes Merfyn Mawr and a long-established dynasty for his family in Man.

:::

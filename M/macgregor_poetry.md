``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Macgregor poetry is a body of Scottish Gaelic verse linked to the MacGregor family. Modern scholars have edited and translated this poetry. Kerrigan placed examples in a collection of verse by Scottish women poets. MacGregor published the collection Polar Twins. Newton studied the verse in Bho Chluaidh gu Calasraid. He also wrote about it in Scottish Gaelic Studies. Ó Baoill discussed the material in Gàir nan Clàrsach. Thomson translated some of the poems. His Introduction to Gaelic Poetry holds these versions. Thomson also published analysis in Scottish Gaelic Studies. The singers RunRig recorded this poetry on the album Play Gaelic. Clò Dubh issued a recording called Clò Donn. The poems are short and lyrical. They preserve a distinct poetic voice in Scottish Gaelic. Modern editors have made the verse available to new readers.

:::
```

Mo Cháemóc, also styled St Caemhoc, Kemoc, Kevoge, or Kevoca, was a disciple of St Patrick. In Oidheadh Chlainne Lir, the tale of the Lir children's tragedy, Mo Cháemóc is the one who carries the Christian faith to the Children of Lir.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mac Óc is a patronymic form of the name of Angus Óg. MacKillop also lists the short form Óg under this headword. Irish sources name the same divine youth Aengus. The entry works as a signpost to the article on Angus Óg.

:::
```

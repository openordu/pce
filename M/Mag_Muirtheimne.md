::: magazinestyle
Mag Muirtheimne, anglicized as Murthemne, is a coastal plain of the Irish Sea, set between Dundalk and Drogheda. It stands at the mouth of the Boyne River. Irish tradition makes it the inheritance of Cuchulainn, and the hero's Ulster stories root themselves in this landscape.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Merlin, also Myrddin, Myrdinn, Merddyn, or Merlinus, was the great magician of the
Arthurian legends. He was the power behind the throne. Merlin created and kept the realm
of Camelot. He moved the stones of Stonehenge to the plain of Salisbury. As a boy he was
chosen for a sacrifice for a new building. He saved himself by speaking a prophecy.
Arthur was given birth by the magic of Merlin. Uther Pendragon took the shape of the
lord Gorlois to wed Lady Igraines. Merlin led young Arthur to the sword that rested in a
stone. He served the king as adviser and close friend for many years. He could not stay
the fall of Camelot. His lover Viviane lock him in a tree or a tower in the wood of
Brocéliande.
Behind this tale may stand an older man. The warrior Myrddin gained the gift of
prophecy inside the larger of his host. He came in part from the unkept wild man of the great forest whom the Welsh kept in their song. Three books of Geoffrey of Monmouth cut the
pattern of the man. In the middle of the first, the wizard has a dark birth. The mother
of Merlin was a nun. An evil guest forced a door of her house. A separate zone of the
craft joins the wizard with the boy-prophet Markus of the early land of the Britain.
The last of these books ran the wizard far from the side of the king. The youngest
writer of the Ronokham, the French of the long east, put the wizard beyond. He gave
Merlin a home on the far isle of the Aid. With him came a great boar and all the
treasures of the realm. The Sea wall held his name to the night of the rest. Three books of Geoffrey set the pattern of his legend. The first
of them was written in the year 1136. A second part took the name of the folded prophecy.
The last of these, the life of the grey, left his story though. In this the wizard keeps
his distance from the king. The stream of his vision flows in his own hands. He becomes
again the made forest figure of the old song. He stands by the tales of two world at
once. On the far island in the west he hides his final years. A beast of the wood keeps
his custom there, with all the treasury of the isle. His name lives for the fishes of
the civilized east. The wild held him from the heat to the fall of the year.
```

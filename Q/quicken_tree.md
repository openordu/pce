quicken tree names the rowan of Celtic tradition. MacKillop points the reader to the rowan headword for the full record of the tree. The Celtic literatures treat the rowan as a protective presence against ill luck and enchantment. Place-names and folklore records place it at many boundary spots across Highland and Welsh landscapes.

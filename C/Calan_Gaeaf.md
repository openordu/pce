::: magazinestyle
Calan Gaeaf is the Welsh autumn festival that ends the harvest year. The name belongs to the first of November in the Welsh year. It stands with the feasts known as Hollantide and Allantide. The observance answers to the Irish feast of Samhain. This day closed the season of gathering and opened the winter. Marked at the turning of the year, it held a place among the great Celtic feasts.

:::

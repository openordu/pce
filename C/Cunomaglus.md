``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cunomaglus is a sometime epithet of the Gaulish Apollo. The compound carries a hound-prince or hound-lord reading, a modern sense drawn from its two parts. The title shows how worshippers paired a god of light with hound imagery. Readers seeking the god himself should follow the Apollo entry.

:::
```

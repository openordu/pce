``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Celtomania is strong enthusiasm for Celtic culture. This enthusiasm arose in France. It grew during a period of political unrest. Nationalist pride in France ran alongside it for a long time. Another force shaped the trend as well. The royalist counter powers of Chouannerie posed a threat. People also added generous French pride to the mix. Today both Celtoscepticism and Celtomania remain lively. Scholars still debate where such passion leads. Study of Breton identity and Celtic themes continues steadily.

:::
```

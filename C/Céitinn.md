::: magazinestyle
Céitinn is the Irish surname of Seathrún Céitinn, the seventeenth-century poet and historian. English readers know him by his adopted name, Geoffrey Keating. He wrote as a poet and as a historian of Ireland. The Keating entry carries his life and his great history of the country. This form routes readers to that record.

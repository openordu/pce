``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cé is a druid who serves Nuadu. He takes a wound at Cath Maige Tuired, the great battle fought against the Fomoiri. A second druid of the same name served Cathaír Mór, the legendary king of Leinster. The tale gives him no deeds of his own, so his record rests on that service and that wound.

:::
```

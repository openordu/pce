``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Courtship of is the English phrase behind the Irish tale-title word tochmarc.
MacKillop points readers from this phrase to that Irish term.
A tochmarc is a wooing tale, and the word heads many story titles.
The Wooing of Becfhola is the best-known example in the dictionary.
Readers should follow the tochmarc entry for the full account.

:::
```

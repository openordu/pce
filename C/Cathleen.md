``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cathleen is the English spelling of an Irish name usually written Caitlín. W. B. Yeats chose Cathleen Ni Houlihan for his version of Caitlín Ní hUallacháin. His play made this form widely known. The name also marks a spot on the map. Assaroe, a waterfall of the River Erne, also bears the name Cathleen's Falls.

:::
```

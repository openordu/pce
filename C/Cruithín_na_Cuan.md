Cruithín na Cuan names a kind of city in Irish story. It stands in the wondrous land of Emain Ablach, the island realm linked with the sea god Manannán mac Lir. The place belongs to the overseas Otherworld rather than to any real map of Ireland. Readers should follow the Emain Ablach entry for that island and its marvels.

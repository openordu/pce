``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Carlingford Lough is a sea inlet of striking size. It runs ten miles long and two miles wide. The water separates County Down from County Louth. Down lies in Northern Ireland, Louth in the Republic. Dundalk stands nine miles to the southwest. Much of the great Cattle Raid of Cooley unfolds on this water. The Newry Canal links the lough to Lough Neagh. The town of Carlingford sits on the southern shore of Louth.

:::
```

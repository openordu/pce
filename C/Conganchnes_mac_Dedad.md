``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conganchnes mac Dedad is a figure of the Ulster Cycle who also goes by the name Daire. The name means horn-skinned. Sources describe him as the brother or the uncle of Cu Roi. He sets out to avenge that hero's death, yet the texts hint he is Cu Roi himself restored to life. The champion Celtchair mac Uthechair later slays the figure called Conganchnes. Niam, the daughter of Celtchair, agrees to wed him so she can learn the secret behind his seeming invulnerability. The secret is narrow. Only spear-points driven through his soles and the back of his calves can kill him. The story ends in a strange turn, for blood from Conganchnes' dog Doelchu later kills Celtchair.

:::
```

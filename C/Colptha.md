``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colptha, who appears also as Colpa, was the fourth son born to Mil Espaine and Scota in the Milesian origin legend. His brothers included Amairgin, the invasion's druid-poet, and Donn mac Miled, progenitor of the dead. Irish etymologists took his name to mean stout or stalwart. Colptha drowned where the Boyne meets the sea as the Milesians came ashore. The drowning fixed his name on that waterside. The estuary kept the title Inber Colptha in scholarly Irish tradition, a name meaning the mouth of Colptha. That place-name anchors one of the invasion legend's deaths to a real river landscape on the east coast of Ireland.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre Cuanach belongs to the warriors of Ulster in the old accounts. He meets his end while fighting a desperate field. The text tells that he drowns in the fight against Cú Roi mac Dáiri. Cú Roi is a celebrated Munster champion of the great battle tale. Cairbre dies in the water before the struggle can close. His name survives only through this single moment of war. He thus holds a small place among the fallen men of Ulster.

:::
```

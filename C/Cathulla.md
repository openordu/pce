::: magazinestyle
Cathulla rules as king of Innistore in Macpherson's Ossian. He keeps his court at the fortress called Carric-thura. His daughter Clatho later weds Fingal and becomes his second wife. James Macpherson published the Ossian poems in 1760. He claimed the work translated ancient Gaelic verse. In truth he wrote the poems himself in the eighteenth century.

:::

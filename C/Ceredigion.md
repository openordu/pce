``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ceredigion is a county found in Wales. About 74,941 people lived there in 2001. Nearly 61 percent of them spoke Welsh. The county sits between the Dyfi and Teifi estuaries. English names for it include Cardigan and Cardiganshire. The Welsh form Sir Aberteifi was used long ago. Ceredigion keeps the name of a kingdom that came after the Romans left. Ceretic son of Cunedda founded that kingdom. The county bears the name of Ceredigion, one of the sons of Cunedda. The Ordovices tribe once held this land. In the eighth century the district united with Ystrad Tywi, and out of that union the kingdom of Seissyllwg emerged. Arthen, a king of the ninth century, ranks among its notable rulers. Later the county joined the Principality of Wales. Two universities grew at Lampeter and Aberystwyth. Cardigan became a noted coastal town.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ceredigion is a county in Wales. About 74,941 people lived there in 2001. Nearly 61 percent of them spoke Welsh. The county sits between the Dyfi and Teifi estuaries. English names for it include Cardigan and Cardiganshire. The Welsh form Sir Aberteifi was used long ago. Ceredigion keeps the name of a post-Roman kingdom. Ceretic son of Cunedda founded that kingdom. The Ordovices tribe once held this land. Later the county joined the Principality of Wales. Two universities grew at Lampeter and Aberystwyth. Cardigan became a noted coastal town.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbar is a character born in the Ossian poem of 1760.
James Macpherson drew him from several Gaelic figures.
He took traits from Irish and Scottish sources alike.
Readers often name him Cairbar the Usurper.
He chases Dar-Thula through the course of the work.
Her nature drew partly from the older figure Deirdre.
Cairbar stands as a stubborn and sharp rival.
His chief enemy is Oscar, the son of Ossian.
The two men contend for land and for love.
That struggle marks Cairbar as a tragic seeker of power.

:::
```

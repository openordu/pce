``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caerfyrddin (Carmarthen) is a market town in south Wales. Its English name is Carmarthen. In the Middle Ages the town grew into the administrative centre of south Wales. The famous Black Book of Carmarthen is connected to this town. Owain Glyndwr captured the town in 1403 and again in 1405. A notable eisteddfod saw the poets meet at the castle in the 1400s. Dafydd ab Edmwnd won a silver chair there. Since the 1500s the town has served as county seat of Carmarthenshire. Between 1974 and 1993 it led a short-lived county called Dyfed. Much later Caerfyrddin became a centre for Welsh printing and publishing. William Spurrell founded a press here that made a well known Welsh dictionary. The dictionary gives Caerfyrddin as the Welsh name for Carmarthen.

:::
```

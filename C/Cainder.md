``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cainder also appears in the spell form Cainnear. This name belongs to a daughter of Medb, the famous queen of Irish legend. A line of Christian saints likewise bore the same name. The name thereby unites royal legend with the holy history. Medb stands as a ruler of great standing in the old stories. Her child thus carries a name that later faith honoured. The spelling shifts across a few different sources. The person class holds this entry among mankind.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cróchnat, also written Cróchnait, is a mother figure of the Fenian ballads. Her name may echo the Irish word for saffron red. The ballads make her the mother of Diarmait and of Oscar. Other tales give the mother of Diarmait as Cochrann, so the two names trade the role. Readers should follow the Diarmait entry for her famous son.

:::
```

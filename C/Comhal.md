``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Comhal is a character in Macpherson's Ossian of 1760. He is the father of the hero Fingal. Early in the action the tribe of Momi kills him. James Macpherson seems to have drawn the figure from Cumhall of the Fenian Cycle, the true father of Fionn mac Cumhaill. The Ossianic renaming gave the father a altered form of the older name.

:::
```

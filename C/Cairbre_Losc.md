``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre Losc takes his name from the Irish word for a lame man. He appears in the sources as the father of Daui Dalta Dedad. Daui brings his line forward through the royal family of Dedad. The old compilers list Cairbre among the ancestors of that house. He is known for no separate deed or journey. His place rests on this single tie of blood. Cairbre stands quiet in the pages as a father before his son.

:::
```

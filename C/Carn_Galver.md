::: magazinestyle
Carn Galver is a giant of a known Cornish place-name story. He guards two villages in Cornwall. Those settlements, Morvah and Zennor, trust his mild and playful nature. One day he throws a stone in jest. That stone strikes and kills a small boy by accident. Grief then grips him hard. He pines for the lad for seven full years. A broken heart, born from that sadness, finally takes his life. A rocking stone now marks where he once stood. That logan stone sits a few miles west of Penzance on the Cornish coast, and it keeps his memory alive.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cano mac Gartnáin is a hero of a sad tale. Born of a Scottish king, he died in the year 688. Exile sent him over the sea into Ireland. A king named Diarmait gave him an honoured place at court. That king's daughter loved Cano from the first. She saved him from danger and kept him safe. Searching for shelter he came to a noble house. The woman of that house spoke her love to him. His heart refused the vow while he stayed in exile. He waited to win back his own land before he gave his pledge. His story reads like the loss and longing of later Arthur and Tristan. Some see in him an early echo of the knight Dyrstan and of Trystan. The love that could not reach its end shaped more than one life. Worlds of ancient text keep this once patient hero.

:::
```

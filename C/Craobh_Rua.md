``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Craobh Rua is a modern spelling of Cráebruad, the Red Branch hall of Ulster.
MacKillop records Craobh Ruadh as a further written form.
The hall was the great assembly house of Conchobar mac Nessa at Emain Macha.
Readers should follow the Cráebruad entry for the full account.

:::
```

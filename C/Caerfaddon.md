::: magazinestyle
Caerfaddon is the Welsh name for Bath. Bath is a spa town whose waters are hot. Those warm waters have drawn the sick since the old age. The Romans knew this place as Aquae Sulis. A shrine rose at the spring and many came to it. Bath prospered around the healing of these waters. Caerfaddon carries Bath into the Welsh tongue.

:::

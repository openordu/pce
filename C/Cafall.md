::: magazinestyle
Cafall is the Welsh form of Cabal, the famous foxhound of King Arthur. The same dog also carries English variant names. Several spellings survive across the sources. Caval, Cavall, and Kawal all point to this one hound. Arthur prized the beast and kept it at his side. The animal appears within tales of his royal court. Readers meet this dog in the older Arthurian matter. Each spelling leads back to the single beloved animal. So Cafall stands as the Welsh title of Arthur's loyal hunter.

:::

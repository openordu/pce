::: magazinestyle
Coinneach Odhar Fiosaiche is Scottish Gaelic for 'Sombre Kenneth of the Prophecies'. The name served as the nickname of the Brahan Seer, Kenneth MacKenzie, a seventeenth-century Scottish seer. Under this byname he delivered the predictions that made him the most renowned prophet of the Scottish Highlands.

:::

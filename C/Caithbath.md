``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caithbath is the name Macpherson gave to a figure in his Ossian verses. He makes this character the father of Semo. Caithbath is also the grandfather of Cuthullin in the same poems. The figure rests in part on Cathbad the druid of the Ulster Cycle. Cathbad gave counsel to the Ulster royal court and shaped its heroes. The invented Caithbath carries that older druid into later verse.

:::
```

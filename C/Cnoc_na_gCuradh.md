::: magazinestyle
Cnoc na gCuradh is an Irish name that means the hill of the heroes. MacKillop gives this headword only as a signpost. The full study sits under Cnoc Gréine, the Limerick hill joined with the figure Grian. Some stories swap the two names, so the heroes' hill and Grian's hill share one lore. Readers who want the tales, the setting, and the ritual ties should go to the Cnoc Gréine entry as the main article.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caladbolg is the great sword of Fergus mac Roich. Fergus stands among the chief warriors of the Ulster sagas. The blade appears within the Tain Bo Cuailnge saga. This Irish tale preserves his name and deeds. Fergus wields the weapon with great strength in war. The Irish name later travels across the sea. The Welsh give the parallel sword the name Caledfwlch. In the later legends the same blade belongs to Arthur. That famous weapon is called Excalibur. Each form springs from one ancient root. The Ulster Cycle keeps the oldest memory of the weapon.

:::
```

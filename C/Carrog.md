::: magazinestyle
Carrog is a Welsh word that means a torrent in English. The name was given to a monster of the Conway Vale. The creature is believed to dwell within the county of Gwynedd. Many stories of the region tell of this beast. The vale itself runs through this northern Welsh ground.

:::

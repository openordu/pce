::: magazinestyle
Crageevil is an anglicized spelling of Craig Liath, the grey rock of County Clare.
MacKillop lists Craglee as a second written form of the same name.
The rock stood about one mile from Killaloe and hosted the fairy palace of Aibell.
Readers should follow the Craig Liath entry for the full account.

:::

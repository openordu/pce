``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Covac is an anglicized spelling of the Irish name Cobthach.
MacKillop records this form among the written variants of that name.
Cobthach means victorious and names several legendary Irish figures.
The best-known bearer is Cobthach Coel Breg of the Cycle of Kings.
Readers should follow the Cobhthach entry for the full account.

:::
```

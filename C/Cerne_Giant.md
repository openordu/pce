::: magazinestyle
Cerne Giant is a vast figure of a naked man cut into a chalk hillside. The hillside lies near Cerne Abbas, seven miles north of Dorchester in Dorset. Workers cut through the turf to the chalk beneath in prehistoric times. Though of incalculable antiquity, people often credit the carving to British Celts of the pre-Roman era. He grips a club about 167 feet long in his right hand and stretches his left arm. Ditches about 2 feet wide form the whole outline. It spans 200 feet head to foot, with an erect 30-foot phallus. In recent centuries it hosted May Day celebrations and fertility festivals.

:::

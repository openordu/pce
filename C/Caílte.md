``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caílte is a Fenian hero, also written Cailte, Caoilte, Caelte, Keelta, and Kylta. Several bear the name, seven of them Fenians. Best known is Cailte mac Rónáin, sometimes a nephew of Fionn mac Cumhaill. Famous for fleetness of foot, he stewards for Fionn and catches wild animals in pairs for Gráinne. He kills giants and recites tales with a golden tongue. In Acallam na Senórach he lives into Christian times, speaking the old values to Patrick. Imram Brain has him reveal Fionn as the reborn King Mongán. Later ballads prefer Oisín. Barrán is his father-in-law, Suain his daughter. Scottish lore calls him Derglas, Macpherson's Ossian Co-alt. Some read Caílte and Oisín as nicknames for Fionn.

:::
```

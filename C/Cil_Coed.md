::: magazinestyle
Cil Coed, also spelled Kilcoed, is a figure in the Mabinogi, the body of medieval Welsh tales. The Mabinogi stands at the heart of medieval Welsh prose storytelling. He is known chiefly as the father of Llwyd, the enchanter who figures in the fourth branch. The surviving record names him only through this family tie, yet his son drives much of that branch's magic.

:::

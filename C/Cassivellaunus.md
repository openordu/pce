::: magazinestyle
Cassivellaunus, or Cassibelanus, was a British chieftain whose lands lay north of the Thames. He led the forces that resisted Caesar's second invasion in 54 BC. He was eventually beaten and sued for peace. Shakespeare knew him as Cassibelen in the play Cymbeline. He may have shaped the figure of Caswallon in Welsh literature. Some accounts name him a brother of Lludd. His defence of the river crossing delayed the Roman advance. The Romans gave this title through their writers, not their own tongue.

:::

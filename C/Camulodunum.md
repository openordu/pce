``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Camulodunum is a Roman town name in the land of Britain. The name means the fort of the god Camulos. The Romans gave this title to two towns they held at that time. One of these towns stood in Yorkshire. The other grew into the modern city of Colchester. That city became the greatest port of its day. Rome equated Camulos with its own god Mars. The fort took its title from the god who guarded the camp. The name lived on and shaped the city that came after.

:::
```

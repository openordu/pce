::: magazinestyle
Cailleach Uragaig is the hag of the isle of Colonsay. That island lies in Strathclyde, which was Argyllshire until 1974. She works as a spirit of winter on that shore. This hag holds a young girl captive beneath her power. The girl's lover tries to reach her with his assaults. The hag avoids his attacks by a strange change of shape. She turns herself into a wet grey crag that leans over the sea. In that stone form she escapes every blow he sends. Her watery cliff still stands as her lasting winter guard.

:::

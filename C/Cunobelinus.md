``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cunobelinus was a warrior of southern Britain. He led the Catuvallauni for forty years, until the force of Rome arrived. Shakespeare later drew the matter of his tragic Cymbeline from this leader. MacKillop records the name as Romano-British and reads its first element as the hound of the god Belinus. He dates the end of the rule to just before the Claudian conquest of 43 BC.
```

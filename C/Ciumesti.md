``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ciumesti is a Late Iron Age La Tene cemetery dug in north-west Romania between 1962 and 1965. The flat burial ground sits on a sand dune near the modern town. Excavators uncovered thirty four graves including cremations, inhumations, and both wheel made and hand made pottery. A spectacular warrior chieftain grave was found accidentally in 1961 near the cemetery. It held a bronze-crested iron helmet crowned with a bird of prey. The cemetery stayed in use about two centuries from near the end of the fourth century BC.

:::
```

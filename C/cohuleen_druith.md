``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The cohuleen druith belongs to Irish folklore. The name runs also as cohullen, from the Irish cochall, a hood. Storytellers gave this magical hood or cap to the mermaids and merrows of Ireland. The cap showed red and carried a covering of feathers. It let its wearer pass below the waves and live under the sea. If a person stole the cap, the mermaid could not go back beneath the water. She then had to stay landbound, cut off from her home beneath the sea.

:::
```

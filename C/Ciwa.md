``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ciwa is a legendary Welsh saint with close bonds to wolves. Wolves suckled the child in her infancy. The animals took the place of a human mother. One of her fingers carried a great nail. The byname the Wolf Girl recalls this rearing among beasts. The dictionary groups her with Ailbe among saints whom animals nursed.

:::
```

Nova Scotia names the Canadian province behind the dictionary pointer Canada. This province keeps a strong Gaelic heritage, carried and sustained by Irish and Scottish settlers. Cape Breton Island is the heartland of that living tradition. There early Gaelic song and verse were passed down through generations. Collectors gathered this material well into the modern era. The songs still resonate among the island's Gaelic speakers today. Nova Scotia is thus a place where the old Celtic voice endures in the New World.

:::

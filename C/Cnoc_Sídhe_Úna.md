::: magazinestyle
Cnoc Sídhe Úna is a prominent hill in north County Tipperary, Ireland. It rises 699 feet and stands about two miles east of Ballingarry. Tradition makes the hill a home of the fairies and holds it sacred to Úna, their queen. Her name stays attached to the summit in the English forms Knockshegouna and Knockshigowna. The hill also carried older ritual life. People once held Lughnasa activities on its slopes.

:::

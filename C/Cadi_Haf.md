::: magazinestyle
Cadi Haf is a Welsh name linked to the May Day festival. The Welsh people mark the beginning of summer on this day. Cadi Haf appears beside the Calan Mai rites in north-east Wales. There the summer branch moves from house to house. Cadi Haf is a buffoonish figure who walks along with the procession. He paints his face black and puts on a man's coat. In another detail the fellow wearing Cadi Haf sports a woman's petticoat over the coat. He collects coins in a ladle from the watching crowd. Calan Mai gives the fuller account of this Welsh festival.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cadineag is a Scottish Gaelic spirit of mourning resembling the bean nighe. Her keening is said to be audible by nightfall near running water, sounding before some disaster befalls a clan. Some accounts of cadineag connect her image with the standing stones of the massacre at Glencoe in north Strathclyde, formerly Argyllshire, in 1692. The figure may be identical with other named mourners of Gaelic tradition.

:::
```

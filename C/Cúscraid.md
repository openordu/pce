``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cúscraid is an Ulster hero bearing the epithet Menn or Mend Macha, the stammerer of Macha. The form Cúscrid appears beside his name. He is a son of Conchobar. His leading part comes in the feast tale Mac Da Thó's Pig. In that story Cet mac Mátach wounds him, and Mac Cécht slays him. The nineteenth-century writer Standish James O'Grady gave him the name Cowshra Mend Macha. The name appears in the imaginative History of Ireland he published in 1878.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cromlech is a cross-reference entry. It sends the reader to the articles on Dolmen and Megalithic Civilization. The word joins Welsh crom, bent, with llech, flat stone. Irish gives crom, stooped, and leac, flagstone. Welsh and Cornish writers prefer this word for the prehistoric structure, which appears in every Celtic country. That structure sets a large flat stone upon three or more upright stones. English writers describing sites in Ireland and Brittany call the same structure a dolmen. The word can also name a dolmen built in a rounder form. French usage stretches the word to a grouping of dolmens, squared or round. Carnac in Brittany supplies the classic example.

:::

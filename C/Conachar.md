``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conachar is a variant spelling of Conchobar, the name of the king of Ulster in the Ulster Cycle. MacKillop lists it as a simple spelling variant and points readers to his main article on the king. The main PCE article on that ruler carries the display form Concobar mac Nessa. See Concobar mac Nessa.

:::
```

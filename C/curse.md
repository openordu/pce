``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
A woman cursed a clergyman over the defamatory remarks he made about her. Her words pinned his horse in place. The horse stayed still until she spoke the freeing words. The woman married several times, and each husband was younger than the one before. At the end of her life she dropped her blue bottle into a stream. Some speakers say the bottle landed in a lake or river. The bottle is said to rest there still. MacKillop adds a heroic register of the curse. The goddess Macha laid her curse upon the Ulstermen, an affliction remembered as ces noinden. The entry closes with a pointer to geis.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cath Maige Ráth is the Irish title for the battle narrative of Mag Rath. The dictionary gives this headword a single line and points readers to Buile Shuibne. That tale holds the fuller account, and it makes the battle the turning point of its hero's life. Buile Shuibne is a twelfth-century Irish work in the Cycle of Kings. English readers know it as The Frenzy of Suibne or The Madness of Sweeney. It ends a trilogy of tales about Suibne Geilt, a minor king in seventh-century Ulster. Suibne went mad at the battle fought at Moira in 637. His mad wanderings then fill the rest of the tale. The fuller story of the fight therefore sits inside that text rather than under this title.

:::
```

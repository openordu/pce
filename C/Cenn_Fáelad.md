::: magazinestyle
Cenn Fáelad, also written Cennfäelad, was a warrior and poet of the seventh century. Tradition credits him with completing the writing of the Brehon Laws. Some accounts also give him the writing of Auraicept na nÉces, the Scholars' Primer. He belonged to the Uí Néill. The Annals quote him as the first poet recorded in their pages. His name appears in learned tradition as a master of both arms and letters.

:::

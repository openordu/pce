``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruach is a bare cross reference form of the idol name Crom Crúaich. MacKillop prints the single word and sends the reader onward. The idol it names was the gold image of Mag Slécht, ringed by twelve figures of stone. King Tigernmas first worshipped it, and each Samain took the firstborn of family and of livestock. Readers should follow the Crom Cruach entry for the whole record of the cult.

:::
```

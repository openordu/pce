::: magazinestyle
Conway, in Welsh Conwy, is a waterway of north Wales. It rises at Lake Conway and runs north to Beaumaris Bay in Gwynedd. Until 1974 it divided the former counties Denbigh and Caernarvon. Close by the town of Conway, on the west side of the river mouth, lies Degannwy, the court of the sixth-century king Maelgwn Gwynedd. Welsh tradition holds that the waters echo the dying groans of Dylan, the sea hero.

:::

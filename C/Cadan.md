Cadan names an early hero of Irish tradition. The spelling Cadhan is the Irish word for a wild goose. Cadan kept a powerful dog in legend. That fine hound killed a piast, a dreaded monster, near Derry. Derry is a city in Northern Ireland. The beast's death became a celebrated story. Early heroes often own such loyal hunting animals. Tales describe the hound's courage with full relish.

:::

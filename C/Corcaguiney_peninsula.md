Corcaguiney peninsula is the spelling MacKillop uses for the Dingle peninsula in west Kerry. The headword entry is a pointer, and it sends the reader to the article on Dingle. The peninsula is the homeland the Corcu Duibne held on both shores of Dingle Bay.

:::

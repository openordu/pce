::: magazinestyle
Comgall, also spelled Comhghall, is the name of at least ten Irish saints. The most famous founded the monastery at Bangor in Northern Ireland and died there in 603. His rule was severe. Seven of his monks died of cold and hunger while living under it. Tradition also credits him with a house on Scottish Tiree. Irish legend gave him a darker role. He offered King Fiachna mac Báetáin a choice between winning battle and suffering eternal damnation, or losing it and gaining eternal happiness.

:::

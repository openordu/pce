::: magazinestyle
Cattwg, also written Catwg, is a Welsh variant spelling of Saint Cadog. Cadog ranked among the most honoured Welsh saints of the sixth century. He founded a monastery at Llancarfan in Glamorganshire and roughly twenty further churches in south Wales, plus others in Cornwall and Brittany. Tradition holds that he taught Gildas and Taliesin. His feast-day falls on 25 September.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ceithern is an Old Irish term for a company of armed fighters. From it come the derived forms ceithernach and ceatharnach, which English speakers anglicized as kern and kerne. The modern Irish descendants are ceard and ceardach. Compare ceithern with the fianna, the warrior bands of early Irish tradition.

:::
```

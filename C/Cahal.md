::: magazinestyle
Cahal is the English spelling of the Irish name Cathal. The name means strong in battle. It was very common in Ireland during the early Middle Ages. Hundreds of historical people in genealogies carried this name. None of these figures appear in imaginative narrative tales. Writers give the English form Charles as a handy equivalent, although no real tie joins the two. So Cathal belongs to real history more than to legend. Therefore a man named Cahal is a genuine recorded ancestor, not a mythic hero.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Combert is a variant spelling of Compert, the Irish term for a tale of conception and birth. The genre gathers stories of how heroes were begotten. MacKillop lists Combert as a spelling variant and folds it into his Compert article. See Compert.

:::
```

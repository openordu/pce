``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ciudach is a cave-dwelling spirit of Scottish Gaelic story. The tradition also reaches Manx ground and districts of Ireland. The oldest tales show the character as an admirable giant. Later stories make the figure a monster that haunts caves. The related headword cughtagh covers the cave-haunting form. Tales from Roscommon and Inverness tell one episode in common. Gráinne takes Ciudach as a lover during her flight with Diarmait.

:::
```

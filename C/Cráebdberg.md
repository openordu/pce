::: magazinestyle
Cráebdberg, written Cráebderg in the dictionary sources, names one of the three royal houses of Conchobar mac Nessa. The name is Irish for the ruddy branch. The hall kept the severed heads and the spoils of war taken by the king's men. Its name marks it as the darker counterpart of the red branch hall Cráebruad, where weapons stayed outside. Readers should follow the Conchobar entry for the king who held both houses.

:::

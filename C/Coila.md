::: magazinestyle
Coila is a spirit and muse of Ayrshire, the poet's home region. Robert Burns addresses her in his poem A Vision, written about 1790. In the poem she speaks as a friendly voice for his native countryside. The figure gives the poet's home country a living voice.

:::

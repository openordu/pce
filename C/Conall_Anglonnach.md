::: magazinestyle
Conall Anglonnach ranks with the finest drivers and chariot warriors the Ulster Cycle knows. His byname anglonnach means warlike or valiant. The Ulster tales prize skilled drivers as much as the champions they carry, and this Conall stands in that ranks of the famous ones. He shares his first name with the hero Conall Cernach, but the two are separate figures. See Conall Cernach for the better-known bearer of the name.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Corrib names a famous lake in the west of Ireland.
MacKillop records the Old Irish form Loch nOirbsen for these waters.
The lake lies in Connacht and touches counties Galway and Mayo.
Its old name recalls Oirbsiu, an epithet of the sea-god Manannan mac Lir.
Readers should follow the Lough Corrib entry for the full account.

:::

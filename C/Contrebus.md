::: magazinestyle
Contrebus is a minor god worshipped in Roman-occupied Britain. The Latin name may mean co-habiter, one who dwells among us, and it relates to a word for a dwelling. A surviving inscription from Overborough in Lancashire carries a dedication to him. He belongs to the group of deities the Romans honoured in Britain alongside native gods.

:::

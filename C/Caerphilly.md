::: magazinestyle
Caerphilly is a town in south Mid-Glamorganshire. It stands roughly seven miles north of Cardiff. Welsh heritage names the Green Lady of Caerphilly as this town's resident phantom. That wraith dons ivy's likeness whenever she wanders away from ruined castles. In modern times the name has become known for a famous cheese. The town thus joins a folk legend to a beloved flavor.

:::

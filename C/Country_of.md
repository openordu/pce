::: magazinestyle
Country of is the English translation of the Irish word tir in place-names.
MacKillop offers the phrase chiefly to point readers onward.
The best-known use is the Country of Youth, the land of timeless life.
Irish tradition calls that land Tir na nOg.
Readers should follow the Tir na nOg entry for the full account.

:::

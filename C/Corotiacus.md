``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corotiacus ranks among the gods of Celtic Britain. Followers venerated him in the Suffolk region. Rome's invading forces matched him with Mars, their own deity of war. The Romans followed their habit of interpretatio romana and set Latin names over native gods. The natives answered with interpretatio celtica and bent the conquerors' gods to local needs, often adding a local epithet. Corotiacus stands as one such epithet of Mars in Britain, beside names like Albiorix, Caturix, Nabelcus, Rigisamus, and Thincsus. Hardly anything else of his cult is recorded.

:::
```

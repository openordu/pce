::: magazinestyle
Cenn Cróich is a variant of Crom Crúaich, also written Cromm Crúaich, Cróich, Cruach, Crooach, or Kerman Kelstach. Christian writers describe Crom Crúaich as the leading idol of Ireland before Saint Patrick. The image stood at Mag Slécht, a site in County Cavan. Its centerpiece was gold, and twelve stone figures ringed it. King Tigernmas began its worship. On each Samain, every family and every herd surrendered a first-born to the god. Preachers such as Patrick appear in the tales breaking idols of this kind. Oral tradition later recast the figure as Crom Dubh.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Carthon, also spelled Carthonn, appears in Macpherson's Ossian of 1760. Clessammor and Maona stand as his parents in that telling. His own father cuts him down in a single combat. The scene later echoes an older Irish tale. Macpherson based Carthon on Connla. That earlier hero met a similar end at the hands of Cúchulainn.

:::
```

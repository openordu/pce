::: magazinestyle
Cathair Chrofhind is an alternate title for Tara. Its first element comes from the Irish cathair, meaning fortress. The Tuatha Dé Danann called Tara by this name. Tara itself is a hill in County Meath, a few miles south-east of Navan. Tradition makes it the seat of the Irish high king. The site hosted religious ceremonies sacred to the goddess Medb. People buried their dead there from the second millennium BC onward. Later it anchored the Uí Néill, whose over-kings ranked among the most powerful leaders in Ireland.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cnoc Meadha is a hill west of Tuam in Co. Galway. Maps spell the name Knockmagha, Knockma, or Knock Ma. Tradition seats Finnbheara there, the king who rules the fairy host of Connacht. Two large cairns crown the hill. One cairn is held to cover Finnbheara. The other is held to cover Queen Medb. Some say her name survives inside the name Cnoc Meadha. The hill joins the fairy king and the great queen in one place.

:::
```

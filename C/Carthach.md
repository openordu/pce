::: magazinestyle
Carthach is an Irish name that carries the sense of a loving or affectionate one. Many members of early Ireland shared this title in the old years. The bearers included both the laity and the churchmen of their day. The most widely known among them gave his mark to the McCarthy line. He stands as the founder of the Mac Carthaigh family in Munster.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Creidhne is a variant spelling of the artificer-god this encyclopedia writes as Creidne.
MacKillop lists Creidne and Creidhne as forms of Credne Cerd, the divine craftsman.
The god served in the craft trio of the Tuathaidé Danann beside Goibniu and Luchta.
Readers should follow the Creidne entry for the full account.

:::

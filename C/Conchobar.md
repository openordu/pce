``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conchobar is a major Irish male name. Writers also spell it Conchubar, Conchobhar, Conchúr, Conor, or Connor. The meaning stays uncertain. Some read it as wolf-lover. Others read hound-lover. Thousands of Irish figures have carried the name across the centuries. Its best-known bearer is the Ulster king Conchobar mac Nessa of the Red Branch age. Conor became the usual anglicization. The surname O'Connor preserves the name. It splits into six divisions, each with its own forebear. Writers since the 15th century have matched it with Latin Cornelius. No real etymological link joins the two. See Concobar mac Nessa for the main king article.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cicollius stands as a sometime epithet of the Gaulish Mars, the god Mars as Roman worshippers knew him in Gaul. An epithet is a descriptive title attached to a deity's name. Religion in Roman Gaul blended native gods with Roman interpretatio, so local figures often received Latin titles of this kind.

:::

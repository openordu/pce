``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caercaradoc is a Welsh word that stands for Salisbury. The name appears in the pages of Geoffrey of Monmouth and in later Arthurian tales. Writers of that age gave the English city a Welsh voice. So the same place carried two titles in old story. Caercaradoc links the town of Salisbury to the medieval world of romance. It is one more sign that the Welsh tongue kept hold of a familiar English seat.

:::
```

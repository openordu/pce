::: magazinestyle
Ciar is a love-child of the warrior Fergus mac Róich and Queen Medb in the Irish stories. The name is Irish and carries the sense of dark or black. Later tradition held that descendants of Ciar settled the south-west of the island. Those people were called the Ciarraighe, the folk of Ciar. Their land became Kerry, with Irish forms given as Ciarraighe and Ciarrai.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Calgacus is the name given by Tacitus to a native leader of the far north. The man was red-headed and carried a sword. The Roman general Julius Agricola defeated him at the battle of Mons Graupius. That fight took place in the north-east corner of Scotland. The clash is dated around AD 78 to 84. Should the name be genuine rather than the writer's invention, it remains the first on record for a Scottish man.

:::
```

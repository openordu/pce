``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
An Claidheamh Soluis means The Sword of Light and was the bilingual newspaper brought out by the Gaelic League from March 1899, with Eoin MacNeill as its first editor. In August 1900 the League took over the weekly paper Fainne an Lae after its publisher failed, and the two sheets merged. From 1903 to 1909 the editor Padraig Mac Piarais printed original Irish literary works and reviews. The paper became a key source for the rising Irish cultural and political nationalism of the day. It finally ran under several names until it closed in 1932.

:::
```

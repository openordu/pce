::: magazinestyle
The chough is a European member of the crow family (Corvidae), and the form most often cited in Celtic folklore is the red-legged or Cornish chough (Pyrrhocorax pyrrhocorax). In the Cornish language the bird bears the name An Balores, a name that has since been adopted as a marker of revived Cornish cultural identity. Outside Cornwall, the chough is frequently mistaken for the ordinary crow or for the raven. The MacKillop entry groups the bird under the wider crow family and records its Cornish name as evidence of regional folk knowledge.

:::

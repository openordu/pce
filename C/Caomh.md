``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caomh (Cóem) is the legendary ancestor who gave the O'Keeffe family its line. That family held land in County Cork and across much of Munster. The old stories present Caomh as a fine athlete. The same tales make him a fighter who faced the Norsemen. He also stands out as a lover. The legend joins his name to both Clidna and Aoibhill. Aoibhill holds her own place among the fairy women of Munster. So Caomh forms a proud, strong root for his clan. His deeds echo down in the lore of the province.

:::
```

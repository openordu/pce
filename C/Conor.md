``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conor is an anglicized spelling of Conchobar. The Irish name also appears as Conchubar, Conchobhar, Conachar, and Conchúr. The best-known bearer is Conchobar mac Nessa, the king of Ulster in the tales of the Red Branch. The common family name O'Connor preserves this form, and it descends from several distinct Irish lineages.

:::
```

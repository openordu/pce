``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
chess is the standard English label, but applied to the early insular board-games it gives a misleading translation. The label covers a handful of distinct Irish and Welsh traditions, including fidchell, gwyddbwyll, and búanfach, each with its own rules and lore. Scholars therefore warn against treating chess as a precise term for any single one of these games.

:::
```

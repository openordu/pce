``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Luel is a Welsh name for the English city of Carlisle. The word acts as a pointer to Caerliwelydd, the fuller title for the same place. Both forms stand for one single city. Carlisle grew from an old Roman station called Luguvalium. The settlement kept its standing under a fresh name in Welsh speech. So the short form and the long form agree on one town. Caer Luel lands on the very same ground as Caerliwelydd.

:::
```

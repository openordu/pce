``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruithne carries two senses in the Irish dictionary tradition. The first sense belongs to a woman. She was the beautiful daughter of the smith Lóchán of Cuilleann. She fell so hard for the Fenian leader Fionn mac Cumhaill that she became his wife almost at first sight. The second sense is a people name. Writers used Cruithne as an Irish name for the Picts. Readers who follow the people sense will find more in the Cruithin and Pict entries.

:::
```

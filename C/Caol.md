::: magazinestyle
Caol is a modern-spelling form of the hero Cáel. The name also serves as another title for Mac Lugach. Readers who seek the warrior's full story should turn to Cáel. The tale of Cáel appears in the Fenian cycle, where he woos Cred, daughter of the king of Manannán.

:::

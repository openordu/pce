``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre Lifechair rules as a high king of Ireland in the Fenian Cycle. He is the son of Cormac mac Airt and stands against Fionn mac Cumhaill. Early in his reign the Fianna hold strength to match his own. When his daughter Sgiamh Sholais prepares to wed, they demand twenty ingots of gold as tribute. His anger flares at this blatant demand on his house. He rallies allies and crushes the Fianna in ruthless war. The blow splits the clans Baiscne and Morna, and the latter joins him. The last clash comes at Cath Gabhra, the Battle of Gabhair. There the Fianna fall but Cairbre takes a mortal wound from Oscar. He still drives a spear into Oscar's breast before he dies. Other accounts name the same battle Cnámross. His brother is Dóel and his son is Eochu Doimlén. That son fathers the Collas, while Fiachu Sraibthine is another son. Cormac begot Cairbre upon Eithne Tháebfhota in Esnada Tige Buchet.

:::
```

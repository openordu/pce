``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cnobga is the Irish form of the name Knowth. The spellings Cnobgha and Cnoba also appear in the sources. Knowth itself is the great passage tomb of the Boyne Valley. Its Irish name preserves the sound of the ancient site. Writers on Irish tradition use these forms when they name the monument in Irish. The forms show how one place carried names in two languages.

:::
```

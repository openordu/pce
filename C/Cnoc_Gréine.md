::: magazinestyle
Cnoc Gréine is the hill of Grian in eastern Co. Limerick. It stands near Pallas Grean. The hill belongs to Grian, a local power of the older religion. Some tales say Grian is Macha in another shape. The name ties her to the sun. Conall and his five sons attacked the hill in story. Conall struck it himself after his sons did. Certain tales give the hill a second name, Cnoc na gCuradh, the hill of heroes. Cnoc Áine lies seven miles away.

:::

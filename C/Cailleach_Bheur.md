``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cailleach Bheur is Scottish Gaelic for the genteel old lady or hag. She stands as the personification of winter in Scottish folklore. She mirrors the Manx hag they call the Caillagh ny Gueshag. Her face is blue, and folk name her a child of the pale winter sun. She rules from the first of November to the first of May. Winter births her old and plain of face. When spring arrives she ends young and beautiful. That cycle mirrors the ever-renewing Cailleach Bhéirre. She also echoes the change of the Cailleach Beinne Bric. People of Ulster know her as Cally Berry. Her watery form carries the name Mwleartach.

:::
```

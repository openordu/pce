``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
More than a dozen Irish figures share the name Connla. One was the child of the hero Cocolann and the warrior woman Ayin. He refused to give his name when he met his father in Ulster, fought him, and was killed by him. A second Connla, known as Connla Colo, is a fairy king. A third, Connla of the Golden Hair, counts the king of Tara as his father. He quit the earth with a woman from the fairy race to win eternal life. MacKillop adds one more bearer to the list. Connla Cóel is the lord of an otherworldly island that Cúchulainn once visited. The figure links the name to the same overseas Otherworld that draws the youth in the Adventure of Connla.

:::
```

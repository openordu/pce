::: magazinestyle
Cnoc na Sídha is an Irish name that means the hill of the fairies. Such names mark heights linked with the dwellings of the fairy folk. The term is generic, so it could fit any of hundreds of hills across Ireland. One link stands out in modern writing. W. B. Yeats cited a hill of this name, and it stands in County Donegal near the falls of Assaroe.

:::

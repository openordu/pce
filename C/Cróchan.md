``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cróchan is a variant spelling of Cruachain. That name belongs to the great royal fortress of Connacht in the saga literature. Medb and Ailill held their court there in the tales of the cattle raid. Readers should follow the Cruachan entry for the fortress and its wonders.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
The caoineag is a Scottish Gaelic spirit of mourning known across the northern Highlands and the Hebrides. The word itself means weeper. Folk tradition frames the caoineag as a local form of the banshee. Hearers in MacDonald country reported cries from the caoineag after the slaughter at Glencoe in 1692. Some sources also identify her with the bean nighe of wider Gaelic tradition.

:::

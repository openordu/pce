::: magazinestyle
Culloden was a battle site near Inverness in the Scottish Highlands. On 16 April 1746 it closed the last rising of the Stuart line. Charles Edward Stuart led the bid for the thrones of England and Scotland. In July 1745 he came ashore along the western coast of Scotland. Over a thousand men joins him. His army entered England, but a stronger force turned it back. The worn force fell on the moor at Culloden. Charles slipped away and left the country. The defeat broke the clan system and hastened the decline of Scottish Gaelic speech.

:::

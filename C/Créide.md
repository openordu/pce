::: magazinestyle
Créide, also called Créide Fírálaind, is Créide the Truly Beautiful. Her father was Fidech, a man of the Long Hair. The quest tale of Art Son of Conn gives her a part beside the hero Art. She waits among the beautiful women of a wondrous island. When Art speaks of his search, she warns him that the road ahead is long and full of hardship. The name may be the same as Créd, the fairy queen of the Fenian love tale.

:::

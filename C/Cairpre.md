::: magazinestyle
Cairpre is a variant form of the name Cairbre. Early writers used both spellings for the same men. The shorter form Cairpre appears beside the fuller Cairbre in old manuscripts. Such a name belonged to heroes, kings, and poets across early Ireland. A reader who seeks full details should turn to the entry on Cairbre. This page records only the alternate spelling of the name.

:::

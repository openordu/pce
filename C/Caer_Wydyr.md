``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Wydyr is a Welsh name meaning the fortress of glass. Welsh tradition gives this title to Annwfn, the Otherworld. The glass fortress stood as one label for that hidden realm. Welsh writers also tied the glass image to the island Ynys Gutrin. This island was later set at Glastonbury in Somerset. So Caer Wydyr joins the glass fort to both Annwfn and that island.

:::
```

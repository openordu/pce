``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corroi map Dayry is the Welsh spelling of the Ulster hero Cu Roi mac Dairi.
MacKillop records this Welsh form as a direct pointer to that hero.
Cu Roi is a shape-shifting king of Munster in the Ulster tales.
Readers should follow the Cu Roi entry for the full account.

:::
```

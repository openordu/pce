::: magazinestyle
cythraul is a Welsh word carrying the senses hostile, devil, and enemy. The spelling cythrawl appears as a variant. The word served in early Welsh narrative as the standard term for the devil and for other incarnations of evil. Its semantic range moved between personal enmity and demonic opposition.

:::

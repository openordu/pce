::: magazinestyle
Ca-Olt is a hero in James Macpherson's Ossian. The poet published Ossian in 1760. Macpherson built the character upon Caílte mac Rónáin. Caílte mac Rónáin is a hero of Irish legend. The two figures share a clear link. Ca-Olt draws his name from that older warrior. Ossian gives the Irish hero a fresh voice. Macpherson reshaped many such legends for his book. Ca-Olt stands among those refashioned heroes.

:::

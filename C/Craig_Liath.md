::: magazinestyle
Craig Liath is a grey rock of County Clare in Ireland.
The name is Irish for grey rock, and writers also spell it Creag Liath.
The stone rises forty feet about one mile from the town of Killaloe.
Folk belief long held the rock to be the palace of the fairy Aibell.
Anglicized forms of the name include Crageevil, Craglee, and Craiglee.
The place carries rich associations in Irish folklore.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Loyw is a variant spelling of Caerlloyw. The straight form Caerlloyw gives the English town of Gloucester in Welsh speech. The city carried that name down the centuries. So Caer Loyw and Caerlloyw both stand for the same place. The word presents Gloucester as a fortified town with an old past. Welsh writers used the shorter form to save the same title. The city kept its place in the local tongue for ages. Caer Loyw thus offers a second road to one same Gloucester.

:::
```

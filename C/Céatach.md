::: magazinestyle
Céatach is a variant spelling of Céadach. The same folklore figure sits behind both shapes of the name, the quarrelsome would-be companion of the Fianna. Readers should follow the Céadach entry for his tales. The name belongs to the folklore of Ireland and to Scottish Gaelic tradition.

:::

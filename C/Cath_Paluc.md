``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cath Paluc, Balug or Balwg is a huge and monstrous cat from Welsh tradition. The name is Welsh for Palug's cat. It is said to haunt the island of Mona, today's Anglesey, more than anywhere else. The Triads record that nine score warriors fell as food for the beast. Monaghan's account gives the creature a still sharper appetite of 180 men at every meal. Chalapu of Arthurian tradition may be modelled on this cat. Kindred beings endure in Scottish lore as the Cat Sith. The tale is sometimes read as a trace of an old cult of the cat.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Highlanders called these fairy beasts crodh mara, the cattle of the sea. The name is Scottish Gaelic. The cattle dwelt in the ocean deeps and belonged to the fairies. They haunted fresh and salt water along the west coast of Scotland. The cattle could leave the sea and pair with ordinary cows. Their bulls mated with mortal cows, and the pairing bettered the herds of the land. The cattle carried no horns. Most wore a dun coat. Animals near Skye ran red and speckled, though people there described them as black. These sea cattle were gentler kin of the each uisce, the water horse. Welsh storytellers knew kindred beasts as Gwartheg y Llyn.

:::
```

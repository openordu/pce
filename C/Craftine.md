``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Craftine is a variant spelling of Craiphtine, the famous harper of early Irish story.
MacKillop lists Craftine and Craftiny as written forms of that name.
Tradition credits Craiphtine with the discovery of the harp.
His music cured a king of dumbness and lulled a garrison to sleep.
Readers should follow the Craiphtine entry for the full account.

:::
```

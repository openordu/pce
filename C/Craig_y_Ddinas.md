::: magazinestyle
Craig y Ddinas is a Welsh place-name meaning rock of the city or fort.
The name belongs to two separate ruins in Wales.
One is a Roman camp seven miles south-west of Caernarfon in Gwynedd.
The other is a British fort five miles north of Barmouth, in the old county of Merionethshire.
The name carries Arthurian associations in Welsh tradition.
One ruin is held to be the burial site of the faithless Blodeuwedd.

:::

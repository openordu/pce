``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caladbolg is the lightning sword of early Irish legend. A line of heroes once bore this blade. The noble Fergus mac Roch carried it with great fame. With his stroke he cut the tops from three hills in Meath. Commentary reads the weapon as a forerunner of Excalibur. The famous sword of King Arthur follows the same pattern. This blade takes its name from the words for the hard. It remains a bright sign of the old heroic age.

:::
```

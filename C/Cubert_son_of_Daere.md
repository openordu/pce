``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cubert son of Daere is a hero of Welsh story. Culhwch names him to Arthur in the long roll of heroes in Culhwch ac Olwen. The mention sits inside the famous court list of the tale. Modern commentators suggest Cubert may match the Irish lord Cú Roí mac Dáiri. Patrick Sims-Williams published that comparison in a study of 1982.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colofn Cymry is Welsh for the pillar of Wales or Cambria. The phrase is an epithet of Caradog, the ancient British chieftain whom Roman writers called Caractacus. MacKillop records the Welsh praise-title under its own headword and points to the hero's entry. See Caradog.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Celliwig is a variant spelling of Kelliwic, also written Kelliwec. The word derives from a Cornish term for woodland. The Welsh Triads place Kelliwic among the three tribal thrones of Britain that Arthur holds. It is the only such seat located in Cornwall. The remaining thrones stand in Wales at St David's and in Scotland at Pen Rhionydd. Cornish tradition names the site often, yet its exact spot is unknown. Likely candidates include Castle Killibury or else Kelly Rounds, a hill-fort of Iron Age date. The town of Callington is another possibility.

:::

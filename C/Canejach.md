``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Canejach is an English spelling of the Scottish name Caointeach. The fuller Gaelic form is Caointeach. Both names carry the sense of wailer or weeper. Caointeach belongs to the family of the banshee. The Scottish Highlands gave her her home. Her loud cry told the people that a death drew near. Before a great disaster she wept for days on end. One old tale binds her to the Macdonalds. She mourned them ahead of the terrible massacre at Glencoe. On other nights she washed the clothes of the doomed. Any gift would make her vanish and end her warning. Folk therefore refused to reward her. The name Canejach collects all these same tales.

:::
```

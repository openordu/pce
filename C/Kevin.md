::: magazinestyle
Kevin is the common English form of the name Cáemgen. At least two saints bear the name. The better-known is the founder and abbot of Glendalough. He died in the early seventh century, and his feast day is 3 June. He was celebrated for his chastity. In one story he resists a temptress by falling into a bed of nettles. In another he kicks her out of his cave, so that she falls into a lake and drowns. The Irish forms of the name include Cáemgen, Cóemgen, Cáomgen, and the modern Caoimhín. The name also appears in a number of Fenian stories.

:::

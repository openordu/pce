::: magazinestyle
Cleena is the anglicized spelling of Clidna, an Irish goddess famed for great beauty. The goddess belongs to Munster lore and is linked with County Cork. Tradition tells how she drowned in Glandore harbour. The wave that took her is remembered as Tonn Chliodhna, the wave of Clidna. Munster tradition also honours Clidna as a fairy queen. MacKillop treats Cleena as a cross-reference. Readers should turn to Clidna for the main article on this goddess.

:::

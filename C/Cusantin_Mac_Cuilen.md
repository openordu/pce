``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cusantín mac Cuilén, also called Constantine III, was king of Alba for only a year and a half. He sat on the throne in 995. His kinsmen and rivals slew him before the reign ended. He was the last ruler of Alba from the line descended from Aer. His killing cleared the way for the newly raising family. This family, the Clann Ruaidrí, later produced Macbeth. Even a short reign settled the dating of genealogies. His own genealogy runs back to Cinaed mac Ailpén. It links to the branch carries his descendants.

:::
```

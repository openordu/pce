``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clontarf is a mythological location on the shore of Dublin Bay. The Irish name cluain tarbh means bull meadow. There Brian Bórama broke the power of the Danes on Good Friday of 1014. The clash became a landmark of Irish memory, retold in story and verse across the centuries. Clontarf now forms a northern suburb of the Dublin conurbation.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Collen is a legendary seventh-century Welsh saint and the eponym of Llangollen. Tradition says he visited fairyland. He had served as abbot at Glastonbury, then resigned and lived as a hermit nearby. Two men spoke of Annwfn, which he called infernal, and he rebuked them. They invited him to see the realm for himself. There he refused the fairy food and denounced the place. He dashed the whole company with holy water. The scene vanished, and he found himself alone. Motifs D2031, F160.0.2, and F382.2 attach to the tale.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cethern mac Fintain is a warrior of the Táin Bó Cuailnge. The name also appears spelled Finntain. He fights on the Connacht side during the great Cooley cattle raid. Stories praise him as a generous man with a bloody blade. His hair is silver-grey, and he wields no weapon beyond a silver spike. Wounds leave him so broken that the Ulster troops haul him to their camp. He distrusts the healers who come to treat him. Their grim reports anger him, so he kills them, fifteen to fifty men in all. The Ulstermen then hear him out as he recounts each hurt. Medb dealt him one of those wounds. Cúchulainn restores him using marrow from bones plus ribs taken from animals. He rides back into the fight, kills more Ulstermen, and finally falls. In the Fenian Cycle the same figure teaches the young Fionn mac Cumhaill. His father is Fintan mac Néill.

:::
```

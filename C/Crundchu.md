::: magazinestyle
Crundchu, also written Crunnchu, is a variant spelling of Crunniuc. That man was the mortal husband whom the goddess Macha chose for herself. His boast that his wife could outrun any horse forced her to race while heavy with child. Her curse after the contest left the warriors of Ulster helpless in their hour of need. Readers should follow the Crunniuc entry for the whole tale.

:::

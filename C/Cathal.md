``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cathal is an Irish name given to men.
The form Cahal appears as a variant spelling.
The name means strong in battle.
It spread widely through early medieval Ireland.
Genealogies record hundreds of historical men who bore it.
Imaginative narrative seldom gives the name to a character.
English speakers often render it as Charles, a habit with no shared root behind it.

:::
```

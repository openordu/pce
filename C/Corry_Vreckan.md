::: magazinestyle
Corry Vreckan is a variant form of Corrievreckan, the great Gaelic whirlpool name.
MacKillop lists this spelling as a direct variant of that entry.
The name belongs to two whirlpools, one between Jura and Scarba, one at Rathlin Island.
Readers should follow the Corrievreckan entry for the full account.

:::

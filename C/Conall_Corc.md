``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conall Corc is another name for Corc mac Luigthig, the ancestor of the kings of Cashel. MacKillop prints the headword under the spelling Conall Core and gives a one-line cross-reference. The same figure also appears under the form Conall mac Luigthig. All these names belong to one man, the Munster ancestor-king. Readers should follow the pointer to the main article on Corc mac Luigthig.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Croghan is a variant spelling of Cruachain, the great royal fortress of Connacht. The sagas make it the seat of queen Medb and king Ailill in the cattle raid of Cooley. Its older site is identified with Rathcroghan near Tulsk in Co. Roscommon. Readers should follow the Cruachan entry for the full dossier of the fortress.

:::
```

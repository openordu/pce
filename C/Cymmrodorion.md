``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion was a Welsh society based in London. Richard Morris founded it in 1751. It declared itself patriotic and charitable. It set out to restore the literary heritage of Wales. Early meetings ran in Welsh with a spirit of unity and fairness. Its first phase closed in 1785. The society revived in 1820, then again in 1873. It has lasted continuously since then. Its members founded a university college at Aberystwyth in 1872. A royal charter in 1951 encouraged literature and science for Wales. The word cymmrodorion means 'aborigines' or 'original inhabitants'

:::
```

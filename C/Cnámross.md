``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cnámross (Cnámhros) names a battle that the Book of Leinster records. Bresal Bélach marched the Leinstermen against Cairbre Lifechair, king of Tara. Bresal killed both the king and the king's son Eochu Doimlén. Fionn mac Cumhaill and the Fianna fought on the victors' side. The story contrasts with the better-known Cath Gabhra, where Cairbre destroys the Fianna's power in Ireland. The site matches the modern hamlet of Camross in County Laois. The Lebor Gabála Érenn sets a second defeat at Cnámross. There the Fomorians crushed the Nemedians in their final battle, before the survivors fled Ireland. That loss stands apart from the Fenian fight at the same place.

:::
```

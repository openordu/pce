``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cullan is an anglicized spelling of Culann. The name belongs to the smith of the Ulster Cycle whose hound died at Sétanta's hand. The young hero then took the smith's name upon himself as Cúchulainn. This smith also forged the weapons of King Conchobar. Readers should follow the Culann entry.

:::
```

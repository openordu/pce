``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcomroe is a tribal name from north Co. Clare. It survives in a barony and in the ruins of a Cistercian abbey founded in 1180. The abbey stands two miles south of the Burren and five miles west of Kinvara. Stories set in the district do not always carry Christian senses. In the voyage tale of Máel Dúin, the hero's first hunt for his father's killers brings him to a druid here.

:::
```

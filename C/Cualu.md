``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cualu is the early name of a territory in Leinster. It stretched from the River Liffey south to Arklow, an area close to modern County Wicklow. The district held the monastic centre of Glendalough. Its people, the Cualainn, appear in Ptolemy's record of the second century. The name also shapes two district terms. Crích Cualann marks the district of Cualu. Slige Chualann marks the road to Cualu.

:::
```

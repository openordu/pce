``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Irish legend presents Cainnlech as a daughter of Gamgelta. Her name may mean shining or lustrous in the old tongue. She became the foster-mother of Cormac Connloinges. This boy rose to stature in early narrative. The slaying of Cormac Connloinges broke her heart. That death drove her from this world through pure grief. Her sorrow proved more than she could bear. Cainnlech thus fades away beside the one she raised. Her tale joins motherly love to loss in Ireland.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Comrac Liadaine ocus Chuirithir is the Irish title of a 9th-century poem. English readers usually call it The Meeting of Liadain and Cuirithir. The title names the two lovers of the tale, Liadan and Cuirithir. Their story of a poet and the man who loved her became one of the most quoted love tragedies of early Irish literature. MacKillop sends readers to his main article on Liadain for the full account. See Liadan.

:::

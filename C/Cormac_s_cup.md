``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac's cup is a golden vessel in the tale Echtrae Cormaic. A mysterious stranger gives it to Cormac mac Airt and is later shown to be Manannán mac Lir. The cup breaks apart when a lie is spoken near it. It welds itself whole again when truth is told. The host uses it to prove that Eithne, Ailbe, and Cairbre are faithful. Cormac keeps the cup and judges falsehood with it for the rest of his reign. After his death the cup leaves Ireland and is never seen again.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac mac Carthaig ruled Munster in the twelfth century. He backed the raising of Cormac's Chapel, the Romanesque church at Cashel, and later generations kept his name upon it. His enemies killed him in 1138. The chapel still stands on the Rock of Cashel.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Caesair is a spelling variant of Cesair. In the Mythological Cycle Cesair leads the initial large invasion of Ireland. The Book of Invasions places her story just before the Flood. Her father Bith names Noah as his own sire. She leaves her country in shame because the Ark refused her aboard. She comes ashore at Dun na Bantry, in Bantry Bay, with fifty women and three men. The men divide the women, hoping to grow a people for the land. Her helmsman Ladra dies, and Fintan turns himself into a salmon. Land is left for Fintan alone. Cesair ends her life broken-hearted.

:::

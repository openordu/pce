``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caoimhín is the modern spelling of the Irish saint whom English speakers call Kevin. The older Irish form is Cáemgen. Kevin founded the great monastery at Glendalough and served as its abbot. He lived from about 498 to 618 and kept his feast-day on 3 June. The saint drew lasting praise for his strict chastity. One legend says he refused a temptress so firmly that her plunge into the water proved fatal.

:::
```

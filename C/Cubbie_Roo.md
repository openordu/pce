::: magazinestyle
Cubbie Roo is the name of a ruined castle on the island of Wyre in Orkney. Builders raised it in the twelfth century from stone. It counts among the oldest medieval buildings that still stand in Scotland. Modern commentators identify the castle's name with Kolbein Hruga, a chieftain of the Orkney Saga.

:::

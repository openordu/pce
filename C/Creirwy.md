``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Creirwy is the daughter of Tegid the Bald and the enchantress Ceridwen. A Welsh source praises her as the loveliest maiden on earth. Her brothers form the opposite pair in that family. Afagddu is the ill-favoured one, and Morfran is the handsome hero of legend. Ceridwen's cauldron of wisdom shapes their story, since its drops miss Morfran and reach Gwion Bach. Readers who follow that boy find he becomes the bard Taliesin.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conar is a king of Ireland in Macpherson's Ossian, first printed in 1760. He fathered the Cormac whom Carbar dethroned. Kinship with Fingal also ties him to that hero. The bond drags the Scottish champion into many Irish wars. Macpherson shaped his Ossianic figures as literary inventions. They do not preserve genuine medieval tradition. Conar corresponds to the Conaire of native Irish story. See Conaire.

:::
```

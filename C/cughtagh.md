``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cughtagh is a cave-dwelling spirit of the Isle of Man. In older Manx folklore the creature stood apart. Tradition now merges it with the buggane. Some scholars link cughtagh to the Scottish Gaelic duthach as a regional parallel. The figure survives as a cave-haunting being in Manx oral memory.

:::
```

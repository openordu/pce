``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coluinn gan Ceann is a variant spelling of Colann gun Cheann, the Scottish Gaelic name of the headless spirit. Highland tales give the formula as body without head. MacKillop files this spelling as a pointer to his article on the bòcan figure. See Colann gun Cheann.

:::
```

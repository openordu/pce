::: magazinestyle
Coluinn gan Ceann is a Scottish Gaelic name-form for the headless spirit. The phrase means body without head. MacKillop files this spelling under the bòcan article. The Irish entry gan ceann carries the same name and the variant gan-ceann. The term translates from Irish as without a head. It names a headless and somewhat threatening Irish fairy. The gan ceann ranks as a figure comparable to the dullahan. Unlike other fairies, the gan ceann fears gold. A gan ceann chased a boat heading for America across the sea. A pin of gold tossed into his path broke the chase. The gan ceann is sometimes confused with the ganconer. MacKillop treats them as very different.

:::

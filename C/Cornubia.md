::: magazinestyle
Cornubia is an ancient British kingdom of Armorica, the Breton peninsula, known from Roman times. Its lands match the later kingdom of Cornouaille and ran south from the River Elorn to the Elle. The name also serves, less often, as a latinized form for early Cornwall.

:::

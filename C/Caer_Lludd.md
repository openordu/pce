::: magazinestyle
Caer Lludd is an early Welsh name for London. The name echoes a belief that Lludd himself raised the city ramparts once more. It placed the ancient British capital under the watch of its own founder. Early Welsh writers carried the name to keep the Roman past near. They showed Lludd as a king who shaped the very ground of the town. The name then bound one fortress to a founding tale. Such a story stayed alive in British memory for a long time. Caer Lludd stands as an older and proud title for London.

:::

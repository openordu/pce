::: magazinestyle
Hector Munro Chadwick and Nora K. Chadwick were two British scholars. They worked as a married research pair. Hector studied Anglo-Saxon and Norse matters. Nora studied early Celtic history and poetry. Their big joint work is The Growth of Literature. The Chadwicks helped shape the study of early cultures. Their view of north-west Europe left a lasting mark. The Cambridge department follows their lead. Hector wrote on the heroic age and national identity. Nora wrote on Celtic Britain and the Druids. Their books still guide students today.

:::

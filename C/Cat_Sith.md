``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Cat Sith is the fairy cat of Scottish Gaelic tradition. This creature matched a dog in size and bore black fur with one white spot on its breast. Its back arched and its bristles stood erect. Highlanders most often judged it a transformed witch rather than a fairy at all. Others still counted the huge dark animal a fairy in disguise. The cat sith may connect with the demonic cat called Big Ears. Conjurors summoned that creature in the taghairm, a brutal divination ceremony. In the taghairm, participants roasted cats alive for four days until the spirit spoke. Irish tradition treated black cats more kindly and held them lucky. A black cat's blood served as a folk cure for erysipelas, the ailment called St Anthony's fire. The Highland cat therefore belongs to a wider Celtic lore of fateful felines.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cai is a variant spelling of the Welsh hero named Cei. He rides with King Arthur and the good Bedwyr. Cai holds the post of seneschal to the royal court. His temper blends bold courage with plain jest. The Black Book of Carmarthen hails him a hero. That twelfth-century book prints his brave deed. Later stories turn Cai into a cross house steward. That steward shows cruel and silly habits. The same Latin name also marks an Irish judge. That judge of the Milesians read Moses' law while in Egypt. The separate figures stay apart in the first books. The Latin root Caius unites the shared names. The knight Sir Kay springs from this older man.

:::
```

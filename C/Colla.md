``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colla is an Irish name, perhaps meaning great lord or chief, borne by shadowy figures of Ireland's early centuries. The fourth-century brothers were Colla Uais, the noble one, and Colla Menn, the notable or stammering one. Colla Fo Chrith, also called Dachrich, completed the trio. Eochu Doimlén fathered all three. Their grandfather was Cairbre Lifechair. They murdered their uncle Fiachu, then founded the kingdom of Airgialla in Ireland's north-east. They attacked Emain Macha, ending its political role. Clan Donald and the MacDonald Lords of the Isles claimed descent from Colla Uais and Conn of the Hundred Battles. Colla Uais, tradition says, held Scotland before Dál Riada arrived. T. F. O'Rahilly deemed Colla a nickname for three sons of Niall Noigiallach: Conall Gulban, Énna, and Eógan.

:::
```

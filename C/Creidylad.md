``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Creidylad is a variant spelling of Creiddylad, the maiden of Welsh legend.
MacKillop records Creuddylad, Creudylad, and Kreiddylat as further forms.
Her story sets Gwyn ap Nudd and Gwythyr fab Greidawl fighting for her each May Day.
Readers should follow the Creiddylad entry for the full account.

:::
```

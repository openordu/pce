``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cigfa, whom some call Kigva, was a Welsh heroine or goddess in the Mabinogi, the medieval body of Welsh prose tales. That work names Gwyn Gohoyw as her father. She wed Pryderi, the child of goddess Rhiannon and her mate Pwyll. Her story belongs to the third branch. There a spell trapped her husband with his mother inside a magical Fairy Mound. After Pryderi vanished, Cigfa lived chastely with Rhiannon's second husband, Manawydan. She feared his advances in that uneasy season. Even so she stayed faithful, holding to her missing mate while Manawydan searched for a way to break the spell on their spouses. In the end he found a path to lift the enchantment, freed the captives, and let their household settle once more.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Credne is the commonest spelling of the divine artificer whom this encyclopedia writes as Creidne.
MacKillop heads his article Credne, Creidne, Creidhne together.
The god worked in bronze, brass, and gold beside Goibniu the smith and Luchta the wright.
Readers should follow the Creidne entry for the full account.

:::

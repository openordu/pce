``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Carnac is one of the most spacious archaeological sites in any land of the Celtic world. The field rests beside the sea in southern Brittany, near the village of Auray. Long rows of upright megalithic blocks extend almost two miles in an east-west path. Some of the larger standing stones reach eleven to thirteen feet in height. Breton oral tradition calls the place Ty Charriguet, the House of the Gorics. That title recalls the gnomes held to haunt the region. Another old tale gives the credit to Komeli, a pseudo-saint. He made the monuments by turning his own pursuers into stone.

:::
```

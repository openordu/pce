``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cynddylan fab Cyndrwyn was a Welsh prince of Powys in the 7th century. He fought as an ally of Penda, king of Mercia. In 642 his side slew Oswald of Northumbria at the battle of Maserfelth. Cynddylan fell on 15 November 655, most likely at the battle of Winwæd. Two poems record him. Marwnad Cynddylan is a 71-line elegy sent to a king of Gwynedd. Canu Heledd is a cycle of 113 carvings spoken in the grief of his sister Heledd. The cycle shows a land ruined after his fall. His kinsmen, the Cyndrwynyn, do not appear among the kings of Powys after the 9th century. Later rulers there traced descent from the line of Cadell.

:::
```

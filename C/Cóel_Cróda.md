``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cóel Cróda is a variant spelling of the name Coil Cróda. The same warrior sits behind both shapes of the name. He belongs to the Fianna of Fionn mac Cumhaill and carries the byname Hundred Slayer. Readers should follow the Coil Cróda entry for his record. The name itself may echo a word for bravery or for cruelty.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
The cú síth is a Scottish mythological being. It is the fairy dog of the Highlands. MacKillop calls it the fearsome fairy dog of Gaelic Scotland. Other fairy creatures were black or red with white ears, but the cú síth was not. Among the fairy dogs of Celtic belief, this one alone wore dark green. It stood as tall as a small bull, with the bulk of a yearling bullock. The coat is shaggy. Each paw spreads as wide as a grown hand. The dog left enormous footprints, each the size of a large man's foot. Each journey held to one straight line. It crossed the world seeking prey and would chase other dogs too. The motif carries the index number F241.6.

:::

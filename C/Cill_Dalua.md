``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cill Dalua is an Irish place-name that means the church of a man named Dalua. The name lives on in Killaloe, the modern town that stands on the Shannon in County Clare, Ireland. That town grew up around a monastery linked with St Flannan. MacKillop treats this headword as a cross-reference to his entry on Killaloe.

:::
```

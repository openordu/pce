``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caradog, a son of Cynfelyn, was an ancient British chieftain. The Welsh claim him as a national hero. The Romans wrote his name as Caractacus. He drilled with the Silures so the west of Britain could hold against Rome. He led his people in battle across eight years under the emperor Claudius. Beaten on a Shropshire hillside, he ran to Cartimandua, queen of the Brigantes. That queen handed him over to her Roman captors. Brought to Rome, his pride so pleased the emperor that he gave a pardon and freedom. In Welsh lore his name stands for bravery and high standing. He earned the title the Pillar of Wales.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caerlloyw is the Welsh name for the English city of Gloucester. The name also appears in the related spellings Caer Lloyw, Loyw, and Caer Loyw. The dictionary records all of these forms for Gloucester, a cathedral city on the River Severn in western England. Each spelling rests on the word caer, meaning a fortified place or fortress. The city itself began as a Roman settlement, and the Welsh name keeps that sense of a stronghold. Caerlloyw thus names the Roman town that later grew into a city. Writers of the Middle Ages carried this Welsh name for Gloucester down through their accounts of Britain.

:::
```

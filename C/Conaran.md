``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conaran is an obscure Irish figure. One telling makes her a goddess and mother of three magician daughters. Those daughters coveted the mighty warriors of the Fianna. They wove a magic net that snared the heroes. The warriors killed all three in the end. Other tales make Conaran a male figure, a Tuatha Dé Danann chief. The Fenian Cycle keeps that male Conarán at the centre of one famous episode. His three ugly daughters punish Fionn mac Cumhaill and a few comrades for hunting. The girls use three holly-sticks to trap the men inside a cave. Fionn's old enemy Goll mac Morna comes to the rescue. He kills two sisters and forces the last one to free the Fenians. When she later tries to avenge her kin, Goll kills her as well. A grateful Fionn then gives Goll his daughter's hand. Some versions name the sisters Camóg, Cúilín, and Iamach. The goddess tale and the cave tale read best as variant tellings of one cycle.

:::
```

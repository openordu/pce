``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cador ranks as the duke of Cornwall in the tale Breuddwyd Rhonabwy, which is the Dream of Rhonabwy. He is a nephew of the great king Arthur. Cador appears often in the Arthurian chronicles written by Geoffrey of Monmouth. The poets Wace and Layamon also tell of his deeds. He dies in the final battle at Mount Badon. His long reign then comes to a close.

:::
```

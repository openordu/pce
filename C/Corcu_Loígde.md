``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcu Loígde, also written Lóegde, Laigde, and Laoidhe, is a people of the far south-west of Co. Cork. They lived with the River Bandon on one side and the sea on the other. That coast is the southernmost area of Ireland. The ancient geographer Ptolemy, writing in the 2nd century AD, may have named them as the Iverni. They led power in south Munster until the Eóganacht rose. The pseudo-history Lebor Gabála Érenn makes them descend from Lugaid son of Dáire, and so from Ith.

:::
```

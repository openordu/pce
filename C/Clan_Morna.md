``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Clan Morna is the name for the followers of the warrior Goll Mac Morna. They long opposed the Clan Baíscne, the rival band that fought for Fionn Mac Cumhaill. After many a skirmish the two enemy clans came together to shape the band called the Fianna. In the Fenian record Goll mac Morna leads the band, and James Macpherson's Poems of Ossian gives it a counterpart named Morna.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Creuddylad and Creudylad are Welsh spellings of Creiddylad. MacKillop lists both forms at one short entry. Creiddylad is the maiden for whom Gwyn ap Nudd and Gwythyr fab Greidawl fight each May Day. Readers should follow the Creiddylad entry for the story and the seasonal battle.

:::
```

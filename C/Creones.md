::: magazinestyle
Creones is a variant spelling of Cerones, a people of ancient Scotland. MacKillop points the reader to the Picts entry for the wider picture. The form belongs to the class of tribal names Roman writers recorded in northern Britain. The spelling shows how scribes moved between letters across the old sources. Students of the Pictish kingdoms treat the two names as one people.

:::

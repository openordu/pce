``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caradog of Llancarfan was a Welsh historian of the twelfth century. People sometimes give him the patronymic ap Llefoed. Records list him as a peer of Geoffrey of Monmouth. If he carried on the work of Geoffrey, that text has not survived. Scholars think he wrote the Latin Vita Gildae. That Life of Gildas holds a great deal of Arthurian lore. He takes his name from the settlement of Llancarfan in Glamorganshire. His work links this village to the matter of Britain.

:::
```

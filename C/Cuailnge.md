``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuailnge is an early name for the hilly peninsula of Cooley in north-east Louth, a corner of Leinster. The early form also appears as Cualnge, and later writers anglicize it as Cooley or Quelgny. Carlingford Lough bounds it on one side, and Dundalk harbour bounds it on the other. The name leads the title of the great Ulster epic, the Táin bó Cuailnge. Much of that epic plays out on this ground. Its great tale is Cúchulainn, who held Ulster alone against the hosts of Connacht. An older leaf in the source keeps one strange deed for the place. It tells of the seer Amairgin, whose force piled the dead about him.

:::
```

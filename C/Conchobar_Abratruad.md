``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conchobar Abratruad is an early king of the Lagin, the stock from whom the Leinstermen of eastern Ireland descend. His byname means of the red eyelashes. Early Irish pedigrees tangle him with Conchobar mac Nessa, the far better-known Ulster king. Genealogists long blurred the two. He stands apart in the PCE. The article on the Ulster king sits at Concobar mac Nessa.

:::
```

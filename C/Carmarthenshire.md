::: magazinestyle
Carmarthenshire is a maritime county of south Wales. It held that role until 1974. The region now forms the easternmost third of Dyfed. In Welsh the area takes the name Caerfyrddin. An older English form carries the same meaning as Carmarthenshire. The county town Carmarthen lends its name to the district. Much of the land runs along the sea coast. The Black Book of Carmarthen ties the area to early Welsh tradition. That manuscript is an important early witness. It preserves old poetry and prophecy from the region.

:::

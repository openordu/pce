``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cath Godeu is a corrupt spelling that stands for Cad Goddeu, the ancient Welsh battle-poem. English readers call the piece The Battle of the Trees. It survives in the Book of Taliesin, a thirteenth-century manuscript. The poem stages a war between Arawn, lord of Annwfn, and the ploughman Amaethon. Their quarrel began over three stolen creatures: Amaethon took a roebuck. He also seized a whelp and a lapwing. Gwydion, a master of magic, turns the trees into warriors by his enchanted staff. The poem's larger purpose still puzzles scholars. Robert Graves reshuffled its lines to serve his theory, and experts rejected that reading.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruithin, Cruithni is an Old Irish group name. It names the Picts of north Britain. A separate people, likely of British stock, bore the name on the northern and eastern coasts of Ulster in early medieval times. The chief kingdom of these Irish people was Dál nAraide, seated in the south of County Antrim. Congal Claen ruled as overking of Ulaid and fell in the fighting at Mag Roth in 637. The name ties to the Welsh Prydyn, meaning the Picts, and to the old name of Britain itself.

:::
```

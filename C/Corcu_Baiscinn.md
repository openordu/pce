``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcu Baiscinn, also spelled Baiscind and Bascinn, is a people of early Ireland. They settled north of the Shannon estuary, on ground now in Co. Clare. Their reach took in the county's south-western peninsula. They held the ground from the earliest records up to the 12th century. Sailors of the people were famous. The poet James Liddy gave one of his collections the title Corea Bascinn, and it appeared in Dublin in 1977.

:::
```

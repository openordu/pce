``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcu, also written Corco and Corea, is an Old Irish word for population groups. By extension it names the territories such groups held. A close English rendering is clan. MacKillop notes the word inside many people-names, among them Corcu Baiscinn, Corcu Duibne, and Corcu Loígde.

:::
```

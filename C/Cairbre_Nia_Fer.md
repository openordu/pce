``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre Nia Fer rules as king of Tara. He fights Cúchulainn through the Ulster Cycle. A quarrel over Conchobar's daughter drives their feud. That daughter carries the name Fedelm Noichride. Cairbre Nia Fer comes to marry her. Cúchulainn spurns Emer's older sister for her sake. Emer's sister steps aside and yields the man. At Ros na Righ Cúchulainn ends Cairbre with speed. The hero Erc then faces Cúchulainn to avenge that kill. Cairbre is famed then for his twelve daughters. His full patronymic stands as mac Rosa Ruaid. Find File serves as his brother and king of Leinster.

:::
```

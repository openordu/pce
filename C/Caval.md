``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caval is a spelling that the poet Tennyson used for Cabal. Cabal is King Arthur's hound. Other spellings of the name include Cafall and Cavall. Tennyson carried the beast into his Arthurian verse. Readers who meet Caval in that poetry can trace the dog back to the older tradition.

:::
```

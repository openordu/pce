``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clachnahrie is the site of a fight between the clans Macintosh and Munro in 1454. The variant form Clachnaharry names the same place. Tradition traces the quarrel to a run of insults and supposed insults between the two clans. The clash happened close to Inverness. Generations of storytellers built a large Scottish Gaelic folklore tradition around the fight. Today the place forms a suburb on the north-western side of Inverness.

:::
```

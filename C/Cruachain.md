``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruachain is one of the leading forms of the name Cruachan, the great fortress of the west province. The sagas set it as the seat of queen Medb and king Ailill in the Táin Bó Cuailnge. Its site is identified with Rathcroghan near Tulsk in Co. Roscommon. Tradition calls the cave at that site the Hells Gate of Ireland. Readers should follow the Cruachan entry for the full dossier.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cahir More is the English equivalent of the Gaelic name Cathair Mór. The Cathair Mór name belonged to a legendary king of the Leinstermen. That king ruled at the head of the realm in the far past. Conn Cétchathach was the lord of the Hundred Battles in time. Some other stories give that deed to Goll mac Morna. His daughter later took the hand of king Cormac mac Airt. Another son of his house took the name Failge. Failge in turn lent his name to the Irish county Offaly. The English word Charles is sometimes used in place of his name. Yet Cathair Mór and Charles hold no link to one another.

:::
```

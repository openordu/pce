``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clettig is the sidh, or enchanted residence, of the figure Elcmar. The name appears in the genitive case in place-names like Sidh Clettig and Sidh Chlettigh. Its nominative form is Clettech, sometimes written Cletech. The residence stands on the Boyne's southern bank, close to the pool called Linn Féic. In the tales, Elcmar holds Clettig before handing it over in exchange for Brug na Bóinne. The recipient of that trade is Angus Óg. Later tradition also names Clettig as a residence of Muirchertach mac Erca.

:::
```

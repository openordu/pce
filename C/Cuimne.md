``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuimne is the hag who helps Mongán win back his wife Dubh Lacha. King Brandub coveted Dubh Lacha and tricked Mongán into giving her up. Mongán, a son of the sea-god Manannán mac Lir, sought the hag's aid. Cuimne changed herself into a beautiful woman, in one telling a Munster princess with a love-spot on her cheek. She offered herself as the price of the wife's freedom. Once Dubh Lacha went free, Cuimne returned to her old shape. That reversal of a hag's transformation is rare in the tales.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Collumcille is an Anglo-Irish rendering of the saint's name, one that W. B. Yeats and like-minded authors put into print. Readers meeting the form in English verse are really looking at Gaelic material through an anglicised lens. MacKillop counts it with the numerous spellings gathered under Colum Cille and points anyone arriving here onward to that entry. See Colum Cille.

:::
```

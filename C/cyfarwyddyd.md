``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cyfarwyddyd names the office of guidance, instruction, information, and knowledge in medieval Welsh literary tradition. The role linked recitation, exposition, and narrative memory across court and assembly. Constance Bullock-Davies studied the office through her work on Professional Interpreters of the Matter of Britain, published in Cardiff in 1966. Proinsias Mac Cana placed related practice in Learned Tales of Medieval Ireland, issued in Dublin in 1980. Patrick K. Ford set out the poet as cyfarwydd in an article in Studia Celtica, volumes 10 and 11, dated 1975-6.

:::
```

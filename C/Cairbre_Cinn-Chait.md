``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Lebor Gabala names Cairbre Cinn-Chait as a ruler whom the Aithech-Túatha set in power. When those plebeian tribes topple the Milesians they appoint him to lead. Rare chronicles mark him as a usurper of the throne. His reign brings a hard famine across the land. Each stalk of wheat holds but one grain and every oak a single acorn. The rivers give no fish to the fishermen and the cattle stand milkless. Nature offers no approval of his wrongful rise to kingship. Even so the Érainn count him among their own ancestors. His son Morann returns Ireland to the Milesians after his death.

:::
```

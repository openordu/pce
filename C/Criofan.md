``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Criofan is a modern variant spelling of Crimthann. The older name means fox and was common in early Ireland. Kings, heroes of the Fenian band, and one saint carried it. MacKillop lists the form beside Crimthan, Criomthann, and Cremthann. Readers should follow the Crimthann entry for its famous bearers.

:::
```

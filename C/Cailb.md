::: magazinestyle
Cailb is an ugly female seer in an old Irish story. Her great tale concerns the fall and ruin of Da Derga. The famous death belongs to a ruler inside that story. Cailb announces the doom of King Conaire in the tale. The story draws her monstrous body in a harsh light. It leaves a crude hint about her form to the reader. Conaire first says that her name means little at all. She then stands upon the one foot and begins a chant. She lists thirty two names with which to name herself. That strange chant answers the slight of the king. So the loathsome seer brings the news of his end.

:::

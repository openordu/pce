``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Condatis is a British god. He was a minor water deity, and his shrines stood where rivers meet. That meeting of rivers was his sacred ground. His very name may mean waters meet. Worship of him is attested in Roman-occupied Britain. His cult centres on river confluences. The Tyne and the Tees shelter shrines of his, excavated between those rivers. More evidence comes from Northumberland, from Cumberland, and from Durham, all in northern England. His name survives in many place-names. Some of those names lie as far away as Gaul. The Romans equated him with Mars.

:::
```

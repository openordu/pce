::: magazinestyle
Ciamat is remembered in the Irish stories as a mistress of Cormac mac Airt, the famed high king. Her tale belongs to the Ulster Cycle of tradition. The name is Irish and carries the sense of dark lady. Her connection to Cormac places her among the court figures gathered around that king.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colcu, also Colga, is a name that several figures of old Ireland carried, saints among them. The most famous Colcu was stepson to the fairy queen Créd. He blocked her plan for a love affair with Cano, and the tale lives on in saga and in verse. See Créd.

:::
```

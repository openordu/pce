``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cearnach is the modern spelling of the old cognomen Cemach. The bearer is Conall Cernach, the famed Ulster hero. Modern writers render his byname in this present-day form. The word marks the same warrior whom the Ulster tales celebrate.

:::
```

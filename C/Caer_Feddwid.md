``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Feddwid is the Welsh name for a bewitching otherworld city. The name means city of carousal and court of intoxication. This city appears in Welsh narratives and in Welsh Arthurian stories. Caer Feddwid is a place of revelry and drunken feasts. Many readers compare it with the otherworld realm of Annwfn. Others link it to the distant fortress of Caer Siddi. Each name points to the same Celtic otherworld. Writers regard these fortress-city titles as very close in meaning.

:::
```

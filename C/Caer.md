``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer is the Welsh word for fort, castle, wall, or citadel. Writers apply this term across numerous Welsh place-names. Sometimes they anglicize the word as Kaer. Many entries in this dictionary begin with the syllable Caer. The dictionary arranges those entries letter by letter. This ordering holds even when Caer forms a compound prefix. It also holds when Caer stands alone as a separate word. Caer must not be confused with Cáer the swan maiden. That name belongs to Irish tradition and carries a different form.

:::
```

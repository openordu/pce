::: magazinestyle
Cnoc Fírinne is the hill of truth in Co. Limerick. The forms Cnoc Firinne and Cnoc Frigrend name it in the sources. English speakers know it as Knockfeerina or Knockfierna. The volcanic hill rises 949 feet, two miles east of Ballingarry. It ranks among the most famous otherworldly heights of Munster. An assembly met on its slopes at Lughnasa in old times. Tradition once seated a god named Donn Fírinne on the hill. Fenian narrative sets many episodes on and around it. Oral tradition kept the hill famous across the centuries.

:::

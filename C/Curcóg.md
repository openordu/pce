``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Curcóg counts as a daughter of the sea-god Manannán mac Lir. She often keeps company with Eithne, another figure of that Otherworld family. The dictionary also shows her marching at the head of a band of women to Brug na Bóinne. That Otherworld hall stands beside the Boyne. The record stops there, so her tale rests on this kinship and this one visit.

:::
```

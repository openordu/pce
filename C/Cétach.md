``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cétach is the Middle Irish spelling of the name Céadach. The same folklore figure sits behind both shapes of the name. He is the quarrelsome would-be companion of the Fianna in Irish and Scottish Gaelic tales. Readers should follow the Céadach entry for his stories. That entry keeps his disguises, his missions, and his great sail warning.

:::
```

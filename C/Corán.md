::: magazinestyle
Corán is a druid of Irish legend, remembered as the druid of Connla.
MacKillop gives him that single line of identity.
He served Connla, the son of the high king Conn.
The druid roundup in the same dictionary also names Corán among its most cited figures.
That list places him beside Cathbad, the most famous of all druids.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cúilín names a small lock or curl of hair at the nape of the neck. In early Ireland people wore the hair long and gathered it at the nape, and cúilín passed into occasional use as a poetic name for Ireland itself. English writers rendered it as Coolun or Cooleen. The shift may owe something to cúil-fhionn, a word for a fair-haired girl or any attractive person. Several minor female figures in Irish tradition carry the name. The anglicised variants Coolun and Cooleen appear often in older English-language sources.

:::
```

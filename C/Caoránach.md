``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caoránach (Keeronagh) is the name given to a monster. Saint Patrick drove this beast to Lough Derg. That lake lies in County Donegal. People saw the creature as female. They said she stood as mother to countless demons and devils. The same monster links to the muirdris and the olliphéist beliefs. Each of those tales tells of a water beast. So the Caoránach builds a bridge from one dread to the next. Her story binds Lough Derg to a deep old fear.

:::
```

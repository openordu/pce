::: magazinestyle
cerd is an Old Irish noun with two related senses. It means a craftsman or artisan, and it also names the handicraft, art, skill, accomplishment, or feat itself. The variant spelling caird points to the same word. See also the craftsman entry for fuller treatment of the figure in early Irish tradition.

:::

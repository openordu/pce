::: magazinestyle
Craddock is a variant spelling of the ancient British name Caradog.
MacKillop lists Craddock and Cradog as written forms of that name.
Caradog is the Welsh hero whom the Romans called Caractacus.
He led the west of Britain against the armies of Rome.
Readers should follow the Caradog entry for the full account.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cullen is an anglicized spelling of Cuilenn. That name belongs to the fairy-mound lord of Sliab Cuilinn, the hill now called Slieve Gullion in Co. Armagh. In the Fenian tales this mound lord makes the aged Fionn young again. The draught of renewal comes from a golden cup. The spelling shows how English scribes heard the Irish name. Readers should follow the Cuilenn entry for the lord's full record.

:::
```

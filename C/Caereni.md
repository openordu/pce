::: magazinestyle
Caereni were a group of the old Roman age. They formed one member of the Caledonian confederation. Their tribe held a district that matched north-west Sutherland. The county area went on intact until 1974 within the Highlands. They filled the far north-west corner of that land. Caereni stood as part of the greater northern alliance. Their name thus recalls a Caledonian folk of that region.

:::

::: magazinestyle
cath is an Irish word that simply means battle. It opens the title of many narrative texts and so marks their shared theme. Tales whose titles begin with cath are filed word by word rather than by initial letter alone. The modern Irish word descends from Old Celtic calu and sits beside the Welsh cad, which carries the same sense of battle.

:::

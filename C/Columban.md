``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Columban, also written Columbanus, was an Irish saint who died in 615. He carried monastic reform to the Continent. He founded the abbey of Luxeuil near Fontaine in France, and later Ebovium, the modern Bobbio in Italy. Some writers called him the Younger Columba. MacKillop warns that the title misleads. The Continental missionary must not be confused with Colum Cille, the older saint of Iona. His houses carried Irish learning deep into Europe.

:::
```

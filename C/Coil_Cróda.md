``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coil Cróda is a member of Fionn mac Cumhaill's Fianna. The variants Cóel Cróda and Cródha also name this warrior. He is sometimes called the Hundred Slayer. His name compares with modern Irish cródha, brave, valiant, heroic. That word comes from Old Irish cródacht, bloodthirstiness or cruelty.

:::
```

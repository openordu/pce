``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crimthann of Tara was raised as the ward of king Diarmait. Queen Becfhola took notice of his good looks. She offered a meeting on a Sunday, yet Crimthann refused to travel on that day. His unwillingness to move then cut their affair short.

:::
```

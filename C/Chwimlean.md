``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Chwimlean, also spelled Chwibleian, Chwimbian, and Hwimleian, is a companion of Myrddin, the prophet better known as Merlin. The Welsh sources give him no deeds of his own. Scholars treat him as a possible model for Arthurian figures like Viviane. A. O. H. Jarman made the scholarly case for that link. His study appeared in the Board of Celtic Studies' Bulletin, volume 16 (1955), pages 71-6.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corca, also written Corco, is a variant spelling of Corcu. The word is an Old Irish term for population groups, and by extension for the lands they held. A close English rendering is clan. Irish tribal and place names keep the form, as in the later peoples Corcu Baiscinn and Corcu Duibne.

:::
```

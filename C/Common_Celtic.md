``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Common Celtic names the oldest reconstructed Celtic speech. It is the shared ancestor of every attested Celtic tongue. Scholars compare the family to recover this stage. Reconstructed forms carry a star in print. Examples are *wiros for man and *fatir for father. The term matches Proto-Celtic in reach. Both label the same unattested proto-language. This stage relies on intra-Celtic comparison. A shared change dropped Indo-European p in most positions. Traces survive in Hercynia silva and the name UVAMO. Innovations after the split are called pan-Celtic.

:::
```

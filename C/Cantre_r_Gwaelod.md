::: magazinestyle
Cantre'r Gwaelod is a Welsh flood legend about a vanished lowland kingdom. The realm of Gwyddno Garanhir once stretched across what is now Cardigan Bay. Earlier tales call this sunken land Maes Gwyddno. There a well-maiden named Mererid let a spring overflow through neglect. The famous version from the early sixteenth century shows the king reigning over sixteen cities. An embankment fitted with sluices ringed this country. The drunken dike-keeper Seithenyn failed at his task and let the sea pour inward. Every dweller drowned except the king. Modern people still say the sunken church bell sounds on quiet evenings. The story also reaches Tyno Helig and echoes the Breton City of Ys.

:::

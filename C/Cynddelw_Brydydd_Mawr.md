::: magazinestyle
Cynddelw Brydydd Mawr was a main Welsh court poet. He worked from about 1155 until around 1195. He sang to the princes and heads of his day. He counts among the most learned bards of the age. He served as court poet in various Welsh kingdoms and praises leading men. He was active for about forty years. His name marks him as a master poet. His poetry covers praise and elegy for his noble lords. Some of his best lines are simple and direct, laced with irony.

:::

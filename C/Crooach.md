``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crooach, with its variant Crooagh, is an Anglicized form of either Crom Crúaich or Cruachain. A single spoken shape could point to the great idol of Mag Slécht. It could as easily point to the royal fortress of Connacht. Which of the two a writer meant depends on the story at hand. Readers should follow both the Crom Cruach and the Cruachan entries.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Camlan is a place of myth told in the Welsh tales. Writers also call it Camlann. The exact spot still stays unknown. King Arthur fought his last battle here, and he lost that fight to his own son and nephew, Mordred. After the peace broke at Mount Badon, the war is dated near 537 or 539. The Lady of the Lake then carried Arthur away into the Otherworld. Many places offer a home for the fight. Some set it by the Camel river near Camelford in Cornwall. Others name the Cam close to Cadbury. A few point to Salisbury, and others to the shores of Somerset. One option picks Camboglanna in the kingdom of Rheged. The site lies on the west of Hadrian's Wall and carries the name Birdoswald today.

:::
```

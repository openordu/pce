::: magazinestyle
Cúchulainn's Leap is a place-name attached to several sites across the Gaelic world. The chief Leap rock rises sheer-sided and rectangular at Loop Head in County Clare, above the Shannon mouth. The local story says Cúchulainn fled an importunate female there and made the jump she could not match. Other place-names recall the hero near Anascaul on the Dingle peninsula in Kerry. A bed, a house, and a grave there all bear his name. The Cuillin Hills of southern Skye carry the related form Cuchullin.

:::

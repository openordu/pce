::: magazinestyle
Carantoc was a Welsh saint of the sixth century. The Church once venerated him in Cardiganshire, now Dyfed. His feast day falls on 16 May. He founded the settlement of Llangrannog. He also travelled across Ireland in his lifetime. People in Brittany honoured him as Saint Karentec. His medieval hagiographers mixed in tales from secular story. These writers blended folk legend with the record of his deeds.

:::

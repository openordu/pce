::: magazinestyle
Cath Cnucha is the Irish title for the battle account of that name. The tale recounts a struggle that began at the hill of Cnucha. This heading points the reader to that fuller story. The full account of the war stays under the name Cnucha. That entry tells how the conflict at the hill was fought.

:::

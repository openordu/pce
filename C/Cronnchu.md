::: magazinestyle
Cronnchu is a variant spelling of Crunniuc. Crunniuc is the mortal man the goddess Macha chose for her own. He is the human figure of the tale behind the Debility of the Ulstermen. Macha's curse left the warriors of Ulster helpless with birth pangs in their hour of danger. Readers should follow the Crunniuc entry for his story with the goddess.

:::

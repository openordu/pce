``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cabadius is a Latinized variant of Cathbad. Cathbad was the chief druid of Conchobar mac Nessa's court. He stood as a leading figure of the Ulster Cycle. Cathbad foretold the fate of Deirdre before her birth. He also taught Cúchulainn. One day Cúchulainn overheard his words. Cathbad said a youth taking up arms on a set day would become champion of Ireland. That same youth would die young. The druid's daughter was Findchóem, the mother of Conall Cernach. Some versions name Cathbad the real father of Conchobar.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre Músc is the legendary ancestor of the Múscraige. That folk gave their title to the land of Muskerry. Muskerry covers several districts of Munster. A district east of Lough Derg borders the Shannon. The same figure also bears a second name. Later writers call him Angus Músc. Older scribes set the title down as Óengus Músc. Every chieftain of Muskerry may trace his blood back to this man.

:::
```

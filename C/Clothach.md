::: magazinestyle
Clothach stands in Irish legend as a grandson of the Dagda, the good god. His descent places him inside the divine family that fills the oldest tales of Ireland. The name itself is Irish and means famous or renowned. It signals high standing for anyone who bore it in early tradition. A separate Christian saint also carries this name, and her feast day falls on 3 May. The shared name links pagan legend with the calendar of the Irish church.

:::

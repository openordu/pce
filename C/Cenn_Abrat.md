``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cenn Abrat, also Cend Abrat or Cenn Febrat, marks a legendary battlefield of early Ireland. Lugaid mac Con lost there to southern hosts raised by the Eóganacht under his brother Eógan. Modern commentators trace the legend to a real defeat of the invading Érainn by Eóganacht and Múscraige forces. The name covers the Ballyhoura uplands of County Limerick. Lugaid endured exile in Scotland before returning for the climactic battle of Mag Mucrama.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairbre mac Ethne serves as the resident satirist of the Tuatha Dé Danann. His father is Ogma, and his mother is Étan. Dian Cécht ranks as his grandfather. His sharp verses make him a feared voice at court. King Bres treats him with harsh rudeness. Cairbre mac Ethne answers with a biting poem. That poem throws red blotches onto the face of Bres. A ruler so marked loses his right to rule. The nobles then demand the resignation of their king. Cairbre turns the same skill against the Fomorians. His searing lines drain their will to fight. In the great second battle the Fomorian morale fails. Cairbre also wields the curse the glám dicenn brings. That power gives him rule over his many foes.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cingciges is an Irish term for Pentecost, the church feast also called Whit Sunday, and the variant form Cingcís carries the same sense. The Latin root quinquagesima lies behind the name. Older beliefs have attached themselves to the feast, and MacKillop points readers to his entry on Whitsun customs for the seasonal practice. Like many church feasts, it drew folk observance of greater age into its place on the calendar.

:::

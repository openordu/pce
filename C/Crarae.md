::: magazinestyle
Crarae is a chambered cairn site beside Loch Fyne in Scotland.
The loch cuts into north Strathclyde, in the old county of Argyllshire.
MacKillop calls the tombs there the most impressive chambered examples in Scotland.
Archaeologists excavated the ground between 1955 and 1957.
The monument rises on the loch's western shore among the country's prehistoric burial grounds.

:::

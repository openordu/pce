``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coed-Alun is the Welsh form of the name of Koadalan. Koadalan is the hero of a Breton tale of magical change and denied immortality. Collectors gathered the tale from spoken tradition in the 1800s. The tale carries kinship with medieval Welsh literature. Its episodes run so loose that some modern commentators call it the Saga of Koadalan. See Koadalan.

:::
```

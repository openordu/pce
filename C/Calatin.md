``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Calatin is another spelling of the name Cailitin. The two forms name the same wizard of the old tales. Scribes wrote Calatin and Cailitin for one druid figure. This sorcerer commands a clan of identical sons in Irish legend. The fuller account sits under the entry for Cailitin. That page tells how the hero Cuchulainn overcame the wizard and his kin.

:::
```

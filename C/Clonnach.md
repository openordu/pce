``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clonnach is a mythological figure, introduced as a son of Crom Crnaich in later oral tales. In those stories the father no longer appears as an idol or a god. He takes the shape of Crom Dubh, a pagan chief who stands against St Patrick. This shift shows how later oral tradition recast old mythic figures into human opponents of the saint.

:::
```

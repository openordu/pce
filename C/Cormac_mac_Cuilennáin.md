``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac mac Cuilennáin was a scholar and king-bishop of Cashel who died in 908. Tradition makes him the compiler of Sanas Cormaic, Cormac's Glossary. An uncritical euhemerist, he took every figure of the old stories for history. Even so his Glossary preserves a wealth of material on early Irish tradition that later scholars could find nowhere else.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac mac Aililla is another name for Cormac Cas, the shadowy seventh-century king of Munster. The name form marks Cormac Cas as a son of Ailill Aulomm. Through that line the Dál Cais kings, the O'Briens and the McNamaras among them, claimed him as their forefather. Déisi attackers slew Cormac Cas at Cam Feradaig near Limerick in AD 713. See the article on Cormac Cas for the fuller account.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cruaidín Catutchenn is another name for An Claidheamh Soluis, the sword of light of Irish story. Oral tradition ties that famous blade to the hero Cúchulainn. The name keeps a rarer form of the weapon's title. Readers should follow the Claidheamh Soluis entry for the sword and its tales.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cathaoir is a modern spelling of the legendary king usually written Cathaír Mór. Scribes have recorded the name as Cathaoir Mór, Catháer Már, and Catháeir Mór, with Cahir More among the variants. MacKillop links the form to Brittonic catu-tegemos, meaning battle-lord. Tradition remembers him as a legendary forebear of the Leinstermen. He held sway there just before Conn Cétchathach won the high kingship. The best known tale says Conn killed him and seized his throne. Other versions lay the killing on Goll mac Morna or unnamed rivals. Writers often render the name as Charles, though the two share no etymology.

:::
```

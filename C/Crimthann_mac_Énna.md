::: magazinestyle
Crimthann mac Énna is a king of the Lagin in fifth-century Ireland. His father was Énna Censelach, a king of the same people before him. MacKillop credits him with widening his sway over the peoples who lived around his realm. The Irish Annals give his death in either 483 or 485. He stands in the early historic layer of the Leinster king lists. His generations saw the Laigin fight to hold their borders.

:::

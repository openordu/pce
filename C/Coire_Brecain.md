``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coire Brecain is the Irish name-form of the great whirlpool, Corrievreckan, between Jura and Scarba off the west Scottish coast (the name means the caldron of Breccan). MacKillop treats Coire Brecain as a cross-reference to his entry on Corrievreckan. No separate article covers the whirlpool under this Irish form. Readers seeking the legend of the drowned hero should follow that pointer. See Corrievreckan.

:::
```

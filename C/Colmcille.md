``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colmcille, also spelled Colmkil, is a modern variant of the saint's name Colum Cille. The form drops the internal i of the older Irish Columb Cille. MacKillop treats both spellings as variants of one headword. His full article sits under Colum Cille. See Colum Cille.

:::
```

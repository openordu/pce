``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cyhyreath names a Welsh spectral figure. MacKillop also records the spelling cyheuraeth. One encyclopedia gives it no separate account and sends the reader to Caointeach. MacKillop gives the figure its own article. The Cyhyreath resembles the Caointeach heard in Gaelic Scotland and the Weeper mourned in English tradition. People usually meet it as an unseen voice without a body. Listeners hear it groan before death arrives. It cries out most when a disaster or an epidemic brings many deaths at once. Its haunts lie in south Wales, in east Dyfed, and in the three Glamorganshires. Dyfed covered Carmarthenshire until 1974. It is heard most near the Towy River. As with the Irish Banshee, the Cyhyreath weeps for natives who die far from home. MacKillop suggests the figure began as a goddess of streams.

:::
```

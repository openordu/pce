::: magazinestyle
Cloch Labrais, also spelled Labhrais and anglicized Cloghlowrish, is Irish for a speaking, answering stone. The name belongs to an oracle rock of County Waterford that stands near Ballykeroge, six miles north-east of Dungarvan. Legend ranks it among the truth-testing stones of Ireland and calls it the Answering Stone. The rock split when people once asked it to witness a lie. A husband had accused his wife of adultery, and she answered with careful wording. She swore she had sinned with no one save a man she had seen only at a distance. That distant figure was her lover, so her answer broke no rule of speech. The hidden deceit still proved too much for the stone, and it split apart.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac, also spelled Cormacc and Carmac, is a common Irish name. The second element mac means son. The first element may come from a word that defiles. Many legendary and historical figures bear it, among them kings, ecclesiastics, and saints. The most famous Cormacs are likely the legendary king Cormac mac Airt, the glossator Cormac mac Cuilennáin of Sanas Cormaic, and Cormac mac Carthaig, who gave Cormac's Chapel at Cashel its name. The McCormick and MacCormack families of Ireland and Scotland do not trace back to one progenitor.

:::
```

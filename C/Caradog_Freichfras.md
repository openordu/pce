``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caradog Freichfras, whose title means of the strong arm, was a Welsh Arthurian warrior. He was an eloquent and bold speaker. He was also a virtuous and proud noble. Welsh records gave him a descent attributed to Llyr. Yet the French legends and romances hold his stories more often. Chrétien de Troyes wrote him into his verse. He became Surieux in old French forms. His wife was Tegau Eurfron, honoured as the paragon of virtue. Standing firm of reach and full of speech, he stays a familiar knight of legend.

:::
```

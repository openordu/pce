``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Iborm[m]eith is an agnomen borne by Cáer the swan maiden. The Irish word for yew is ibor, so this epithet names the yew-berry woman. Cáer is a princess of Connacht and a daughter of Ethal Anbúail. Her beloved is Angus Óg, a god of the Irish. Angus suffers an ailment only a dream woman can cure. When he finds Cáer the two become lovers. They turn into swans and circle a lake three times. International tale type four hundred frames her as the swan maiden. See Aisling Oenguso for the fuller story.

:::
```

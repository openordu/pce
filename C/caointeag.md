``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The caointeag is a regional name used on the isle of Islay and in nearby Kintyre for the spirit otherwise known as the caoineach. It belongs to the wider family of Gaelic mourning figures. The variant label keeps the local tradition distinct within that family.

:::
```

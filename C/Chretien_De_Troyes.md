``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Chretien de Troyes was a French poet of the later 12th century. He wrote court romances between about 1170 and 1190. His works built the Arthurian tradition on the Continent. He wrote in octosyllabic rhyming couplets. His romances include Erec and Enide and a poem about Yvan. He left the story of Perceval and the Grail unfinished. Later writers added endings to that tale. The romance of Erec and Enide may rest on older Welsh sources. The court poet's work shaped many later writers. The school disputes the debt to the Welsh and to Geoffrey.

:::
```

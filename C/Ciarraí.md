``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ciarraí is the modern Irish spelling of the name Kerry. Older sources give the forms Ciarrai and Ciarraighe. The word Ciarraighe names the people of Ciar, whose descendants were said to have settled that corner of the island. Kerry is the south-westernmost county of Ireland.

:::
```

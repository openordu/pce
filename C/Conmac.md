::: magazinestyle
Conmac is a figure of early Irish tradition whose name appears also as Conmacc and Conmhac. The name means wolf's son. Tradition makes him the eponymous founder of Connemara, the west Galway district whose Irish name, Conmaicne Mara, means Conmac's people of the sea. The sources disagree over which bearer founded the district. One line names Conmac the by-blow of Medb and Fergus mac Roich. Another names Lugaid Conmac, a figure whom some texts call Lugaid mac Con. The question stays open in the tradition.

:::

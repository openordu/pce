``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac Cas is a shadowy historical king of Munster of the late 7th century. He is the legendary ancestor of Dál Cais families such as the O'Briens and the McNamaras. His grandfather is Eógan Mór, also called Mug Nuadat. His father is Ailill Aulomm. For that reason he sometimes carries the patronymic mac Aililla. The invading Déisi slew him at Cam Feradaig, by today's city of Limerick, in AD 713.

:::
```

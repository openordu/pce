::: magazinestyle
Cahirconree is an Iron Age stone fort in County Kerry. The fort stands upon a high promontory in the far west of Ireland. That rock rises about two thousand fifty feet above the plain. The ground sits within the Sliab Mis range of the mountains. The fort looks across the Dingle peninsula and the open sea. The town of Tralee lies roughly eight miles to the east. The name of the fort seems to recall the hero Cú Roi. An old tale says that the hero's own wife betrayed him there. The wife Bláithíne gave the hero away to the champion Cúchulainn. So the ruin keeps the memory of a long betrayal in stone.

:::

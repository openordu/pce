``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Mac Gréine was an Irish god and the husband of Ériu, the earth goddess from whom Ireland takes its name. With his brothers Mac Cécht and Mac Cuill he formed a trio of parallel gods. Their wives were land goddesses. His name means son of the sun. The word for sun is feminine in Irish, so scholars read the name as a matrilineal title, not proof of an ancient solar god. Mac Gréine did not carry his father's name. He was, like his brothers, a son of Ogma, the god of eloquence. The warrior poet Amairgin of the Milesians killed him. The name Cethor serves as one of his alternate names.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
cuachag is a kind of fuath or water-sprite. The name comes from Scottish Gaelic words for girl with curly hair, curl, and cuckoo. The cuachag lives in the central Highlands of Scotland, in the area that was Inverness-shire until 1974. The creature takes its name from Loch Cuaich, which lies eight miles south-west of Kingussie. Like all the fuathan, the cuachag can be dangerous to those who meet it.

:::

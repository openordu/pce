``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cúcán is a diminutive form of the name Cúchulainn. The dictionary records it from the Táin bó Cuailnge and other early texts. The same hero stands behind both shapes of the name. Readers who want his deeds should follow the Cúchulainn entry. That entry keeps the Ulster Cycle dossier and the full list of tales.

:::
```

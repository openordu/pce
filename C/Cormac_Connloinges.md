``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormac Connloinges, also written Conn Loingeas, Cond Longes, and Conloingeas, is a son of Conchobar mac Nessa, king of Emain Macha. He goes into voluntary exile because the sons of Uisnech were treacherously killed. He had been their surety in passage. In the epic Táin Bó Cuailnge he travels with the army of Connacht that Queen Medb leads against Ulster. Later he stops Fergus mac Róich from killing Conchobar. Later still, the dying Conchobar sends for Cormac to take the kingship. As Cormac starts that journey of reconciliation, Connachtmen coming home from a raid in Ulster murder him. Some accounts think he married Niam, daughter of Celtchair, after her treacherous union with Conganchnes. Genealogical tracts say he later took Étain as a wife, presumably once her usual mortal husband, Eochaid Airem, had died. He also appears in the Destruction of Da Derga's Hostel, a tale of a doomed feast. He may be identical with Cormac, the husband of Étain Óg.

:::
```

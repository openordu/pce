::: magazinestyle
curmudgeon is a common English translation of the Irish bodach. The bodach also appears in Scottish Gaelic tradition as a hobgoblin figure. MacKillop records curmudgeon as the regular English rendering of this Celtic word. The Irish and Scottish Gaelic bodach denotes a hobgoblin or similar uncanny being in folk belief. curmudgeon thus names the English term that scholars and translators often reach for when conveying the Celtic concept.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cainche also appears as Caince and bears the second name Cébha. In the Fenian ballads she stands as the daughter of Fionn mac Cumhaill. The Fenian tales set her beside Fionn's rival Goll mac Morna. She mates with Goll mac Morna to produce a son, that child being Feadha. Fionn mac Cumhaill later kills Feadha. Goll mac Morna blames this killing for his parting quarrel with Fionn. Her name turns into the anglicized form Keeva of the White Skin. Some writers give the anglicized spelling Keva of the White Skin. The Irish root of her name carries the senses of melody and of a songbird. This same root also suggests the bough of a tree.

:::
```

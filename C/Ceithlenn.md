::: magazinestyle
Ceithlenn is a spelling variant of the Irish name Caitlin. Ceithlionn is another form of that same name. Caitlin itself serves as an Irish equivalent of Catherine. Since the 1100s, many people in Ireland have carried it. Folklore best remembers the figure Caitlin Ní hUallacháin. Her surname appears in varied spellings across Kilkenny, Clare, and other regions. She has come to stand for Ireland only in recent times. The 1902 play Cathleen ni Houlihan by W. B. Yeats shaped that image strongly. Within it, the Poor Old Woman presents her own story.

:::

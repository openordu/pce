``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuthullin is a variant spelling of Cuchullin. The name covers the same character, the druid son of Seuma who leads the Caledonian host in Macpherson's Ossian (1760). He there appears as the husband of Bragela, a commander Macpherson shaped out of older Gaelic matter. Readers should follow the Cuchullin entry for his full portrait.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cala' Me is the Cornish term for the first day of May. The phrase marks the festival that opens the summer half of the year. In Wales the same celebration carries the name Calan Mai. The two names point to one shared spring tide. This day also stands close to the Irish feast of Beltaine. The Cornish and the Welsh kept the same way of talking about the season's start. Both peoples treated the day as a time of renewal.

:::

::: magazinestyle
copóg Phádraig serves as the Irish folk name for two related low-growing herbs, the plantain and the water-plantain, both grouped under the genus plantago. The literal English rendering of the Irish form is leaf of Patrick, linking the herb to Ireland's patron saint. Folk tradition credited the leaves with power to repel fairies from households and pastures. The water-plantain form favours wet ditches and slow streams across the British Isles. Plain ribbed foliage marks the plant for herbal collectors. Modern herbalists still prize the genus for its soothing qualities.

:::

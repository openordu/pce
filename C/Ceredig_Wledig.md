::: magazinestyle
Ceredig Wledig was a sixth-century ruler of Dumbarton, within the kingdom of Strathclyde in Scotland. His Welsh title Gwledig may mean kind, or else rustic and rural in a second sense. He is possibly the Coroticus of St Patrick's writings, who kidnapped some of the saint's earliest converts to Christianity.

:::

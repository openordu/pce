``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Camulos held great power in early Britain and in Gaul. The Belgae and the Remi gave him special honour. The Romans saw a warrior in him and tied him to Mars. A stone at Rindern carries the name Mars-Camulo under an oak crown. Some carvings give him a ram-horned head. His fame shows in many place names. Camulodunum keeps his memory and later grew into the city of Colchester. A few writers tried to link him to Old King Cole and to the hero Fionn's father. Modern scholars reject those claims. The hard evidence does not hold for such a link. His true standing rests on the stones and the town name.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cape Breton Island forms part of the Canadian province of Nova Scotia. The island keeps a proud Gaelic heritage. Early Gaelic song and verse reached the modern era on its shores. That spoken and sung tradition tended to flower where the old tongue stayed strong. The island offers a living house for Celtic lore in the New World. Nova Scotia as a whole carried the same Gaelic story across the water.

:::

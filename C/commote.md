``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The commote (variant spelling commot; Welsh cwmwd) is a territorial and administrative division of medieval Wales. In most arrangements a cantref or cantred grouped one or more commotes beneath it, so the commote sat one level below the cantref in the Welsh land-hierarchy. A given cantref could therefore be assembled from several smaller commotes rather than forming a single undivided unit. MacKillop frames the term strictly as a Welsh administrative unit, separate from the Irish and Scottish tale-cycles and place-name material that surrounds the entry in the dictionary. The MacKillop entry closes by leaving the reader with the cantref above and the local land-unit beneath it as the operative frame.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cináed is the name of several figures in the early chronicles of Ireland and of Gaelic Scotland, written in the variants Cináeth and Cionaodh. The best-known bearer is Cináed mac Alpin, a man of Dál Riada, counted as the first king of Scotland. His reign ran from 844 to his death in 858. He joined the Scoto-Irish settlers of Argyll, drawn from his father's line, with the native Picts, drawn from his mother's line. English usage knows him as Kenneth MacAlpin, and some writers style him Kenneth I.

:::
```

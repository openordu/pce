``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Calan Awst is the Welsh counterpart of the great feast of Lughnasa. The name marks the first day of the harvest season in Wales. It falls at the same point of the year as the Irish festival. The term carries the sense of the month of August. This day opens the period when people gathered in the crops. Its place in the year echoes the wider Celtic calendar of feasts.

:::
```

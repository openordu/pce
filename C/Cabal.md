::: magazinestyle
Cabal is the hound of King Arthur. People write the name in several forms. These include Cafall, Caval, Cavall, and Kawal. Every form points to the same trusted creature. Cabal accompanied Arthur on his adventures. The hound played a part in Arthurian legend. Cabal remains recognized under all of these alternate spellings.

:::

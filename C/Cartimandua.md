``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cartimandua was chieftainess of the Brigantes, a north British people, at Roman expansion. She cast aside her husband and seized charge of her own people. Yet she asked Rome for protection and served, in a way, as their client. When the beaten Caradog fled to her, she gave him over to the Romans. In her devotions she hailed the goddess Brigantia, the high one. People often set her beside Boudicca. The Brigantes held much of the northern frontier zone. Their lands included York and the country around Hadrian's Wall. Rome backed the queen and helped her keep the throne. Her first husband Venutius turned against her. She picked Vellocatus to rule beside her. Seven dedications to the goddess Brigantia survive today.

:::
```

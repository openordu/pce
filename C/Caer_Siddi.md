``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Siddi is another name for the Welsh Otherworld Annwfn. The title may mean the revolving castle. Seen as an elysium, the place spares its guests from sickness and old age. Enchanting music plays through Caer Siddi, and a fountain pours a drink more sweetly than white wine. Gwair sits held in a well-equipped prison inside the land. The site also draws a tie with the small isle of Grassholm close to Dyfed. Caer Feddwid shares this Otherworld circle.

:::
```

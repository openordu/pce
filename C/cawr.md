``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cawr is the Welsh word for a giant. The term marks the towering being of Welsh tradition and appears in tales of the old north. The word itself is the standard label, and MacKillop gives no separate article beyond the brief definition that the giant of Welsh folklore is called a cawr.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Catan, Saint is a legendary figure of eleventh-century Scotland. The account credits him with giving a name to the Clan Chattan. That Clan is a broad union of Scotland's kindreds. Its members all lay claim to a Pictish line of descent. The group forms a larger whole than any single clan. It guards a shared bond. Catan reads as a shadow rather than a real person. His fame rests on the confederation he names. Sparse records leave his life obscure. Scholars therefore treat his story as legend, not settled fact. The Clan Chattan endures as his memorial in the Scottish Highlands.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cornavii is the name Ptolemy records in the second century for a people of Caithness in northern Scotland. Readers have taken the name as people of the promontory, people of the horn, or people of the horned god. No single reading has won agreement.

:::
```

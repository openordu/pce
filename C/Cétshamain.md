::: magazinestyle
Cétshamain is an Irish festival name. The form Cétsamain appears beside it. The words carry the sense of proto-spring, the first bright days of the season. The dictionary gives both forms as alternate names for Beltaine, the great fire feast. The feast and its fires sit in the Beltaine entry.

:::

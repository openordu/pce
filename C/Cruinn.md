``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruinn is a variant spelling of Crunniuc, the Ulster farmer of saga. The goddess Macha chose that mortal man and shared his farm. His boast against his pregnant wife brought a horse race she ran and lost at the finish. Her dying curse laid the Debility of the Ulstermen on the province. Readers should follow the Crunniuc entry for the tale.

:::
```

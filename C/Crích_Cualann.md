::: magazinestyle
Crích Cualann is the name used in Old Irish narratives for the territory of Cualu. The Irish word crích means boundary or territory. The land ran from the River Liffey to Arklow. It covered roughly the modern county of Wicklow, south of Dublin. The same district appears in place names like the road to Cualu. Readers should follow the Liffey entry for the river at its northern edge.

:::

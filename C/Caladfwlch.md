``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caladfwlch is a variant spelling of Caledfwlch. That Welsh name describes one sword of King Arthur in the Arthurian narratives. The blade stands among several forms that herald the later Excalibur. The tale Culhwch ac Olwen lists the weapon among the king's most cherished possessions. A hero named Llenlleawg Wyddel carries the sword in that story. The Irish blade caladbol connects to the same ancient root.

:::
```

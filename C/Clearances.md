::: magazinestyle
The Highland Clearances were the forced eviction of populations across the Scottish Highlands and Islands from the 1780s to the 1850s. Landowners brought in sheep and then deer runs to raise the income of estates. Whole communities were driven out and settled on poor coastal crofts or forced to emigrate. Between 1762 and 1886 roughly one hundred thousand Highlanders left for Canada, the United States, and Australia. The clearances wrecked the clan system and speeded the decline of Scottish Gaelic. The harshest events, such as the Sutherland clearings, left bitter memories.

:::

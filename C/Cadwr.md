``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cadwr is an alternate Welsh spelling for the name Cador. The source directs the reader to the entry for Cador. Cadwr refers to the same duke of Cornwall. He holds rule in the tale Breuddwyd Rhonabwy. He is also the nephew of the king Arthur. Cadwr appears in the chronicles written by Geoffrey of Monmouth and by other poets. The duke Cadwr falls in the closing fight at Mount Badon. The name simply marks one form of Cador.

:::
```

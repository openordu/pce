::: magazinestyle
The coblynau (singular coblyn; variant spelling coblynnod) are Welsh sprites of the mines, whose name MacKillop derives from the English word goblins. They resemble the Cornish knockers in type and role. Although usually described as quiet and only about eighteen inches tall, the coblynau are perceived as friendly and helpful. Welsh miners credited them with knowing where rich lodes of ore could be found. The creatures are recorded under folk motif F456 in the international folk-tale index. The MacKillop entry closes by drawing the parallel between the Welsh coblynau and the Cornish knockers, placing both in a shared Atlantic family of mine-spirits.

:::

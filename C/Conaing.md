Conaing is several figures of early Irish genealogy and of the island's civil and church history carried it. The word looks related to a Germanic royal title, Old English cyning, king. Some commentators have confused the name with that of Conand, the leader of the Fomorians, but the two are distinct. The name therefore sits at a crossroads of native genealogy and borrowed royal vocabulary.

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caerleon is a Welsh town on the River Usk, and its name is an anglicized form of Caerllion ar Wysg. The Romans raised the fortress Isca near the ground chosen later for the walled medieval town. Caerleon stood as a residence of Arthur and is often named as Camelot. The stones of Isca may have persuaded Geoffrey of Monmouth to favour Caerleon as the seat of Arthur. There the king must gather his host before the battle of Bedegraine, and afterwards he celebrates the triumph. Caerleon Castle keeps a flight of two hundred and eight steps that ends in a chamber linked to Merlin. Today Caerleon is a modest urban quarter lying in south Monmouthshire, east of Newport beside the Usk. Some people mistake this town for the English city of Chester, which the Welsh call Caerllion Fawr.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ceisnoidhe Uladh is a cross-reference entry. MacKillop glosses the Irish phrase as nine days' affliction, the pangs of the Ulstermen. The name points the reader to the entry Debility Of The Ulstermen. The two names describe the same subject. MacKillop likewise sends the reader on to nóinden Ulad. The entry carries no separate narrative. It exists to route readers toward the fuller account of the Ulster pangs.

:::
```

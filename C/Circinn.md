::: magazinestyle
Circinn is one of the chief kingdoms of the Picts in early Scotland. Medieval writers also spell the name Circenn and Girginn. The kingdom lay north of the Forth and covered ground near the present counties of Angus and Kincardine. People once called Angus Forfarshire, and Kincardine joined the Tayside region in 1974. Tradition counts four chief Pictish realms, and Circinn stood among them.

:::

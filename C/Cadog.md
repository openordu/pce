::: magazinestyle
Cadog is a celebrated Welsh saint of the sixth century. He founded a monastery at Llancarfan in Glamorganshire. He also raised about twenty other churches across the south of Wales. He set up further places of worship in Cornwall and Brittany. Cadog was the grandson of the saint Brychan. Invaders murdered him about the year 577. People often call him Cadog Ddoeth, which means the wise. Many miracle stories grew around him in the eleventh and twelfth centuries. The Wisdom of Cadog held witty sayings that an aspiring bard had to learn. By tradition Cadog served as teacher to Gildas and Taliesin. His feast is held on the twenty-fifth of September.

:::

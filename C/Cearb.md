::: magazinestyle
Cearb is a Scottish Gaelic word. It means cutting, or perhaps slaughtering. In Scottish Gaelic folklore Cearb names a malevolent spirit. Writers cite the figure widely yet explain it little. People believe the spirit kills men and cattle. Motif scholars record the tale type as F402.1.11. The belief survives as a brief but grim note in the tradition.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Cainwen is a Welsh name that serves the Church. The coin part of her name stands for beautiful. The gwen part of her name stands for fair. In Welsh tradition she names the saint called Keyne. Keyne ranked among the earliest Celtic Christians. Welsh speakers granted her this pleasing title. Both halves of her name praise matchless grace. Her record carries the beauty of that holy woman. Writers keep Cainwen as a gentle mark of honor.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuirithir is the poet-lover of Liadain in the old Irish story of the pair. Their ninth-century poem of meetings and partings is often known in English as the meeting of Liadain and Cuirithir. A churchman placed them under a vow, and the pair lived on apart from each other. The name also serves later readers as a pointer. It sends the reader to the account of Liadan for the fuller story.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colgán, also Colga, is an evil king of Lochlainn across several Fenian tales. His name echoes Irish colg, the word for sword. He appears in Bruidhean Chaorthainn, the Quicken Trees hostel tale, and there his son Midac plays a leading role. Midac later traps the Fianna in a bewitched palace and dies there. See Midac.

:::
```

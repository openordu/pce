::: magazinestyle
Cronk-y-Keillown is the Manx name of Tynwald Hill on the Isle of Man. The site is a round mound of earth, only a few feet tall. It stands near St John's Church, three miles from Peel. Tradition says people fetched its soil from every parish of the island. Public ceremonials were held there, among them the reading of the laws of the House of Keys. The annual Tynwald Fair opened on 5 July, a date once kept as Midsummer's Day.

:::

::: magazinestyle
crúba leomhain is the Irish name for a plant known in English as vervain. The phrase translates literally as lion's feet. The scientific name for vervain is Verbena officinalis. crúba leomhain appears in Irish sources as a folk name for this medicinal herb. The window in MacKillop records the name and its literal meaning but does not extend further.

:::

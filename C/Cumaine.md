``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cumaine is a variant spelling of Cuimmíne. The form carries the pet shape of the name Colum among early Irish saints. Its best-known bearer is Cuimmíne Fota, the abbot of Kilcummin. He backed the Roman Easter and drew a rebuke from Iona. Scribes moved between the spellings with no shift in sense. Readers should follow the Cuimmíne entry.

:::
```

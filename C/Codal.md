``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Codal is the foster-father of Ériu, the goddess who gives Ireland its name. Genealogists spell his name Codai in the source dictionary. The Lebor Gabála preserves conflicting genealogies for Ériu. One strand makes Codal her father alongside Delbáeth, and names her mother Emmas or Eimin. Codal therefore belongs to the tangled family lore that surrounds the eponym of Ireland itself.

:::
```

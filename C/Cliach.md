``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cliach is a harper who seeks the hand of one of the daughters of Bodb Derg. The father refuses the match and calls on his magic to guard his door. His spell keeps the suitor away for more than a year, so the courtship fails. In Irish tale convention a harper's craft carries high standing. Music wins favor at the otherworld courts where such suits are made. Yet even that art cannot break the enchantment around the hall. Cliach gains neither entry nor the bride.

:::
```

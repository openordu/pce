``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cnú Deiréil is the dwarf fairy harper of the Fenian Cycle. Irish storytellers call him the little nut of melody. He plays the harp for Fionn Mac Cumhaill and fills the hall with delight and play. The same tiny musician brings on sweet slumber with his songs. He stands perhaps 4 feet high, and golden hair crowns him. He claims Lugh of the long hand as his father. The encyclopedia sends the reader to the account under the Dwarves for more detail on him.

:::
```

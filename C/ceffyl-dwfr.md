::: magazinestyle
ceffyl dwfr is the Welsh water horse of folklore. The creature has the shape of a horse yet carries a hostile intent toward humans. Folk accounts place the ceffyl dwfr in streams and lakes across Wales. It stands as the Welsh counterpart of the Irish and Scottish each uisce and uisge, and of the Scottish kelpie tradition.

:::

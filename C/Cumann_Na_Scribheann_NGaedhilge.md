``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cumann na Scríbheann nGaedhilge is the Irish name of the Irish Texts Society. The body began in 1898 in London, where its base remains. It grew out of the Irish Literary Society, an older body set up in 1892. Douglas Hyde served as its first president until 1949. The Society exists to publish and translate texts written in the Irish language. The Society issued a dictionary of Irish by Patrick S. Dinneen in 1904. A much enlarged edition followed in 1927 and became a standard reference. The Society also produced the bilingual journal called the Gaelic Journal. That paper first appeared in November 1882. The Gaelic League took it over in 1894, and it lasted until 1909. Its editors included Eoin MacNeill and Torna. The Society now runs several series of scholarly volumes.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cauth Bodva is a goddess honored in Gaul. She anticipates Badb, the Irish war-goddess. MacKillop presents her as a continental forerunner of that figure. Cauth Bodva may be the same figure as Cathubodua. The tie links Gaulish cult names to their Irish counterparts.

:::

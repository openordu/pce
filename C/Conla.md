::: magazinestyle
Conla is one spelling among the many variants of Connla. The full list also runs Conláech, Conlaí, Conlán, Conlaoch, Conle, Condla, and Cúnla. Dozens of figures in Irish legend and history carry the name. MacKillop notes that writers sometimes mix it up with Conall. The two names are unrelated. The name may mean great lord or great chief. The main PCE article on this name sits at Connla (Conla, Conlaí, Conle). See Connla (Conla, Conlaí, Conle).

:::

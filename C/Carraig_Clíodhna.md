Carraig Clíodhna is an alternate drawing of Carrigcleena. The two spellings point to the same pair of sacred Irish rocks. Both stones carry an old tie to the goddess Clidna. Head here toward Carrigcleena for the whole account of the place. That entry gives each rock its place in County Cork.

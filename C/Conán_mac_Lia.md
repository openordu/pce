``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conán mac Lia is the lesser-known of the two Conáns of the Fenian Cycle. His father is Liath Luachra, the warrior from whom Fionn mac Cumhaill took the crane bag. Conán first raids against the Fenians for years. After a defeat in Munster he changes sides and joins Fionn, and he proves himself a keen, hardy fighter. In certain stories he mistreats Finnine, the sister of Ferdoman, at times as her husband. Ferdoman challenges him to a duel to defend her honour, and both men die in the fight.

:::
```

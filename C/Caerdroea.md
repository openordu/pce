``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caerdroea is the Welsh word for the city of Troy. Ilium is the older title for that same town. The Welsh tongue held its own form for the famous place. This city lay far from Britain in ancient days. Yet the Welsh people gave the town a name of their own. The Trojan names fade since the royal line of Britain came from Troy. Brutus led that fabled descent. His tales crown the city with a royal weight inside Celtic story.

:::
```

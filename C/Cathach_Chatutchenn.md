::: magazinestyle
Cathach Chatutchenn is an Irish name that means 'bellicose' or 'hard-headed'. She is a female warrior famed for her love of Cúchulainn. MacKillop lists her among the notable warrior women of Celtic tradition. He sets her beside Boudicca, the historical British leader whom legend calls Boadicea. Cúchulainn himself stands as the greatest champion of early Irish literature and the chief figure of the Ulster Cycle. The epic Táin Bó Cuailnge centres on him. Born as Sétanta, he won his famous name only after daring feats at age seven. Commentators once ranked him with Hercules and Siegfried.

:::

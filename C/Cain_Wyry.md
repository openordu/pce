``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cain Wyry is the Welsh form for the Keyne Virgin. The title points to the holy woman called Keyne. Welsh writers use it for the same figure as Cain. That figure is the saint of lasting memory among them. The name praises her pure and faithful life. She ranks among the chronicle of Welsh holy women. Her devotion stayed strong through many years. Even today the Welsh honor her fair renown. The title keeps her virtue always in view.

:::
```

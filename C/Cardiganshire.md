``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cardiganshire was a maritime county of south Wales until 1974. It now forms the northernmost third of Dyfed. Cardigan Bay borders it on the west. The land carries the name of Ceredigion, a son of Cunedda. Welsh legend says a portion of Cardigan sank beneath the sea. That sunken land is Cantor'r Gwaelod. Its flooding created Cardigan Bay as it now stands. The later county took its identity from these older stories.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crimthann Mór is the legendary ancestor of the Osraige, the people of Ossory. MacKillop places his territory in the lands of the modern county of Kilkenny. The name means the great Crimthann, and the Irish fox name marks him as one of many bearers. This ancestor must not be confused with Crimthann Mór mac Fidaig, the high king of pre-Patrician story. The two figures share the byname but belong to different peoples and different stories.

:::
```

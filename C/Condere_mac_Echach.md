Condere mac Echach is an Ulster warrior. MacKillop credits him with good sense and eloquence. He stood first to face the mysterious Connla. That young man was the unacknowledged son of Cú Chulainn. Connla said he could deal with Condere with ease. Condere wisely withdrew from the fight.

:::

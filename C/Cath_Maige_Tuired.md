``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cath Maige Tuired is the Irish title of this key Mythological Cycle document. It tells of two clashes at Mag Tuired, the Plain of Pillars, twenty-seven years apart. The first fight falls at Beltaine near Cong in County Mayo. There the Danann overcome the Fir Bolg. Their king Nuadu loses an arm to the warrior Sreng. Dian Cécht and the smith Credne fit him with a silver arm, but a maimed man cannot reign. Kingship passes to Bres, born to a Danann mother and the Fomorian ruler Elatha. Bres rules harshly and shows no generosity. The poet Cairbre satirizes him, and red blotches break out on his skin. Nuadu then takes the throne again. The young hero Lug Lamfhota arrives, proves mastery of every art as Samildanach, and Nuadu yields him the kingship. Lug leads the Danann against the resurgent Fomorians and slays Balor at Samain. The tale places Mag Tuired at Moytirra near Lough Arrow in County Sligo. That landscape holds megalithic monuments raised between 2000 and 2500 BC.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cain appears in the Lebor Gabala, a text that is called the Book of Invasions. This book brings the biblical figure into the Irish legend. The Lebor Gabala numbers Cain among its early players. The account names Cain as the first person to look upon Ireland. He stands in a history the Irish tell about their own origins. The story weaves a foreign scripture into a native tale. Ireland therefore gains a biblical note at the very start. This role marks Cain in the wider legend.

:::
```

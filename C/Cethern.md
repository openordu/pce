``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cethern is a shared name in early Irish storytelling. The related spelling Cethren also belongs here. Several figures bear it. The best known is Cethern mac Fintain of the Táin, and his own entry covers that warrior. One Cethern serves as an alias for the god who rules the Otherworld. A second Cethern is an alias of Cian, father of Lug Lámfhota, the god known here as Lugh. A third Cethern fathers the druid Mog Ruith.

:::
```

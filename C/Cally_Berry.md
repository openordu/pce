``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cally Berry is the Ulster version of Cailleach Bheur, the Scottish Gaelic weather spirit. In this telling she acts more like a harmful supernatural hag. The storm spirit of the north turns into a real threat in Ulster. She calls up the wild weather of the winter season. The people treat her with fear rather than respect. She stands for the sharp cold of the dark months. The name links her to the same dread family as her cousin to the south. A reader may compare her with Cailleach Bhéirre as well. The Ulster figure gives the shared hag her own dark edge. Each province shapes the old woman toward its own fears.

:::
```

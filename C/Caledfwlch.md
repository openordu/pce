``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caledfwlch is the Welsh name for the sword carried by King Arthur. Many Welsh stories of Arthur give his blade this very name. The sword numbers among the several blades that foreshadow the famous Excalibur. In the tale of Culhwch and Olwen it counts among the treasures most dear to the king. There the hero Llenlleawg Wyddel uses it, and the sword slays Diwmach Wyddel with every member of his company. The Irish blade Caladbolg offers another parallel weapon.

:::
```

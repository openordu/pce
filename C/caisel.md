``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
caisel is an Irish word that English often renders as castle, by way of the Latin root castellum. The Irish term in fact covers any large secular building put up with drystone walls, not a church. Caisel Muman serves as the Old Irish name for Cashel. The headword appears in the spellings caisel and caiseal.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conand is a leader of the Fomorians. Some texts spell the name Conann or Connan. He holds a fortress on Tory Island near the Donegal shore. A huge glass tower named Tor Conaind serves him there. He forces a heavy tribute on the Nemedians. The Nemedians rise at last. They attack the island, storm the tower, and Fergus Lethderg strikes Conand down. The survivors then sail away to Britain. Readers should tell him apart from Conaing, who bears a similar name.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Cébhra is a variant form of the name Cainche. The dictionary treats the two shapes as one figure, the daughter of Fionn who mates with Goll mac Morna. Readers who want her tale should follow the Cainche entry. That entry keeps the Fenian ballad story and the anglicized form Keeva of the White Skin.

:::

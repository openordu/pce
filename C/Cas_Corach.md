::: magazinestyle
Cas Corach, also spelled Cascorrach or Cas Corrach, was a harper of the Tuatha De Danann. The Irish name cas means curled or skilled, a fitting word for a musician. He is described as playing his harp for Saint Patrick. In the Acallam na Senorach, he follows Cailte to hear the lore of the Fenians. He wished to learn the patriotic stories of that old warrior band. His music bridged the ancient pagan world and the new Christian faith.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cnoc Áine is the hill of the fairy queen Áine in Co. Limerick. The name means the hill of Áine. English speakers call it Knockainy. The hill stands three and a half miles east of Bruff, south of Lough Gur. A village nearby carries the same name. Tradition gives the hill to Áine as her dwelling place. The fuller local tradition sits under the separate article Knockainy, which carries the anglicized name. Cnoc Gréine lies seven miles away.

:::
```

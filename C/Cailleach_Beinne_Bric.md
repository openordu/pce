::: magazinestyle
Cailleach Beinne Bric is Scottish Gaelic for the old woman of the speckled mountain. Her name may mark a trout, salmon, wolf, or badger. She stands as the Highland counterpart of the Cailleach Bhéirre. Like that larger hag she personifies the hard edge of winter. She claims the frost and the failing light of the year. The named mountain sets her apart as a spirit of the old places. She shares a winter hag's role in seasonal folklore. In Gaelic lore she guards the bare ridges through the cold. She acts as one of several weather spirits of the north. Her tale ties her to the Cailleach Bheur and to the Cailleach Bhéirre. The end of the year belongs to her lonely form.

:::

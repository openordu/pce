::: magazinestyle
Custennin is a hero of Welsh tradition. His part in the tale of Kulhwch and Olwen is small. In that tale he is the herdsman of a giant. The giant is named Yspaddaden Penkawr, and he is the father of the maiden Olwen. MacKillop heads the entry with the alias Kistennin and calls the name a frequent one in early Welsh tradition. The best-known bearer served Culhwch as the shepherd who found the maiden Olwen. His son Goreu is the slayer of the giant Ysbaddaden.

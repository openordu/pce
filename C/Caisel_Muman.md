``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caisel Muman is the royal seat of the Eoganacht in the province of Munster. People today call the rock stronghold by the name Cashel. Kings of Munster ruled this seat for centuries. A round tower from the 10th century is all that remains of the early church. A carved sarcophagus dating to the 1100s or 1200s shows the Urnes animal style. In 1101 the high king Muirchertach Ua Brian gave the rock to the church. In 1111 the council held at Ráith Bressail made Caisel an archbishop seat second to Armagh. King Cormac Mac Cárrthaig built a chapel for the monks. Building ran from 1127 to 1134. The small chapel remains well preserved. Its carved stone marks deep contacts across western Europe. The ruined great church was built mostly between 1224 and 1289. State care began in 1874.

:::
```

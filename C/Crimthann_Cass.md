``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crimthann Cass is a king of Connacht in the Adventure of Lóegaire. MacKillop names him the father of Lóegaire, the hero whom the Otherworld draws away. The story belongs to the Echtrae Lóegairi, the tale of Lóegaire's ride to the realm of the dead. The father's part is small but the kinship fixes the hero's house. An old poem also lists this king among the dead of the otherworld host.

:::
```

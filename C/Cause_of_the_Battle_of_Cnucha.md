``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cause of the Battle of Cnucha is a title that points to the tale of how Cumhall died. MacKillop names it as the best-known account of that death. Cumhall led the Fianna before his son Fionn mac Cumhaill rose. Goll mac Morna slew Cumhall in the fighting at Cnucha. That hill is Castle Knock, close to Dublin.

:::
```

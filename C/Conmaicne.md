``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Conmaicne ranked among the peoples of early Ireland, perhaps kin to the Lagin. They spread across parts of Connacht and into north-western Leinster. Their branch west of Lough Corrib in Co. Galway took the name Conmaicne Mara, the people of the sea. That branch gave the district of Connemara its name. In eastern Galway the kindred called Conmaicne Rein held lands where the Tuatha De Danann first showed themselves in Ireland. The scholar T. F. O'Rahilly argued in 1946 that the Conmaicne were not a Goidelic people. Few students of the subject accept that view.

:::
```

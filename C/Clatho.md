::: magazinestyle
Clatho is a figure from Macpherson's Ossian, the work James Macpherson published from 1760 as translated ancient Gaelic epic. Modern scholarship treats it as his own literary invention. Within that invented world, Clatho is the daughter of Cuthulla. She becomes the second wife of the hero Fingal. Fingal's first wife is Roscrana. Clatho bears Fingal three children named Reyno, Fillean, and Bosmin. Her story survives only inside Macpherson's poems, and it belongs to that literary world rather than to genuine Gaelic tradition.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Crevroe is an English form of the Irish name Cráebruad. MacKillop marks it as the Anglicization of that hall of Ulster fame. Cráebruad is the Red Branch, the house of Conchobar's warriors at Emain Macha. Readers should follow the Cráebruad entry for the hall and its heroes.

:::

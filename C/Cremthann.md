::: magazinestyle
Cremthann is a variant spelling of Crimthann, a common name in early Ireland. MacKillop lists the form beside Crimthan, Criomthann, and Criofan. The name itself means fox in Irish. Kings of Munster, figures of the Fenian Cycle, and a saint all bore it. Readers should follow the Crimthann entry for the family of bearers.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Colann gun Cheann, also Coluinn gun Ceann, is Scottish Gaelic for body without head. The name belongs to a headless spirit of Scottish Gaelic folklore, one of the bòcan kind. Highland belief counted it a guardian spirit that kept faith with the Macdonald clan of Morar in the western Highlands, a region of old Inverness-shire. The creature haunted a mile-long path that ran from the Morar river to Morar House. It showed fierce hostility to strangers outside the family. Women and children, though, stayed beyond its malice. Folklorists classify the motif as E422.1.1. See bogan.

:::

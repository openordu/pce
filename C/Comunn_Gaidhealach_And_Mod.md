``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
An Comunn Gaidhealach formed in Oban in 1891. It advanced Scottish Gaelic literature and music. The society pressed for Gaelic teaching in Highland schools. It stayed non-political and drew gentry leaders. The word mod names an assembly in Gaelic. The Mòd became its great festival. It ran first in Oban in 1892. It has met every year since. Contests cover songs, poetry, and speech. Local branches staged private Mòdan from 1905. The event mirrors the Welsh eisteddfod. Critics saw the Mòd as old-fashioned. It still holds flagship status for Gaelic culture.

:::
```

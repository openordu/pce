``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cruachán Aigle is a variant form of Croagh Patrick, the older name of Ireland's holy mountain in Co. Mayo. It honoured Aigle, the man of legend said to have died on the slopes with stones in his pockets. The Irish shape of the older name survives in Cruach Phádraig, Patrick's stack. Readers should follow the Croagh Patrick entry for the mountain itself.

:::
```

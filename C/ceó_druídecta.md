::: magazinestyle
ceó druídecta is an Irish phrase that stands as a heading in this encyclopedia. MacKillop lists a variant spelling, ceo draoidheachte. The heading sends the reader to the Druid's Fog entry for the full account. MacKillop likewise routes the phrase to the féth fíada entry. No separate tale is told under this title. The form exists to catch variant spellings and to pass readers on to the fuller treatment of the magical mist.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corc mac Luigthic is a legendary king in the line of Munster. His fairy mother Fedelm presided over his installation at Cashel, and the rite left his ear singed, a mark that gave him his name. Some early writers read the name wrongly as red or crimson. A swineherd had foretold that he would reach Cashel and gain its kingship. Sent to the Pictish king in Scotland carrying on his shield an ogham message whose words ordered his death, Corc escaped when a scholar he had freed from slavery altered the letters into a welcome. The king received him warmly and gave him his daughter in marriage. On his return he founded two dynasties, the first at Femen and the second, the greater, at Cashel. Trapped in a snowstorm, he beheld a vision, a yew bush rising over a stone, angels in flight above it, and his druids told him that whoever lit a fire on that stone would hold Munster forever. The line of Eóganacht, the descendants of Eógan, kept the kingship of Cashel for centuries. MacKillop records Conall Core and Conall mac Luigthig among the forms of his name.

:::
```

Catoc, also written Catog, is a variant form of the name Cadog. Cadog ranks among the most honoured Welsh saints of the sixth century. He founded the monastery of Llancarfan in Glamorganshire. He planted roughly twenty further churches across south Wales. Invaders killed him around the year 577.

:::

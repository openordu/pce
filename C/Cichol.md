``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cichol, sometimes spelled Cical, led the Fomorians in the Lebor Gabála Érenn, the early Irish pseudo-history of successive invasions. Some texts attach the cognomen Gricenchos to his name, with Grichnos and Gricenchoss as further variants. He meets a rare setback when the arriving Partholonians defeat him. A note on the name links it to Irish cicul, a term for an astronomical cycle.

:::
```

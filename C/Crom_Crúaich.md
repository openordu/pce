::: magazinestyle
Crom Crúaich is the chief idol of the old Irish religion in Christian accounts. The gold image stood at Mag Slécht in Co. Cavan, ringed by twelve figures of stone. A shadowy king, Tigernmas, led the first worship of it. On each Samain the people gave the idol the firstborn of family and of livestock. Readers should follow the Crom Cruach entry for the full record of the cult.

:::

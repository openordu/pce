``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cobhthach is a name carried by several legendary figures of early Irish history, mostly in the south of the island. Variants include Cobhthach, Cofach, and Covac, while that Old Irish form became the surname Coffey. The name means victorious. One bearer was a legendary ancestor of the Eóganacht, the great family of Munster. He was father of Meilge, who caused the death of Aige, and Faifne satirized him for it. The best-known bearer is Cobthach Cóel Breg, the slender of Breg, a leading actor within the Cycle of Kings. He seized the kingship of Tara by killing his brother Lóegaire Lore and his nephew Ailill Áine. He sent Ailill's son Labraid Loingsech into exile. Cobhthach was cruel to Labhraigh Lore. He made the young brother swallow mice. The child was so frightened that he stopped speaking. Greedy for Leinster's provincial throne, Cobhthach put Labhraigh to death and took the province for himself. Labraid came home from banishment in Gaul and burnt Cobthach to death inside an iron house alongside thirty allies. Some stories hint that the early Uí Néill counted Cobthach among them.

:::
```

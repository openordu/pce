``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caillagh ny Gueshag is a further Manx name for the winter hag. Her Manx title means the old woman of the spells. She answers to the same spirit called Caillagh ny Groamagh. People on the Isle of Man meet both names in their talk of the cold season. She shares all the habits of that well known Manx figure. She keeps a careful eye on the first day of February. A clear start coaxes her outside to take the sun. A wet morning persuades her to stay inside her cottage. Her mood then sets the tone for the months ahead. The Manx read her choice like the Groundhog Day of Pennsylvania. She shows a close kin to the Cailleach Bheur of Gaelic Scotland. Together these tales weave rain and sun into the welcome of spring. Corn and its fields wait on her quiet decision.

:::
```

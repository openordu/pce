::: magazinestyle
Craignish is a district on the western shore of north Strathclyde in Scotland.
The name is Scottish Gaelic for rocky place.
The old county was Argyllshire, and the region sits just south of the town of Kilmelford.
A peninsula, a sea loch, and a castle all carry the name.
The area was once a reservoir of folklore.
The Craignish Tales open the first volume of the 1889 collection Waifs and Strays of Celtic Tradition.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcu Duibne is a people of western Co. Kerry. They held ground along both shores of Dingle Bay, north and south. In some writings Corcu Dhuibhne means the Dingle peninsula itself. The Cailleach Bhéirre, the Old Woman of Beare, belonged to them. Their ancestress may have been a divine figure named Dobinia. Cú Roí kept his fortress inside their territory. Land they once occupied holds some of the densest groupings of ogham-inscribed stones in Ireland. Gleann na nGealt, a valley full of wild men, lay among them too.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cairell was a son of the great champion Fionn mac Cumhaill. Goll mac Morna killed him during the bitter feud with his father. Goll stood as Fionn's sometime rival in that long struggle. The record of Cairell himself stays very brief in the tales. This one slaying sums up most of what texts recall about him. His death shows how tenacity of the family feud touched even the sons of the hero.

:::
```

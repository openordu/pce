``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Colum Cille was an Irish abbot who died in 597. He traced descent from Niall of the Nine Hostages. He founded several monasteries in Ireland before 563. That year he sailed to Iona. The island became the centre of his mission. He brought Christianity to the Picts from there. Adomnán wrote the Life of Columba about a century later. That text, the Vita Columbae, is our main record. It shows him as a scholar and scribe. The Cathach, an early psalter, is tied to his work. His exile made him a pilgrim for Christ. He shaped insular monastic practice on islands.

:::
```

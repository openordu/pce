``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cormoran is a giant of Cornish folklore who is said to have built St Michael's Mount. The islet stands near Marazion, three miles east of Penzance. He starts the building with white granite. Lazy and often asleep, he fails to notice his wife Cormelian at work. She carries green stone from a shorter distance. When Cormoran wakes he kicks her, and her dropped stone becomes the causeway that joins the islet to the mainland. English folklore also gives his name to the giant in the tale Jack the Giant-Killer.

:::
```

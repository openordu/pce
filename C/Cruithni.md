::: magazinestyle
Cruithni, also written Cruithnig, is an Irish name for the Picts. The word belongs with Cruithin, the Old Irish group name for peoples of Pictish connection. One early account makes the Cruithni the folk who preceded the Érainn in Ireland. Readers should follow the Cruithin entry for the peoples behind the name.

:::

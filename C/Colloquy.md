::: magazinestyle
Colloquy is MacKillop's cross-reference to ymddiddan, the Welsh colloquy genre. Colloquy of the Ancients, also called Colloquy of the Old Men, is the English title of Acallam na Senórach. This Middle Irish tale is the great Fenian dialogue-tale.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Crave Rua is an anglicized form of Cráebruad, the Red Branch hall of Ulster.
MacKillop records the short form Rue beside it.
The hall of Conchobar mac Nessa gave its name to the Red Branch warriors.
Readers should follow the Cráebruad entry for the full account.

:::

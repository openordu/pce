``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ceingalad carries the Welsh and Breton hero Gwalchmai fab Gwyar. The name also appears as Keingalet in Breton. French writers record the forms Gringolet and Guingalet. A further Breton variant is Grangalet. The meaning may be hard back or durable spine. Gwalchmai contributes to the romance figure Gawain in English tales and Gauvain in French ones.

:::
```

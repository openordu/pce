``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clydno Eiddyn is a prince of north Britain, the Old North the Welsh call the Hen Ogledd. His home lay around Eiddyn, the stronghold near modern Edinburgh that stories call Din Eidyn. Tradition says he left that northern world for Wales and spent his life there as a monk. His horse's halter ranks among the Thirteen Treasures, the famed objects of the Island of Britain. Through that halter his name endures in the roll of Britain's legendary treasures.

:::
```

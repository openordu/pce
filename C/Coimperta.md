``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coimperta is the plural form of Compert, an Irish word for conception, the act of begetting. The word names a genre of tales about how heroes are conceived and born. The plural gathers many such birth-tales of heroes under a single heading. See Compert.

:::
```

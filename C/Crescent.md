The Bloody Crescent is an alternative name of the idol Crom Cruach. MacKillop sends readers of this form straight to the god's own article. Crom Cruach is the Irish deity who received grim firstborn offerings. The image of the bent or bloody stone is a feature of that cult. Readers should follow the Crom Cruach entry for the full account.

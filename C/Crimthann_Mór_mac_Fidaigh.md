``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crimthann Mór mac Fidaigh is a shadowy king of pre-Patrician Ireland. MacKillop calls him third in line before the Lóegaire who disputes with the evangelist. He is of Munster stock and unrelated to the other kings at Tara. He would have ruled just before Niall Noigiallach, the man of the nine hostages. Many Irish records carry his name, yet O'Rahilly deems his historicity unworthy of credence. His reputed sister Mongfhind is clearly supernatural. Another otherworldly woman destroys him through sorcery. The cannibal Eithne Úathach is sometimes called his daughter. The fortress Dind Tradui stood beside the River Dee in Britain. Irish tradition sometimes gives it the name Dún Crimthainn for him. That fort must not be confused with the Howth fortress named for Crimthann Nia Náir. The tale of his death survives as the Aided Chrimthaind maic Fhidaig.

:::
```

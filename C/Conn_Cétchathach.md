``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conn Cétchathach is the Irish form of the name Conn of the Hundred Battles. The epithet means of the Hundred Battles or the Hundred-Fighter. The annals date this shadowy prehistoric king to the 2nd century. Tradition treats him as an ancestral figure for Irish kings. The Lia Fáil speaks first to him. That Stone of Prophecy shows him the kings of his line yet to reign and the coming of St Patrick. He stands as eponymous ancestor to the Goidels of the midlands. The province of Connacht takes its name from him. The main PCE article on this ruler sits at Conn of the Hundred Battles. See Conn of the Hundred Battles.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cermait means honey-mouthed in Irish. Writers also spell the name Cermat and Cearmaid. Two figures bear it. First, Cermait was a son of the Dagda. Lug Lamfhota killed him because Cermait had lain with Lug's wife. Cermait's son Mac Cuill later avenged him and speared Lug in return. Second, Ogma too bore this name. He serves as the god of poetry and of eloquence.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuchullin is a character of Macpherson's Ossian, the poem cycle of 1760. Macpherson built him in part on the Irish hero Cúchulainn, yet the figure departs from the older tales. Cuchullin is the son of Seuma and the grandson of Cathbaid, whom Macpherson alters from the Irish Cathbad. Slissama is his brother, and Bragela is his wife. His strength is a byword among the poems' warriors. In one memorable combat he slays the champion Feard.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
cantref is a Welsh division of land recorded in early Welsh literature, formed from cant, meaning hundred, and tref, meaning dwelling-place. The unit grouped around one hundred dwellings or hamlets. A cantref in turn comprised two or more commotes. The form cantred passed into English from Welsh, where it survives as a regional term.

:::
```

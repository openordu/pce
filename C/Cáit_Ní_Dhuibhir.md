::: magazinestyle
Cáit Ní Dhuibhir is a poetic personification of Ireland. Her name carries the sense of Kate, daughter of darkness. The older sense of the second half stays open to doubt. She belongs to the family of female figures in whom poets clothed the land itself. Her nearest kin in that company is Róisín Dubh, the Little Dark Rose. Readers may compare the two entries side by side.

:::

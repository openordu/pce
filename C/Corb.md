Corb, also spelled Corbb, is a god of the Fomorians in Lebor Gabála Érenn, the Book of Invasions. The name may relate to an Irish verb meaning defiles. The record of this deity is brief, and the Book of Invasions is the source that names him among the Fomorian kind.

:::

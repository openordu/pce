``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Clooth-na-Bare is a literary figure created by W. B. Yeats. He reshaped the older Cailleach Bhéirre of Irish tradition into this new persona. The change strips away the sovereignty role that the Irish hag carried. Instead Clooth-na-Bare has grown weary of her fairy life. She therefore hunts for the deepest lake of all, seeking water deep enough to drown that life.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Colmán is an early Irish personal name. It means woodpigeon or ring-dove, a diminutive of the Latin columb, dove. MacKillop counts at least 234 saints who bore the name. Genealogies and chronicles add many more shadowy figures called Colmán. One tradition names Suibne's father, a man who kept the old religion, among the name-bearers. A sixth-century poet, Colmán mac Céníni, carried the name into Wales as well. The name's spread tracks the cult of doves and churchmen across early Gaelic Europe.

:::

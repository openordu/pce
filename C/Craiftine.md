::: magazinestyle
Craiftine is a variant spelling of Craiphtine, the celebrated harper of early Ireland.
MacKillop records Craftine and Craftiny as further written forms.
The harper receives credit for inventing the harp from a willow tree.
Readers should follow the Craiphtine entry for the full account.

:::

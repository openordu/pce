::: magazinestyle
Cynfeirdd is the Welsh label for the earliest poets of Wales. It gathers the first bards of the tradition. Aneirin and Taliesin rank among its most famous members. Their work stands at the head of Welsh verse. Later writers looked back to it as the first voice of their craft.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Calum Cille is a Scottish Gaelic form of the name Colum Cille. Most readers know the same figure as Columba. He came from Ireland as an abbot. He founded monasteries there before 563. That year he sailed to the island of Iona. Iona grew into the centre of his mission. He brought Christianity to the Picts from Iona. Adomnan wrote the Life of Columba about him. See the fuller entry for Colum Cille below. This word only crosses the reader over to that account.

:::

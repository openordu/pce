``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caer Seint is one of the assembly sites of Bendigeidfran. The great hero marshals his forces there in the Mabinogi. The place lies within the land of Gwynedd. It faces the island of Anglesey across the open water. The Roman ruins of Segontium stand near that ground. So the site joins a Welsh tale to a Roman past. Bendigeidfran chose Caer Seint for a gathering in the old story. The name still marks a corner of Welsh country.

:::
```

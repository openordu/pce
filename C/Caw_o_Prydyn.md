``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caw o Prydyn is a hero of Welsh Arthuriana. His name also appears as Caw o Brydyn, and as Kaw in Breton. He came from Prydin, a land in Scotland, and travelled to the court of Arthur. The tale Culhwch ac Olwen gives him a leading part. There he attacks the great boar Ysgithrwyn Pen Beidd, splits its head in two, and takes its tusks. Caw then gathers blood still warm from the dark witch and carries it away. This blood serves to shave the giant Ysbaddaden.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corleck Hill is a promontory near Drumeague in Co. Cavan. Its Irish names include Sliabh na Tri nDée and Sliabh na nDée Dana, the hill of the three gods. People once called the place the pulse of Ireland. A stone head of Brigit was worshipped there. The hill held pagan worship at the feasts of Imbolc, Beltaine, Lughnasa, and Samain. The three gods behind the Irish name are probably Conn, Dagda, and Ogma. Earlier commentators suggested Brian, Iuchair, and Iucharba instead. Dub Chomar lies near the hill.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Children of Dôn stand for light and good, like the Tuatha Dé Danann. They often fight the Children of Llyr, the powers of dark and evil. Their mother is Dôn, the Welsh mother-goddess and sister of Math fab Mathonwy. Her noted children number at least five: the daughter Arianrhod and the sons Gwydion, Gilfaethwy, Gofannon, and Amaethon. The grouping thus gathers the chief figures of the Mabinogi's fourth branch.

:::
```

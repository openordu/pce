::: magazinestyle
Crann Buí is the yellow-hafted spear of the Fenian hero Diarmait Ua Duibhne.
The name is Irish for yellow tree, and writers also spell it Crann Buidhe.
MacKillop counts the weapon as one of three spears that belonged to Diarmait.
The dictionary's weapons index lists the spear beside famous arms such as Gáe Assail.
Readers should follow the Diarmait Ua Duibhne entry for the hero's story.

:::

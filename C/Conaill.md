::: magazinestyle
Conaill is a cross-reference headword for Irish clann and cenél names built on the root Conall. MacKillop uses it to gather the kindred names and points readers to two articles. The first treats Tír Chonaill, the kingdom of the Conailli in what is now County Donegal. The second treats Conall Gulban, nine-hostaged Niall's son, who raised that kingdom. See Conall Gulban.

:::

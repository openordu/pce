``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cúldub is a food-stealing villain of the Fenian Cycle. The form Cúldubh appears beside his name, which may mean black hair or black eye. He sometimes carries the patronymic mac Fidga. Fionn mac Cumhaill kills him as he carries off a pig. The killing happens at Sidh ar Femen, called Sliab na mBan, in County Tipperary. Some commentators make him yet another persona of Fionn's great antagonist Goll mac Morna.

:::
```

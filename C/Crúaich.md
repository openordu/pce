``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crúaich is a bare cross-reference form of the idol name Crom Crúaich. The dictionary entry holds no tale of its own. It points to the great gold idol of Mag Slecht that Christian writers condemn. Twelve stone figures ringed that idol, and King Tigernmas first led his people to honour it. Readers should follow the Crom Crúaich entry.

:::
```

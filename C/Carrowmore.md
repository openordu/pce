::: magazinestyle
Carrowmore is an Irish place name that means the big quarter in English. The site lies in County Sligo, several miles northeast of the town of Tubbercurry. Oral lore holds the mounds there to be burial places of the Fir Bolg. A field of megalithic stones spreads across the open ground. Travelers see cairns and circles of early date in this western land.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cochrann is the mother of the romantic hero Diarmait Ua Duibne and of Oscar in the Fenian Cycle. She is the daughter of Cathaír Mór, the legendary king of Leinster. In the ballads she is known as Cróchnat. One text records a dream of the king. In the dream a woman stayed pregnant for a long time. At last she delivered a son beside a fragrant songful fruit tree. The king's druid then read the dream. The lady proved to be the River Slaney flowing past Tara. Her child was the harbor at the river's mouth. The singing tree was the king himself.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Coinchenn, also called Coinchend, is the monstrous wife of Morgan and mother of the lovely Delbcháem. Her Irish name Coinchend joins the words for hound and head. It marks her as a dog-headed being. Her tale is the Echtrae Airt meic Cuinn, which tells how Art son of Conn won a bride. A death curse hangs over her house. The curse says she will die once her daughter is wooed. Coinchenn meets each wooer at the door of Delbcháem and takes his head. She beats most of the men who come. The heads of her victims go up on a bronze fence before her fort. Older tellings set that fence of poles ringing her dwelling. She keeps Delbcháem shut in a high tower. Art Mac Cuinn, king of Tara, comes to woo the maid. Only he can match her in fight, and even he needs fairy aid to win. Art kills Coinchenn and sets her head on a spike. Some tales give her the agnomen Cennfhata, the long-head.

:::
```

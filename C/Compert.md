``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Compert is an Irish word meaning conception, the act of begetting or procreation. It names a whole genre of tales about how heroes were begotten and born. The plural form is Coimperta. Combert is a variant spelling of the same term. Stories of this kind open the lives of figures such as Cúchulainn, whose birth required divine intervention and mortal fosterage. See Combert for the sibling form.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Creidne counts among the gods of Ireland, an artisan member of the magical Tuatha Dee Danann. His craft title was brazier, his work lying in bronze and gold and other fine metals. With Goibniu and Luchtar he made up the craft of the three deities, the na tree dui Dana. His people's weapons against the Fomorians came from his forge. Some tales name him the maker of the silver hand given to king Nuada on his wounding, a task other versions give to Cian Ceccht. A tale tells that Creidne drowned carrying gold out of Spain. Another figure of the name served among the Warrior Women beside the Fianna. MacKillop names the craft trio na tri dee Dana, and gives Credne its members' work. Credne shaped the rivets for spears. He also formed sword hilts and the raised rims and bosses of shields.

:::
```

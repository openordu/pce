``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Conary Mor is the Anglicized form of Conaire Mór. The underlying Irish name belongs to the high king celebrated in the saga of Da Derga's hostel ruin. That fuller article carries the birth omens, the bird bans, and the fall of the hostel. Readers who land here should follow the Irish form. See Conaire.

:::
```

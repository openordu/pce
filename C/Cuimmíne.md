``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cuimmíne is a pet form of the name Colum, and several early Irish saints bore it. The best known is Cuimmíne Fota, a tall man of the sixth and seventh centuries. He founded an abbey at Kilcummin in Offaly. His great work was the introduction of the Roman way of reckoning Easter. The abbot of Iona rebuked him for it, because Iona kept the Celtic reckoning dear to Colum Cille. A hymn attributed to him survives. Popular story makes him the child of an unlawful union. King Fiachna of west Munster fathered him on his own daughter Mugain. He also appears as the spiritual guide who forbids Liadain her love affair with Cuirithir.

:::
```

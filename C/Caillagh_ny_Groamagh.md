``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caillagh ny Groamagh is a Manx weather spirit. Her Manx name means old woman of gloominess and sullen witch. She is a probable variant of the Scottish Gaelic Cailleach Bheur. Her behaviour follows the first day of February. That date honors the feast of Bride and the old festival of Imbolc. If the sky is fine she comes outside to warm herself. If the day is wet she stays within her shelter. A bright first of February is read as a poor omen. The people see the sign as trouble for the whole year. The custom clearly mirrors the American Groundhog Day. That festival runs on the second day of February in Pennsylvania. Her sour temper is blamed on a hard fall. She tumbled into a crevice on the mountain Barrule. That summit measures 1842 feet on the Isle of Man. Some say sailors threw her out to sea instead. She then drifted back to the shores that sent her. Her other name is Caillagh ny Gueshag.

:::
```

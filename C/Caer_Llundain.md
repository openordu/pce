::: magazinestyle
Caer Llundain is a later Welsh name for London. The dictionary keeps the variant Llundein beside it. This newer word gave the English capital a form that fitted Welsh speech. It looked upon London as a fortified town under its own title. Daily users of the language carried the name with ease. The older phrase Caer Lludd lived on in memory all the while. Caer Llundain thus speaks the capital in a fresher and modern voice.

:::

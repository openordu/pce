``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caturix is a deity of the Continental Celts. Inscriptions honoring this little-known god surface across central Europe. His name appears to mean king of battle. The occupying Romans equated Caturix with Mars, their own war god. MacKillop reads the name itself as an epithet of the Gaulish Mars, not a separate deity. He remains an obscure figure in the surviving record.

:::
```

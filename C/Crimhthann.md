::: magazinestyle
Crimhthann is a variant spelling of Crimthann, one of the commonest early Irish names. MacKillop lists it among forms that include Crimthan, Criomthann, Cremthann, and Criofan. The name means fox in Irish. Kings of Munster bore it, many Fenian heroes bore it, and a saint bore it too. Readers should follow the Crimthann entry for the well-known bearers.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cooley is a peninsula in the north-east of County Louth in Ireland, between Dundalk Bay and the waters of Carlingford Lough. Cuailnge is the Irish form of the name. Many of the climactic scenes of the Irish epic Táin Bó Cuailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley, play out on this ground. The name also points readers to the fuller account under Cuailnge.

:::
```

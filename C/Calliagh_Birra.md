::: magazinestyle
Calliagh Birra is the Ulster form of Cailleach Bhéirre, the famed hag of Ireland. The Irish name carries the plain sense of an old woman. Ulster speakers keep this variant alive for the same grim figure. Cailleach Bhéirre is the sovereignty person of the old kings. She meets a hero as a plain old woman who asks for his love. When he grants that love, she turns into a young maiden. That change renews the land and its kings. Calliagh Birra shares this same shape in the north. The story proves the hag legend survived far outside Munster. The Ulster form stands as a strong branch of the one great tale.

:::

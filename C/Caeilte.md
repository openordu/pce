``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Caeilte is a variant spelling that MacKillop gives for the name Cailte. Several men bore this name, and seven of them stood among the Fenians. The most celebrated is the running hero Cailte mac Rónáin, whom many stories account a nephew of Fionn mac Cumhaill. He won fame for the swiftness of his feet. Caeilte filled the office of steward at the side of Fionn. Once he aided his lord in rounding up two beasts of every kind because Gráinne sought them. He owned the power to slay giants. With a golden tongue he recited tales and poems. In the Colloquy of the Elders this runner endures into the Christian age. He pleads the older values before Saint Patrick. Several verses in praise of the open land carry his authorship.

:::
```

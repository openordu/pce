::: magazinestyle
Caractacus is a corrupt Latin form of the Welsh name Caradog. Roman writers used this spelling for the Celtic chieftain Caradog, a son of Cynfelyn. Carried to Rome after his defeat in Britain, he stood before the emperor Claudius. His dignity there earned him a pardon and his freedom. Another face of the name appears across stage plays and music. This headword sends the reader to the fuller Caradog entry.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Cruach Phádraig is the Irish form of Croagh Patrick. The name means Patrick's stack, or cone, for the holy mountain of Co. Mayo. Irish writers kept this shape in older accounts of the pilgrimage. Readers should follow the Croagh Patrick entry for the mountain's stories.

:::

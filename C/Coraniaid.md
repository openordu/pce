``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Coraniaid, also called the Coranians, form a demonic people who invade Britain and work havoc in the tale Cyfranc Lludd a Llefelys. Their name may relate to Welsh words for a dwarf. They rank among the three invaders that threaten the kingdom of Lludd, and they can hear every sound the wind carries. Writers have linked them in a general way with fairies, yet their malice runs far deeper than that of the tylwyth teg whom Welsh oral tradition remembers.

:::
```

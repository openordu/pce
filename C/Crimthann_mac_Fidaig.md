::: magazinestyle
Crimthann mac Fidaig is the shorter form of the name of Crimthann Mór mac Fidaig. MacKillop uses this headword as a direct pointer to the long article. That king is the shadowy pre-Patrician ruler of Munster stock. Readers should follow the Crimthann Mór mac Fidaig entry for his reign and his death by sorcery.

:::

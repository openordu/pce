::: magazinestyle
Cuartag Mhòr a’ Chuain is a Scottish Gaelic epithet. The phrase means the great whirlpool of the sea. Gaelic story pins this name on Cirein Croin, the monstrous sea-beast of lore. Tales give that beast a bulk big enough to fill the ocean. This entry records the epithet and its gloss.

:::

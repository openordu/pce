``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cofach is a variant spelling of the name Cobthach. The name family also appears as Cobhthach and Covac. Its best-known bearer is Cobthach Cóel Breg, the legendary king of Tara. The exiled Labraid Loingsech killed him in an iron house. MacKillop treats this headword as a cross-reference. See the Cobhthach entry for the full story.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Crunniuc, also called Cronnchu or Crunnchu, is a wealthy farmer of Ulster in the Ulster Cycle. Some writers give him the patronymic mac Agnomain. The goddess Macha, a fairy queen by some accounts, chose this mortal man and shared his farm. She brought riches and good fortune to both the man and his land. When Crunniuc meant to ride to the Assembly of Ulster, Macha was already in her last hours of pregnancy. She begged him to stay, but he would hear none of it. So she won his word that he would name her to no one and never say she lived with him. Crunniuc broke that vow, boasting that his wife could outrun any horse in a public race. Macha won that contest, only to fall at the line. The twins came even as she died. Her curse after the race set the Debility of the Ulstermen on the whole region. The records fall silent on Crunniuc after his wife's death, yet he can hardly have stayed well liked.

:::
```

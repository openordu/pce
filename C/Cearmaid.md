``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cearmaid is the modern spelling of Cermait. The older name means honey-mouthed. Tradition makes Cermait a son of the Dagda. The god Lugh Lámfhota killed Cermait. The cause was Cermait's sexual transgression with Lug's wife. Cermait's son Mac Cuill later avenged his father. He speared Lugh in payment for the killing. The name Cermait also belongs to Ogma. Ogma is the god of poetry and eloquence. Writers give that god the same name as the slain son of the Dagda.

:::
```

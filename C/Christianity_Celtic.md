``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The term Celtic Christian made one sacred family of all Celtic speakers. Modern scholars no longer accept any single Celtic Church. Practice of the faith varied widely across the lands. An Easter reckoning in an 84 year cycle spread through the region. That system travelled along with the devotion linked to Martin of Tours. Tonsure and baptism habits differed from place to place. Irish clerics followed a fuller Latin faith. No custom proved unique to the Celtic peoples alone. The story of a separate Celtic Church arose in the 19th century.

:::
```

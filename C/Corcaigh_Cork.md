``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Corcaigh, better known as Cork, is an Irish settlement that spans both a city and a county; its name likely comes from corcach, the word for marsh. Cork grew from a seventh-century monastic settlement near the cathedral named for Finbarr, its patron saint, and later became a Viking town. It grew into a central learning centre in the Middle Ages and throughout the early twentieth century played a major part and suffered heavy damage during the fight for independence. It now hosts University College Cork, a constituent college that belongs to the National University of Ireland.

:::
```

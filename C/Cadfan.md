::: magazinestyle
Cadfan is a Welsh saint of the sixth century. His name blends the Welsh cadf for battle with ban for summit. Cadfan founded a monastery on Bardsey Island. Welsh speakers call the island Ynys Enlli. The island lies in the north of Cardigan Bay. Pilgrims sought out that abbey for calm worship. The monastery made Bardsey Island a lasting site of faith. Cadfan's holy work kept the island within the northern Welsh coast.

:::

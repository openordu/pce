``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cilydd was a Welsh hero and the son of Celyddon Wledig. As the father of the young hero Kulhwch, he urged the lad to search for his true love, the maiden Olwen. After his wife died, Cilydd looked for a new wife whose rank matched his own, and that search opens the tale Culhwch ac Olwen. The daughter of his new bride was Olwen herself, the maiden his son had to win. Cilydd sent Kulhwch to King Arthur of Camelot, his cousin, so that the young man could ask for help in winning his bride.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The certificate of Urdd Gobaith Cymru admits a young person to the Welsh League of Youth. Urdd Gobaith Cymru is the Welsh word for the order. Members receive this certificate when they join. It shows the member number on the paper. The holder promises to keep seven rules. One rule is to speak Welsh with every child. Another rule is to buy and read Welsh books. Members must also sing Welsh songs. They must play in Welsh at all times. The leader Ifan ab Owen Edwards signed the certificate. The organisation runs a large annual youth festival. Around 45,000 members compete in it.

:::
```

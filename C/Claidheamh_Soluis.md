``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Claidheamh Soluis is the sword of light, a symbol of Ireland that oral tradition links to the hero Cúchulainn. In one tale an ugly giant bullied a poor widow into retrieving the weapon from beneath another giant's head. Patrick Pearse and the rebels of the 1916 Rising preferred the unreformed spelling Claidheamh Soluis. Print still favours the unreformed spelling over the reformed form Claiomh Solais. An older name attached to the blade is Cruaidin Catuchenn.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Cumian is a Latinized spelling of Cuimmíne. The Latin shape marks the saint's name in church writing, the same family of forms that carries the Cumaine variant. The best-known bearer founded Kilcummin abbey and pressed the Roman Easter reform that Iona resisted. Readers should follow the Cuimmíne entry.

:::
```

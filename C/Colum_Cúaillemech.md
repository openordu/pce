Colum_Cúaillemech, also written Colum Cúaillemech, is a smith of the Tuatha Dé Danann. His byname compares to the Irish word cúaille, a stake or pole. MacKillop gives the figure a short entry and sends readers onward to the great smith-god Goibniu. The pairing marks Colum Cúaillemech as one of the craft figures gathered under the Tuatha Dé Danann's forge lore.

:::

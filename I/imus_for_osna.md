::: magazinestyle
imus for osna is a MacKillop variant spelling of the Irish Druidic divination system imbas forosnai. MacKillop treats the form as a brief pointer that resolves to the canonical headword. The fuller record of the seer's ritual, its appearance in early Irish literature, and its use in finding hidden objects sits under imbas forosnai.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Inis Fáil is a poetic name for the isle of Ireland. The phrase means the island of destiny. The title comes from the standing stone of the land of the high kings. Used with the other poetic title for the country, it speaks of the old age of the island.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Iuchra is a name given to two women in Irish tradition. The first changes Aife, the wife of Manannan mac Lir, into a crane. When the bird dies, the sea god makes his crane bag from her skin. A second Iuchra, daughter of Abartach, turns a rival Aife into a heron. Both tales belong to the shape-changing lore of the otherworld.

:::

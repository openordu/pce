``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Inber Colptha is the old Irish title for the mouth of the River Boyne on the east coast of Ireland. The name also covers the broad estuary of the Boyne where that river meets the sea. Many of the early events of Irish history and myth belong to this stretch of water. The most famous of those events is the landing of the Milesians told in the Book of Invasions. That landing opens the old tale of how a people came to the island. The inber that names this place belongs to a wider family of river-mouth names across Ireland. Old Irish records the word as inber, with the spelling indber beside it. The same word sits at the head of countless place-names throughout the country. Each of those names marks a point where a river yields to the tide.

:::
```

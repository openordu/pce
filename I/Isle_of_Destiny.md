``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Isle of Destiny is the English form of the Irish name Inis Fail. The term is a poetic title for the island of Ireland. It points to the stone Fail at Tara, said to cry out beneath the true king. A claimant of the kingship stood upon the stone to prove his right. Isle of Destiny therefore keeps the memory of the stone of sovereignty in an English phrase.

:::
```

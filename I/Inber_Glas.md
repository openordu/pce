::: magazinestyle
Inber Glas is an Irish phrase that means green harbour. It is the title by which the old Irish knew the long water where the River Bann enters the sea. That estuary lies today in County Derry of Northern Ireland. After the young woman Tuag drowned in these waters, some called the place Tuagh Inber.

:::

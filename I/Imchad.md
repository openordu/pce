``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Imchad is a figure found in the older Irish texts. The older accounts call him the cruel husband of Uime. Uime is the woman who later marries the hero Cuchulainn. In these early stories Imchad stands only as her first husband. The narrative says nothing further about him.

:::
```

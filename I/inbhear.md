``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
inbhear is a modern Irish equivalent of the geographical headword inber. MacKillop treats inbhear and inbher as alternate modern spellings that resolve to the same record. The fuller record of the river-mouth place-name element in Celtic geography sits under the headword inber.

:::
```

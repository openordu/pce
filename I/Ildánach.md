``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ildánach is an Irish word that means master of many arts. The name comes from the words for many and for art. It is a shortened form of the title Samildánach. That title was applied to the god Lug Lámfhota. The full form means all-crafts or master of all the arts. Ildánach therefore names the many-skilled god of the Irish. The title appears widely in the literature of the gods.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Íor is a modernized spelling of Ír noted in MacKillop's Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The dictionary records the form as a single-line cross-reference, redirecting the reader to the longer article on Ír. No independent narrative, genealogy, or name-bearing episode attaches to the spelling itself; the entry functions as a typographical signpost rather than a stand-alone mythographic node. MacKillop treats Íor as identical with the older Middle Irish form Ír, the third son of Míl Espáine, after whom Ireland is named in the Lebor Gabála [Book of Invasions] tradition.

:::
```

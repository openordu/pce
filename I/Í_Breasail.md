::: magazinestyle
Í Breasail records speculative Irish-language forms for the phantom island known in English as Hy Brasil. MacKillop's Dictionary of Celtic Mythology lists the forms under the heading Í Breasail, with Í Breasil as an alternate spelling, and explains both as attempts to give an Irish-language name to the island that appears on maps west of Ireland. The forms are speculative because Hy Brasil itself has no fixed Irish-language pedigree. PCE treats this entry as a signpost rather than a body article, pointing readers to the full Hy Brasil article for the cartographic and folkloric detail. The MacKillop entry is short, occupying roughly the line that introduces the OCR-corrupted reading f Breasail alongside the corrected Í Breasail form.

:::

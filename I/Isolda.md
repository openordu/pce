``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Isolda is a variant spelling of the name Iseult. The Arthurian story of Tristan and Iseult gives two women one title. Iseult of Ireland loves Tristan, though she had sworn to avenge her father on him. Iseult of the White Hands makes an unreal marriage with the same hero. Isolda therefore stands as one of several spellings of the heroine of the romance.

:::
```

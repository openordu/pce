::: magazinestyle
Inber Domnann is the old Irish name for Malahide Bay. The bay lies on the coast north of Dublin. The name comes from the Domnainn, a people of the Laginian settlement. Both the Fir Bolg and the king Tuathal Techtmar also land here in the old accounts. The inlet is one of several by this title in early Irish story.

:::

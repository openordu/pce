::: magazinestyle
Inis Fionnchuire is a place in early Irish story. It is an island imagined to lie beneath the sea. The isle is peopled by women of the other world. In the tale of the sons of Tuirean, the heroes are sent to bring a rod from this island. The name also has other spellings in the old texts.

:::

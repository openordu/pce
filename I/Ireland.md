::: magazinestyle
Ireland is the large island that lies to the west of Britain. It is the second largest of the islands of the old world and the largest of all the Celtic lands. The Irish name of the country takes the form Eire, from the early name of the earth-goddess. Most of the island came together in 1922 to form a new state, later the Republic of Ireland. The small north-east corner was kept apart as Northern Ireland. The island keeps one of the oldest written tongues of the western world. The poets of the land called it by many names, among them the noble isle and the island of destiny.

:::

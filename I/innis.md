``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
innis is the Scottish Gaelic word for island. MacKillop treats innis as the Gaelic form of the same place-name element recorded under inis. The fuller record of the Celtic island-place-name element, its Irish counterpart, and the Welsh cognate ynys sits under the headword inis.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Iarlly Cawg is the Welsh name given to the Knight of the Fountain. The title belongs to Owain in the Welsh Arthurian romance. After Owain defeats the Knight of the Fountain and marries the lady of the fountain, he takes this title. The Welsh words carry the sense of lord of the cup or of the fountain. Owain governs the land for three years as the new knight of the fountain. In the end he is restored to his own kingdom and keeps the name. Iarlly Cawg is thus the Welsh title of the hero of the romance of the Lady of the Fountain.

:::
```

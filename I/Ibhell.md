::: magazinestyle
Ibhell is a variant spelling of the name Aibell. She was a goddess and fairy queen of the north of Munster, and a sponsor of the O'Brien family. Her fairy mound is at Craig Liath, near Killaloe in County Clare. She is sometimes compared with the goddesses Áine and Clidna. In one story she rivals Clidna for the love of Caomh. Clidna put a spell on her and turned her into a cat of white fur. In other accounts she accompanied Brian Bóruma to the battle of Clontarf. There she became the lover of an attendant of the king's son. Ibhell therefore names a powerful fairy queen of the south-west of Ireland.

:::

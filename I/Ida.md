``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ida was a king in north Britain. He ruled from 547 to 559. Ida was the first Anglian king of Brynaich. The Anglo-Saxon Chronicle records him at 547. Beda also names Ida in his Historia Ecclesiastica. Ida founded the dynasty of King Oswald and several others. His power stayed near the coast of Northumberland. Ida built the fort at Bamburgh. Welsh sources call that fort Din Guairoi. Ida fought the kings of the northern Britons. He was a contemporary of Maelgwn Gwynedd. His dates anchor many undated Celtic events.

:::
```

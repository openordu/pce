::: magazinestyle
The Irish World-Chronicle is a name given to a piece of early Irish annalistic writing. The fragment has come down to us in one old manuscript at Oxford. Editors have set it beside the Annals of Tigernach in printed books. The work shows the hand of the early annalists at their task.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Iomramh is an Irish word that means a voyage by sea. It also gives its name to a class of sea stories. In these tales a traveller sets out over the water in a small boat. The modern word stays close to the oldest forms of the same account.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Íth is the first of the Milesians to set foot on Ireland in the pseudo-history of the Lebor Gabála. He is a son of Breogan in Spain. He sails to the island to settle a quarrel among the Tuatha Dé Danann. His eloquence alarms the disputants, who fear he means to claim their kingship. They kill him. His nephew Míl Espáine leads the invasion that avenges his murder. The Irish map carries several places named for him, including Mag Ítha in County Donegal. Medieval genealogists placed him at the head of many pedigrees. The fictitious king Eochaid mac Luchta claimed descent from Íth. The hero Lugaid mac Con was given a Goidelic heritage by an invented patronymic Lugaid mac Ítha. Íth is often confused with Ír.

:::
```

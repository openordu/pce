``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bresal Bélach belongs to the shadowy figures of early Ireland. The old books name him the builder of Bare Bresail. That was a timber fortress shaped like a boat set upon dry land. He is said to have defeated Cairbre Lifechair at the storied battle of Cnámross. He may well have won that field with the help of Fionn mac Cumhaill standing with him.

:::
```

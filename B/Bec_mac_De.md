``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bec mac De stands as the finest seer of his era. He held the rare gift of speaking to nine men at the same time. He could answer every question they asked with a single reply. This talent placed his counsel in high demand among the curious. He fixed his sight upon Diarmait mac Cerbaill and foretold the manner of that king's death. He declared that Diarmait would lose his life inside the home of Banban the hospitaller. His words cast a dark warning over the ruler's fate. Many came to seek his insight before they made their plans.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Barrex is a god of Roman Britain, also written Barrecis. His name may hold the sense of supreme or of a high god. He is known from a single inscription found at Carlisle. The Romans set him beside their own god of war, whom they called Mars. Little more survives of his worship.

:::

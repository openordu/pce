::: magazinestyle
Brandan is one more way to spell the name Brendan. MacKillop lists Brandan and Brandon as two forms of the same holy man's name. Brendan the Voyager was an Irish saint famed for his sea travels. Readers who meet the spelling Brandan should turn to the entry named Brendan for the fuller account of that saint.

:::

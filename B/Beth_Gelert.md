::: magazinestyle
Beth Gelert is an alternate way to spell the English glen-name Bedd Gelert, listed in the dictionary as a cross-reference. A reader who wants the Welsh legend should turn instead to the fuller Bedd Gelert entry. There lies the tale of the loyal hound of Prince Llywelyn, set in Gwynedd, whose memorial stone stood at Beddgelert. That story tells how the prince saved his child from a wolf and later mourned the faithful dog.

:::

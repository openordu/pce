``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bríg is another common spelling that Celtic writers use for the name of the honoured woman whom the dictionary lists under Brigid and older texts often set down as Brigit. The plain form and the accented variant Brigh both appear in the source book. This entry acts only as a cross reference that points the reader toward the article on the celebrated saint and goddess, because it holds no separate narrative of its own.

:::
```

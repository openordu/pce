The Book of Hy Many is another name for the Book of Uí Maine. Its Irish title is Leabhar Uí Maine. The codex dates from the late fourteenth or early fifteenth century. The O'Kelly family long held it. They were descendants of the Uí Maine sept. Their lands covered much of County Galway and parts of Roscommon. See the entry Book of Uí Maine for the full account.

:::

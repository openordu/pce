::: magazinestyle
Bolcáin is a valley, sometimes called Glenn Bolcáin. It appears in several early Irish tales as the home of exiled madmen. Story of the Suibne, driven to frenzy, dwells there in the Buile Shuibhne. The place is marked as Glenbuck, near Rasharkin in County Antrim. A dropped state through the surrounding wild sets the tone for the whole narrative.

:::

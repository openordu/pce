``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brumo names a place of worship found inside the poems that James Macpherson passed off as Ossian. Macpherson set this invented shrine on the isle of Craca amid the Shetland group. His Ossian appears to date from 1760. The name shows up only inside that fabricated landscape and never in any genuine Celtic record.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bé Find is a variant form of the Irish name Bébinn. Both spellings hold the same meaning of a fair woman. The dictionary gives Bé Find only as a guide to the main head. The full account rests under the entry Bébinn. Many women carried the name across early Ireland. One such figure was a goddess who guarded the birth of infants. That same goddess acted as sister to Boand and as mother of a champion. Another woman with the name appears as a daughter of the wizard Elcmar. A reader who faces the term Bé Find should consult the Bébinn entry for the full tale.

:::
```

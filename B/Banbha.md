``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Banbha is an old poetic name for the island of Ireland, written also as Banba. She is one of three goddess figures, with Eriu and Fodla, who each offered a name to the land. In the tale, the Milesians used the names but let that of Eriu stand. First the name marked only a southern part or the Meath plain, later all of Ireland.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baiscne names a clan that runs through the old tales of Ireland. It ranks among the two great families inside the Fianna of the Fenian Cycle. The chief of this clan was the hero Fionn mac Cumhaill. A separate clan, the clan Morna, followed a rival leader. The spelling shifts across the sources, and Baiscne takes the forms Baoiscne and a host of variants.

:::
```

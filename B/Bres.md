``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bres is a name carried by one of the great figures of Irish tale. The best known Bres was born to the king of the far people and a lady of the bright folk. He rose to the high seat when the true king was put aside for a wound. His rule at last brought the sharp verse of a poet, and that verse drove him from the throne. He passed to the side of the far people. He fell at the great battle of the plain of the two armies.

:::
```

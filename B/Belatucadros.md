``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Belatucadros carries the meaning of fair and shining as a name. He was a god honoured across the northern region of Britain under Roman rule. His devotees gathered most thickly in what people today call Cumberland and Westmorland. Humble soldiers and civilians who were not Roman citizens seem to be the ones who courted his favour. The many ways his name was written suggest his worshippers had little reading skill. In what he stood for he is close to the god Belenus. The Romans linked him with Mars, their deity of war. Some hold that Cocidius is only another form of the same god under Belatucadros. His altars look much like those raised for Vitiris. A few thinkers count him as a forerunner of Bran the Blessed, who is also named Bendigeidfran. Belatucadros appears nowhere with a female partner.

:::
```

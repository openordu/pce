``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brân Llyr names a sea god who later ruled as a king of Britain. His other title in the old books is Bendigeidfran. That name means the Blessed. Christian scribes chose it because they thought Brân brought the faith to Wales. The hero stands as the son of the lord Llŷr and as brother to Branwen and Manawydan. The giant could wade across the channel that splits Britain and Ireland. He owned a magic horn that poured out food for his men. The Mabinogi sets the story of Branwen as its second tale. That tale plays out while the giant holds the sway. A poisoned spear ended his life. Before death he asked that his head be cut off and set upon the White Hill in London. The head faced France so the land stood safe from invaders. His friends fed and kept that head at Harlech for eighty seven years. Later Arthur dug up the head so his own men could guard the island. After that the head looked over the otherworldly island of Gwales.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bregia is the latin form of the name given to the eastern plain of Ireland. One word marks the same land between the Boyne and the Liffey as the native name Brega. The latin form appears in the books of the later writers who shared the same ground. So Bregia and Brega name one old district of the island.

:::
```

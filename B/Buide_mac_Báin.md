``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buide mac Báin figures inside the Táin Bó Cuailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley. Early in that epic he is the one driving the bull Donn Bui Cuailnge. Cúchulainn kills him in the fight, yet the animal still escapes. Buide counts as the son of Bán Blai, whom some copies name Benblai. A few accounts give Buide the title of fool. The term buide may link to a word meaning yellow, grateful, or foolish.

:::
```

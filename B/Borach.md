``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Borach is a man who appears in the legend of Deirdre and her lover Noise. The medieval Gaelic tale Longes mac nUislenn never gives him a name. At Conchobar's request he arranges a large banquet that keeps Fergus away from his duty for a while. Fergus had pledged himself as a surety that the couple would come back safely to Emain Macha. The feast held him firm there, so Fergus could not reach them in time and Noise met his death. Later narrators fill out this figure with far more detail. James Stephens in his 1923 book Deirdre describes him as a shark hunter.

:::
```

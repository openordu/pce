``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bréifne is the name of a region of early Ireland. The district covers the counties of the north central part of the country. Old books and a learned journal carry the same name. The area kept a separate identity and a firm place in the old memory of the land. Its tales belong to the early people of the north. The name holds a long story that links the region to the wider island.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
The name Bodach an Chóta Lachtna means the churl in the grey coat. He is a figure in folk-tales of the Fenian Cycle, and some of those stories carry his name as a title. This Bodach is large and homely to look at, but he still aids the Fenians. He takes part in a foot-race against a challenger who is sometimes called Ironbones. Although he can outrun anyone, he feigns disinterest and picks blackberries while the others compete. At the end the story tells that he is the sea-god Manannán mac Lir wearing a disguise. A fuller telling is found in Echtra Bhodaigh an Chóta Lachtna.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Boadach rules the Land of the Living. His name means victorious or triumphant in Irish. A fairy woman in the tale Echtrae Conli, the Adventure of Connla, tells of this land. The Land of the Living is a variation of Mag Mell, the Pleasant Plain. His rule places him in the otherworld of early Irish story.

:::
```

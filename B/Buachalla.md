``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buachalla is the latter half of the name Mes Buachalla, which the source book renders also as Mess Buachalla and in one word as Messbuachalla. The term identifies the young woman described in early Irish narrative as a cowherd's fosterling, whose origin tale shifts from one text to another. This entry exists to direct readers toward the fuller article on Mes Buachalla, where her links to Cormac and the Ulster cycle figures are set out.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buí names one of the four wives that the Irish hero possessed in the ancient tales. The hero carried the name Lugh and stood as a leading figure in the great stories. Buí takes her place among his several wives in the epic material of Ireland. Under this name she holds a link to the great monument at Knowth in County Meath. She also stands the same as the old figure behind Irish and Scottish folklore known as the Cailleach. This wife Buí therefore joins the hero in the remembered world.

:::
```

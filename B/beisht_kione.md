::: magazinestyle
beisht kione is Manx for the beast with a black head. MacKillop records the creature as yn beisht kione. The word names a sea-monster of Manx coastal legend. Fishermen and sailors believed it lived in the waters off the southern shore of the Isle of Man. All who sailed that coast held the beisht kione in dread. The creature belongs to the dark seamonster lore that surrounds Manx fishing waters.

:::

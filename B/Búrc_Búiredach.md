``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Búrc Búiredach was the father of a famed princess. That daughter was the legendary Érne. She placed her name upon a great waterway. The waterway is the river Erne. Érne left a lasting mark upon the land. Búrc Búiredach appears nowhere else in the tales. His honour rests upon his daughter. He gave Ireland a princess who named the Erne waterway.

:::
```

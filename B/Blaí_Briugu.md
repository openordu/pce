::: magazinestyle
Blaí Briugu is an Ulster warrior in early Irish legend. His name carries the sense of a hospitaller, one who gives generous welcome. He wins renown for great hospitality. The hero serves as a fosterer of Cúchulainn. He promises that the child would meet neither contempt nor neglect in his care. Blaí Briugu likes to sleep with many women. The warrior Celtchair kills him for that habit.

:::

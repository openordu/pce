``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bresal Etarláim was a druid of the Irish tales. He handed Fuamnach the spells she needed to cast Etain out of Midir. This deed appears in Tochmarc Etaíne, the Wooing of Etain. In one telling he stands as Fuamnach's foster-father, while in another he is her lover. For that bond Midir takes Fuamnach's life.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Uí Maine carries the Irish title Leabhar Uí Maine. It is also called the Book of Hy Many. The codex is smaller and dates from the late fourteenth or early fifteenth century. The O'Kelly family held it for a long time. They were the descendants of the Uí Maine sept. That sept in medieval times ruled much of what is now County Galway and lands in Roscommon. The book holds parts of the Lebor Gabála, poems, genealogies, and pedigrees. R. A. Stewart MacAlister edited it in Dublin in 1942.

:::
```

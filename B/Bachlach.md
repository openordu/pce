``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bachlach is an Irish word for a rough or lowly man, a servant, or a worker on the land. English readers often meet it as churl. In the stories the word marks the strong man who stands before the heroes and sets them a test. He braves the warriors of Ulster in the hall with a hard asking of their heads.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The wild boar held deep symbolic weight for the ancient Celts. Art and coinage from the Continent and the islands show it. Writers linked the Gaulish god Moccus to the Roman Mercury, and he may have stood for a pig or a boar. In the Welsh tale Culhwch and Olwen, Arthur's men first hunted Ysgithrwyn, a chief boar called the white tusk, before the great chase of Twrch Trwyth and his offspring. The Middle Welsh name hob set the tame pig's origin in the Otherworld. In the Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi, a sow from the Otherworld guided Gwydion to the slain Lleu.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Bocánach is an Irish term for a goblin with the form of a goat. The creature ranks among the demons of the battlefield in Irish tradition. MacKillop notes its frequent association with the bánánach. Bocánachs shrieked through the air while Cúchulainn fought Ferdiad.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Black_Dog appears across Irish, Scottish, and British legends. This spectral creature is shaggy and as large as a calf. It stands as a sign that great change and death will come, yet one glance from its eyes can kill. Local people give the creature regional names. They call it the Barguest in northern England and Black Shuck in East Anglia. They call it the Shriker in Cumbria and Mauthe Doog on the Isle of Man. People reported the creature at Irish sites in the 1990s. MacKillop gives the black dog a brief entry of cross-references. The entry points readers to dog, to moddey dhoo, and to mauthe doog. The Manx form moddey dhoo thus joins Mauthe Doog among the names of the creature on the Isle of Man.

:::
```

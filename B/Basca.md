``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Basca is one more spelling of the clan name Baiscne. The forms Basca, Bascna, and Baskin all appear in the sources. Baiscne is the family of the hero Fionn in the Fenian tales. Basca thus belongs to that great fighting clan of old Ireland, home of Fionn mac Cumhaill.

:::
```

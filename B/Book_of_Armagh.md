``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Armagh carries the Latin name Liber Ardmachanus. Scribes began it about 807 under Feardomnach in Armagh, the seat of Ireland's primate. The codex holds materials in Latin and in Irish. The Irish passages rank among the earliest that survive. Many Latin sections tell of Saint Patrick. People once honored them as written in his own hand. A later addition from the eleventh century speaks of Brian Bórama. It calls him the Emperor of the Irish. The manuscript rests today at Trinity College in Dublin.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Báinleannán is a female spirit in the folk tales of Ireland told by word of mouth. She keeps company with heroes who set out on quests. She acts as a kind of fairy lover. Those tales compare her to the leannán sídhe. Oral tradition holds her close to questing figures.

:::
```

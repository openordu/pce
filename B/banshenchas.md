``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The banshenchas, the Lore of Women, is a Middle Irish text from the twelfth century. It lists famous women, chiefly Irish, along with their husbands and children. The work grew over roughly two hundred years and survives in verse and in prose. The verse copy confines itself to the lineage of Adam and Eve. The prose copy adds names from the Bible and the classical past. The account follows a set chronology. It starts with the Mythological Cycle and the Lebor Gabála Érenn, the history of Ireland's settlement. It passes through the Ulster Cycle and the Fiannaíocht, then ends with the kings of Ireland.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bladma counts among the Milesian settlers who invaded Ireland in early legend. The ridge named Sliab Bladma, known today as the Slieve Bloom, bears his title. Irish tradition says those mountain peaks drew their name from his own. Bladma himself leaves no fuller story in the old records. The range still keeps his memory alive across its slopes.

:::
```

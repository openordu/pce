::: magazinestyle
Bec mac Buain kept a well of wisdom at Cahemarry in County Limerick. The legend appears in the story titled the Feast at the House of the hero Conan. Three daughters of Bec held guard over the well. The hero Fionn mac Cumhaill came with two companions and asked for water. One of the girls let water slip from a bowl. The drops fell into the open mouth of Fionn and wisdom passed to him. The Norse scholar Turville-Petre decided this scene matched the drink of Odin from the mead of Kvasir. He recorded that view in the book Myth and Religion of the North.

:::

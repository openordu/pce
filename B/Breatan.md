``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Breatan names Britain in the Irish and Scottish Gaelic languages. In Irish the word Breatan usually marks Great Britain apart from Scotland. That northern land is called Alba. The form Breatach more often means a Briton. In Scottish Gaelic the word Breatan usually names Great Britain. It may also point to the fortress at Dumbarton, seen in Dùn Breatann.

:::
```

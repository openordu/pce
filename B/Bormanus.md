``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bormanus is a second title for the god Borvo. The name comes into use most often when that god stands as the consort of Bormana. It points to the very same healing deity whom other accounts call Borvo. No separate figure hides behind the longer label when a reader meets it.

:::
```

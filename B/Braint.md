::: magazinestyle
Braint is a Welsh personal name whose sense gives privilege, right, honour, or status. It may have grown from the goddess Brigantia, a title that points to one who stands high. The same word also clings to a river that runs across Anglesey. Readers may meet the name used for both a person and a watercourse.

:::

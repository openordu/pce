::: magazinestyle
Broichan was a Scottish druid. He served as druid to King Brude. He challenged Saint Columba and was bested. Broichan resented the arrival of the Christian evangelists. He brought storm and darkness over Loch Ness. The storm made navigation seem impossible. Columba ordered the sails unfurled. Then the waters became calm again.

:::

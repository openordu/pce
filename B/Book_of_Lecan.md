``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Lecan also carries the title Great Book of Lecan. That label sets it apart from the older Yellow Book of Lecan. The same family of scribes made both codices inside one castle. Its Irish name is Leabhar Mór Mhac Fhir Bhisigh Leacáin. The text took shape about fourteen hundred. Six hundred leaves hold genealogies plus a charter of rights. The word Lecan marked a castle that now lies ruined. That ruin rests deep in the west of County Sligo. The site stands two miles north of Inishcrone. Kathleen Mulchrone issued her edition in Dublin in 1937.

:::
```

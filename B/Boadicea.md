::: magazinestyle
Boadicea is a variant spelling of Boudicca. Boadicia is another variant form. The ancient queen of the Iceni appears in older writings under these names. Her full story is told under the entry Boudicca. Her revolt against Rome and her defeat kept her name alive.

:::

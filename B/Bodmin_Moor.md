Bodmin Moor is a highland stretch in central Cornwall close to the town of Bodmin. Its ground carries many megalithic ruins. A number of folk tales describe the region as a holy centre for the Cornish people. The dry plateau stays quiet and known as a spiritual heart of the district.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Saint Bieuzy was a holy man remembered across Brittany. He stands as the patron of a sacred spring, the holy well of Bieuzy. Traditions say the waters of that well carry healing power. The faithful seek its waters for ease of a troubled mind. The folk of Brittany count this spring among the most honoured of all their holy wells.

:::

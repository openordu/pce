``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ben-varrey is the Manx term for the mermaid. The word belongs to the sea-folk lore of the Isle of Man. Her male counterpart in Manx belief is the dinny-mara. Together the two names cover the merman and mermaid pair of Manx coastal tradition.

:::
```

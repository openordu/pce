::: magazinestyle
Bro Waroch was a petty kingdom in early Brittany. It occupied the south and east of the region. In early Christian times Brittany divided into three small states. Domnonia held the north. Cornouaille held the south and west. Bro Waroch held the south and east. Its name preserves the memory of a Breton ruler. The kingdom formed part of the land later called Brittany.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Celtic groups entered the Balkans during the Iron Age. Illyrian speakers had lived there in the Bronze Age. Classical writers named tribes such as Triballi and Scordisci. Alexander hosted a Celtic delegation in 335 bc. A great invasion came in 279 bc under Brennos. The invaders threatened Delphi but failed there. The Scordisci founded a centre at Singidunum. A kingdom named Tylis stood on the Thracian coast. Celtic culture left colorful goods in the Balkan lands. Rome later reduced the power of these eastern Celts.

:::
```

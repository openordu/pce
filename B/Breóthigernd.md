``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Breóthigernd is a name found in the Celtic record as a wife of Mongán. The book gives it a second spelling, Breóthighearn. Both forms point to the same married woman. Her place in the record is brief and slight. She appears only as a consort of the noted king known as Mongán. She has no long story of her own in this source. Her worth rests in her bond to that monarch. A reader of the old tales will meet her as a royal mate. Her presence ties her to the rich world of the king. The companion tale of her husband sets out her role with care.

:::
```

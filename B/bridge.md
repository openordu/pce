bridge names the Bridge of Leaps, which Cúchulainn had to cross to reach Scáthach's realm. The bridge stood tall and thin, slick as an eel's tail, so travellers who slipped plunged to the sea-monsters beneath. Cúchulainn crossed it; on a return crossing he killed a hag who stood in his way.

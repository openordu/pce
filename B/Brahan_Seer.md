::: magazinestyle
The Brahan Seer stands as a title for Kenneth MacKenzie of 17th-century Scotland. People also knew him by the name Coinneadh Odhar Fossaiche. That name means Sombre Kenneth of the Prophecies. He also carried the name Kenneth Oaur. He declared that the Highland districts would fade and that workmen would cut the waterway called the Caledonian Canal. He named the graves scattered across the Highland region. His gift for prophecy lingered with the people of Gaelic Scotland. The facts of his own life are not well known.

:::

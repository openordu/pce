``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Béfionn is a variant spelling of the same woman who is called Bébinn in Irish stories. The dictionary gives this form its own main line. It then directs the reader to the fuller Bébinn entry for the account. Both names point to the very same figure in the legend. Bébinn carries a sense of beauty and a fair white woman. Variant names like Béfionn let a writer keep the older shape while a plain form stays easy to find. The reader who meets Béfionn lands back on the main record. One being therefore hides under these two known forms.

:::
```

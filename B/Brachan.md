``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brachan is an alternate way to write the name Brychan. The fuller form is the one kept by the old records for that leader. A reader who meets Brachan should look to the Brychan entry for his tale. The two spellings point to one and the same person in the old traditions.

:::
```

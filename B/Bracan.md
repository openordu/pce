``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bracan also appears in the old books under the spelling Bracann. The name stands as an alternate title for a father of Muirene, the woman who gave the figure his descendant. She herself is known as the mother of Finn mac Cumhaill in the tales. Yet Tadag is the title people reach for more often when they speak of him.

:::
```

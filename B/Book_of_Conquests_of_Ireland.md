``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Conquests of Ireland is another name for the great pseudo-history of the invasions. It is the Lebor Gabála, whose title describes the seizing of Ireland. This text recounts the successive arrivals on the island. It names the Nemedians, the Milesian settlers, and the people of the Tuatha Dé Danann. For the whole account read the entry Lebor Gabála Érenn.

:::
```

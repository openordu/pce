::: magazinestyle
Bran mac Febail is the hero of a celebrated early Irish voyage tale. The work carries the name Imram Brain, also set down as The Voyage of Bran. It dates from the eighth century. One day Bran grows drowsy after hearing lovely music. He then travels onward through magic to the Otherworld, a land steeped in rich delights. When he comes back to Ireland he declares that his name is Bran and his father is Febal. The folk answer that he has long time and that his tale is ancient. Still he is able to relate what befell him. Then he crumbles into dust.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bearrach is the modern spell form of the name Berrach. It appears as a cross-reference in modern dictionaries of Celtic tradition. The older form Berrach stands as the canonical version in most retellings. A reader who meets Bearrach should look to Berrach for the fuller entry. Both spellings point to the single same character in the old tales. No separate deity or hero is intended by either form. The shift is only a matter of later spelling, not a split in identity. Bearrach and Berrach therefore carry shared legendary meaning together.


:::
```

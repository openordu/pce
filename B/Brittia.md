::: magazinestyle
Brittia is the name the Byzantine historian Procopius gave to a fabulous island. He wrote in the fifth century and set the island in the tales of the peasants of northern Gaul. His account is one of the earliest records of how a distant land appeared in the imagination of the Continent.

:::

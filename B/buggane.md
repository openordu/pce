::: magazinestyle
buggane is the Manx term for a mischievous creature from the folklore of the Isle of Man. Each example carries a black, bristling mane and eyes that flare like a torch held in the dark. Arch in manner and given to pranks, the buggane delights in chasing passers-by and startling them out of their wits. All of them are masters of disguise and can shift form at will. One is said to serve a wicked sorcerer as a bound attendant. The buggane is set alongside the cabbyl-ushtey, the Manx form of the water-horse, and its wider relatives include the pooca of Ireland and the bòcan of Gaelic Scotland, the last of which sits at the headword bogan.

:::

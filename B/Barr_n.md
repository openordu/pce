::: magazinestyle
Barrán is a name from early Irish story, a little form of the word for tip or point. Several figures of old Ireland bear this name. One of these is the father-in-law of the hero Caílte in the Fenian Cycle. The name thus joins the line of the legendary heroes of the hunt.

:::

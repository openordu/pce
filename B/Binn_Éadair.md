::: magazinestyle
Binn Éadair is an alternate spelling of the Irish name Benn Eader. Both forms point to the same place, the Hill of Howth. That height rises on the Howth peninsula around nine miles northeast of Dublin. Many tales count it as the easternmost point of Ireland. A reader who meets the variant Binn Éadair should turn to the Benn Eader entry for the fuller account. This cross-reference joins the two spellings of one summit.

:::

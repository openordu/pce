::: magazinestyle
bledlochtana is an Irish word meaning monsters. The term names a host of monstrous beings in Irish story. Their screams roll across the earth when the Battle of Mag Tuired reaches its fourth day. The full account of that battle sits in the text Cath Maige Tuired.

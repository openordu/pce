``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bleherus is a Latin rendering of the name of Bledri ap Cydifor. He is the only named storyteller in medieval Welsh tradition. Welsh speakers call such a storyteller a cyfarwydd. Bleherus is also recorded as Bledericus Walensis. He stands as the single named example of the storyteller's craft.

:::
```

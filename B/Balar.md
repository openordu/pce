``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Balar is the older Irish spelling of the name of the one eyed king. He is a king of the hostile folk of the old war. The dictionary sends the reader of this word on to the fuller entry under the name Balor. There the tale tells of the eye that could fell a host. It also tells of the king's fate in the great battle.

:::
```

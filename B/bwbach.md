::: magazinestyle
bwbach is Welsh, glossed by MacKillop as bogy, scarecrow, or bugbear, and named in the plural as bwbachod. It names a Welsh supernatural being that can act as a household helper or a domestic troublemaker. The bwbach berates teetotallers and dissenting ministers, treating them as unwelcome guests. The same word can describe a warrior leader whose mere presence spreads terror on the march. The tradition is strongest across the three Glamorganshires of south Wales.

:::

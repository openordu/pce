``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The bed of Diarmait and Gráinne is a poetic nickname for the dolmen tombs scattered across Ireland. Folk once held that the pair of lovers rested upon these stones. The two slept there as they fled the jealous Fionn. During their escape they moved swiftly through the open country. MacKillop calls the title a fanciful one and keeps the escape at the centre of the legend. The cross-links lead on to the fuller tales told of Diarmait and of Gráinne.

:::
```

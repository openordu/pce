``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buan is an Irish heroine. Several women and men in early Irish stories bore this name. One such Buan was the mother of the boy Baile Binnbérlach. Another Buan was the wife of the hero Mac Dathó. When Mac Dathó died, his head was carried home to her. She posed questions to the head. It answered her and drew out the treachery behind his death. Buan wept until she passed away. A magical hazel named Coll Buana rose from her grave. Cut rods of those hazel bushes served believers for divination. Scholars remark that Buan may descend from the early goddess Búanann. The Irish term buan carries the sense of lasting and enduring, a meaning borne across all the tales.

:::
```

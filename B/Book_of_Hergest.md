::: magazinestyle
The Book of Hergest is another title for the Red Book of Hergest. Its Welsh name is Llyfr Coch Hergest. This manuscript dates from about 1382 to 1410. It holds many of the prose tales of the Mabinogion. Lady Charlotte Guest used it in her translation of 1846. See the entry Red Book of Hergest for the full account.

:::

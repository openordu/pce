::: magazinestyle
baríoghan an bhrogla is an Irish phrase. It translates as queen of the fairy palace. MacKillop groups it with the variant forms banríon na brug and bean ríghan na burgh. The phrase may denote the otherworld woman Úna or the figure Grian.

:::

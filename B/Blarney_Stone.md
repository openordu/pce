``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Blarney Stone is an Irish folkloric site. It sits in Blarney Castle in County Cork. Cormac MacCarthy raised the castle in the fifteenth century above an earlier English keep. The stone gives eloquence to any person who kisses it. This gift does not require honesty. MacKillop treats the kissing custom as recent. Father Prout helped spread the tradition. One legend credits the spirit Clidna with the stone's power. She revealed its secret to Cormac when a lawsuit threatened him. He placed the stone on the parapet, out of easy reach. People travel to the castle for the sake of that speech.

:::
```

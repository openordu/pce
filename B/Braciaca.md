::: magazinestyle
Braciaca is a god of Roman Britain and one only dimly known. The sole record carried for it is a carved inscription at Haddon House beyond the town of Derbyshire. The wording suggests a link to the Welsh word that means malt. No wider story of this deity has reached us.

:::

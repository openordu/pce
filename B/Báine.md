::: magazinestyle
Báine is a female figure in early Irish legends about places. Writers may present her as the child of the man who lent his name to Aileach. That great stone fort stands in County Donegal. This stronghold is called Aileach Frigrenn or Grianán of Aileach. A second tale makes her a child of Tuathal, a forebear of the monarchs of Ireland.

:::

Bolar is a variant spelling of the name Balor. The longer form Bola also names the same being. Balor is the one-eyed leader of the Fomorians in Irish legend. A reader who meets Bolar should turn to the fuller Balor entry. Bolar and Bola both point the same balanced world of myth.

:::

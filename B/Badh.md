``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Badh is the modern Irish spelling of the name of the old war goddess. It is a later form of the name Badb. In the east of Munster the word once meant the banshee, the woman who told of a death at hand. The single form carries both the raven of war and the cry of the dead.

:::
```

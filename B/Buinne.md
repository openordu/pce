``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buinne is a retainer who fails Deirdre and Noíse in Longas Mac nUislem. That tale tells of the exile of Uisnech's sons. He sometimes goes by the name Ruadh, meaning the Red. Buinne was a son of Fergus Mac Roich. At first he defends Deirdre with courage. Then Conchobar gives him a bribe. Buinne turns away from the lovers. He receives every parcel of land on one side of a mountain. When he tries to take that land, it becomes a desert.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bosgeal appears in the Ossian poetry that James Macpherson released in 1760. Her name carries the sense of white palm in Scots Gaelic. She is the daughter of Colgar, a chief of Connacht. In the tales she weds Cairbre and becomes the daughter-in-law of Cormac. That marriage ties a western chief's household into the line of the high king. The writing recalls her kinship rather than any deed of war she performs.

:::
```

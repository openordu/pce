``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Builg names a people of history who came to settle in southeastern Ireland. They made their home near present-day Cork long before the Christian era. The group formed a division of the Belgae, who were P-Celtic speakers in Gaul and Britain. Writers also call them the Eranni, with the Latin form Iverni or Ivernians. They appear identical to the Fir Bodh, a people of the Lebor Gabala, also known as the Book of Invasions. The records name Lugaid as their ancestor. Lugaid came from Britain and was sent to conquer Ireland.

:::
```

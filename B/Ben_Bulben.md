``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ben Bulben is a tall ridge in County Sligo. It rises along the coast not far from Sligo town, beside the village of Drumcliff. Its summit stands more than seventeen hundred feet above the sea. The mountain takes its title from Conall Gulban. His father was Niall of the Nine Hostages, and the boy was fostered in this district. The great boar hunt carries the title The Pursuit of Diarmait and Grainne. In many versions the hero Diarmait Ua Duibne met his death on this slope. A boar had carried him upon its back, and once Diarmait grazed a poisonous bristle that pierced his flesh. Fionn Mac Cumhaill was a healer and could have saved him. He chose to taunt the dying man instead. Grainne was Fionn's intended wife. She loved the younger Diarmait for years. That love wore Fionn down as he hunted the pair across Ireland. It is also the place where the Fenian Cycle figure Oisin was found as an infant. At the foot of the hill sit the remains of the earliest stronghold raised by the O'Conors of Sligo. William Butler Yeats refers to Ben Bulben repeatedly in his poems, and the peak now shadows his grave.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Biast Bheulach belonged to the Isle of Skye as a demon spirit or demonic ghost. The creature usually stayed near the Odail Pass. Sometimes it took the shape of a man. At other times it appeared as a man who stood on just one leg. On many occasions it looked like a greyhound or some wild beast prowling about. The creature let out terrible shrieks. It seemed eager to find blood to feed on. Gaelic terms called it Biasd, a prating beast.

:::
```

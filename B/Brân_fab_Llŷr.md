``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brân fab Llŷr is a Welsh king and a leading figure of early Welsh tale. His other name, Bendigeidfran, means Brân the Blessed. He is the son of Llŷr and the brother of Branwen and Manawydan. His half brother is Efnisien, and his own son, Caradog, carries on the royal line. In the old accounts Brân stands as a giant. He can wade the open water between Britain and Ireland. He carries a magic horn that gives out food and drink at each call. One old cycle, the second of the Mabinog branches, which the scribes call Branwen, turns on his rule. A poisoned spear kills him. He asks his men to cut off his head and place it in London on the White Mount, with the face toward France, so the country stays safe from an enemy landing. Writers set that head at the center of a rich and powerful tale of the Otherworld.

:::
```

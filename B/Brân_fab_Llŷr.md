::: magazinestyle
Brân fab Llŷr is a Welsh king, known as Bendigeidfran, a name that means 'Brân the Blessed.' His sister Branwen also carries a name that can be read as 'Brân the Blessed,' so the two may rest on one original figure. The word brân is the Welsh name for the crow, reaching back to an older Celtic form. Across the old tales the crow turns up over the broken ground of a fight, and the Irish war-goddess Bodb stands as a crow, which may be why the noun stayed feminine. Among ancient peoples, Brennos of the Prausi comes closest to Brân's history: he led a huge raid, lost nearly all his men, and fell in the last great clash, asked his own men to slay him, and left a charm that guarded the city of Tolosa. Welsh Brân and the sea-lord Manawydan fab Llŷr may at first have been a single raven god joining water and wisdom.

:::

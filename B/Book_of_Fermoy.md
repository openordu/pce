The Book of Fermoy is a manuscript from the mid-fifteenth century. It now rests at the Royal Irish Academy in Dublin. The codex holds a copy of Altrom Tige Dá Medar. That tale takes its title from the nurturing of the two dairy homes. Fermoy, a village in the north-eastern part of County Cork, lies about sixteen miles east of Mallow.

:::

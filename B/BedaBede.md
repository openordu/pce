``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beda, also named in English as the monk Bede, wrote from Jarrow. He lived out his life at Wearmouth and Jarrow. He finished the Historia Ecclesiastica in 731. That work became the chief evidence of early Britain. Beda set the dating of the calendar and Sunday. He used the name 'English' for all Germanic peoples. He turned cold on the Welsh and clashed with them on Easter. He favoured the Picts and spoke well of some Irish mission. These details shape how Bede is studied today.

:::
```

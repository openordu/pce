``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Britán Mael was head of one of the three Nemedian lines that survived. According to the Book of Invasions he led his kin to Britain. He settled there and left his name upon the island. His name in Irish may mean the crop-headed Briton. The Nemedians came after earlier invaders and left their mark on the naming of the land.

:::
```

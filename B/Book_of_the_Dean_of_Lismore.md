::: magazinestyle
The Book of the Dean of Lismore is a collection of Scottish Gaelic writing. The work dates from 1512 to 1526. It forms part of the great written tradition of the Celtic languages. That tradition is far smaller in volume than the medieval Irish one. The book came into being after Christianity had grown well established. Later bards of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries carried the tradition on.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Brasil is another spelling used for the phantom isle of Hy-Brasil. MacKillop also notes the form Brazil for the very same island. Seen far out at sea, Hy-Brasil was a wondrous land of early belief. Some thought the place a dream of paradise. Readers who meet the spelling Brasil should look to the Hy-Brasil entry for the story of that isle.

:::

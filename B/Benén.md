``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Benén appears in the record as Beanán and under the Latin name Benignus. He was a saint whose life ran close to St Patrick. He served the great missionary as companion and as the one who chanted the psalms. The Church keeps his memorial on 9 November each year. His name carries a sense of mildness drawn from the Latin stem for kindly.

:::
```

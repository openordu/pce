::: magazinestyle
Biobal is a merchant in the ancient Irish text Lebor Gabála. That work is the Book of Invasions. Biobal counts as a follower of the leader Partholon. With his partner Babal, Biobal carries gold into Ireland. The pair stand among the first traders remembered in the legends of the isle. Their tale marks an early account of precious metal reaching the island.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bebo is an Irish heroine of the fairy world. She was the small wife of the tiny fairy king Iubdán. Her story first appears in the 13th-century tale Echtra Fergusa maic Léti, the Adventure of Fergus mac Léti. She traveled with her husband to the land of Ulster. The region seemed full of giants to the pair. The lovely queen caught the eye of the Ulster king, Fergus. Bebo became his mistress for a year. The bond held despite the great difference in their size. Fergus then gave Bebo back to her husband. Iubdán offered him a pair of magical shoes. The enchanted pair sealed their exchange.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Buchet was a hospitable man who lived in Leinster. He served as foster father to Eithne Tháebfhota. Most people praised him for his generosity in early Irish story. One tale, the Esnada Tige Buchet, tells how Cormac begets a child called Cairbre Lifechair upon Eithne Tháebfhota. She was a daughter of Cathaír Mór. The house of Buchet gave the narrative its name. Through fostering he took his place in the lines of the royal family.

:::
```

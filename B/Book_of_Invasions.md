``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Invasions is an Irish literary text. Its Irish title is Lebor Gabála Érenn. The title carries the sense of the Takings of Ireland. The work gathers about twelve separate manuscripts. Most are from the twelfth century. Older passages survive in them. The text is poetic. It tells how Ireland was won in six invasions. The tale runs from the great flood to the final settlers. It is a strong aid for scholars of ancient myth.

:::
```

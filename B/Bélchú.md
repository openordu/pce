``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bélchú is a warrior of Connacht in the Ulster Cycle tales. He comes upon the battered hero Conall Cernach after the defeat of Cet. Conall Cernach bleeds so much that he pleads to be killed. Bélchú refuses to end the man and refuses to spare his pain. Instead he carries Conall Cernach home and heals him well. The three sons of Bélchú see the warrior strength of Conall Cernach. They plan to slay him before he can recover his power. Conall Cernach learns of the plan and turns the trap. He makes the sons bring about their own father instead. Then the hero overthrows all three and bore their heads to Ulster.

:::
```

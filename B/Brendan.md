Brendan is a name shared by seventeen Irish saints. The most celebrated saint is Brendan of Clonfert, who died in 577 and earned the title Navigator. His father was Finnlug. Brendan founded the abbey at Ardfert in his native Kerry. He also set up Clonfert in county Galway. Two Latin works record his story. The Vita Brendani gives a life. The Navigatio Sancti Brendani Abbatis, dated about the ninth or tenth century, tells of a voyage. A hermit named Barinthus spoke of a western land of promise. Brendan then sailed with fourteen companions in a leather curragh. He met islands of giant sheep, a whale mistaken for land, and a burning peak. Some writers claim he reached Iceland and beyond.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bave is an English rendering of the war goddess title. The older Irish shape of that figure is Badh. She is shown as a hooded crow that moves over the field while the armies clash. The spelling shifted through the writing of old Ireland, and Bave stands as one of those later forms.

:::
```

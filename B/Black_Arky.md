``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Black Arky, also written Arcan, is an alternate name that MacKillop points to Accadubh. MacKillop's dictionary treats Black Arky and Accadubh as one being, so the two titles name a single figure. The shortened form Arky also appears in reverse order as Arky, Black. A reader who meets either spelling should find the fuller account in the Accadubh entry.

:::
```

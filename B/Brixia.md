``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brixia is an obscure Continental Celtic goddess. She is the consort of Luxovius, the water-god of Luxeuil. Her other name is Bricta. Scholars often suggest a link between Brixia and the Irish Brigit, but they have yet to prove it. She belongs to the healing and water cult of the warm springs of Luxeuil in eastern France.

:::
```

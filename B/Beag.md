``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beag is an Irish name that carries the sense of the one that is little. The older shape of Béc is Becc, and Beag along with Béc turn up in the texts. Several persons in the early stories bear this name, some men and at least one woman. One such bearer is a fighter from the western lands. The form stays common in the old literature.

:::
```

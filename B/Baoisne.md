``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baoisne is a later spelling of the name Baiscne. Baiscne is a clan in the old tales of the Fenian Cycle. The hero Fionn mac Cumhaill led the men of this family. The two great families of the Fianna were the clan Baiscne and the rival clan Morna. Baoisne is one of the many forms of the clan name.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bergusia was the female consort of the Gaulish hammer-deity Ucuetis. Worshippers rendered homage to this pair at Alesia in eastern France, land of Burgundy. She may have served as a patron of the crafts. An underground chamber excavated next to their shrine seems to have held a crafts guild. That discovery ties her to the artisans who worked beside the temple.

:::
```

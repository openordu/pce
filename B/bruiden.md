::: magazinestyle
A bruiden is a great lodging house in the Irish legends. In the story of Mac Dá Thó's pig, the hostels of Ireland included the houses of Mac Da Réo, Da Choca, Da Derga, Da Thó, and Forgal Manach. A sixth hostel, called Bruiden Blaí Briugad, appears in the tale of Da Choca's hostel. The tales show the rich hospitality of these halls. Seven doors lead into the building. Seven ways run through it. Seven hearths keep warm seven cauldrons, and every cauldron holds a whole ox with a side of bacon. Such details set out the great generosity given to guests in these legendary Irish halls.

:::

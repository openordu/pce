``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Búadnat was a princess of the far north. Her father held the throne of Norway. Ireland linked that realm to Lochlainn. In the Fenian Cycle she stands as a royal child. Copyists wrote her name in several ways. The spellings Buadhnat and Buanait also occur. Her name may carry the sense of victorious lady. No deed of her own survives in any tale. She is known only through her father the king.

:::
```

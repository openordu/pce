``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bove Derg, also written Bove the Red, is a variant form of a well-known god's name. It points to the deity Bodb Derg. Many old tales honour him as a leading figure of the Tuatha Dé Danann people. The fuller account of him sits under the name Bodb Derg, where his deeds are told. A reader who meets Bove Derg should pass on to that god's own entry.

:::
```

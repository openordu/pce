::: magazinestyle
Brion is a variant spelling of the name Brian. The two spellings name the same figures in Irish tradition. For the full account the reader should see the entry for Brian. Brian in turn may belong to the god of the Tuatha Dé Danann or to the high king Brian Bóruma.

:::

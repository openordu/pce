::: magazinestyle
bunadh beag na farraige is an Irish phrase built from bunadh, meaning stock, family, or host, and na farraige, meaning of the sea. It serves as a collective name for the small marine folk of Gaelic tradition, the household of fairy beings thought to inhabit the waters around Ireland. Idiomatic translations render the phrase as the small people of the sea or the little people of the ocean, and MacKillop groups the term with its sibling entry boctogai, the single-word label for the same order of sea-fairies.

:::

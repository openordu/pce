``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
A barrow is a pre-Celtic grave. Builders set stones in a line that ends in a burial mound. Some barrows hold more than one body. The Wiltshire Downs carry the largest group of barrows in England. Stonehenge rises above those downs. Barrows also appear throughout Ireland. Local people revere the graves near their homes. They tell tales of hauntings at the mounds. Black Dogs of the fairy race and wandering ghosts frequent these sites. MacKillop records the Irish word duma for a mound, tumulus, or barrow. In his account, the immortals of legend make their home in ancient barrows and cairns. See the entry for the Fairy Mound.

:::
```

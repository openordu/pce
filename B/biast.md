::: magazinestyle
biast is the Scottish Gaelic word for a beast. Variants include béist and biasd. The term descends from Latin bestia and matches Old Irish biast. In folklore it labels a range of monstrous characters. Named examples include the Arrach, the fuath, and the Muilearteach. Each of those beings carries its own entry.

:::

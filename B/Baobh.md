``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baobh is another spelling of the name Badh. Badh is a figure of battle in early Irish belief, close to the crow or the raven. She haunts the field where men fight. The forms of the name shift across the sources, and Baobh is one of the later spellings of that figure.

:::
```

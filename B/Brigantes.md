``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Brigantes were a powerful confederacy of Brythonic Celtic tribes. They centred on the north of Britain during Roman times. Their chief goddess was Brigantia. Under the rule of Claudius their queen was Cartimandua. Some Brigantes later moved to south county Wexford in Ireland.

:::
```

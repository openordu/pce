``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
bérla ne filed, also given in the entry as bélrae na filed, names the poetic language of the Irish filid. MacKillop treats it as the cryptic vocabulary of verse, woven from kennings, riddles, and inherited figures of speech that allude to myth and ritual. The same vocabulary reached beyond the poets. Cúchulainn uses it when he addresses Emer. The entry is filed under the OCR-corrupted printed headword bévia ne filed; the clean scholarly form is bérla ne filed.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Banba is an Irish goddess. She is one of three divine eponyms of Ireland, with Eriu and Fodla. She was a daughter of Delbaeth and Eirnin. A well-known tale in the Book of Invasions explains the three names of Ireland. When the advancing Milesians met Banba at Slieve Mis in County Kerry, she gave them her name. Some sources place her on the plain from which the hill of Tara rises. Banba offered the Milesians happiness and wealth if the land bore her name. The Milesians gave precedence instead to Eriu, who made the same promise from the hill of Uisneach. Her name survives today as a poetic name for Ireland. The pig is an emblem of Banba, and her name resembles the Irish word for piglet, banb.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Borbar, who also goes by the name Borbaduthal, shows up in the poems of James Macpherson's Ossian published in 1760. His sons include Cathmor the Generous and Cairbre, the man who would become a usurper of power. The tales also name his brother as Colgulla. That warrior leads a revolt against Cormac mac Airt while he rules over Ireland as king. Through his sons and this sibling, Borbar touches several challenging players who fill the Ossianic tales with conflict and ambition.

:::

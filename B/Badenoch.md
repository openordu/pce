::: magazinestyle
Badenoch is a mountain district in the north of the isle of Britain. It lies in the county of Inverness, in the Highland part of the land. The people spoke the Gaelic tongue there into our own time. In the eighteenth century it became one of two seats of a false Gaelic learning.

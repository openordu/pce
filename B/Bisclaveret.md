``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bisclaveret is a term with Breton roots that names a werewolf. It reaches legend through the verse tales that Marie de France wrote in the twelfth century. Her lay follows a knight who takes on the shape of a wolf and later returns to his human form. A modern Breton speaker still keeps two words for the creature, bleiz-garv and den-bleiz. Bisclaveret therefore stands as an early Breton word for the wolf who lives inside a man.

:::
```

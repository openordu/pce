``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Breccán comes from the Irish word for freckled or speckled. Many early Irish people carried the name, and so did thirteen saints or more. In legend the best-known Breccan turns out to be a trader, not a holy man, lost off the Scottish coast. He drowned in the whirlpool whose name he keeps. Corrievreckan swells in the channel between Jura and Scarba, two isles in the Inner Hebrides.

:::
```

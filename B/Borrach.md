``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Borrach is a spelling variant of the name Borach. With either form the same figure is meant, so no new person appears under the longer spelling. One book may show Borach while another shows Borrach. Both names stand for the same being. The two spellings point alike to that one person in the old tales.

:::
```

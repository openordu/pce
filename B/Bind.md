::: magazinestyle
Bind is a fairy woman of the Irish tradition. Her name holds the sense of melodious, sweet, or sweetness. Folklore records her as the daughter of Modarn. Writers also spell the name as Binde, Binn, and Binne. Those forms all point to the same gentle Irish figure.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Bel is an alternate spelling for the name Belenus, the Continental Celtic deity of light and of the sun. A reader who meets the shorter form should look to the fuller Belenus entry for the tale of this solar power. There the rites paid to it are also set out. For this work Bel and Belenus name the same shining god, honoured across ancient Gaul.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The boobrie is a shape-shifting specter of Highland folklore. Its other name is tarbh boibhre. It is a giant water-bird native to Argyllshire, the region now called Strathclyde. It haunts both the lakes and the salt-wells of that region. It swims on webbed feet and cries with a harsh voice. It can swallow sheep and cattle whole. Its common form is a waterbird far larger than any duck or swan. Its footprint spans the antlers of a large deer. It also takes the shape of a cow, a water horse, or a huge insect. That insect has long tentacles and sucks the blood of horses. It preys on calves and lambs, a danger to subsistence farmers. When cattle are absent it eats otters.

:::
```

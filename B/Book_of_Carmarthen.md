``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The title Book of Carmarthen points to another work. It directs the reader to the Black Book of Carmarthen. That codex holds some of the oldest Welsh manuscripts that survive. Its Welsh name is Llyfr Du Caerfyrddin. The book preserves early poetry, including verses of Myrddin. See the entry Black Book of Carmarthen for the full account.

:::
```

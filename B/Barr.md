``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Barr is the name of a saint of the Outer Hebrides. He is the one who gives a name to the island of Barra. His feast, once marked on a day of September, came to join that of Saint Michael. His name and his day show him to be the same as Barra, the figure also known as Finnbarr.

:::
```

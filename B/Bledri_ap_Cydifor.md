::: magazinestyle
Bledri ap Cydifor is the one named storyteller of medieval Welsh tradition. He is also known as Bledericus Walensis and as Bleherus. In Welsh a storyteller is called a cyfarwydd. Bledri ap Cydifor stands apart because the other cyfarwyddiaid are anonymous. He belongs to the tradition that produced the Mabinogi.

:::

Fañch Jaffrennou was a leading figure in the Breton revival. In 1904 he helped carry the Breton cause abroad. A well-known image shows him gathered with other delegates at the congress held in Caernarfon. He stood there in the national dress of Brittany. The Marquis de l'Estourbeillon joined him; the family house was de la Garnaché. Ange Mosher and Francis Even completed the small group. Seated on the right is the Welsh painter John Kelau. The men wear the traditional Breton costume. Its many regional forms survive without a break. Among the Celts the Bretons stand out as the only people who still wear a living national dress. They display it at pardons and meetings all through the year. The dress shows the region the wearer comes from. The lace koef or headdress names the village of the wearer. It may also tell the wearer's rank in society. Jaffrennou wore this costume at the congress with pride. The picture fixes a moment of Celtic unity and records how far the Breton revival had gone by 1904.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brianan is the name of a divinity the Scottish Gaelic people call out in their cries. They could ask him for good fortune or for evil upon another. More often they wanted him to bring misfortune and grief upon their enemies. Sometimes the people gave him the extra name Brianan Buidhe, which means the Yellow. He has no fixed home or family in the tales, only a wry role in speech.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The brehon was a legal officer of early Ireland. The office may have grown in part from the old writers of lore. These were the vates of the song and the fili of the word. He did not sit as a judge of the court. The king of the land guarded the peace and the order. The brehon knew the body of law and kept it safe. The office passed from one keeper to the next pupil of the craft. So the name brehon came to stand for the vigilant keepers of the ancient law of Ireland.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Bodua is a war-goddess honoured in Gaul. The battle figure closely mirrors the Irish goddess Badb. Old writers also gave her the titles Cathubodua and Cauth Bova. Bodua stands for the fury and carnage of fighting. Her name links the war-gods of the Gaulish and Irish worlds of battle.

:::

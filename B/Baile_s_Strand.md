``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baile's Strand is a shore of the sea near the town of Dundalk in Ireland. It is named for a prince of Ulster who loved a maid of Leinster in the old story. The shore is the setting of a drama by the poet William Butler Yeats. That play keeps alive the sorrow of the two lovers of the tale.

:::
```

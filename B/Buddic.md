::: magazinestyle
Buddic is one of the Welsh forms that the source book gives for the name of the British queen celebrated as Boudicca, who led an uprising against the Romans. The middle Welsh spelling sets down her name as Buddig, following a native pronunciation that shifts the vowel. This entry only routes the reader toward the fuller article on Boudicca and her revolt, because the headword carries no campaign narrative of its own.

:::

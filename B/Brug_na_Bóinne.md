``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brug na Bóinne is a fairy palace beside the River Boyne. The site now carries the name Newgrange. Angus Óg made the palace his home. He won it from the master Elcmar. Elcmar first ruled the place as its lord. He also raised Angus as his foster son. The goddess Boand, who became the Boyne, conceived a child outside her marriage. She hid that fault by asking Elcmar to care for the boy. Some old tales treat Elcmar as a form of Nuadu Airgetlám. In another version Elcmar married Boand and the Dagda came to want her. The Dagda sent Elcmar on a trip that felt like one day and one night. Nine months truly passed while the master stayed away. Angus was born in that long pause. Elcmar returned and knew nothing of the birth. Angus then asked the lord to live in the hall for a single day and night. That brief visit gave young Angus the palace forever. One version throws Elcmar out like wicked angels flung from heaven. The master then settled at Sidh Clettig beside the southern flank of the Boyne.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bonduca is the form of the name seen in Holinshed's Chronicles printed in 1577. That same name appears in later accounts as the queen Boudicca of the Iceni. She led the great rising against Roman rule in Britain. A reader who meets Bonduca should follow the entry on Boudicca. The two spellings name one woman.

:::
```

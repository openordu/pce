``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bregon is one old form of the name Breogan. This hero was a leader of the Milesians in the account of the ancient records. He raised a high tower on a far western shore. From the top of the tower his son Ith first saw the isle of Ireland across the water. That spark of a vision drew the Milesians at last to the land. So the tower holds a place in the founding tale of Irish story.

:::
```

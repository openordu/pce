``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brí Léith is a hill of Ireland that works as a gate of the Otherworld. It is the modern Ardagh Hill, three miles from a Longford town. The fairy king Midir makes Brí Léith his home. The hero Angus Óg lives there too in older accounts. In the Wooing of Étain, the men of Eochaid Airem plan to tear down the hill as they search for the lost woman. People keep the Lugh festival on the hill until recent times. So the high place holds both a royal tale and a live custom.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Brigantia was a British goddess of the Roman occupation. She was the personification of the rule of the Brigantes people. Her concerns covered river and water cults. A centre of her worship stood in what is now the West Riding of Yorkshire. She is probably the same goddess as the Gaulish Brigindo, known only from inscriptions. The Romans equated her with Minerva, while modern readers often see a link with Brigit. The River Brent, a tributary that joins the Thames at Brentford, takes its name from Brigantia. The Welsh name Braint also appears to rise from the same goddess.

:::

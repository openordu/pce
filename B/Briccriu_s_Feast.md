::: magazinestyle
Briccriu's Feast is the English title of the Ulster Cycle tale named Fled Bricgreenn in Irish. A writer composed the story as early as the eighth century. The tale also lives in four variant texts from the eleventh century. One of them appears in the Book of the Dun Cow. In the story the heroes quarrel and fight over the champion's portion. For the full account the reader should see the entry for Fled Bricgreenn.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Baranton is the name of a fountain in the forest of Broceliande in Brittany. In later story the fountain is bound to the figure of the knight. The place appears in the lore of the forest, where wonders can be seen. Baranton is remembered chiefly as the fountain of that enchanted wood.

:::

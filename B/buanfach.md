::: magazinestyle
búanfach is the name of an Irish board-game whose rules no longer survive. The word may draw on Irish buan, meaning good or lasting. Scholars compare the lost game to fidchell and brandub. Eóin MacWhite discusses búanfach and related early Irish board-games in his article Early Irish Board Games, published in Éigse 5 (1945), pages 25 to 35.

:::

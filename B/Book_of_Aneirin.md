``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Aneirin is known in Welsh as Llyfr Aneirin. It is a manuscript copied in the middle of the thirteenth century. It rests now in the South Glamorgan library. Most of its writing shows thirteenth-century spelling, while a fifth carries older forms. The codex is the chief carrier of the poem Gododdin. Aneirin gave that verse its voice, so a note in the text says his name. The work stands apart from the Canu Aneirin, another collection of the same bard.

:::
```

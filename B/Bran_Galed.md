::: magazinestyle
Bran Galed is a Welsh hero praised for one great treasure. He owns a horn that pours out liquor in a steady stream. The flow is rich enough to satisfy any guest who drinks from it. His fame rests on that endless vessel, which never runs dry in the old Welsh tales. He stands among the admired champions remembered in Welsh story.

:::

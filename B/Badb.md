``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Badb, also written Bodb, means the scald crow. In early Irish tradition she was a supernatural woman linked to battle and slaughter. The name works as a proper name and as a general term for a battle creature. She sometimes merges with the Morrigan, Nemain, Be Neit, or Macha, and at other times stands apart from them. The stock phrase badb catha means the Badb of battle. Translators of Latin tales recast the Furies and Bellona as fern battle goddesses named Badb. Her appearance was an evil omen. The male figure Bodb Derg, king of the sidhe of Munster, shared her name.

:::
```

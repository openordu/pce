::: magazinestyle
Benntraige was a people dwelling in the south of Ireland before the coming of Christianity. They may have been the survivors of a tribe that Ptolemy recorded under the name Coriondi. He wrote of them in the second century of the common era. These folk made their home in today's Wexford. One set of them later travelled towards the far south-west of Cork. There they lent their own name to the Barony of Bantry and to the Bay of Bantry.

:::

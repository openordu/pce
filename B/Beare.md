``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beare is an Irish goddess. She gave her name to a peninsula in the far south-west of Ireland. The land lies in the province of Munster. MacKillop places the peninsula between the Bantry inlet and the Kenmare river mouth. It is divided between two counties and is rich in both antiquities and legend. Beare is also called Bera, Beirre, or Momea. Little of her own legend survives. She is said to have been a Spanish princess. The greater fame belongs to the Cailleach Bheirre, the Hag of Beare. She was an ancient land goddess of the pre-Celtic people. The Hag stays bound to the peninsula. A boulder in her shape looks toward the sea from the north side.

:::
```

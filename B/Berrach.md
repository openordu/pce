::: magazinestyle
Berrach caught the notice of legend as a woman renowned for her giving. She carried the alternate spellings Bearrach and Berrach Brecc, a title that means she was freckled. This figure is recorded as the daughter of Cass, a man linked with Cuailnge. She married the famed leader Fionn mac Cumhaill and became his third wife within that band of warriors. Storytellers of her day called her the most generous woman Ireland had yet produced. Her open hand and gentle manner kept her name bright in the tales that long outlived her.

:::

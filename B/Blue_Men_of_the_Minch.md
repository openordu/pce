::: magazinestyle
The Blue Men of the Minch are figures of Scottish maritime folklore. Scots Gaelic names them Na Fir Ghorma, meaning blue men. The Minch runs between the Outer Hebrides and the mainland's Inner Hebrides. Gaelic calls its stream Sruth nam Fear Gorm, the Stream of the Blue Men. They swam out to capsize any ship that crossed. A captain who kept the last word through rhyme could repel them. They moved best around the Shiant Islands in the Hebrides.

:::

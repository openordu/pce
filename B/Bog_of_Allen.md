``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Bog of Allen is a wide boggy plain in central Ireland. The flatland is almost wholly filled with peat and turf. Its spread passes well beyond the ground that adjoins the Hill of Allen in County Kildare. On the summit of that hill the goddess Alend once ruled. Her capital Almu stood there, and the hill became the home of Fionn mac Cumhaill with his fianna. The name of the goddess passes over to the great plain around it. The Bog of Allen carries the Irish name Main Almhaine.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brutus is a contrived eponym and founder planted as an ancestor of the British people. Geoffrey of Monmouth traced this pedigree in 1136. As a Trojan leader Brutus dreams of a temple of Diana far toward the sunset. Landing on the island he overcomes the giant Gogmagog. He then sets law upon the land he calls Britain, or Prydain.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Biróg stands as both a druid and an Irish heroine in the tale of Lugh's birth. She lent her aid to Cian, the mortal who would become Lugh's father, and guided him to the tower of Balor. In MacKillop's telling she is the druidess who helped Cian find his way to that tower and slip inside it, where he pressed his suit upon Eithne. Biróg showed the man how to pass hidden in women's garments, and the disguise let him reach the daughter of Balor of the Evil Eye. The seduction that followed brought about the conception of their child within the keep. When that child of the union came at last into the world, Biróg refused to let it perish alongside those Balor meant to destroy. She drew the infant back from the peril of drowning and kept him safe, and thereby the boy survived to grow into the long-armed champion remembered as Lug Lamhfhota.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Báetán mac Cairill ruled Ulster as its king. He held a fortress at Knocklayd, a place south of Ballycastle in Northern Ireland. His reign ran from 572 to 581. Synthetic historians have cast a shadow over his tale. Yet he appears to have carried the reach of Dál Riada into Scotland and over to the Isle of Man.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ballgel is a lady of the fairy people in the old tales of Ireland. Her name carries the sense of bright-limbed, a figure of shining movement. She dwells on the royal plain of the east known as Brega. The same name belongs to several persons who stand in older Irish genealogies.

:::
```

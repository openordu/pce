::: magazinestyle
Bardsey is an island off the coast of Wales, called Ynys Enlli in Welsh. It lies at the mouth of a great bay and is small in size. According to the Triads, the wise figure Merlin kept a glass house there with the Treasures of Britain. In the sixth century the saint Cadfan founded a monastery on the island.

:::

::: magazinestyle
The Boyhood Deeds of Fionn is one English title for a linked set of tales. These tales retrace the youth of the hero Fionn mac Cumhaill. Irish sources give the cycle the name Macgnimartha Finn, and later spellings render it Macghniomhartha Finn. The records look much like the comparable deeds of the boy Cúchulainn. Readers who want the full account should turn to the separate entry under that Irish title.

:::

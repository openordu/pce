``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Breizh is the Breton name for Brittany. Breizh Izel marks western or lower Brittany. Breizh Uhel denotes eastern or upper Brittany. The term Breizh therefore reaches two distinct halves of the land. Readers who meet Breizh should consult the Brittany entry for the fuller account.

:::
```

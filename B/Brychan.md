``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brychan, also written Brachan or Brochan, stands as a chief of Irish stock who ruled in the fifth or sixth century. He grew his power sharply once the Romans withdrew. The medieval kingdom of Brycheiniog bore his name, and he sometimes pairs with that kingdom as a sobriquet. Report says he took three wives and fathered a great many children, several of them saints such as Berwyn, Endellion and Keyne. His feast once fell on 6 April even though he was hardly a saint himself.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Bellinus is a variant form of the name Belenus, the Continental Celtic deity who embodied the sun and its light. A reader who meets this longer spelling should turn to the full Belenus entry for that radiant power's tale. That entry sets out the worship once offered to the god across ancient Gaul. The two names point to one and the same shining deity, known also as Bel and Belinos.

:::

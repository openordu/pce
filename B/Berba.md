``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Berba stands as an Irish river goddess who guards the River Barrow. In early Irish narrative the name Berba is set on the Barrow itself. MacKillop records that usage and links the name to the river. The Book of Invasions joins Berba to Cesair, remembered as the first settler of the isle. Berba thus serves both as the river's title and as the guardian spirit who watches over its waters.

:::
```

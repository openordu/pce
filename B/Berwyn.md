Berwyn, a Welsh name that means white peak, is a mountain range across north Wales. It stands on the border where Gwynedd and Powys meet. Before 1974 the area was part of Merioneth and Montgomeryshire. Before the Anglo-Norman conquest the whole range lay within Powys. The name Berwyn also belongs to a saint of the fifth century. It likewise marks a minor figure in the Welsh tale Culhwch ac Olwen.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bruidhean Bheag na hAlmhaine is an Irish tale title. It is a prose narrative of the Fenian Cycle. The English name is The Little Brawl at the Hill of Allen. It tells of an older Fionn mac Cumhaill. He still performs feats of valour in the tale. He fights beside his great-grandsons Eachtach and Ulan. S. H. O'Grady edited and translated the story. It appears in his Silva Gadelica of 1892.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beli is a Welsh ancestral character who often appears as a deity. He stands behind several royal lines in Wales as a founding figure. Beli probably grew out of the earlier god Belenus. Some writers link Beli to a shadowy historical person, Beli ab Elffin, who died in 721. The Mabinogi recalls Beli many times. In that cycle he is the husband of Don and father of Arianrhod and Caswallawn. In Cyfranc Lludd a Llefelys the sons of Beli are named Lludd and Llefelys. The later Arthurian hero Pelle seems to lean heavily on Beli Mawr. Older lore also treats Beli as a god of death and of the sea. The London place name Billingsgate may carry his name. Some writers tie Beli to the little Irish god Bile.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Barc Breasail is the name of the bark of Breasal, a vessel bound to the otherworld island. In Irish tradition the island is called Hy Brasil. The name Barc Breasail points to the craft that carries the traveller to that far shore. The account binds the boat to the seeking of the western isle.

:::

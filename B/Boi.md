``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Boi is a variant spelling of the name Bui. MacKillop records this alternate form in the dictionary entry for the headword. Bui ranks among the four wives reported for the hero Lug Lámfhota. The full account of that figure, along with her link to the monument at Knowth, sits under its own entry. Readers who meet the spelling Boi in older sources should treat it as a name for that same one person.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Bleiddwn is a figure of Welsh myth. His name means wolf cub. Gilfaethwy and Gwydion, who were brothers, once took on the shapes of a wolf and a female wolf. Their union produced Bleiddwn at the end of that transformation.

:::

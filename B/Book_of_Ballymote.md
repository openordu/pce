::: magazinestyle
The Book of Ballymote bears the Irish title Leabhar Bhaile an Mhóta. Scribes compiled it about 1390 at Ballymote in County Sligo. Much of the volume is historical in nature. It also holds important literary and imaginative pieces. These include the decoding of the ogham script together with bardic essays on metre and grammar. There is a mark of the infant Cormac and the tale of the loathly hag who becomes a beautiful woman. The volume also carries an Irish rendering of the Aeneid. The manuscript now rests at the Royal Irish Academy in Dublin. Robert Atkinson edited it there and published it in 1887.

:::

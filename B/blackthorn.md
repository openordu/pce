``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
blackthorn is the thorny shrub prunus spinosa. People in Ireland trusted it to shield them from ghosts. The Modern Irish name draighean carries that protective lore. The wood has long been favoured for light walking-sticks. Custom bars cutting the shrub on 11 May and on 11 November.

:::
```

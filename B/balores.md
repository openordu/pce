``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The balores is a Cornish name for the chough, a crow-like bird of the species pyrrhocorax pyrrhocorax. The bird shows black plumage with a red bill and red legs. Cornish usage usually sets the name behind the definite article, giving An Balores. The chough stands as a symbol of Cornish identity. R. Morton Nance chose it for the title of his play in the Cornish language, An Balores, staged in 1932. Cornish tradition also ties the chough to King Arthur.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
battle gods is a headword that MacKillop uses to gather Irish war deities under one pointer. The entry directs readers to badb and néit for the main record. It also notes bócánach and bánánach, whom it names as the battle demons. The Battle of... titles sit under Welsh cad and Irish cath.

:::

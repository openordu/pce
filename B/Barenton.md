``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Barenton is a Breton mythological site, also called Belenton or Baranton. It holds the magical Fountain That Makes Rain. The fountain carries rain-making power in the folklore of Brittany. MacKillop records the site under the spelling Baranton. He refers the reader to the forest of Broceliande. In Breton tradition, Barenton is set within that fabled wood.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
The Boyhood Deeds of Cuchulainn gather the stories of the hero's youth. They tell of his early adventures before the Cattle Raid of Cooley begins. That great raid opens the fuller telling of his deeds within the Tán Bó Cuailgne. The boyish feats of Cuchulainn stand as the opening chapter of that epic tale.

:::

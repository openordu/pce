Battle of works as a cross-reference marker in this source dictionary. The term sends a reader toward a longer account stored under its own title. The Welsh story of the fighting trees is Cad Goddeu. The parallel Irish account is Cath Maige Tuired, the tale of the gods who clashed with their foes.

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Book of Taliesin keeps the Welsh name Llyfr Taliesin. The volume came together near 1275 and holds sixty poems or more. The sixth-century poet Taliesin inspired these lines. Sir Ifor Williams judged twelve as likely his own work. The volume also holds Armes Prydain, the Prophecy of Britain. It keeps Cad Goddeu, whose title is the Battle of the Trees. That story tells of Myrddin, the seer folk call Merlin. The book rests at Aberystwyth inside the chief library of Wales.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Brynaich was a mocking Welsh term aimed at the English people in the verses of the twelfth and thirteenth centuries. This word Brynaich comes from Bernicia, an old northern English kingdom that had by then passed into Northumbria. A poet who wrote out Brynaich therefore called his enemy back to a realm long gone. The one word itself carries a sharp sting of memory that plain English could not match. So Brynaich gave Welsh bards a crude and memorable weapon for their verbal battles.

:::

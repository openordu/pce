``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
bánánach names preternatural female beings of early Irish literature. They appear above the field of battle, marking the carnage in the manner of the war-furies of older tradition. The MacKillop entry cross-references the badb and the bocánach, two related battle-haunting figures of early Irish lore.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brittany is a land that faces the Atlantic on the Celtic fringe of France. The Breton speakers call the region Breizh. The western half, Breizh-Izel or Lower Brittany, was largely Breton in speech and way. The decline of the spoken tongue came fast in the twentieth century. The eastern half, Upper Brittany, is home to the cities of Nantes and Rennes. In those towns the near tongue is a Gallic dialect called Gallo, with a touch of Breton. In this book the name Brittany also points the reader toward the entry for GAUL.

:::
```

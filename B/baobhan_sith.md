::: magazinestyle
The baobhan sith is a fearsome female figure of Scottish Gaelic oral narrative. The name itself means fairy woman. She behaves as a succubus, evil and highly dangerous. A man who accepts her embrace dies drained of blood. Her character differs sharply from the Irish death omen, the banshee. In Scottish Gaelic usage that Irish figure appears as ban-sith, bean-shith, or bean sith. Motif references: E251.3.3 and F471.2.1.

:::

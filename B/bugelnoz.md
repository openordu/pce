::: magazinestyle
bugelnoz is a Breton word meaning a night imp or goblin, attested in the folklore of the Vannes region in south-central Brittany. Within that local tradition the creature comes out under cover of night, in the hours after midnight and before dawn, in order to snatch victims away from the clutches of the devil. He performs this rescue by opening his mouth wide and drawing the endangered soul inside it, a protective act that distinguishes the bugelnoz from the merely threatening night-imp of neighboring tales.

:::

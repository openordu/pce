``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bedwyr is a Welsh warrior from the tales of Arthur. Writers treat him as the match of the knight Bedivere and of Beduerus. They also name him the origin of Pellinor and of Bellinor. His father is the man Pedrawg. Bedwyr keeps company with the knight Cei. He minds the drink cabinet of King Arthur. Bedwyr hurls the sword of Arthur into the lake. He travels with Culhwch when that hero seeks the maiden Olwen. In that tale the warrior has only one arm. Yet he masters the lance better than any rival. He also runs faster than most men. Later authors reduce his rank to that of a royal butler. Bedwyr stays trusted with the great sword Excalibur. He casts that blade back into the water. The Lady of the Lake then receives the weapon. He afterwards ferries the dying king into the isle of Avalon.

:::
```

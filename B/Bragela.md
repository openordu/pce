``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bragela appears as the wife of Cuchullin in the Ossian poems of James Macpherson from 1760. Macpherson shaped the figure from an older Irish source. In this she mirrors Emer, celebrated bride of the hero Cuchulainn in the early tales. A reader who meets Bragela in Ossian should recall that Emer came before her.

:::
```

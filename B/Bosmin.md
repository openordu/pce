::: magazinestyle
Bosmin is the only daughter of the hero Fingal in the Ossian tales. James Macpherson issued those poems in the year 1760. The name carries the sense of a smooth palm in Scots Gaelic. Her mother is Clatha, the child of Cathula. Because she stands alone among his children, Bosmin holds a singled place in his family. The verse recalls her lineage rather than any deeds of her own.

:::

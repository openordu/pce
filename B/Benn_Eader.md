::: magazinestyle
In Irish tradition the name Benn Eader points to the Hill of Howth. That peak stands about five hundred and sixty feet high on the Howth peninsula. The peninsula lies roughly nine miles northeast of Dublin. Many sagas and narratives count the summit as the easternmost point of Ireland. The celebrated warrior Oscar rests buried there beside his wife Etain. The champion Fionn mac Cumhaill kept boats ready on this shore. He sailed from here to the ruinous fight at Gabhair. Folk memory ties the place name to a figure called Etar. Some accounts name him as the father of Etain Fholtfhind. Over time the hill has also carried the name Dun Etair.

:::

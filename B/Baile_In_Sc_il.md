``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baile In Scail is an Irish story of a vision, written before the year 1056. The name carries the sense of the frenzy or the rapture of the phantom. It tells how Conn of the Hundred Battles stood on the height of Tara, the seat of the old kings. There he saw the line of those who would follow him to the rule of the isle.

:::
```

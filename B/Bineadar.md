::: magazinestyle
Bineadar is an alternate spelling for the name Benn Eader. The Irish name Benn Eader marks the Hill of Howth, the easternmost point of Ireland in early Irish tradition. A reader who meets the briefer form Bineadar should turn to the main Benn Eader entry. There the hill and its story are set out in full.

:::

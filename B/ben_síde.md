``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ben síde is an Old-Irish spelling of the banshee. MacKillop prints the headword as ben side, sith. The form belongs to the Old Irish language stratum of the name. The full record of the death-portending being sits under the headword banshee.

:::
```

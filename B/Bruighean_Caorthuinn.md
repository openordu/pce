::: magazinestyle
Bruighean Caorthuinn is the Scots Gaelic spelling for the fairy tale that most writers call Bruidhean Chaorthainn. Gaelic copy may render the title under either form. The shifted vowel marks the Scottish version of the name. A reader who wants the full story should turn to the main article, because this entry serves as a cross reference rather than the whole tale.

:::

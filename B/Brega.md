``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Brega is a plain in the east of Ireland. It lies between the river Boyne and the river Liffey. The area covered the eastern part of County Meath and part of north Dublin. The site of Tara rose within this region. The plain took its name from a son of the hero Breogan. Its later kings claimed a line from the old stock of Mide. The plain joined a new Mide at the close of the age. Scholars often write the latin name Bregia for this district.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Brandub is a board-game played in early Ireland. It is a pastime easily mixed up with the games called fidchell and búanfach. The name may mean the board itself. Looked on as a map, it seems to stand for the whole island of Ireland in miniature. The word also served as a common name in old Ireland. At least two saints of the country bore it, and so did a run of lesser kings. The best known holder was Brandub king of the province later named Leinster. He died about the year 605. He won the lady Dubh Lacha, the wife of Mongán, through a trick. Her husband later took her back with a second ruse.

:::

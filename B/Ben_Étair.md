::: magazinestyle
Ben Étair forms the Irish alternate name for Benn Eader. Readers who meet the shorter form should turn to the Benn Eader entry for the full account. Benn Eader names the Hill of Howth, which rises on the Howth peninsula some nine miles northeast of Dublin. Many texts call that height the easternmost point of Ireland. This cross-reference links the two spellings of one place.

:::

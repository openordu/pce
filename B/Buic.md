::: magazinestyle
Buic is a short variant that the source book sets beside the name Buide mac Báin, whose fuller article appears under the story of the great Ulster cattle raid. The dictionary draws the reader through this short cross reference and then to the record of Buide and of Donn Cuailnge. This entry offers no full narrative of its own, as it serves only to restate the spelling that the source book prints.

:::

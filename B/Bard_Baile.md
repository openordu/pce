``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
A bàrd baile is a Gaelic village poet of the Highlands. The term names a local bard who acted as a social commentator. His view centred on a small settlement. His songs covered homeland, war, love, and local news. Elegy, eulogy, and satire moved from the clan tradition into that work. Radio and television later took the role away. The village bards have nearly vanished in the last century. The old gathering-houses of the Highlands are also gone. Some critics dislike the label, for it sounds narrow. Yet many such poets had fought wars and sailed the wide seas.

:::
```

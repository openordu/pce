::: magazinestyle
Bé Téite is a woman of the Irish magical people of Danu. She counts as a daughter of the goddess Flidais. The folk to whom she belongs are called the Tuatha Dé Danann. The name Bé Téite carries the sense of a wanton or luxurious woman. Her later Irish spelling is Bétéide. Flidais shields the deer and leads the beasts of the forest in legend. Bé Téite moves as an active and willful woman within the tales. She stands among the lively women of the whole shining race.

:::

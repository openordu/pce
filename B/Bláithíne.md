``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bláithíne means flower or blossom. She belongs to the Ulster cycle. Her name matches Bláthnat, Bláthnait, and Bláthnad. She loves Cúchulainn and betrays her husband Cú Roí. Her father may be Conchobar or Midir. Cúchulainn took her on a raid into the Otherworld in Scotland. With Cú Roí's aid he carried off a magic cauldron, three cows, and the girl. Cú Roí recovered them and buried him to his armpits, shearing his locks. At Samain he made a tryst with her, and she helped the Ulstermen kill Cú Roí. His poet Ferchertne clasped her at a cliff and fell with her to the shore. Her tale resembles the Welsh Blodeuwedd.

:::
```

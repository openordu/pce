``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Belgae are a P-Celtic people of northern Gaul. Julius Caesar called them the fiercest of all the tribes. Parts of that people settled in Britain before the end of the first century BC. They chose lands that lay open to the sea. Irish writers linked the Belgae to the Fir Bolg of Irish pseudo-history. Roderick O'Flaherty made this claim as early as 1685. Caesar stated that the Belgae came from Germanic lands. Scholars now read that statement as geographic rather than ethnic. He placed their home east of the Rhine. Their lands spread across the modern districts of Hampshire and Wiltshire. In 1946 T. F. O'Rahilly argued that they matched the Builg and the Erainn. Scholars now reject that argument.

:::
```

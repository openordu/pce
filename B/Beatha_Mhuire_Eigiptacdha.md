``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beatha Mhuire Eigiptacdha is a Middle Irish life of Mary of Egypt. Three recensions of the text survive. The first is a 15th-century prose adaptation. The second depends on the Legenda Aurea, a Latin legend. The third is a short exemplum on the Virgin. The text stresses repentance and reform in holy life. It sits within a wider hagiography of Ireland. A Welsh version is named Buchedd Mair o'r Aifft. Mary lived in the Egyptian desert and her story shaped the saints. The work links Celtic religious letters to the Egyptian theme.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Beóthach is the Nemedian forebear of the divine people later known as the Tuatha Dé Danann. The name draws on the Irish idea of the living. In the pseudo-history known as the Book of Invasions he appears as grandson of Nemed and son of Iarbanél. He survived the rout at Tory Island and later fell victim to plague. His clan then quit the dynastic wars of Ireland once the Nemedians had been defeated. They wandered across northern Europe and advanced the arts of divination, druidism, and philosophy there. Eleven generations then went by before his descendants returned to form the people the lore calls the Tuatha Dé Danann.

:::
```

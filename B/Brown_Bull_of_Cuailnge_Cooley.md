``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Brown Bull of Cuailnge/Cooley is the bull that Queen Medb desired. His Irish name is Donn Cuailnge. He appears in the Táin Bó Cuailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley. Dáire mac Fiachna owned him. Medb of Connacht wanted him for her wealth. Her wish to win the bull set the great war in motion. Originally he was a pig-keeper named Friuch. He contended with Rucht, the pig-keeper of Ochall. Their conflict carried them through many forms. They became ravens, water beasts, stags, and dragons. In the end they became the Brown Bull and the White Bull of Ulster. Donn Cuailnge slew his rival Finnbennach. He left the battle mortally wounded.

:::
```

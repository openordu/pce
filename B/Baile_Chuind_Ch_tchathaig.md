``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baile Chuind Chetchathaig is an older form of a text of Irish vision. The name names the frenzy of the king Conn of the Hundred Battles, who saw the future. The dictionary sends the reader of this headword to the fuller work that tells the whole tale. The older text holds a place among the early writings of the court.

:::
```

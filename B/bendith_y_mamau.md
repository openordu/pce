``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
bendith y mamau is a Welsh phrase meaning the blessings of the mothers. Speakers in Glamorganshire use it as a euphemism for the fairies. Welsh tradition more often calls those beings tylwyth teg. Users prefer the euphemism to avoid kidnapping and the fairies' mischief. Related lore also names a hound pack, cwn bendith y mamau, the dogs of the blessed ones. MacKillop treats that phrase under the entry c’wn annwn.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Bough of Dathi is the English name for the ash tree known as bile Dathi. That tree belonged to Dathi, a ruler also called Nathi. He was a nephew of Niall of the Nine Hostages. Writers of early Ireland count this tree among the six wondrous trees of the land. The name translates to the branch of Dathi from the older Irish phrase. Readers find it grouped with ash and tree in the old listings.

:::
```

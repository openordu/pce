::: magazinestyle
Belus is an alternate spelling for the name Belenus, the Continental Celtic deity of light and of the sun. A reader who meets this fuller form should look to the Belenus entry for the tale of that solar power. There the rites paid to it are also set out. For this work Belus and Belenus name the same shining god, honoured across ancient Gaul.

:::

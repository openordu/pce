``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bacorbladra is a teacher of the Partholonian people in the Lebor Gabala. The old text names him as the first foster father of Ireland. His office is to raise a child of the settlers and to hold the place of a father to him. The name is also written with a second form in the book.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Blood is a mythic theme among the Celts. Roman writers called it sacred to them. They told of drinking slain enemies' blood. Their claims need a skeptical eye, because their audience feared the Celts. Yet other signs support blood rituals. The Roman commentator Lucan described how blood marked the trees of a sacred grove near Marseilles. Animal sacrifice blood was smeared on sacred grove trees. Blood was seen as carrying the essence, or life force. Drinking it would restore the warrior. Blood brotherhood lasted in the Scottish isles into recent times. At Mag Tuired the trio Badb, Mórrígan, and Macha sends up frogs, blood, and fire against the Fir Bolg. While drawing a sling stone from Emer, Cúchulainn drank some of her blood. That act left him forbidden to marry Emer. After Cúchulainn killed Eochaid Glas, a banshee outraged by Eochaid bathed in that blood, washing away the shame. Ransoming Fionn mac Cumhaill requires the blood of three kings.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Binnbérlach is a variant spelling of the Irish name Baile Binnbhéarlach, the sweet-spoken figure whose name means the sweet-speech. MacKillop, writing at baile, treats Binnbérlach as a bare cross-reference. He directs the reader to the fuller baile entry for the tale of the parted lovers. In this work Binnbérlach and baile name one same sweet-spoken figure, and the full story lives under baile.

:::

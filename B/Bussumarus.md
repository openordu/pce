``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bussumarus was a deity of the Continental Celts who lived on the European mainland. The Romans set this god beside their own Jupiter and treated the pair in the same way. The name itself carries a meaning tied to large lips. Records from the Gallic lands pass this deity down to us. Bussumarus thus appears often in lists of Celtic gods whom Rome chose to accept. His place shows how one native power gained a second identity inside the Roman world.

:::
```

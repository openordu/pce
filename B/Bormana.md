``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bormana is a goddess who stands beside the healing deity Borvo. She serves as his female consort on the occasions when Borvo takes up the title of Bormanus. Yet the goddess Damona is the partner most often assigned to Borvo. Bormana fills that role only beneath the alternate title. So Bormana joins one single god who appears under two different labels.

:::
```

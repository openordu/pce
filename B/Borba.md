::: magazinestyle
Borba is an enemy of the great hunter Fionn mac Cumhaill who turns up in Fenian folklore. The tales portray him as rough, cutting, and haughty in his dealings with the Fianna. The records name Sinsar as his father. That puts him clearly inside the family lines of this storytelling world. His constant clashes with Fionn and his band make him a recurring source of danger across many traditional adventures. He stands out mainly through his proud and aggressive tone rather than any single notable deed.

:::

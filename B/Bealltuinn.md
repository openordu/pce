``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bealltuinn is the Scottish Gaelic form of the feast fixed at the first warm day. It is rendered into English as Beltaine. The celebration numbers among the four great feasts of the Celtic year, placed beside Imbolc and Samhain. Fires were built and the cattle were driven between the flames. The name keeps the old speech of the May festival.

:::
```

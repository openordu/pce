``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Baillgel is a name of a woman of the fairy court folk of the old plain of Ireland. The sense of the name is bright limb. It is also written with a second letter in place of the first, as Ballgel. The figures that bear it are found among the people of the royal plain in the old family lines.

:::
```

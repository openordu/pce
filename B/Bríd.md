``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bríd is one of several spellings that Celtic writers use for the Irish saint's name rendered as Brigid in the standard dictionary and often as Brigit in older scholarship. The acute accent marks vowel length in the Gaelic form. This entry serves only as a cross reference that points the reader toward the fuller article on the shared figure, because the headword carries no separate narrative of its own.

:::
```

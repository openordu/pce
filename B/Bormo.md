``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bormo is yet another alternate name for the god Borvo. The two labels describe one and the same deity, so no separate being hides behind the shorter word. The old tales use Bormo as a second title for that god of the healing. He was seen in Gaul as the master of the warm springs.

:::
```

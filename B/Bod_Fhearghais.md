``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Bod Fhearghais points to the word fál. The name fál is tied to the Lia Fáil, the Stone of Destiny of Irish legend. The stone stood on the Hill of Tara. Each king of Ireland was crowned upon it. See the entry Lia Fáil for the full account.

:::
```

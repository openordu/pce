::: magazinestyle
Becfola is a character from the cycle of Irish kings. Her name carries the sense of a small dowry. She becomes the wife of Diarmait, the king of Tara. She finds little joy in that arranged match. Becfola longs for Crimthann, the king's fosterling. She urges the young man to elope with her. He refuses to travel on a Sunday, so he does not come. She then meets a fairy lover named Flann ua Fedach. They spend one night on an islet inside Lough Erne. Their love is not consummated. The time apart seems an instant to ordinary people. Becfola returns to Tara in the morning. Flann later returns in disguise, and the two depart together.

:::

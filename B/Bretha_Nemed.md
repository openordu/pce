::: magazinestyle
Bretha Nemed, the judgements of privileged persons, is an eighth-century collection of Irish law texts from Munster. Cormac's Glossary quotes two named legal sources, the Senchas Mar and the Bretha Nemed. The citations raise the question whether Bretha Nemed was a law book as large as the Senchas Mar. One surviving text in the Cotton manuscript carries the title Corus Breatha Neimead. Medieval glosses call it Bretha Nemed Toisech, the first Bretha Nemed. It stands among the earliest native Irish legal collections.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Jasconius is the name of the great fish on which Saint Brendan and his party set foot. The Latin name carries the sense of fish. Brendan was the sixth-century abbot of Ireland. His voyage tale told how the crew landed upon a whale the size of an island and lit a fire on its back. Jasconius therefore marks a wonder of the early Irish voyage story.

:::

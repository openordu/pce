``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
javelin is a MacKillop pointer headword that resolves to the gaebolga, the Gáe Bulga of early Irish heroic literature. MacKillop lists javelin as the English term for the mythical Irish weapon. The fuller record of the barbed spear of the Ulster Cycle, its use by Cú Chulainn, and its poisonous tips sits under gaebolga.

:::
```

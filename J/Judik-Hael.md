::: magazinestyle
Judik-Hael is the son of Jud-Hael, the legendary chief of Brittany. The poet Taliesin read the dream of the father and told of the boy's rise. He said the son would govern with ease and stand among the great names of Breton story. Jud-Hael's dream came true. Judik-Hael therefore took a place among the early rulers of Brittany.

:::

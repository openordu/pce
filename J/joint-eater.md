``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
joint-eater is the English name MacKillop uses for the Irish fairy called alp-luachra. MacKillop treats the English folk-name as a brief pointer to the same record as the Irish term. The fuller record of the joint-eater fairy, its habit of lodging in the body and causing wasting disease, sits under alp-luachra.

:::
```

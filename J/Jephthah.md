``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Jephthah is a judge of Israel in the Bible's Book of Judges. He gave his daughter to die to keep his vow. The story fills chapters eleven and twelve. Celtic writers cited it to set belief beside the biblical tales. Upon the Manx island, the daughter of Jephthah replaced the goddess Eithne at the feast of Laa Luanistyn. The story thus joined the Bible to the rites of Lughnasa.

:::
```

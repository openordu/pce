``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Japheth is the third son of Noah in the Book of Genesis. The Irish pseudo-history Lebor Gabala names him often to join native memory to the biblical story of creation. The invader Nemed is set out as his descendant. Early genealogists made him an ancestor of many families. Japheth therefore entered the working kin of the Irish book of invasions.

:::
```

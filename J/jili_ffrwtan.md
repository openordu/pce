::: magazinestyle
jili ffrwtan is a MacKillop variant term for the tylwyth teg, the Welsh fairy folk. MacKillop applies the term especially to a small Welsh fairy of forward romantic character. The name jili ffrwtan is unglossable, much like the German Rumpelstiltskin or the English Tom Tit Tom. MacKillop treats jili ffrwtan as a Welsh household fairy with a cryptic proper name and a confident bearing. The fuller record of the Welsh fairy folk, their domains, and their relations sits under tylwyth teg.

:::

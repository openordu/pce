::: magazinestyle
Julius Caesar is the Latin name of the Roman general and history of the first century before the present. His account of the Gallic wars stands as the chief written source for the ways of the continental Celts. He led legions in Gaul and wrote of the tribes he met. Caesar appears among the classical writers who set down the old Celtic world. Julius Caesar thus marks the Roman witness to the Celts.

:::

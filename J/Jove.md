``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Jove is an older form of the name Jupiter. Jupiter was the father of the Roman gods and the god of sky, thunder, and oaths. The Celts of the Roman empire took him as their own under local titles. They placed him below Mercury, Apollo, and Mars in their worship. Jove therefore names the Roman sky god as the Celts met him.

:::
```

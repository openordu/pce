``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Jud-Hael is a legendary chief of Brittany. It is set in the time when the Britons left the island of Britain. He dreamed a riddle that the poet Taliesin read. Taliesin said the dream told that the son would rule with greater ease and rank among the great men of Breton story. That son, Judik-Hael, came to the prophecy. Jud-Hael therefore stands at the head of a royal line of Brittany.

:::
```

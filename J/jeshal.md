``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
jeshal is the Manx word for the sunwise turn, the deiseil in Irish and the deosil in Scottish Gaelic. MacKillop treats the Manx form as a brief pointer to the same record. The fuller record of the Celtic ritual direction, its use in folklore, and its role as a boundary gesture sits under sunwise_turn.

:::
```

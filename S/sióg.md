``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
sióg is the Modern Irish word for a single fairy. The unreformed spelling sídheóg appears in older texts. MacKillop points the reader to the sheogue headword for the anglicized rendering. The sióg belongs to the wider Irish fairy family that includes the síth and the sídhe.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sliab na mBan is the printed main form of the Tipperary mountain better known as Slievenamon. The headword also lists the forms Sliab Ban Finn and Sliabh na mBan Fionn. Slievenaman, Slieve-na-Man, and Knockfefin appear there too. The phrase means the mountain of the women. The Slievenamon node carries the fuller record of Fionn's foot-race and the cairn door. This row keeps the Irish main form on its own page.

:::
```

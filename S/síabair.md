::: magazinestyle
síabair, with the variants siabraid, siabhra, siofra, siabra, siabur, and sheevra, names the large, malicious elf or fairy MacKillop records for Irish tradition. The Irish verb siabraid reads to arouse to fury, enchant, distort, bewitch. One síabair is said to have sought to strangle Cormac mac Airt when the pagan king showed interest in Christianity. The figure belongs to the wider family of Irish evil fairies.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Sliab Bladma, printed also as Sliabh Bladhma and Slieve Bloom, is a range of hills to 1,733 feet. It stands on the borders of counties Offaly and Laois. The range bears the name of Bladma, invader of the Milesian race. Action in many Fenian stories takes place on these hills. One text tells how Muirenn took refuge here while pregnant. There she gave birth to Demne, the boy later known as Fionn mac Cumhaill. John Feehan published a study of the range's landscape at Dublin in 1979.

:::

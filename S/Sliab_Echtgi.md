::: magazinestyle
Sliab Echtgi is an Irish form of the mountain range recorded under Echtge. The dictionary routes this entry to the Echtge article. There the range sits between Clare and Galway. Legend ties those hills to the lady Echthge of the Tuatha Dé Danann. English usage knows them as Slieve Aughty.

:::

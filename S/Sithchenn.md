``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sithchenn is a druid at the court of Eochaid Mugmedón. His name builds on old Irish words for long head. He devises two contests by which Niall Noígiallach, Niall of the Nine Hostages, wins the succession to his father. The tale lives in the Echtra of Eochaid Mugmedón's sons. Monaghan's account fills in the trial. Niall and his four brothers came to Sithchenn to have their futures read. The druid set fire to his own forge and watched what each youth seized as he ran from the flames. One brother carried the fagots, another the bellows, another the beers. Niall came out bearing the anvil and its blocks, the heaviest and most telling load of all. That choice marked him for the kingship, for the anvil held the forge together as a king holds a people. Sithchenn stands beside the smiths and druids whose test sets the true king on his throne.

:::
```

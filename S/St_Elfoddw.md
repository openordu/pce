``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Saint Elfoeddw, St Elfoddw in the older form, was a bishop of the Welsh church in the eighth century. He served in the region of Bangor in Wales. A notice in the Annales Cambriae names him archbishop of Gwynedd. The same record fixes his death in the year 809. Another entry in the Annales Cambriae says that in 768 he altered the Britons' rule for Easter. He brought the last churches in Wales into line with the Roman date. This reform carried real weight. Later texts of the Historia Brittonum name a writer as a pupil of Saint Elfoeddw. He therefore stands out in the history of Wales.

:::
```

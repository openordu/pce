::: magazinestyle
Senchán Torpéist, printed also as Seanchán Toirpéist, was a Connacht poet who lived from about 570 to 617. Repute credits him with saving the Tain Bo Cuailnge, the Cattle Raid of Cooley, from oblivion. A widely known story tells how he tried in vain to piece the narrative together. Fergus mac Roich then stood before him in a vision. The dead hero recited the whole Tain. He led the great bardic assembly, so less fabulous accounts state. His chiefship of Ireland's poets followed Dallan Forgaill, who lived from about 540 to 596.

:::

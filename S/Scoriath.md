Scoriath rules Fir Morca in the tale Orgain Denna Rig, the Destruction of Dind Rig. He aids Labraid Loingsech in winning back that hero's kingdom after exile. He also yields his own lovely daughter Muiriath to Labraid in a marriage alliance.

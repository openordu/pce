``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sreng is a Fir Bolg warrior who fought at Mag Tuired in the first of its battles. There he cut off the right arm of Nuadu, the Tuatha Dé Danann king. Sreng first served his people as an ambassador skilled in delay. At the first meeting with the invaders Bres proposed a division of Ireland between the two hosts. Sreng saw how far Tuatha Dé weapons outclassed his own and refused the deal. Battle followed, and in it he cut off Nuadu's arm. The next day brought a strange request. Nuadu asked Sreng to bind his own right arm behind his back. Only so would their combat be fair. Again the answer was no. For peace, the Tuatha Dé Danann ceded the province of Connacht to Sreng and the Fir Bolg. He took the settlement. His one-armed foe held the kingship only until the next battle. The settlement bound the old people of Ireland to the west.

:::
```

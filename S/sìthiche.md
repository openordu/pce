sìthiche names the headword variant MacKillop gives for the druid Sithchenn at the court of Eochaid Mugmedón. The druid devises two contests by which Niall Noigiallach wins the kingship of his father. The full record sits under the headword Sithchenn and the adventure Echtra Mac nEchach Muigmedóin.

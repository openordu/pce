::: magazinestyle
sianach names the large, malevolent, predatory deer of Highland folk memory. The creature belongs to the wider family of monstrous animals that haunt the Scottish landscape. Gaelic sources preserve the form as a beast of dread to travellers who crossed the empty hills. The sianach shares its ecological niche with other folkloric beasts of the Highlands and often features in oral warnings to children.

:::

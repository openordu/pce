sea-monster marks a category in Celtic tradition that MacKillop treats under the broader water-monster theme. MacKillop points the reader to the afanc headword for the fuller record of the lake-haunting monster. Related entries include bèist, bledmall, caoránach, cìre in cròin, muirdris, oilliphéist, and suileach.

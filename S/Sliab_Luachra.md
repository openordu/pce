``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sliab Luachra, printed also as Slieve Logher, is a mountain range where County Cork meets County Kerry. It stands near where the River Sullane gathers its sources. Stories from oral tradition set both heroic and magical action upon it.

:::
```

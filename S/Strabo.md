::: magazinestyle
Strabo was a Greek geographer who worked in Augustan Rome. His dates run from about 58 BC to about AD 24. His seventeen-book Geographia holds much descriptive material on early Celtic society. Writers cite it widely. The Loeb Classical Library issued his translation in eight volumes. H. L. Jones prepared that New York edition of 1917-33.

:::

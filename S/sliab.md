``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
sliab names a hill or peak in Celtic speech, the older Irish and modern Scottish Gaelic form. The word opens many place-names across Ireland and Scotland and anglicizes as slieve. The full record of Celtic hill terminology sits under the headword hill.

:::
```

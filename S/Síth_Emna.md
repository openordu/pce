Síth Emna is a cross-reference head in MacKillop's dictionary. The form points readers to the fuller Emain Ablach entry. It sits among the variant renderings of that otherworld island. The full record of the island sits under Emain Ablach in the dictionary. The first element, síth, is an Old Irish spelling. That spelling comes from the same root as *sidh. The word *sidh marks fairy dwellings in the older language. The síth element places Síth Emna in the family of otherworldly place-names. Those names carry fairy associations in the Irish tradition. The two lines under the síth head give a brief etymology. The same lines also point to Emain Ablach. All depth on geography, lore, and sources sits with the Emain Ablach node.

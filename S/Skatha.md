::: magazinestyle
Skatha is the anglicized form of the warrior woman recorded under Scáthach. The Scáthach node carries the record of Cúchulainn's teacher on the isle of Skye. The name reaches print in that anglicized shape beside the fuller Gaelic form.

:::

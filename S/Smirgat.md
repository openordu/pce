``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Smirgat, also written Smirnat, is the daughter of Fothad Canann. The lists of Fionn mac Cumhaill's wives name her among their number. She had the gift of foresight, a standing power in the tales of Fionn's women. She warned her husband that he would die if he ever drank from a horn. From that day Fionn drank only from a cup or a bowl, and so held the doom at bay. Her care followed him at every feast. The warning held for his whole life, and the death she read never came by that road. Her word joined the chain of prophetic women whose sayings bind even the great heroes of the Fianna. In a world where a geis or a prophecy can kill, one wife's warning was armour.

:::
```

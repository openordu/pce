::: magazinestyle
Seisyllwg, printed also as Seissyllwg and Seisylwch, was a petty realm, a gwlad, of early medieval Wales. King Seisyll ap Clydog of Ceredigion formed it in the mid-eighth century by force. He united the southern gwlad of Ystrad Tywi with his own realm. More than a century on, in 871, Seisyllwg joined with Gwynedd through a marriage into Rhodri Mawr's line. Seisyll's great-great-granddaughter became that union's bride. After a second short spell of independence, the kingdom merged into Deheubarth when Hywel Dda annexed Dyfed. Seisyllwg's land area matches the modern counties of Dyfed and West Glamorgan, drawn in 1974.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Sainrith mac Imbaith is the father of Macha (3), the figure of the Emain Macha story. The dictionary glosses his name as strange son of the ocean in its Macha article. There his daughter declares this parentage as she defends her husband, and a perpetual evil descends on Ulster.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Sliab Betha, printed also as Sliabh Beatha, Slieve Beagh, and Siabay, names a range of high hills. Its highest ground reaches 1,255 feet. The range stands on the borders of County Monaghan, Fermanagh, and Tyrone. It takes its name from Bith, the early invader of Ireland in the pseudo-history Lebor Gabála, the Book of Invasions.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Setlocenia is a minor goddess of early northern Britain. Her name carries the sense of goddess of long life or the long-lived one. Her worship rests on a single record, an invocation at Maryport in Cumberland. No fuller story of her survives. The one inscription shows a deity of prosperity and long life. She likely guarded a long and healthy existence along with the goods that go with it. Writers compare her in concept, though not in word origin, with the Irish Buanann.

:::
```

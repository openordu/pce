``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sithallin is a character of James Macpherson's Ossian, published between 1760 and 1763. Swaran killed him along with his brother Ardan. The dictionary judges the name an adaptation of Ailbhin, more often written Ainle. Ainle numbered among the sons of Uisnech.

:::
```

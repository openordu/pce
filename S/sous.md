``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
sous names learning acquired by legitimate means in early Irish tradition. The form so-us, so-as, so-fiuss marks the contrast with sorcery and the occult art of mantism or divination. Christian scribes used sous for both trained practice and Christian revelation. The word is the antonym of dichetal do chennaib, imbas forosnai, and teinm laida. The wider record of knowledge and its forms sits under the headword knowledge.

:::
```

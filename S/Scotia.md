``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Scotia first served as a Latin name for Ireland. After Q-Celtic Gaelic Gaels invaded Argyll, and later merged with the Picts, the name turned into an honorific for Scotland. The Lebor Gabala also carries Scotia as a variant of the name Scota, the Egyptian ancestress. The name thus travelled from Ireland to Scotland across the early medieval period.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sean-bhean Bhocht is the Irish spelling of the phrase meaning poor old woman. It renders the name printed under Shan Van Vocht, the personification of Ireland current in the eighteenth century. That figure apparently derives from Cailleach Bheire. Under this spelling the phrase also served as a pseudonym of Roger Casement, the Irish patriot of 1864-1916. The Shan Van Vocht node carries the fuller record of the personification.

:::
```

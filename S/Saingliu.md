``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Saingliu is the last and favourite horse of Cúchulainn. The printed forms include Dub Saingliu, with the genitives Sainglend and Dubsainglend. The element dub, black, marks the longer form. Speed and beauty set this horse above every other in the story. The entry pairs the name with Liath Macha, the hero's other noted mount.

:::
```

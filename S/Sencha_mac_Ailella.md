::: magazinestyle
Sencha mac Ailella, printed also as Aillelea and Ailill, is the peacemaker among the Ulster warriors. His name relates to senchaid, custodian of tradition or historian. He serves as an Irish counterpart of Nestor, the wise elder of the Iliad. Writers compare him also with Merlin of the Arthurian legends. He holds the offices of chief judge and poet in the reign of Conchobar mac Nessa. Fostering Cuchulainn was his offer, yet in the event he taught the hero only eloquence.

:::

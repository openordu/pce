::: magazinestyle
Snowdon, in Welsh Eryri, is the highest mountain in Wales and England at 3,560 feet. It stands 12 miles south-east of Caernarfon in Gwynedd, north-western Wales. A small range of peaks surrounds it, sometimes known collectively as Snowdonia. Welsh oral tradition celebrates the mountain widely. It is the home of a famous eagle and of the giant Rhita Gawr. J. E. Lloyd wrote on the mountains' history and legend in a Cardiff volume of 1925.

:::

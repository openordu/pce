``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Sualtam mac Róich, printed also as Sualdam, is the mortal father or stepfather of Cúchulainn. Conception stories give his wife Deichtine a miraculous pregnancy, then marriage to Sualtam. In the Táin he hears the fighting while his son lies wounded. He fears for the boy, who sends him to Emain Macha to rouse the men of Ulster. At the palace he calls out three times, and nobody answers. The druid Cathbad rebukes him for speaking unbidden. He turns away in anger and falls on his own shield edge, which beheads him. Even his severed head, carried back on the shield, roars out his warning.

:::
```

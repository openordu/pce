::: magazinestyle
sínach names the sea-monster MacKillop records as the later name for the stormy creature who fought with Fergus mac Léti. The Irish sínach means stormy, giving the figure its etymology. In earlier texts the creature is called muirdris. Related figures include caoránach and oilliphéist, both of which belong to the wider family of Celtic sea-monsters. The broader record of Celtic sea-monsters sits under the headword sea-monster.

:::

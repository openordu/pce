::: magazinestyle
Scoti is the name the Lebor Gabala, the Book of Invasions, uses for the Gaels or early Irish. Elsewhere it stands as a variant spelling of Scotti, the Latin name for the Irish people. The Scotti article carries the fuller record of the name and its history.

:::

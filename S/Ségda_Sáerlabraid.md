``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ségda Sáerlabraid is a prince of the Otherworld. His name elements mean noble and speaker. This son of the sinless pair who rule Tír Tairngire had the father Dáire and the mother Rígru Rosclethan. His parents coupled only once, at the moment of his conception. Conn seeks the boy in the tale Echtrae Airt meic Cuinn. Conn hopes the prince will carry fertility back to a wasted Ireland. Ségda shows Conn ungrudging affection. He is even ready to be sacrificed for him. His mother Rígru rescues him before that can happen.

:::
```

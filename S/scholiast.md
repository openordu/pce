``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
scholiast names an unnamed copyist or commentator who annotated manuscripts in the Celtic scholarly tradition. MacKillop classifies the figure as part of the scholarly apparatus of medieval Celtic texts. Such copyists added glosses and notes to works of much earlier origin. The role appears across the manuscript traditions of Irish, Welsh, and Cornish material.

:::
```

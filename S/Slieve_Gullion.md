``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Slieve Gullion is a high peak of about 1,893 feet in County Armagh. It stands five miles south-west of Newry and eight miles west of Dundalk. The mountain is one of the best-known heights in Ireland. It guards the Gap of the North, the route from Ulster to the south. On the south slope sits a cruciform passage tomb that people call Cailleach Bhearra's House. The mountain takes its name from Cuilenn, a lord of the side in old tales. One story tells how Fionn mac Cumhaill was weakened by his adventures here. In a common version of the tale he bathes in a lake and grows old at once.

:::
```

Sliab na Trí nDée, printed also as Sliabh na nDée Dána, is an Irish name for Corleck Hill. The phrase means hill of the three gods. The Corleck Hill record carries the fuller account of the Cavan site and its stone head. This row keeps the Irish name on its own page.

:::

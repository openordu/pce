``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Samildánach is an epithet of Lug Lamfhota meaning master of all the arts. The name parses from sam, together, il, many, and dan, art, so it counts his many skills. The dictionary also prints the shorter form Ildanach. The Lug node carries the full record of the god who bears this title.

:::
```

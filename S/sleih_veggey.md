::: magazinestyle
sleih veggey is the Manx name MacKillop records for the little people of Man. The phrase translates as the little folk in Manx Gaelic. MacKillop points the reader to the fairy headword for the wider Celtic record of the supernatural diminutive beings. The Manx form sits alongside the Irish and Scottish equivalents in the broader fairy tradition.

:::

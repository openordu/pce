::: magazinestyle
Senmag Étair is the printed variant of the plain-name recorded under Mag nElta. The Mag nElta node holds the fuller record of the Dublin plain. That plain, the old field of the bird flock, lay between Tallaght, Clontarf, and Howth. The Partholonians of the Book of Invasions settled on it.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Sliab Cuilinn is the printed main form of the Armagh peak better known in English as Slieve Gullion. The dictionary also lists the spellings Cuillinn and Cuilind in its headword line. The Slieve Gullion node carries the fuller record of the mountain, its passage-tomb, and the tales of Fionn and Cuilenn. This row keeps the Irish main form on its own page.

:::

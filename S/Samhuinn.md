``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Samhuinn is the Scottish Gaelic spelling of Samain, the November feast. The dictionary prints the variant forms of that feast under the Samain head, which carries the full record. The encyclopedia keeps each printed spelling on its own row.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
sìthich names a wantonly mischievous Highland sprite that MacKillop ties to Scottish Gaelic tradition. The figure is known to pester women in birth pangs and is said to steal newborn infants, leaving fairy changelings in their place. The creature ranks among the stock figures of early Highland lore and drew the ire of a seventeenth-century clergyman of the Kirk, who denounced its peculiar, flint-like deadly weapons. The broader fairy record sits under the headword fairy.

:::

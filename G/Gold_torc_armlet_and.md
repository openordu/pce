``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
This jewellery set from the Waldalgesheim burial contains a gold torc, an armlet, and two matching bracelets. A torc is a stiff neck ring of twisted metal, and the armlet encircles the arm. Workers opened this early La Tène grave near Bingen in Germany in the year 1869. The ornaments belonged to a Celtic noblewoman known as the Waldalgesheim princess. Their decoration uses the late Waldalgesheim style of the period. Mask motifs on the bracelets echo an older Early Style. Stylistic dating places the set in the last third of the fourth century BC. These objects show influence from western Europe and the Mediterranean world.

:::
```

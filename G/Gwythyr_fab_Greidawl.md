``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwythyr fab Greidawl is an eternal combatant in Welsh tradition. The name can be read as victor, son of the scorcher. He was the intended husband of Creiddylad, the beautiful daughter of Lludd Llaw Ereint. Gwyn ap Nudd carried off Creiddylad before the two could marry. Gwyn defeated the force that came to rescue Gwythyr's bride. The captives of Gwythyr were treated very savagely. So bitter was the hatred that Arthur condemned the two men to fight each May Day until the end of time. Each Calan Mai, or May Day, the two renew their combat.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Giona mac Lugha is a champion of the Fianna in Irish tradition. Some accounts call him a grandson of Fionn mac Cumhaill. His own men held him in contempt, thinking him lazy, vain, and selfish. Fionn then taught Giona the maxims of the Fianna to stiffen his resolve. After that lesson Giona grew into a true leader of the warrior band. English writers of an older day spelled his name Geena mac Luga.

:::
```

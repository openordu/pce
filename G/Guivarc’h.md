``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Guivarc’h is the name that Breton tradition gives to King Mark of Cornwall. In Breton retellings of the Arthurian story the king bears this form of the name. Scholars connect it with Mark, the uncle of Tristan. The Welsh branch of the legend calls the same ruler by a different name. The appearance of Guivarc’h shows how Breton writers carried the Arthurian matter across the Celtic world. The reader should consult the entry for Mark.

:::
```

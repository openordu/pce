::: magazinestyle
Gwrhyr Gwalstawd ieithoedd is a Welsh figure who speaks with the animals in a shamanic way. He goes with the hero Culhwch on the great hunt of the Tale. The gift of the tongues lets him ask the beasts and birds for aid. He stands between the people and the wild, ready to call on the oldest creatures of the land. His role shows how the Welsh story gives voice to the whole of creation as the hunter seeks what is lost.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwri was the childhood name of the hero that Welsh tradition calls Pryderi. He was the son of Rhiannon and Pwyll in a story from the Mabinogi. The boy vanished soon after his birth, so Rhiannon was wrongly blamed for the loss. Teyrnon Twrf Liant, an upright lord, found the child and raised him as his own. Teyrnon gave the boy the name Gwri, and the epithet Wallt Euryn, golden hair, sometimes followed it. MacKillop records that the foster father gave the boy the name. When Gwri grew older he was restored to his birth parents. The boy then took the name Pryderi, under which he plays a large role in later Welsh tales.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Gwaelod is the Welsh name for the drowned land of Cantre’r Gwaelod. The story describes a fertile lowland that sank beneath the sea. Welsh tradition places this lost territory along the coast of Cardigan Bay. The fuller form Cantref-y-Gwaelod also appears in Welsh sources. The reader should consult the entry for Cantre’s Gwaelod.

:::

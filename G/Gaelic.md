``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gaelic names a family of Celtic tongues spoken by the Gaels. This branch takes in Irish, Scottish Gaelic, and Manx. The English word comes from Old Irish terms. The forms Goídel and Goídelg meant a Gael and the Gaelic speech. The borrowed source forms were Guoidel and Guoidelec. They grew from primitive British shapes. The shift of early British w- into gw- ruled how these were taken in. The earliest named cases sit near the year 600. Early Irish verse and record the words in use. A native lore says Goídelg was formed from the speech of many peoples. The term has no real bond with Gaul or the Gauls.

:::
```

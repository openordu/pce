``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwyddno Garanhir appears as a minor figure in the Welsh tale of Kulhwch and Olwen. He holds a place in the list of wonders that the hero Kulhwch must gather. The chief marvel connected with his name is a magic basket. The basket stays full of food at all times from its own power. Kulhwch must take the basket as one of the gifts demanded by the giant's daughter Olwen. MacKillop records the basket among the objects of the tale. The story shows how a small character can guard a prize that a hero needs.

:::
```

glaistyn is a variant spelling that MacKillop records as a pointer to the headword glashtin. MacKillop groups the spelling under the Manx water-horse entry. The fuller record of the mischievous shape-shifter, his ears, and his human form sits under glashtin. MacKillop treats glaistyn as one of four variant spellings of the same creature.

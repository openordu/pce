::: magazinestyle
Glaschu is the Scottish Gaelic name for Glasgow, a city in Scotland. The city stands on the River Clyde. Its early legend held that Glasgow's ecclesiastical origins lay in a small cemetery beside the Molendinar Burn. The first bishop, John, was placed in Glasgow between 1114 and 1118 by David I. The endowment of the cathedral is documented from 1136, when an inquest listed the holdings that belonged to the church of Kentigern. Those lands lay in the later Barony of Glasgow and the Upper Ward of Clydesdale. The cathedral stands among the truly grand buildings of medieval Scotland.

:::

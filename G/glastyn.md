``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The glastyn, also spelled glashtin, is a Manx folkloric figure. This form of the spectral water horse dwelt on the Isle of Man. He appeared as a handsome man rather than a horse. MacKillop lists glastyn as a variant spelling of glashtin. He belongs to the wider family of Celtic water horses found across the islands. The creature forms part of the wider Celtic water-horse family that runs from the Manx glashtin to the Scottish kelpie and each-uisge. Folklore ties it to riverbanks, lake shores, and the strand.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Gorsedd Beirdd Ynys Prydain, the Welsh bardic order of the island of Britain, provides the ritual ceremonies of the National Eisteddfod of Wales. The permanent body, Cymdeithas yr Orsedd, was set up at the Wrexham Eisteddfod of 1888. David Griffith, known as Clwydfardd, became the first archdruid in that year. The archdruid was long chosen from the winners of the crown or the chair. Since the year 2000 every member may stand for the office. The modern order counts about thirteen hundred members. The white robes and regalia evoke the dress of an ancient druid. Welsh scholars such as John Morris-Jones judged the ritual to lack any basis in history. They traced its forms to the myth-making of Iolo Morganwg in the late eighteenth century. The Cornish Gorseth of Kernow followed this Welsh model from 1928.

:::
```

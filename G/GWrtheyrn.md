``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
GWrtheyrn was a ruler of the Britons in the fifth century. Later Welsh tradition came to know him by this name. The Latin writers called him Vortigernus. Gildas described him as a proud usurper. He is said to have brought Anglo-Saxon mercenaries into Britain. Those men later rose against the Britons and seized their land. Beda placed their arrival at the year 449. The Historia Brittonum of 829 made his story a central theme. Geoffrey of Monmouth used several of these details. His doomed stronghold came to be linked with Snowdonia. The name may have been a title meaning overlord. Old Welsh gave the form Guorthigirn.

:::
```

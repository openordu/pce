``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Guto'r Glyn was a Welsh poet active during the 15th century. He composed formal praise for noble Welsh patrons. He belonged to the cywyddwyr. Historians hold few firm facts about his life. He probably lived from about 1418 to 1493. His poems set his work near Oswestry in Shropshire. He backed the Yorkists during England's civil wars. He praised commanders Richard Gethin and Mathew Gough. He honoured King Edward IV. He retired to Valle Crucis Abbey. He died there in the 1490s.

:::
```

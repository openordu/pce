``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwalchmai fab Gwyar is the Welsh counterpart and possible ancestor of Gawain in the Arthurian stories. The compound of his Welsh name is usually read as the hawk of May or the hawk of the plain. He is a nephew of Arthur in the native tradition. In the tale Culhwch and the hero he rides the horse Ceingalad and helps the knight of the hunt. He acts as the peacemaker between Tristan and March ap Meirchion. Yet the Welsh Triads give no link between him and Arthur at all. The two traditions, the French one and the Welsh one, therefore disagree about the same knight. Later Welsh texts also know him under the form Gwalmei. His many parts show how the Arthurian hero changed as the story crossed the borderland of Wales.

:::
```

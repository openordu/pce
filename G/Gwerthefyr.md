``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwerthefyr, a fifth-century Brythonic leader and son of the ruler Gwrtheyrn, commanded the Britons in their fighting against Saxon forces in Kent. The Historia Brittonum counts a series of victories for him, including one where the English leader Horsa fell. Welsh tradition tells that he asked to be buried at a port so the invaders could never sail back. He is treated alongside Urien and Cadwallon as a great war-leader who was never beaten by the English.

:::
```

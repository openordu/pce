::: magazinestyle
Gwales is the Welsh name for an Otherworldly island off the south west coast of Dyfed. The district used the same name for the old shire before it became Pembrokeshire. The word itself means shelter or lair. Writers identify Gwales with the small islet of Grassholm, which lies to the west of Skomer. The severed head of the giant Brog presided over eighty years of bright feasting on the island. The banquet gathered together the seven survivors who came through the laying about with Matholwch, and none of them felt how the years closed in. Heilyn, the son of Gwyn, ended the enchantment by opening the door. The island therefore ties a real rock in the sea to the true Otherworld of the Welsh, Annwfn, and it belongs to the best loved scenes of the Mabinogi.

:::

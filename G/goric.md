``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
goric names a kind of Breton gnome that MacKillop records as haunting dolmens and other megaliths. MacKillop treats the goric as one of the Breton spirit folk attached to prehistoric stone monuments. He notes that the goric shares the megalith-haunting habit with related beings across the Celtic countries. MacKillop places the goric in the wider family of Breton nature spirits. The fuller record of Breton megalith folklore sits under the headword gnome.

:::
```

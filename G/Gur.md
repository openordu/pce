::: magazinestyle
Gur is a short form of the name of Lough Gur, the lake in County Limerick. The Dictionary of Celtic Mythology sends the reader from Gur to the fuller headword Lough Gur. The waters of the lake carry a body of Irish story. The reader should consult the entry for Lough Gur.

:::

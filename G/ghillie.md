``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ghillie is the anglicized spelling of the Scottish Gaelic word gille. MacKillop records the form as a cross-reference to the canonical headword gilla. The fuller record of the youth, page, or manservant sits under gilla. MacKillop lists ghillie alongside the spelling gillie in the same pointer entry.

:::
```

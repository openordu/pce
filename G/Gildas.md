``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gildas was a 6th-century cleric and writer who lived in Britain. He wrote the religious work De Excidio Britanniae, which means the ruin of Britain. The text blames Roman rule and British sins for the fall of Britain. Gildas records a letter that the Britons sent to a Roman consul named Agitius. He also names five kings who ruled parts of Britain, including Maelgwn, a king in north Wales. The work describes resistance by the Britons against the Anglo-Saxons. Gildas reports a British victory at a place called the Mount of Badon. Later writers such as the monk Beda used his account. Gildas also wrote a Penitential, a short list of penances. He gained the name Sapiens, which means the wise one.

:::
```

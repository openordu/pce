::: magazinestyle
Gwénnolé is one of the spellings used for the Breton saint Guénolé, the holy man of the sunken Breton city. He sets his face against the vice that grows around the lost daughter. The alternate spelling Gwénnolé shows the many ways Breton speech wrote the founder of the church. The reader should seek the account of Guénolé in the entry of that name.

:::

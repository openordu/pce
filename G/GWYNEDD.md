``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwynedd is a kingdom that once covered the north-west of Wales. In the years before shires were drawn the name could reach far across the land. Modern Gwynedd differs from the old kingdom in both size and shape. The present county took form in 1974 from several earlier shires. After Rome left, the first kingdom took hold along that coast. The Historia Brittonum says Cunedda began the royal house there. Later rulers came from the same line, including Maelgwn and Rhodri Mawr. Rhodri pressed his rule over three neighbouring lands. From Aberffraw the kings came to lead the smaller realms around them. Llywelyn ap Iorwerth brought the Welsh line to its greatest reach. Edward the First's conquest of Wales struck hardest at this region. Snowdon and many ranges stand here, and the walking is hard. The children of Don and the poet Taliesin both find a home in the tale. Math of Mathonwy rules here, and so does Lleu Llaw Gyffes.

:::
```

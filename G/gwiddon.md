``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
gwiddon is the Welsh word for witch, hag, or sorceress. MacKillop records the headword as a related label for the Welsh prophetic tradition. MacKillop treats gwiddon as the female Welsh counterpart of the male office gweledydd. The fuller record of the Celtic seer and prophet class sits under the headword gweledydd. MacKillop links gwiddon to the Latin vates and the Irish fáith.

:::
```

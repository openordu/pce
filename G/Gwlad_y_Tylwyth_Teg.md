``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwlad y Tylwyth Teg is the Welsh phrase for the land of the fair folk. In everyday speech the same words may also mean sleep or the grave. The name points to the home of the Tylwyth Teg, the fairies of Welsh custom. The phrase therefore joins a place of the imagination with the country of the dead. The reader should seek the fuller account in the entry of the fair folk.

:::
```

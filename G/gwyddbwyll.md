``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
gwyddbwyll is the early Welsh board game that MacKillop often compares to the Irish fidchell and búanfach. MacKillop notes that gwyddbwyll is commonly translated as chess, though the comparison is imperfect. He states that we lack sufficient evidence to describe the board, the rules, or the social context of the game. MacKillop treats gwyddbwyll as a member of the wider family of Celtic board games. The fuller record of the Irish counterpart sits under the headword fidchell. MacKillop notes the spelling variant gwydbwyll as a recorded alternative.

:::
```

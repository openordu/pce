::: magazinestyle
Gírle Guairle is the nonsense name of a fairy woman in an Irish version of the Rumpelstiltskin story. She is a spinner who offers to do the weaving for a woman of mortal birth. In return the woman must keep the fairy's hard name in her mind. At first the woman lets it slip. Later she speaks it aloud, and the anger of Gírle Guairle turns upon her. The tale keeps the old pattern in which a name holds power over its bearer. The figure belongs to the fairy women of the wider Celtic tradition.

:::

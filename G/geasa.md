``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
geasa is the Irish plural form of the headword geis. MacKillop treats the two spellings as the same record. MacKillop records geasa in the 2008 Dictionary of Celtic Mythology as a cross-reference to the singular headword. The fuller record of the binding taboo, its its role in Irish heroic cycles, and its its ritual uses sits under geis.

:::
```

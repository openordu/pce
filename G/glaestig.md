``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
glaestig is a variant spelling that MacKillop records as a pointer to the headword glaistig. MacKillop groups the spelling under the canonical entry for the water fairy of Scottish Gaelic lore. The fuller record of the green maiden, her haunts, and her shapes sits under glaistig. MacKillop notes the spelling drift as a feature of the printed Celtic folklore sources.

:::
```

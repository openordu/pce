::: magazinestyle
gwarwyn a throt is a Welsh label meaning the white-naped one who runs with a trot. MacKillop records it as a covert regional name for the tylwyth teg in Monmouthshire. He notes that the tylwyth teg is the Welsh name for the fairy folk. MacKillop treats the term as one of the regional labels for the Welsh fairies. The fuller record of the Welsh fairy folk sits under the headword tylwyth teg.

:::

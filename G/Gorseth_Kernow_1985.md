::: magazinestyle
Gorseth Kernow is the Cornish branch of the bardic order, modelled on the Welsh Gorseth. The caption Gorseth Kernow, 1985 records one of its congresses. Members of the Kernow community gathered in that year for a ceremony that celebrated Cornish identity. By that date the order had awarded more than one thousand bardships. The 1985 assembly renewed the tradition of promoting the Cornish language through literary and cultural competitions. Activists used the gathering to strengthen links with the wider Cornish diaspora. Some critics still regard the order as invented and out of date. Yet the 1985 meeting remained a focal point of the Cornish revival.

:::

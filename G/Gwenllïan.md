::: magazinestyle
Gwenllïan (died 1136) was daughter of Gruffudd ap Cynan, ruler of Gwynedd, and wife of Gruffudd ap Rhys, ruler of Deheubarth. When her husband sought help in Gwynedd against the Anglo-Normans, Gwenllïan raised the Deheubarth forces and led them against a garrison at Kidwelly castle. The battle went ill, and Gwenllïan fell at a site still called the field of Gwenllï. Her son Rhys ap Gruffudd became famed as the Lord Rhys, and her courage held a lasting place in the legendary history of Wales.

:::

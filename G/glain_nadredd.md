``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
glain nadredd names a Welsh and Breton term that MacKillop records as a pointer to the headword adder stone. MacKillop treats the phrase as a regional label for the magical bead. The fuller record of the adder stone, its origins in snake lore, and its protective uses sits under adder stone. MacKillop notes that the Breton term neidr carries the same meaning as the Welsh glain nadredd.

:::
```

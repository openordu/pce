``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwernabwy, also called Gwern Abwy, is the holder of a great eagle in Welsh legend. The name may reach back to the Welsh word for the alder tree. In the tale of Culhwch and the Hero the young man turns to this eagle while he seeks the missing prisoner Mabon. The eagle is one of a chain of old creatures that pass their wisdom onward. The ancient bird proves that the longest-lived beings of the land still hold the memory of the Britons. Gwernabwy acts as the keeper of time among the speaking animals of the Welsh hunt.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
gillie is an anglicized spelling that MacKillop records as a pointer to the headword giolla. MacKillop groups gillie with the variant spelling gilly in the same entry. The fuller record of the youth, page, manservant sits under the canonical headword giolla. MacKillop treats the anglicizations as interchangeable forms of the same Gaelic root.

:::
```

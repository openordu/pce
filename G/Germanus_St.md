``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Germanus, St, a fifth-century bishop of Auxerre, was known as Garmon in Welsh tradition. He and Lupus, bishop of Troyes, helped British soldiers defeat a joint band of Pict and Saxon raiders inside a hidden valley. That fight became known as the Alleluia victory, for the troops shouted that word at their foes. About 447 he returned with Severus, bishop of Trier, to fight Pelagianism, which still troubled British Christians. The tenth-century prophecy Armes Prydain regards Garmon as a patron saint of the Britons, second only to Dewi Sant. Welsh dedications to Garmon span north and central Wales, including the name of the radio play Buchedd Garmon.

:::
```

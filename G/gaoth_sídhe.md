``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
gaoth sídhe is the Irish phrase for fairy wind, the enchanted breeze that signals the passage of the Other Crowd. MacKillop places the fuller record under the headword fairy wind in the 2008 dictionary. The variant form gaoth sí stands beside it in the same entry. No independent record sits under gaoth sídhe in the 2008 dictionary; the Irish form functions as a cross-reference only.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Glastonbury is a town in southwest England famed for myth and history. Legend ties the place to Avalon, the island of the Otherworld, and to early Christian tradition. Some say Joseph of Arimathea built the first church there, and old writers linked the site to Arthur and to later saints. The abbey grounds hold the old church named the Vetustus, the 'old church', along with a holy well and high crosses. Beyond the tales, the land carries deep traces of people. A timber track, the Sweet Way, crossed the wet levels and was dated by tree rings to about 3807 BCE. Arthur Bulleid dug an Iron Age lake village on the marsh edge in the late 1800s. So the town unites Christian legend with a real ancient community that lived on its bogs.

:::
```

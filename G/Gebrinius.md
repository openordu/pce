``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gebrinius, also called Mercurius Gebrinius, is a Continental Celtic deity known from ten inscriptions and a second-century shrine, all at Bonn in Germany. The Romans set him beside Mars in their warrior pantheon. Most of the record comes from the Ubii, a people of eastern Gaul, so the god may belong to a frontier zone where Celtic and Germanic worship met. The meaning of the epithet is not settled. One reading points to a horned god and compares Old Irish gabor and Welsh gafr, both meaning 'goat'. No firm parallel supports that view. Images show a creature with a lion-like head on a body closer to a fat sheep. The small corpus and the single cult place leave his character open.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
Gwreg Houarn is a Breton nickname that means the iron woman. Bretons gave the name to the Venus of Quinipily, a stone figure in western Brittany. The sculpture stands at Baud. Its age and maker are not certain, yet it draws the devotion of the place. The reader should consult the entry for the Venus of Quinipily.

:::

::: magazinestyle
gáe sídhe names the fairy dart or fairy spear in Irish tradition. MacKillop records the headword as a variant label for the projectile weapon of the fairy folk. MacKillop treats gáe sídhe as one of several Irish labels for the fairy weapon. The fuller record of the fairy weapon sits under the headword fairy dart. MacKillop notes that the variant spelling si appears alongside gáe sídhe in the same entry.

:::

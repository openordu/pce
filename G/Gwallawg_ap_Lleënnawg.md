``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwallawg ap Lleënnawg, a northern Brythonic chieftain, governed Elfed later in the sixth century, and the Welsh genealogies reckon his dynasty among those tracing descent from Coel Hen. He appears in the Harleian pedigrees and in the tracts on the descent of the northern men and of the saints. Under Urien of Rheged he joined the alliance that besieged the Angles of Brynaich, a deed noted in Historia Brittonum.

:::
```

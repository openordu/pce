``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
geancánach is the Irish form of the headword ganconer, the love-token spirit that follows a rejected lover. MacKillop records both spellings, geancánach and gean-cannach, as variant forms pointing to the same canonical headword ganconer. The fuller record of the love-token spirit sits under ganconer in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

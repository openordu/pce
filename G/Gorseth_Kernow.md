``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Gorseth Kernow is the Cornish bardic order. It is the central institution of the Cornish Celtic revival. The name marks both the ceremony and its body of bards. The assembly borrows its rite from the Welsh Gorsedd of Britannic bards. Revivalists wanted a Cornish gorseth from the 1880s. The Celto-Cornish Society pressed for one after 1901. Jenner and Nance shaped the rite together. The Welsh archdruid directed its design. The first Gorseth met at Boscawen Un in west Cornwall on 21 September 1928. The site held a stone circle. The Welsh triads called it Beisgawen. Jenner became the first Grand Bard. Nance acted as his deputy. The order promotes the Cornish language. It awards bardships for skill in the tongue. Each bard wore a blue robe. A black and gold headband marked the colours of the Duchy of Cornwall.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
gans an howl is the Cornish phrase for a sunwise turn, the motion of moving in the same direction as the sun across the sky. MacKillop records the phrase in his Dictionary of Celtic Mythology and treats it as the Cornish equivalent of the Irish deiseal. The fuller record of the ritual direction sits under the headword sunwise turn in the 2008 dictionary. The opposite direction is called widdershins.

:::
```

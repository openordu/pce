``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Gáe Assail is the spear of Lug Lamfhota and strikes like lightning. Its name, spear of Assal, remembers an earlier holder. Once thrown, the weapon flies back to the hand that cast it. If the thrower speaks ibar, the word for yew, the target cannot escape death. The sons of Tuirenn carried the spear to Lug as part of their debt. It counts among the four great treasures held by the Tuatha De Danann.

:::
```

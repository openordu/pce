``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gwion Bach is the name of a servant in the Hanes Taliesin, the tale of the poet Taliesin. That servant gains the gift of poetic inspiration meant for Morfran. The poet's mother figure, the shape-shifting goddess Ceridwen, was to brew a cauldron of wisdom. Three drops fell on Gwion instead, so the power passed to him. The angry goddess then chased him through many changes of form. In the end she swallowed him and later gave birth to him anew. That reborn child became the great poet Taliesin. Gwion's many transformations are often set beside those of Tuan mac Cairill. So a humble servant rises to become the master of Welsh poetry.

:::
```

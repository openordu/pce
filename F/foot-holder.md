``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
foot-holder names an officer in the courts of medieval Welsh kings. The Welsh laws designate a male foot-holder whose duty is to support the king. The Mabinogi records an exception to that male office in the person of Math's foot-holder, the virgin Goewin. The role appears in MacKillop as a piece of medieval Welsh court terminology in the 2008 Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The function survives in no living court today.

:::
```

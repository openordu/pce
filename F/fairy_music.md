``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Fairy music is a folkloric motif that MacKillop gives the Irish name ceol sidhe. He describes it as melody that draws people beyond this world. The fairy folk loved music and chased after every fine tune. They carried off a skilled piper or harper to play at their dances, then sent the musician home. The pay was often a haunting tune with a planxty, a hypnotic break in rhythm that marks fairy music. The harper Turlough O'Carolan slept on a fairy rath and woke filled with wild music. Fairy music heard from midnight to dawn came from fairy mounds or from all around an unseen place. A dim patch on a bog could open a way into Fairyland. The music was never somber. It made the body dance. Endless dance in Fairyland let years slip by, while on earth a cursed dancer died from the strain.

:::
```

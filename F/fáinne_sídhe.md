``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fáinne sídhe is the Irish name that names the fairy ring, the circle of mushrooms or bare grass that marks a fairy dancing place. MacKillop places the fuller record of the fairy ring under its English headword fairy ring in the 2008 dictionary. The variant spellings sí and fáinne sith appear alongside it in the same entry. No independent record sits under fáinne sídhe in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

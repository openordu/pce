fionndruine is a MacKillop cross-reference, recorded in his dictionary as a Middle Irish spelling of the Old Irish metal-name findruine. The headword carries no independent article and serves only as an alternate route to the canonical entry findruine, where the metal's value, composition, and craft uses are set out at length.

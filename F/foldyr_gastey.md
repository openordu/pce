``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
foldyr gastey is the Manx-language nickname for the fenodyree, recorded by MacKillop in his Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The Manx phrase translates as nimble mower. MacKillop uses the nickname as a synonym pointing back to the headword fenodyree for the full account of the figure. No separate record sits under foldyr gastey in the 2008 dictionary; the Manx form merely furnishes an alternative label.

:::
```

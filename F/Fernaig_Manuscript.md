``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Fernaig Manuscript is a Gaelic collection of poetry compiled by Donnchadh MacRae, known also as Donnchadh nam Pìos, from Inverinate in Kintail. He put the anthology together between 1688 and 1693 in two notebooks that hold fifty-nine poems. Most of the verse dates from the seventeenth century and follows religious themes, alongside some early Jacobite poetry. The compiler shaped its spelling from the everyday Scots writing of the age rather than the customary Gaelic style. This unusual orthography gives the manuscript real value for studying the Scottish Gaelic language. The text stands as a key witness to the Scottish Gaelic literary tradition, and poets with strong links to Kintail are well represented among its pages.

:::
```

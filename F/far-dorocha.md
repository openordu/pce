``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
far dorocha, also spelled fear dorocha, comes from the Irish fear dorcha. It means dark man. The figure is a malevolent fairy in Celtic folklore. He serves as the chief agent of mortal abduction. He works as the butler-like servant of the fairy queen. He carries out her commands without emotion or wasted motion. He pours her tea with the same ease with which he rides out to fetch a chosen mortal on his black charger. Silent and utterly obedient to his queen, he commands others to surrender their will. Many mortals have travelled with him to the land of the fairy, yet few return. He belongs to a wider family of death-bearing figures. That family includes angau, ankou, the death coach, and the dullahan.

:::
```

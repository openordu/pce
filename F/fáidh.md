``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fáidh is the Modern Irish word for a seer or prophet, the present-day form of the older fáith. MacKillop treats the term as the modern cognate of the headword fáith. The fuller record of the Irish seer or prophet sits under that canonical headword in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

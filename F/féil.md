``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
féil is the Old Irish term for feast-day or festival, drawn from Latin vigilia, the vigil. Welsh gŵyl, feast or holiday, is the cognate. MacKillop notes that the Old Irish form carries a religious sense above all, with secular or pagan use rare. The headword féil gathers the record of the Irish feast cycle in the 2008 Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. MacKillop points the reader to related headwords fair, festival, and feis, each of which carries a distinct shade of the same idea in the same dictionary.

:::
```

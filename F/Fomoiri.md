``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Fomoiri are a hostile race of supernatural beings in Irish legend. They appear frequently in Irish myth. Writers usually show them as monstrous in appearance. The first element of their name is the preposition fo, meaning 'under'. The second element of the name is uncertain. Later tradition took the word as a synonym for giants. The fomoiri fight the Tuath Dé in the Cath Maige Tuired. They also oppose the early settlers of Ireland in the Lebor Gabála. The people are always cast in a negative light, unlike the Tuath Dé.

:::
```

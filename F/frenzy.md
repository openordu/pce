frenzy is a MacKillop cross-reference headword that points the reader to the entry for battle fury. The 2008 Dictionary of Celtic Mythology places the full record of the warrior's contortion under the canonical headword battle fury. Related terms MacKillop gathers beside it include warp spasm, the heroic battle fit of Cúchulainn. No independent entry sits under frenzy in the 2008 dictionary.

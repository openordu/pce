``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fairy herbs is a short cross-reference in MacKillop that lists plants bound to fairy lore. MacKillop names dandelion, eyebright, foxglove, and yarrow, and adds ivy, plantain, polypody of oak, and vervain. The full record of these plants sits under the headword dandelion.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fab is a mutated form of the Welsh noun map, mab meaning boy or son. The form appears at the start of patronymics that name a child by reference to the father. MacKillop lists fab alongside two parallel mutated prefixes. The first is ap, ap. The second is vab, vab. Compare the unmodified Welsh mab in the same patronymic slot.

:::
```

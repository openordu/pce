::: magazinestyle
far liath is the cleaned modern form of the grey man of Hiberno-Irish tradition. Older Scots Gaelic records the name as leea. Older Irish records show fear liath. Even older print renders it as fear Hath. He is a malevolent fairy who personifies fog. He spreads his mantle over land and sea. He darkens roads so that travellers stumble unawares over precipices to their deaths. He also drapes rocks so that ships founder against them in the gloom.

:::

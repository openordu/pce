``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fóidín mearaí is an enchanted piece of turf that scrambles the wits of anyone who steps on it. The Irish word finds a parallel in fóidín seachráin, the sod of wandering. MacKillop derives fóidín from findín, a piece of soil kicked up by hurrying feet. The sense of mearaí comes from mearbhaill, meaning craziness or distraction. Seachráin adds the wandering. The condition of being led astray by the enchanted turf is called being pixie-led or pixilated. The fuller pixie-led record sits under the headword pisky in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

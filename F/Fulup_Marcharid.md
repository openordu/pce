::: magazinestyle
Marc'harid Fulup, also known as Marguerite Philippe, lived from 1867 to 1909. She was a Breton singer and storyteller born in Pluzunet, a rural community in the district of Trégor in Brittany. Collectors of Breton folklore chose Fulup as a principal informant. François-Marie Luzel called her the Homer of Brittany and relied on her as a main source for his Breton folktales. Anatole Le Braz also drew on her songs. Through her exact memory and powerful voice, Fulup helped preserve a vast store of Breton songs and tales spoken in the Breton language.

:::

::: magazinestyle
fairy rock names the many megalithic monuments scattered across the Celtic world that folklore ties to the fairy folk. The phrase covers standing stones, dolmens, and other prehistoric remains. The best-known example is the cluster of dolmens called La Roche aux Fées near Essé in Brittany. Other named examples appear throughout Brittany, Ireland, Wales, and the rest of the Celtic-speaking lands.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fé fiada is a variant spelling of the Irish headword féth fiada, the magical concealing mist. MacKillop records both spellings as pointing to the same canonical headword féth fiada. The fuller record of the warrior's protective mist sits under féth fiada in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fód seachráin is the Irish phrase for an enchanted sod of wandering, the patch of earth that leads a traveller astray. MacKillop places the fuller record under the headword fóidín mearaí in the 2008 dictionary. The phrase combines fód (sod) with seachráin (wandering or straying) in Irish. No independent record sits under fód seachráin in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

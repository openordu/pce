``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Feiseanna and the Oireachtas are cultural festivals of the Irish that the revival movement promoted. A feis is a gathering for the Irish language, music, song, and dance, and for storytelling. Entrants compete in recitation, essay writing, and the collecting of folklore. The Gaelic League championed these events from the 1890s and licensed feiseanna for the Irish-speaking counties from 1898. The Oireachtas is the principal annual festival of Irish language and culture. Its national competitions drew the winners of local and provincial feiseanna. Its founders shaped it on the pattern of the Welsh eisteddfod, and the League held the first one in Dublin in 1897. The event later grew into a large annual gathering that alternated between the provinces and Dublin. In Scotland the feis tradition survives through the mod gatherings.

:::
```

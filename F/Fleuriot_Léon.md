Fleuriot, Léon was a French Celtic scholar and specialist in Breton who lived from 1923 to 1987. Born in Morlaix, he studied the native tongue and the early story of Brittany. His grammar and old Breton dictionary are still the standard works on the subject, the first full survey since Joseph Loth. Patient reading of manuscripts brought out many Old Breton glosses that earlier readers had left unidentified. He pressed Romance philologists to treat Gaulish and Continental Celtic as possible sources of Romance words whose origin was unknown. From 1966 he still occupied the Celtic chair at the University of Rennes, and he served the committee of Études Celtiques. His books included Le vieux breton (1964) and, from 1980, Les origines de la Bretagne, together with the Dictionary of Old Breton released in 1985.

:::

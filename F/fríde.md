::: magazinestyle
fríde is the Scottish Gaelic word for gnome, pigmy, elf, or rock-elfin. MacKillop places the fríde among the supernatural creatures of Highland folklore who live in or under rocks. They devour all spilled milk or crumbs left in their reach. A widely known story tells of a piper and his dog who followed the fridean into a winding cavern. The piper's music was still heard by mortals above ground for years afterward. The piper never returned, but the dog came back hairless and immediately died. The folk motif V12.9 marks the tale.

:::

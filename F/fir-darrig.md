fir darrig is a MacKillop cross-reference. MacKillop's dictionary marks the headword as a plural form of Far Darrig. The entry carries no independent article of its own and serves only to redirect the reader to the article Far Darrig, where the full record of the red man of Irish folk belief sits under that canonical headword. The related form fir dhearga is recorded in the same line as a further plural variant. Anyone tracing this figure through MacKillop's dictionary should follow the pointer to Far Darrig for the complete treatment of lore, sources, and attributes.

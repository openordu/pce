``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fé-fíada is a hyphenated variant spelling of the Irish headword féth fiada, the magical concealing mist. MacKillop lists both fé fiada and fé-fíada as variant forms pointing to the same canonical headword féth fiada. The fuller record of the warrior's protective mist sits under féth fiada in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

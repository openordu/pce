``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fled bainisi is an Irish term that MacKillop records in his Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The compound joins fled, the Irish word for feast, with banais, the Irish word for wedding. MacKillop treats the term as a variant spelling of the canonical headword banais ríghe, the royal wedding feast. The fuller record of the ritual feast sits under that headword in the 2008 dictionary, with cross-references to the related rites of the Irish wedding tradition.

:::
```

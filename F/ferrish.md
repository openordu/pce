::: magazinestyle
Ferrish is a cross-reference. It directs the reader, under the Other Celtic heading, to the entry for FAIRY. MacKillop gives ferrish its own article as the Manx word for fairy, with ferrishyn as the plural. A rough equivalent name, yn sleigh beggey, means the little people. These beings stand between one foot and three feet high and rank below the fairy gentry of Ireland and Wales. No monarch leads them. As trooping fairies they hunt with their own white dogs, red-eared. They know all things, and they can steal babies. Country people therefore speak of them in favourable terms only.

:::

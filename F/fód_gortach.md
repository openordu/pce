``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fód gortach is the Irish phrase for hungry ground, the enchanted sod that makes a traveller lose their way. MacKillop places the fuller record under the headword hungry grass in the 2008 dictionary. The phrase joins fód (a piece of earth or sod) with gortach (hungry or famine-stricken) in Irish. No independent entry sits under fód gortach in the 2008 dictionary.

:::
```

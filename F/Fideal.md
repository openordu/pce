``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Fideal is a figure from the folklore of Scotland. This Highland water spirit led attractive young men toward lakes and ponds, where she drowned them. The name echoes Scottish Gaelic words for entwining and twisting. Fideal embodies the tangled bog grasses and water weeds that haunt Loch na Fideil near Gairloch. That loch lies in the Highlands of the north-west, in the old county of Ross and Cromarty. Ross and Cromarty held that ground until 1974. MacKillop classes the creature as a subspecies of the fuath, and Monaghan counts her a kind of Vough.

:::
```

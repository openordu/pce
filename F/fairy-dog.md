::: magazinestyle
fairy dog names a recurrent black hound of Celtic folklore that roams wild places. Irish tradition near Galway tells of a large unnamed hound with white rings about its neck. Related named hounds include Bran and Sceolan, Cù Sith, Cŵn Annwfn, Far Bhann, Gwyllgi, and Mauth Dooog. An unnamed black dog haunts Slieve Mish in Kerry. Brittany adds unnamed menacing black dogs at the Youdic quagmire.

:::

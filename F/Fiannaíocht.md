``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Fiannaíocht is the lore of the fian, the roaming band of youthful fighters, told partly in prose and mostly in verse. Much of it is sung in a semiharmonic style, seen in the Lismore dean's poem book and in Duanaire Finn, a collection written at Ostend that shows how far the songs traveled at home and across the sea. A central tale concerns Gráinne, daughter of Cormac, who leaves the old lord Finn for his close companion Diarmaid. The story looks back to an older pattern of a woman caught between two men, one that yielded a lasting body of writing in both Ireland and Scotland.

:::
```

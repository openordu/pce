``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
fionn is a Modern Irish word, also usable as a prefix, meaning fair, bright, white, lustrous, or light-hued. MacKillop's dictionary marks the word as the Modern Irish reflex of an earlier concept recorded in Old and Middle Irish under the forms find and finn. Several figures in the cycle literature bear names built from this root. They include Find, Finn, or Fionn. All of these heroes except Fionn mac Cumhaill appear in this dictionary under the older Old and Middle Irish spellings. The Modern Irish headword fionn itself functions chiefly as a colour adjective. It also serves as a pointer for any reader tracing the family of light-named champions back to their Old Irish origins. The earliest spellings preserve the same basic sense of pale brilliance that the modern word still carries.

:::
```

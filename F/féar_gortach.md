``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
féar gortach is the Irish name for hungry grass, the enchanted patch of grass that makes a traveller lose their way. MacKillop places the fuller record of the phenomenon under the English headword hungry grass in the 2008 dictionary. No independent record sits under féar gortach in the 2008 dictionary; the Irish form functions as a cross-reference only.

:::
```

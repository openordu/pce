``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Celtic peoples marked the midpoints between great solar events. Imbolc fell on February 1, when winter moved toward spring. Lughnasa fell on August 1, when summer passed into fall. MacKillop counts four mainline festival days on the Celtic calendar. Samain arrived in November, Imbolc in February, Beltaine in May, and Lughnasa in August. Other peoples divided their year by the solstices and the equinoxes. Those points fell on June 21, December 21, March 21, and September 21. The Celts watched the midpoints rather than the points of change. This choice may spring from a belief in the sacred center, a concept both geographical and spiritual. The word festival itself descends from Medieval Latin.

:::
```

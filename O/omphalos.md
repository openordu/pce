``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
omphalos is a MacKillop headword naming the cultural heart of a people, by analogy with the famous navel-stone of Delphi in ancient Greece. MacKillop lists several Celtic examples of the centre-stone tradition across Brittany, Wales, Ireland, and the Isle of Man. The full record of the centre-stone tradition sits under the headword uisnech.

:::
```

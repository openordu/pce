::: magazinestyle
O'Malley is the English surname of Grace O'Malley, the sixteenth-century pirate whose Irish name Gráinne Mhaol Ní Mháille heads the granuaile article in MacKillop 2008. The dictionary introduces her there with the dates c.1530 to 1603 and the phrase celebrated pirate of the Mayo coast, and its cross-reference web ties both name forms, Irish and English, to that single treatment. The row carries the anglicized surname so that a reader arriving from English sources reaches the same body of legend and history.

:::

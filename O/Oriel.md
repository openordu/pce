::: magazinestyle
Oriel is the anglicization that MacKillop 2008 lists for the ancient northern kingdom Airgialla, treated in full under that headword. The English form has a long life in ecclesiastical and administrative contexts, most visibly in the title of the Roman Catholic archdiocese of Armagh's old rival see, and in surname-lore and local history across counties Monaghan and Armagh, where the kingdom's memory survived its political extinction. The dictionary files the anglicized name as its own heading so that readers meeting Oriel in modern sources can trace it to the proto-kingdom article, which places Airgialla beside Ailech and Ulaid among the realms of pre-Nordic northern Ireland and records its early conquest by the Uí Néill. The entry completes the dictionary's three-way set of signposts, Irish modernized and English, back to one political name.

:::

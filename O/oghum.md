``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
oghum is a MacKillop headword naming the Scottish Gaelic spelling of ogham. MacKillop treats oghum as a regional orthographic variant of the canonical headword. The full record of the Celtic ogham alphabet and its traditions sits under the headword ogham.

:::
```

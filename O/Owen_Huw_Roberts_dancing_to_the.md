::: magazinestyle
Owen Huw Roberts appears in a photograph in Koch that shows him dancing a Welsh step dance. In the image he is supported by two musicians, the harpist David Elio Roberts and the clarinettist Robert Ifor Roberts. The caption records the performers who accompany him. The photograph belongs to the documentation of Welsh folk dance and its revival, a subject Koch treats under the section on the dancers who kept the tradition alive. Welsh step dancing and clog dancing were carried forward in the twentieth century through the work of collectors such as Lois Blake along with the society that she helped found, later styled the Welsh Folk Dance Society, in partnership with W. S. Gwynn Williams. The society researched, interpreted, and published Welsh dances, and new compositions continue to appear. Folk dancing in Wales has earned national recognition at the Urdd festival, at the Wales national Eisteddfod, at the Cerdd Dant Festival, and at the Llangollen International Musical Eisteddfod. The photograph of Owen Huw Roberts records one performer within this living tradition of Welsh traditional music and dance.

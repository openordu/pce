``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
ogum is a MacKillop headword naming a variant spelling of ogham, treated as a synonym of the Scottish Gaelic form oghum. MacKillop lists ogum alongside oghum as alternate spellings of the same tradition. The full record of the Celtic ogham alphabet and its traditions sits under the headword ogham.

:::
```

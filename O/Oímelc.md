``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Oímelc, also written Oimelc and Oimelg, is a variant form MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the feast of Imbolc, treated in full under that headword. The main article sets the celebration at the first of February on the Gregorian calendar and counts it among the four great Celtic seasonal feasts beside Samain, Beltaine, and Lugnasad. Its root phenomenon is the visible lengthening of daylight and the promise of spring, and the alternate name is glossed as marking the time when ewes come into milk, another signal of the season's turn. From the earliest records the feast belongs to the goddess Brigit, and after Christianization it passed to St Brigid of Kildare, whose day the date became. A reader who meets Oímelc in an Old Irish calendar context should follow the variant back to Imbolc, where the dictionary keeps the fuller account of the feast and its goddess.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Gerallt Lloyd Owen is a Welsh-language poet. His verse shows a sharp visual imagination. This appears most fully in Afon (River). That awdl took the male-voice chair at the 1975 National Eisteddfod. He took the chair once more in 1982. The winning poem was Cilmeri, a work of great force. It remembers Llywelyn ap Gruffudd, the final native prince of Wales. Much of his output shows a wry wit. This made him a well-liked voice on the broadcast poetry contest Talwrn y Beirdd. For younger readers he wrote storybooks and comics. He often added his own drawings. His chief titles are the collected poems Ugain Oed a'i Ganiadau (1966). Cerddi'r Cywilydd (1972) and Cilmeri a Cherddi Eraill (1991) also matter. He wrote the memoir Fy Nghawl Fy Hun (1999).

:::
```

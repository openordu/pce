``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Oirghialla is the modern Irish spelling that MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the proto-kingdom Airgialla, whose name he renders as those who have submitted, or perhaps hostage-givers. The main article places Airgialla among the three realms of the north of pre-Nordic Ireland, beside Ailech and Ulaid, its lands stretch between Lough Foyle and where the Newry River meets the sea, embracing most of Armagh and Monaghan together with parts of Louth, Down, and Fermanagh. The dictionary notes that the Uí Néill overran the realm in early times and turned its territory into a springboard for expansion. The modernized spelling matters for readers of recent Irish-language writing, where Oirghialla is the standard dress of an ancient political name.

:::
```

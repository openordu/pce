``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Orghialla is a modern spelling that MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the northern proto-kingdom Airgialla, treated in full under that headword. The variant matters for readers of modernized Irish texts, where this form of the name appears in historical and genealogical writing about the realm whose territory stretched from Lough Foyle to the Newry River and embraced much of Armagh and Monaghan. The dictionary's convention, maintained throughout, is that every spelling an inquisitive reader might meet gets its own heading and a short route back to the governing article. Orghialla accordingly joins Oirghialla and the anglicized Oriel as signposts to the Airgialla entry, which describes the conquest of the kingdom by the Uí Néill and its conversion into a base for their expansion.

:::
```

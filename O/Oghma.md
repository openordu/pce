``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Oghma is the modern Irish spelling that MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the orator-warrior Ogma among the Tuatha Dé Danann. The main article counts him one of that people's three principal champions beside Lug Lámfhota and the Dagda, patron of poetry and eloquence, fabled maker of the ogham alphabet, his own name philologically bound to the script he created. A reader who meets the modernized form in a text should follow it to the fuller entry, which carries his honey-mouthed sobriquet, his family ties among the gods, and his leading part in the battle narrative of Mag Tuired. The spelling pair shows how the dictionary keeps older mythological names and their contemporary Irish dress separate on the page.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Óengus, with Oenghus as an alternate spelling, is the preferred Irish form of the name rendered in English as Angus, MacKillop notes. The dictionary treats the Irish-language spelling as a cross-reference to the longer article, rather than as an independent entry with its own narrative. No separate myth, genealogy, or episode attaches to the spelling itself; it functions as a typographical signpost pointing readers to the parent Angus article where the full mythology is set out, including the cycles of Angus Óg and the Brug na Bóinne tradition.

:::
```

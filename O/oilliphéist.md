::: magazinestyle
oilliphéist is a dragon-like monster from Irish oral tradition, named from the Irish words oil meaning great and prist meaning fabulous beast or reptile. In the best-known story, the oilliphéist dams the watercourse of the Shannon River on hearing that Saint Patrick has arrived to expel its kind from the land. In a comic sequel, the monster swallows a drunken piper who keeps playing inside it until the creature coughs him up. The Scottish Gaelic counterpart is uilepheist. The wider tradition also names related monsters caoránach, muirdris, and sinach.

:::

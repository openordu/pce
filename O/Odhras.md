``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Odhras is the modern spelling that MacKillop 2008 sets as a pointer to Odras. The article under that headword tells of the luckless mortal woman whom the Morrigan turns into a pool of water, and this row keeps the modernized form findable for readers who meet it in retellings. The dictionary's habit with modernized spellings is to file them bare, with the cross-reference doing all the work.

:::
```

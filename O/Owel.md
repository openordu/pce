``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Owel is the anglicized spelling MacKillop 2008 files as a pointer to the divine figure Eógabal, treated in full under that headword. The main article makes Eógabal a foster-son of the sea-god Manannán mac Lir and the parent, in most accounts, of the regional goddesses Áine and Grian of County Limerick, whose cult hills Cnoc Áine and Cnoc Gréine stand seven miles apart. Family lines vary across the sources, since other texts make Eógabal the grandfather of the two goddesses with Fer Í as their father, or place him as father with Fer Í as brother. He is likewise tied to the divine druid Fer Fidail, again as father or grandfather through Fer Í. Commenting on this tangle, T. F. O'Rahilly in 1946 proposed identifying Eógabal both with Fer Í and with the Eógan from whom Munster's Eóganacht dynasty took its name. The article closes with his death at the hand of Ferches. Readers meeting Owel in place-lore or modern Irish orthography should follow the spelling back to Eógabal for the full account.

:::
```

::: magazinestyle
tarbh uisge, the Scottish Gaelic water-bull, is the malevolent bull of Highland folklore. By night the creature rises from the sea to menace the countryside. The beast is huge and black, with a head the color of a thunder cloud and nostrils redder than lightning. Its great bull neck parts the waves as it swims. Calves born with split ears are said to have been sired by the tarbh uisge. Farmers traditionally slaughter such calves at birth to spare the herd disaster. The wider family also includes the tarroo ushtey.

:::

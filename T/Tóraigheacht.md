``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tóraigheacht is the conventional opening word in the titles of a broad family of early Irish narratives. The word means a pursuit. Printed variant forms include Tóraidheacht, Tóraidecht, Tóraigecht, Tóruigheacht, and Tóraíocht. The dictionary sends readers on to the wooing tales and the adventure tales for comparison.

:::
```

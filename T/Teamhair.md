::: magazinestyle
Teamhair is the Modern Irish word for the royal site of Tara. The dictionary prints the head as a one-line cross-reference. The fuller record of the hill, its halls, and its legends sits under the Tara node. Readers of Modern Irish texts meet this spelling where English writers print Tara.

:::

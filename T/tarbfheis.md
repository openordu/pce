::: magazinestyle
tarbfheis, with the variants tarbhfheis and tarb feis, names the Irish term MacKillop records for the bull feast. The phrase translates as bull feast. The full record of the bull-feast tradition sits under the headword bull, with related coverage under kingship.

:::

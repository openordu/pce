``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tír na mBéo is the Land of the Living, where life never ends. Printed forms include na mBeo, inna Beo, Tir-nan-Beo, and Tir-nam-béo. It is one of several far lands settled by the semi-divine Tuatha Dé Danann. Their defeat by the Milesians drove them to those distant shores. Much of Irish oral tradition paints it as an otherworldly paradise. In that telling it is little to be told apart from Tir na nóg and Tir Tairngire. Lug Lámfhota fetched his sword Frecraid, the Answerer, from this land.

:::
```

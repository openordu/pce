::: magazinestyle
tunnerez noz is a Breton death-omen figure related to the washer at the ford tradition. MacKillop reads the form as a probable Breton recasting of the death omen known across the broader Celtic oral record. The principal record of that washer tradition sits under washer at the ford.

:::

::: magazinestyle
Tuirn is a variant form of the name Uirne. The dictionary prints it among the variants Uirne, Tuireann, Tuirreann, and Tuiren. Uirne is aunt to Fionn mac Cumhaill and bore his famous hounds Bran and Sceolang by magic. The fuller record sits under the Uirne head.

:::

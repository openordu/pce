Tuaim Tenba is a variant form of the name Dind Rig. The dictionary prints the head as a one-line cross-reference. Dind Rig was a great Leinster fortress on the Barrow. Its destruction story belongs to Labraid Loingsech. The fuller record of the fortress sits under the Dind Rig head.

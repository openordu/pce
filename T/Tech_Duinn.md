``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tech Duinn is the Irish House of Donn. The name glosses exactly as Donn's house. It marks the Otherworld as the land of the dead, the realm Donn rules. Some texts set the place beyond mortal geography. Others fix it on a rocky islet close to Dursey Island. That islet stands at the far western tip of the Beare peninsula in County Cork. Even when the site stays vague, the name keeps its link with Munster. MacKillop records the name as a plain Irish title. Donn holds rule over the dead, and the islet is literally his house. Monaghan's account holds the darker face of the place. Tech Duinn was a place of darkness and dread, not a happy western paradise. Later tradition often painted the Otherworld as a land of delight. The house of Donn kept the older, grimmer doctrine. The dead passed into the first ancestor's ground, not into a summer land. The name thus anchors the whole Irish map of death in one grey island.

:::
```

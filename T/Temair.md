``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Temair is the Irish spelling of Tara. The form Temuir carries the same name. Both stand beside the anglicized Tara for the great royal hill of the high kings. A second place hides in the same cluster of names. Temair Luachra, also written Temuir Luachra, was the royal seat of the south Munster kings. MacKillop names Eochaid mac Luchta as its special lord. The site possibly stood in Kerry. Some commentators also make it the residence of Cú Roi, the magician king of the west. The pair of names can confuse, for one is Tara's Irish dress and the other a Munster seat. Temair belongs to Tara and its kingship ground. Temair Luachra belongs to the Munster border and its own kings. Readers meet the plain Temair most often in the older Irish texts. The full Tara story stands under its own head.

:::
```

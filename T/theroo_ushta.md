``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
theroo ushta is a Manx-Gaelic spelling for the Manx water-bull. The form stands as a variant of tarroo ushtey, the standard modern spelling. MacKillop records it as an epithet of the Gaulish Mars in the intermediate tradition. The principal record sits under tarroo-ushtey.

:::
```

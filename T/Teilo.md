::: magazinestyle
Teilo, the Welsh form of the name Eludd, was a sixth-century Welsh saint. He lived as a contemporary of Dewi Sant and was known for zeal and asceticism. His work lay in south Wales, and his cult reached across to Brittany. Wells, chapels, and churches in great number carry his name. The centre of his cult is Llandeilo Fawr in Carmarthenshire. Modern scholars reject Geoffrey of Monmouth's twelfth-century claim that Teilo served as archbishop of Dol in Brittany. A twelfth-century Life of Teilo comes down in two versions.

:::

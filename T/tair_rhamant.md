``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Tair Rhamant is the Welsh name for three medieval prose romances. The name stands for the Three Romances in Welsh. The tales are Geraint ac Enid, Owein, and Peredur fab Efrawg. Each tale adapts a verse romance that the poet Chretien de Troyes wrote in French. The Welsh authors did not translate word for word. They reshaped the borrowed plots with native Welsh storytelling habits. They also tied the action to the Welsh landscape around them. The three texts survive in two medieval manuscript books. The White Book of Rhydderch holds copies of the tales. The Red Book of Hergest holds another version of them. These pages show the meeting of two worlds. The continental courtly romance and the older insular Celtic legend come together. Some readers trace old hero legends in these stories. The name Owein lingers from a Britain of an earlier time. These prose pieces count as some of the most studied works in early Welsh literature.

:::
```

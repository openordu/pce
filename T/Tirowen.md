``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tirowen is an anglicized form of the Irish kingdom name Tír Eógain. The dictionary prints the head as a one-line cross-reference. The fuller record of the land of Eógan sits under the Tír Eógain head. That kingdom belonged to the northern Uí Néill federation.

:::
```

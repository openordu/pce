``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tír Chonaill is the land of Conall, a kingdom named after Conall Gulban. Gulban was a son of Niall Noigiallach, the taker of the Nine Hostages. The kingdom belonged to the northern federation of the Uí Néill. Its extent runs beyond modern County Donegal. Its reach covers the land from Lough Foyle down to Ballysadare in County Sligo. Writers also print the name as Tir Connell.

:::
```

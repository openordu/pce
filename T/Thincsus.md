::: magazinestyle
Thincsus is recorded as a sometime epithet of the Gaulish Mars. The name survives in a single dictionary line without further discussion. It pairs the Roman war god with a local Celtic cult title. The worship of Mars in Celtic lands often absorbed native gods of this kind.

:::

téte brec [twinkling hoard] is a MacKillop cross-reference to the Concobar mac Nessa legend. MacKillop reads the form as a cross-pointer to the king of Ulster and the Emain Macha tradition. The principal record of the Ulidian king sits under Concobar mac Nessa.

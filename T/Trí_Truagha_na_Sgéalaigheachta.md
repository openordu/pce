``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Trí Truagha na Sgéalaigheachta is the Irish title meaning the three sorrows of storytelling. English writers print the name as The Three Sorrows of Storytelling, with sorrowful-stories wording in some heads. The dictionary head is itself a routing entry that names the three tragedies. The first is the Deirdre story, the great Ulster tale of doomed beauty. The second is Oidheadh Chlainne Lir, the tragic fate of the children of Lir. The third is Oidheadh Chlainne Tuireann, the tragedy that befell Tuireann's offspring. Together the three form the most famous sorrow-triad of Irish storytelling.

:::
```

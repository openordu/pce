``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tegau Eurfron is a paragon of the Welsh Arthurian court. Tradition ranks her among the three splendid maidens who sat beside Arthur. The other two are Dyff and Enid. She is the wife of Caradog Freichfras, the knight of the strong arm. MacKillop calls her golden-haired, a pattern of beauty and virtue in one person. One story tests both gifts at once. She rescued Caradog from a poisonous snake. The serpent bit her on the breast as she saved him. The wounded breast was removed and replaced with a piece of gold. Her second wonder is her cloak. It is named among the treasures of ancient Britain. No woman false to her marriage vow could wear it. The cloth would not cover an untrue wife. The test made Tegau the very figure of married fidelity. Her golden hair and her golden breast belong to the same emblem of worth.

:::
```

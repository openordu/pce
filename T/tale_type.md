``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
tale type names the cross-classification MacKillop records for Celtic folktales against the international Aarne-Thompson-Uther index. The tale type framework groups stories by plot and motif across cultures, letting scholars compare Celtic examples with their counterparts elsewhere. Celtic examples appear under many AT-U numbers; MacKillop points the reader to the related entries for fuller record.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Teyrnon Twrf Liant holds the lordship of Gwent Is Coed in the Mabinogi's first branch. His second name means the roar of the sea in Welsh. The name traces back to the British Tigemonos, the great king. Some texts render it Teirnyon. Teyrnon and his wife become foster-parents to the stolen child of Rhiannon and Pwyll. The boy's hair gleams gold, so Teyrnon names him Gwri Gwallt Euryn, Golden Hair. Monaghan keeps the wonder of his court. On Beltane night a huge claw came down from the sky at the newborn foal. Teyrnon slashed at it, and the claw dropped the child it carried. The couple raised the boy as their own. In his fourth year Teyrnon saw his likeness to Pwyll. He restored the child to his birth parents, and they named him Pryderi. The rescue of the foal thus returns a lost prince to his house. MacKillop anchors the whole tale inside the Mabinogi's first branch.

:::
```

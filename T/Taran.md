``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Taran is a Welsh hero named in the Mabinogion. The tale leaves him a survivor of the ruinous battle between Bendigeidfran and Matholwch. Bendigeidfran was the giant king of Britain, and Matholwch held the kingship of Ireland. Taran's story runs on through his son, for he fathered Glunen, a name also written Gluneu. The name Taran carries the Welsh word for thunder. Students of names hold him philologically related to Taranis, the great thunder god of Gaul and Britain. MacKillop records a second life for the name. The Welsh-influenced tales fed a modern series of juvenile novels. Lloyd Alexander built Taran Wanderer and its sequels on figures of this world. The first book appeared in New York in 1980. So the old hero lives twice over. One life is the scattered tale of the Mabinogi's great battle. The other is the bright cover of a children's classic. Few other Welsh names have crossed that bridge so whole.

:::
```

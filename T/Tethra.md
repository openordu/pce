``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tethra is a chief of the Fomorians, the semi-divine powers who fought the gods of Ireland. MacKillop calls him the nephew of Forgall Manach. In the Fomorian role he carries a dread sword and uses it savagely. He falls all the same in the Second Battle. That great fight of Mag Tuired is told in the Cath Maige Tuired. The story does not end with that defeat. Before the Mythological Cycle fixed his literary shape, Tethra was evidently a divinity. The old sense of him points to the sea. In later texts he rises again, now as a deity of the happy Otherworld. Some tales make him the consort of the battle goddess Badb. In that role he stands in the place of Néit. Monaghan's entry keeps the title of fairy king of the Otherworld. The record thus holds him twice, a fallen Fomorian chief and an otherworldly king. Scholars know little else about him. That thinness is the story itself, a god dimmed into a battle figure.

:::
```

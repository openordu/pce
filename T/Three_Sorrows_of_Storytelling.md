::: magazinestyle
Three Sorrows of Storytelling is the English name of a famous tale triad. A second printed form gives Erin's trio of mournful narratives. The Irish head form is Trí Truagha na Sgéalaigheachta. That record names the three tragedies it gathers. The Deirdre story stands first among them, beside the fates of the children of Lir and of Tuireann.

:::

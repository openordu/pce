::: magazinestyle
Tigernach, also printed Tighearnach or Tiarnach, comes from the Irish word for lord. Many early Irish churchmen and saints carried the name. The best known are two figures. One is the Tigernach who patronized the church of Clones in County Monaghan. The other is Tigernach Ua Braoin of Clonmacnoise, who died in 1088. Ua Braoin began compiling the Annals of Tigernach. His byname is sometimes anglicized as Tierney O'Breen.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tuiren is a cross-reference heading. The source points the reader to the Uirne entry. MacKillop lists Tuiren, Tuireann, and Tuirrann together as variant forms of Uirne, Fionn's aunt. The shared spelling with the hero Tuireann misleads. Tuireann is a different figure, the father of the tragic sons, and the dictionary warns the reader apart. Uirne, also called Tuiren, was mother to the hounds Bran and Sceolan. Those twin hounds became the favorite hunting dogs of the hero Fionn Mac Cumhaill.

:::
```

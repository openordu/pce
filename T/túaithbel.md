``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
túaithbel is the Old Irish word for withershins, the counterclockwise ritual course. MacKillop reads the form as the early Irish term for the lefthandwise turn. The full record of the counterclockwise ritual sits under the withershins headword.

:::
```

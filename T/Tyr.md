``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tyr is a sometime anglicization of the Irish word Tir, meaning land. The element appears in names such as Tyrone. It has no link with the Norse god who shares the spelling. The record of the word element sits under the Tir head. Tyrone keeps the element at its front. English forms of the name open with this prefix.

:::
```

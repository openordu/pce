::: magazinestyle
Tuirill is a variant form of the name Tuireann. The dictionary prints the head with the fuller forms Tuirill Bicreo and Bicrenn. The record of that father of three doomed sons sits under the Tuireann node. The Thurneysen study of 1918 prints the same variant forms.

:::

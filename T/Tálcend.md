``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tálcend is the nickname that the Fenian dialogues give to St Patrick. The name means adze-head and points to the shape of a bishop's mitre. Printed forms include Taillcenn and the corruptions Tailkenn, Talkenn, and Talkend. The word mocks the hard-headed refusal of the saint to hear the pagan side. Oisin and Cailte trade sharp words with him across those dialogues. A separate record keeps the corrupt spellings as its own head.

:::
```

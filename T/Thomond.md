::: magazinestyle
Thomond is the anglicized name for North Munster. Its Old Irish forms include Tuadmumu and Tuadmumain. The Modern Irish form is Tuathmhumhain. The O'Brien petty kingdom bore the name, and it broke away from greater Munster in the twelfth century. From soon after the conquest of 1169 until the reign of James I, Thomond counted as part of Connacht. When Ireland was shired in the seventeenth century, most of Thomond became County Clare. The modern county town of Ennis sits at the heart of that old kingdom. Historically the name also covers parts of neighbouring Limerick and Tipperary.

:::

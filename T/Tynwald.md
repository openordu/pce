::: magazinestyle
Tynwald is the man-made mound near St John's Church on the Isle of Man. The site stands a few miles from Peel, close to the centre of the island. Tradition holds that each parish on the island sent earth for its construction. The mound has served public ceremony from the earliest memory of the island. There the island legislature, the House of Keys, has its laws read aloud. The yearly Tynwald Fair falls on 5 July, a date once read as Midsummer's Day. The dictionary links the mound to the idea of the omphalos, the symbolic centre.

:::

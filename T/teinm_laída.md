``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
teinm laída, with the variants laédajaeda, laido, laogha, laegda, and láida, names an incantation of divination MacKillop records for the fili and their peers in early Ireland. The compound translates as chewing open of the pith. In the Fenian Cycle the rite is tied to the powers Fionn mac Cumhaill wins by gnawing his thumb and chanting. T. F. O'Rahilly in 1946 felt the rite was Fionn's prerogative alone. The tenth-century Sanas Cormaic cites teinm laída alongside dichetal do chennaib and imbas forosnai as the three methods for obtaining hidden knowledge.

:::
```

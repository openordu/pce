``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
tàcharan is the Scottish Gaelic word for changeling. MacKillop reads the form as the Scottish Gaelic counterpart of the broader tradition of fairy-substituted children. The principal record of that tradition sits under the changeling headword.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
In Celtic usage a taboo is a sacred restriction. The Irish record calls such a rule geis, and the plural form is geasa. A king had to keep his taboos and his sacred promises. Breaking one could put the well-being of the whole land at risk. The entry GEIS covers these rules in full. MacKillop treats the term within the wider tradition of sacred Celtic restriction, citing the geis as the principal Irish form.

:::
```

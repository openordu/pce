``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Táin is the conventional opening word in the titles of a whole class of Old Irish stories. The word itself means a cattle raid. English readers often shorten the name of the great Cooley raid to The Tain. Other narratives carry titles built on the same opening word. The prefix marks the tale-type before any single raid is named. The class sits beside other title-prefix words in this dictionary.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tír is the Irish word for land. It opens countless real and imagined place-names as their first element. English writers once anglicized the element as Tyr-. The dictionary sends the plain sense of the word to its mag entry. The element marks territories and otherworld lands alike. Readers meet it across the whole place-name alphabet of this book.

:::
```

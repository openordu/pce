``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tuadmumu is an Old Irish form of the name of North Munster. The dictionary also prints the variant Tuadmumain alongside it. English writers render the same territory as Thomond. A separate Modern Irish form, Tuathmhumhain, carries the name forward. The fuller record of the kingdom sits under the Thomond node.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Tigernmas is a legendary high king of early Ireland. His name also appears as Tighernmas and Tiernmas. MacKillop credits him with two great firsts. He introduced the smelting of gold to Ireland, founding one of its oldest crafts. He also set up the worship of Crom Cruach, the grim idol god. Monaghan remembers that cult as cruel. The worship of the idol was the dark mark of his reign. The king thus stands at the head of both the gold craft and the darkest cult of the old tales. Few Irish kings carry so sharp a double name.

:::
```

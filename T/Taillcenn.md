``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Taillcenn and Taillkenn are corrupt spellings of Tálcend. Tálcend is the nickname the Fenian dialogues give to St Patrick. The word means adze-head. The pun touches his bishop's mitre and his hard-headed refusal to hear the pagan side.

:::
```

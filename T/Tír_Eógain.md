::: magazinestyle
Tír Eógain is the land of Eógan, a kingdom named after Eógan son of Niall Noigiallach. Like Tir Chonaill it belonged to the northern Uí Néill federation. The kingdom matches modern County Tyrone, with parts of Derry and Donegal. Its power was felt for many miles beyond those borders. Later centuries made Tir Eógain the realm of the O'Neill family. That family, a branch of Cenél Eógain, took its name from Niall Glúndub, who died in 919. The anglicized spelling Tirowen appears beside the Irish head.

:::

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Trojan legends connect the medieval Celts to the fall of Troy. Welsh tradition said Brutus the Trojan founded Britain. It claimed a Trojan ancestry for the Britons. The Irish Lebor Gabála Érenn traced the Gaels through a Mediterranean descent. Middle Irish writers made Troy their own subject. Togail Troí turns the destruction of Troy into Gaelic. The tale drew on Dares Phrygius. These works anchor the Celtic world to the classical past.

:::
```

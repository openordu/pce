``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
The Tuath Dé Danann is the Irish race of the gods. The name of the race is tied to the goddess Dana, the mother of the gods. The people of the síd shelter in the mounds. They dwell also beyond the sea. They are said to master the weather. They may change their own form at will. They never age and they never fall ill. Early scholars set them beside the heroes as ancient gods. Some writers later named them fallen angels. Others made them a mortal line of the grandsons of Noah. The good god of the immortals is named Dagda. Patrick met them, and spoke as the old faith faded. They fall by the sword in the sagas. 

:::
```

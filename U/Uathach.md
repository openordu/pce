``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Uathach, sometimes Uathach of the Glen, is the daughter of Scáthach. Her name is the Irish word for a spectre. She is a sometime lover of Cúchulainn. When he arrives for training, Uathach welcomes him at first. Scáthach later promises her to Cúchulainn without requiring any bride-price. Forgetful of his strength, the hero breaks her finger while she serves him. Her scream brings her current lover running. Cúchulainn makes short work of that rival. To make amends he takes the man's place, including becoming Uathach's lover herself. The Ulster Cycle lists her among its frequent personages.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ulaid, Ulaidh, with genitive Ulad, is the people of early Ireland who named Ulster. They held mainly the eastern part of the later province. Their capital stood at Emain Macha. At various periods their hegemony reached south to the Boyne and west to Co. Leitrim. Their strength faded once the fifth century AD had passed. The people called themselves Clanna Rudraige. That name was thought to hold the particle ruad-, meaning red. The Ulster Cycle keeps this people at the centre of its heroic world.

:::
```

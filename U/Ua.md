``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ua, Ua- is the Irish word for a grandchild or spiritual descendant. It opens the early Irish patronymics, as in Ruaidri Ua Conchobair. The word does not serve as a family marker. Alphabetical order follows the given name, so the family line gets no marker. The plural form is Uí. The head cross-notes the kinship prefixes ap-, mab-, mac, and vab. Readers meet Ua- most often in the names of annalistic kings.

:::
```

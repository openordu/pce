::: magazinestyle
The Ulster Cycle is the dominant body of early Irish saga tales. It ranks as one of the four great groups of the old literature. The cycle centres on the royal seat of Emain Machae. Its hero is Cú Chulainn, chief champion of the Ulaid. The long story called Táin Bó Cuailnge anchors the whole complex. This cattle raid tale stands as the great war epic. Thurneysen listed near eighty items for this group. Later pieces push the total close to one hundred. Many tales survive only in heavily rewritten versions. Anonymous writers kept reshaping the older material for centuries. Keating retold several old stories in the seventeenth century. Later writers still added new works to the stock.

:::

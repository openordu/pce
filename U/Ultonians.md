::: magazinestyle
Ultonians is an obsolete English form for the Ulstermen. It comes after the medieval Latin Ultonia. The record of the province sits under the Ulster node. The adjective survives in older studies of the Ulster Cycle. The Ulster Cycle carries the heroic literature of that people.

:::

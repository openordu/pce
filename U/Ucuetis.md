``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ucuetis is a Gaulish deity. The dictionary usually shows him beside his consort Bergusia. The pair appears so at the shrine of Alesia in eastern France, in Burgundy. Some representations give him a hammer. His images there sit with thededication of the stone cutters guild. That attribute suggests a divine patron of craftsmen. The shrine find of 1908 carried this pair's dedication in stone. His consort carries her own record in this dictionary.

:::
```

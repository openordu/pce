::: magazinestyle
urisk is the Lowland Scots anglicization of the Scottish Gaelic ùruisg, a Highland water-sprite. MacKillop records the form as the vernacular anglicization used in Lowland Scots speech. The ùruisg inhabits rivers, lochs, and seashores, and overlaps with the broader fuath family of Highland water-spirits. The wider spirit class sits under fuath, and the full record of the Highland water-sprite tradition lives there.

:::

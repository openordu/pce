::: magazinestyle
Uthr Bendragon, printed also as Uther Pendragon, is the father of Arthur. The Welsh elements of the name mean terrible, head, and leader. Merlin arranged the intrigue by which Arthur was begotten on Eigr. The same mother figure appears in the store as Igerna. Welsh tradition knew the name before Geoffrey of Monmouth wrote in the twelfth century.

:::

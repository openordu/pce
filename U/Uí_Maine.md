``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Uí Maine, also printed Mhaine and anglicized Hy Many, is a great early tribe. Its lands lay in east Galway and the south of Roscommon. The tribe was not of Goidelic origin. Its learned families built a Goidelic pedigree anyway, tracing descent from Maine Mór, whose father was Eochu. King Marcán took Créd as his wife. Créd was the lover of Cano. An Ui Maine king named Eochu Rond fights Cúchulainn in the saga record. The Book of Uí Maine is a late fourteenth-century miscellany. Macalister printed it in Dublin in 1942. The manuscript gathers genealogies and other materials of the tribe. Kelleher's 1971 Celtica study covers the annals and the pedigrees through the year 1225. The tribe's non-Goidelic root and built pedigree mark it among the great hybrid lines.

:::
```

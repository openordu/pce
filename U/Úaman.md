::: magazinestyle
Úaman, also rendered Sídh Úamain or Sídh Úamuin, is a Connacht sidh of Ethal Anbúail in MacKillop's Dictionary of Celtic Mythology. The father of Cáer (1) lives there, and the lover of Angus Óg in the Brug na Bóinne tradition. MacKillop's article is a brief two-line note, identifying the site by its three name-forms and naming the household attached to it, without narrating an independent episode. The full story of Cáer's transformation into a swan and her union with Angus Óg is set out in the parent article on Cáer, and the entry functions as a place-pointing cross-reference there.

:::

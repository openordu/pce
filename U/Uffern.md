``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Uffern is the Welsh otherworld under its Latin-text spelling Uffem. Walter Map and Giraldus Cambrensis use the name in their Latin writings. Map lived from about 1140 to 1209. Giraldus lived from about 1146 to 1223. The label is a semantic dismissal with unmistakable Christian influence. The word now serves as the Welsh term for the Christian hell. The record of the otherworld itself sits under the Annwfn head.

:::
```

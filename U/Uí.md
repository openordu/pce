``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Uí is the plural of the kinship prefix Ua-. The word means grandchildren or spiritual descendants. It stands as a separated prefix in many tribal and family names of early Ireland. The singular form is Ua-. Modern Irish shortens the singular to Ó, and English to O'. The dictionary cross-notes the prefix mac for comparison. The great tribal names Uí Maine and Uí Néill open with this word.

:::
```

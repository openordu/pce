``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Urmhumhain is the Modern Irish name for Ormond. The dictionary prints the head as a one-line cross-reference. The fuller record of that kingdom sits under the Ormond node. Ormond means East Munster, the O'Brien lordship of the south. The spelling Umhumhain drops the same initial vowel in some texts.

:::
```

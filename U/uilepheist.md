``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
uilepheist is the Scottish Gaelic counterpart of the oilliphéist, the Celtic water-monster. MacKillop reads the form as a direct Scottish-Gaelic equivalent. The principal record of the water-monster tradition sits under the oilliphéist headword.

:::
```

``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Uchtdealb is the rejected mistress of Iollan Eachtach. Her name means an empty, mean-spirited breast. She transforms Uime, an aunt of Fionn, into a dog. The Uirne record prints her again as the cast-off mistress of Iollann Éachtach. In that variant the transformed aunt bears the hounds Bran and Sceolan.

:::
```

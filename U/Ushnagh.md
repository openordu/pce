``` tab [group1:Info]
::: magazinestyle
Ushnagh, Usna, Usnech, and Usney are variant forms of the name Uisnech. The dictionary prints the head as a two-line cross-reference. The record of the hill of divisions sits under the Uisnech node. The genitive form carries its own record as well.

:::
```
